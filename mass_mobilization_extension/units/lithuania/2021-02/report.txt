Lithuania — February 2021

Methods and scope

The registered worker rollout contains 22 actual search actions. All 22 were recovered from that rollout and matched to the saved search log by original event ID, exact query/action, and ordered result headers: 443 headers total. Search coverage used Lithuanian and English terms, month-wide and weekly date windows, Vilnius and regional-city terms, small-business demonstrations, teacher/medical/worker actions, 16 February national-day terms, and Russia/Belarus solidarity searches. Search headings support discovery only. This open-web first pass is not LexisNexis-equivalent and does not prove that no other protest occurred.

Current event leads

LTU202102-E001: One full contemporaneous LRT article was read. It reports symbolic small-business protest actions on 5 February in Vilnius, Klaipėda and Panevėžys, objecting to quarantine restrictions and unequal treatment of small businesses versus large retailers and calling for safe reopening. The article reports that about 50 people joined the Panevėžys action over one hour. It does not establish whether this means cumulative arrivals or simultaneous attendance, and it is not a precise lower bound. Klaipėda had a few protesters plus a few supporters; Vilnius turnout is not reported. No city counts are pooled. A social-media call and same-day actions are documented, but whether the three sites form one national episode or separate local gatherings remains unresolved; eligibility is pending_unit. Police prevented wreath placement at a government-representative office in Panevėžys and participants relocated. The article does not establish protest-caused policy accommodation, dispersal, arrests or protester violence; these coded fields remain unknown.

LTU202102-E002: Search-result headlines identify a possible 16 February Vilnius protest against quarantine requirements associated with teacher Astrauskaitė. No article body was read, so physical date/location, turnout, demands and state response remain unverified; the lead remains pending_evidence.
