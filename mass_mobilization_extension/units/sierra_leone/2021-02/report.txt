Sierra Leone — February 2021
First-pass open-web dossier; pending review and official-baseline gate.

Search coverage
The frozen native log contains 54 actual search actions (54 unique query IDs, 52 distinct query texts), 928 ordered result headers and 517 distinct exact URL strings. Searches included four weekly February windows; general protest/demonstration/riot/strike terms; local outlets (Politico SL, SwitSalone, Calabash, Awoko, SierraLoaded, AYV, A-Z and Sierra Leone Observatory); port/Water Quay, teachers/lecturers/students, transport/fuel, COVID/NACOVERC workers; police, State House and human-rights searches; and follow-up around the January university dispute, Njala, the CID/youth lead and March/neighboring-month boundaries. Location probes named Freetown, Bo, Kenema, Makeni and Koidu. Language probes included a Krio-oriented query and a French-language query, but no fluent/manual local-language review was completed. Open-web search is not LexisNexis-equivalent, and these searches do not establish exhaustive or nationwide absence.
