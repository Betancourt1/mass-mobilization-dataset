Azerbaijan — February 2021
Open-web first pass; pending coordinator review

Coverage and method
Captured 47 web-search responses: 46 with result headers and one explicit empty response (Q040), totaling 906 aligned headers. The first-pass scope included English, Azerbaijani and Russian; generic national and weekly searches; Baku, Ganja, Sumqayit and Lankaran/regional searches; labor, student, political-prisoner and gender/femicide searches; episode-detail queries; and January/February and February/March boundary checks. Five early actual responses (three initial searches and two retries) were recovered as Q042-Q046 with observed clocks and aligned headers. Full capture recovery then restored 27 of the 46 saved rows from their original worker search events and appended one previously uncaptured response as AZERBAIJAN-RECOVER-001; the other 19 rows were preserved unchanged. Q040 was matched to the captured empty response and was not duplicated. The provider did not expose result totals. This is not an exhaustive search and is not equivalent to LexisNexis.

Five current-unit source cards contain body-read evidence: VOA's 4 and 9 February reports, selected OC Media text for the 4 February action, the Netherlands Ministry of Foreign Affairs COI report, and Scholars at Risk's Lankaran report. Reads were partial where recorded. OC Media says its interviewee had told VOA, so those two reports are not independent confirmation. The COI is a later secondary account: it gives six activists for 4 February and about ten women for 19 February, with media citations; the original reports behind the 19 February note were not opened. Scholars at Risk draws on ACLED and local accounts, so it is not an independent turnout source. Search-only titles are preserved as discovery leads; no snippet is treated as body evidence. Restored headers retain the Milli.az Fountain Square lead, OC Media/Eurasianet police-relocation titles, and five further Turan, OC Media and JAMnews cards about Lankaran students, job losses/unpaid leave, a Baku student-protest action and health workers. These titles do not establish article bodies, dates, episode relationships, attendance, demand recipients or February relevance; no body facts or new February event are inferred.

Candidate and exclusions
One February 9 Baku family action is retained as pending threshold evidence. The VOA report documents a domestic political demand despite the US Embassy venue: a family representative said Azerbaijani state offices had already been approached, and the banner called for release/acquittal in domestic political-prisoner cases. The request for foreign organizations or embassies to observe proceedings is supplementary. Attendance, physical end and all seven state-response dimensions remain unknown; taking signs does not by itself establish dispersal, and the embassy's statement is not Azerbaijani-state accommodation.

The February 1 Lankaran student action is excluded from candidate events because the total crowd is unknown and >=50 is not established. Four activists are named/detained; one participant says three were together at detention. About 100 describes student complaints, not attendees. Recovered Turan titles add no attendance or independent confirmation because their dates, bodies and relationship to the episode remain unknown. The February 4 feminist action is below threshold at six according to the later COI report; VOA and OC Media disagree with the COI about the venue, and OC's interviewee had spoken to VOA. The February 19 female-relatives action is about ten according to the COI and is kept separate from February 9. The grievance over killings of women is not coded as state_response.killings. The AzTV individual-action lead remains unverified: S009's 9 February publication date is copied from the January unit's alternate AzTV URL, and S010 has no captured publication date; neither establishes incident date, crowd or demand recipient. No onset is assigned from these labels. The January 8 wording in one progress note was a transcription error; the January unit's 8 February continuation lead remains unverified.

No continuation record is created; this is a coverage result, not evidence that no follow-up occurred. The Lankaran student action is February-owned, not a January continuation. March 8 women's-action leads are treated as next-month activity, not as continuity from February 4.
