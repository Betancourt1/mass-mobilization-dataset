Azerbaijan — 2020-12 (research cutoff: 2026-10-07, America/Mexico_City)

Methods and coverage

The researcher ran 42 actual managed public-web searches. Discovery covered English, Azerbaijani and Russian; national queries; four date-window searches in English, four in Azerbaijani and four in Russian; local publisher probes for Report.az, APA, Azadliq and Meydan; and targeted searches about employment/unpaid leave, missing-service-member families, veterans, school/education and December public events. Coverage included Baku, national-level references and a Salyan workforce lead. Result URLs, titles, refs and order are retained in the header-only search log. The complete rendered query responses remain at the immutable paths and SHA256 values listed in raw_evidence_references.json. The first English/Azerbaijani/Russian batch had one combined, unattributed response; the same headers were recorded transparently against each submitted query as the protocol permits. Search totals and native query/response clocks were not exposed.

Search indexes did not reliably honor the requested date windows: results included 2019/July 2020 protests, April 2021 stories, Armenian protests and diaspora activity. Event dates were therefore checked against the actual available result/page content. The work used open-web methods only; it did not replicate LexisNexis or systematically read every regional newspaper archive. Search noise and unsearched district archives limit claims of coverage.

Findings

There are zero verified December 2020 candidates in the documented method. This is not a claim that no protests occurred. The identified 10 December Baku victory parade was a state military ceremony; its published count of military personnel is not a protest crowd, and no public demand to the Azerbaijani state was reported. It is recorded as excluded.

Workplace results concerned April-era wage/unpaid-leave actions and did not establish a December date or, on the available report, a demand for Azerbaijani state policy. A possible Salyan item remains search-only: its exact date, simultaneous crowd threshold and target are not confirmed; the attempted browser retrieval returned a technical 520 error, not a denial, and the single pre-amendment shell fallback did not receive an HTTP response. No retry was made.

On servicemembers, an RFE/RL report published 25 November says families were seeking missing soldiers at hospital casualty lists and reports no public protest signs in Baku at that time. This cannot rule out later December activity. A separate OC Media account reports a Baku gathering by families of around 20 missing service members in April 2021, with a demand for information from Azerbaijani authorities and foreign leaders; it is later than this unit and does not establish 50 actual participants. Azadliq surfaced same-day April coverage, but its relationship to OC Media is not established. Armenian families protesting at Armenia’s Ministry of Defence were in Armenia and directed their appeal to Armenian officials; they are excluded from this Azerbaijan unit.

Continuations and response

No event with a documented onset before December and continued action in December was established. The November and April family evidence is recorded as continuity context, not merged into a December episode. With no December event candidate, protester violence, state response, accommodation, arrests, beatings, dispersal, ignoring, killings, shootings, end date and consequences remain unknown rather than coded negative.

Evidence and limits

Six relevant source cards were saved: three opened pages and three search-only results. The opened sources were the December 10 official parade notice, the November 25 RFE/RL report and the April 2021 OC Media family report. The local OC Media worker story could not be opened; its actual outcome and receipt history are retained. Written-origin relations are explicit only where a search result attributes the SIA report to Sputnik Armenia; the RFE/RL article identifies RFE/RL Azerbaijani Service reporting. The photo credit to Meydan TV on the worker story is not treated as a written-story origin. No AP, HRW, New Arab, Facebook or L’Orient material was opened.
