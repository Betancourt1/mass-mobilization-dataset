Azerbaijan, February 2024 — bounded, non-exhaustive open-web first pass.

Methods: Eight captured English, Azerbaijani and Russian searches covered the month, protest/demonstration/strike/wage terms, and the Feb. 7 early presidential election. Exact rendered tool output and invocation parameters are preserved privately; parsed header projections are discovery leads. Native search event IDs, clocks, result counts and refs are unavailable.

Result: No verified February public protest gathering was established. Election reporting and an opposition boycott lead do not document a public gathering or a protest demand at the state. Routine campaign/election activity was not turned into an event. No qualifying candidate or continuation is recorded.

Coverage limits: Search-engine retrieval cannot replicate LexisNexis All News; no result counts are exposed. The searches are not an exhaustive press review, and failure to find a candidate is not evidence none occurred. Official baseline and universe remain pending.
