Armenia — February 2024 open-web research

Methods and coverage: 9 actual queries were preserved with exact invocation objects and exact rendered outputs. Queries used English, Armenian and Russian generic protest/demo/strike/march terms, half-month windows, refugee/workplace sector terms, regional terms and one local-outlet search. Parsed headers were checked against preserved rendered outputs; all parsed headers matched, and two empty local-outlet searches retained the actual empty-result message. Native event IDs, refs, clocks, order and result totals are unavailable and remain null. Search results were weakly aligned to February and repeatedly returned later April–June stories. Open web is not LexisNexis and this pass is not exhaustive.

Findings: No qualifying in-country February gathering was established. One search-only lead describes a February 24 protest at the Armenian Consulate in Glendale, California, which lies outside Armenia; attendee count, exact demands and execution are unknown. It is retained as an excluded scope lead rather than counted as a domestic event. No absence claim follows from the search results.

Continuations: None confirmed.
