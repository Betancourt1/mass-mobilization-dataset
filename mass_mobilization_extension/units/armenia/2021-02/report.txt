Candidates and exclusions
11 separate provisional action candidates are recorded: 6 pending_unit and 5 pending_threshold. No lead is confidently excluded in this first pass; this is not a claim that every lead qualifies.

The new February11 E011 candidate records Verelq’s opened account of missing-servicemen relatives blocking a road near Republic Square and an earlier Government-building action. The report gives no assembled crowd count; the figure of 40 is missing servicemen, not protesters. The demand, relationship between the two locations/actions, physical end and >=50 threshold remain unresolved. January missing-soldier family episodes share a broader campaign key, but the February action is not treated as a continuous occupation or assigned to January by assumption.

The February12 Manukyan rally has UIC’s individual count of 1,800 in Freedom Square at 15:37. The February20 HSM rally has multiple qualitative “thousands” reports plus UIC’s direct 12,000 square snapshot at 15:40 and a separate approximately 800 café-roof estimate; they are not summed. The February23 evening march has UIC’s 1,204 count at 18:28, eight minutes after its 18:20 start; it is separate from that morning’s government-building attempt and more than 50 detentions are not used as attendance. The February25 opposition and PM-support crowds are separate records. Their source-specific estimates remain distinct: Al Jazeera’s “more than 20,000” opposition and “several thousand” PM supporters, RFE/RL’s estimated 20,000 PM supporters, and Azatutyun’s collective “thousands” for both without a split. No estimates are averaged. The PM rally remains pending_unit: Pashinyan attributes the crowd’s insistence against early elections to the audience, while his military orders and call for Gasparyan’s resignation are not participant demands; party composition and the narrower election exclusion need review.

For E003, Armenpress’s dated headline/body supports that the February22 rally ended at France Square that day. The article’s 20:40 line is its publication timestamp, not a verified physical end time. Attendance and a direct crowd demand remain unknown. The February25 parliament-side tent camp is recorded separately from discrete rallies. Armenpress’s February26 report says demonstrators demanding the PM’s resignation spent the night of February25–26 on Baghramyan Avenue and set up tents; this supports the event-specific removal demand for that reported overnight/tent demonstration. RFE/RL retrospectively attributes camp establishment to HSM. Onset turnout, exact physical owner, end and daily continuity remain unresolved. The AFP February27 “few thousand” Yerevan estimate is not assigned to Hetq’s Baghramyan rally because the locations/crowd linkage is unverified.

Missing-servicemen and conscript-parent searches found reports in both January and February, but common grievances do not determine physical continuity or month ownership.
