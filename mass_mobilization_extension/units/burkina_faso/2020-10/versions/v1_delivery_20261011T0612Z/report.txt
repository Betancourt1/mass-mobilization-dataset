Burkina Faso — 2020-10 open-web research report

Cutoff: 2026-10-07. Research/query date: 2026-10-11 UTC. Languages: French and English. Twenty managed web queries were logged for this unit. Search calls returned shared result sets for batched queries; the returned headers are not attributable to individual query strings, and the tool did not expose total result counts. No LexisNexis-equivalent search or full national archive sweep was performed.

Discovery used a generic country/month search, weekly/date-window searches, and targeted follow-up terms for housing/land, labor/transport, public services/education, health where leads arose, regional/provincial reporting, election-period civic activity, and neighboring-month continuity. Searches gave emphasis to Centre/Ouagadougou and Hauts-Bassins/Bobo-Dioulasso; other provincial mentions were pursued through surfaced leads. Search indexing produced false positives from adjacent years/months. No claim of an exhaustive census or absence of events is made.

Material leads and disposition:
• 20 October: three opened local reports describe one CADDL march from Bourse du Travail to the National Assembly over housing, plots, land appropriation and policy. One says “hundreds of women,” another “many people”; preserve as approximate evidence and proposed inclusion for independent review.
• 14–18 October: transport sources conflict. Wakat Séra documents a planned 96-hour UCRB action and policy dispute; Sidwaya says suspension after about half a day, while L’Express reports trucks blocking a Bobo-Dioulasso road through 18 October. No attendee count or distinct 50-person crowd is established; retain as pending evidence.
• 31 October: an opened Burkina24 report describes a CDAIP-led civic/labor meeting seeking justice/support for victims of the 2014 uprising and calling for an election boycott in response to state policy. It is not a party campaign rally; attendance is unknown, so threshold remains pending.

The eight core dossier files and copied base assignment are in this addendum. Candidate coding is investigator-proposed only. Review and official baseline validation remain pending; no acceptance or verified status is asserted.
