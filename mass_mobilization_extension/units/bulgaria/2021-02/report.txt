Bulgaria, February 2021 — first-pass research dossier

Scope and method
This unit records possible events beginning February 1–28, 2021, under the fixed October 7, 2026 cutoff. The search used all four weekly windows, English and Bulgarian Cyrillic terms, national and Sofia/Plovdiv/Varna/Burgas/Ruse/Blagoevgrad searches, retrospective and source-specific queries, and terms for unions, teachers, medical staff, public transport and miners. The 20 actual queries produced 485 ordered result headers. Exact arrays, URLs, refs, titles, query clocks and event IDs are preserved in search_log.jsonl and captures/. The search-result capture repair preserved prior rows and recorded the original serializer/recovery history; no search was repeated to fix capture metadata.

There are 37 source cards: 9 opened, 25 search_only, and 3 blocked. 6 current-unit body opens; 3 prior-unit-only consultations. Six current-unit articles were read (S001, S003, S004, S005, S006, S012). Three previously opened January-dossier sources were consulted only for continuity or the month boundary (S008, S010, S011). The newly surfaced result-only links remain discovery leads and are not used as body evidence. Nineteen body requests are recorded with fresh access receipts and manual publisher/origin review, indexed by the hash manifest in access_receipts/manifest.json; one additional AUBG receipt/review pair was saved but intentionally unused after the source-attempt ledger changed.

Candidates
E001 is the February 7 Sofia anti-government gathering and march outside the Council of Ministers. BNT reports a 16:00 gathering organized by mothers from System Kills Us and Protests 20-21 and a government-resignation demand (S001). Bgonair’s previously opened body identifies it as campaign day 214 (S011). Turnout sufficient for the 50-person rule, physical ending, protester violence, and event-specific state responses remain unknown; eligibility is pending_threshold. Keep the gathering separate from the longer campaign continuation C001.

E002 is the February 13 anti-fascist march in Sofia. AUBG Daily’s February 18 article reports an approximate 100 participants organized by Anti-Fascist Bulgaria (ANTIFA BG), and records a demand voiced during an activist speech opposing fascists entering government (S012). The event is proposed under political_behavior_process. The article says the demonstration had ended before Sofia municipality and police acted against the separate BNU Lukov March later that afternoon; those actions are not coded as a response to E002. Its end date is recorded as February 13; exact ending time, a precise headcount, and event-specific response fields remain unknown. BNR and BNT also describe a Sofia anti-fascist march (S004, S005); source independence and independent turnout corroboration remain open.

Continuation and month boundary
C001 tracks the anti-government/chief-prosecutor campaign begun July 9, 2020, based on the source history carried by S008. Bgonair describes February 7 as the 214th campaign day (S011); BNR describes February 19 as day 226 (S006). A separate BNR report calls a protest the 215th consecutive evening, but its exact date was not verified (S003). Separate assemblies remain distinct from the campaign record. X001 excludes the restaurant owners/staff gathering dated January 27 in the previously opened BNR body (S010). February 1 was the requested reopening date, not a confirmed new street action.

Access and unresolved leads
BTA S002 displayed a purchase-confirmation prompt instead of the report body, and exact bTV S007 and Baricada S009 URLs returned HTTP 403. These denials were not retried or bypassed. They leave the possible February 7 auto convoy and February 19 System Kills Us protest/march unresolved. Other result-only sources about the anti-fascist march, auto convoys, anti-government evenings, and restaurants are recorded in sources.jsonl with their exact URLs and refs; no fact is coded from their titles or snippets.

The nine gaps cover the auto-convoy lead, the unverified February 19 announcement, the date of BNR’s 215th-evening story, E001’s attendance threshold, event-specific endings/responses, the independence of E002 reporting, the January–February restaurant boundary, sector and regional search limits, and exact-URL access denials. See gaps.json for source-linked follow-up.

This is a bounded open-web first pass, not an exhaustive source-corpus search. LexisNexis and comprehensive local archives were not available; the official MM baseline/version and original codebook remain pending. Candidate coding is provisional for coordinator and official-baseline review. No official dataset integration, external contact, or publication occurred.
