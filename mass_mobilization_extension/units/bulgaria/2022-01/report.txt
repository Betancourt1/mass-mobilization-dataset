Bulgaria, January 2022 — first-pass research handoff (open-web pending review)

Scope and result

The assigned unit is Bulgaria (ccode 355), January 2022, queue row 1939. Eleven actual search invocations are preserved in search_log.jsonl. Twenty-five source cards are retained, including search-only leads, one opened article body, and scoped retrieval outcomes. One candidate is proposed for review: the 12 January gathering against the COVID-19 green-certificate system outside the National Assembly in Sofia. No official Mass Mobilization ID or approval is implied.

The full Sofia Globe article body was read after an exact-URL check and stable canonical/physical-sibling ledger review. It dates the event to 12 January, reports more than 1,000 participants, and says four police officers were hospitalized after injuries in a struggle. It reports that some protesters pushed the police cordon to the Parliament doors, that arrests of more aggressive participants occurred, and that National Security Service staff barricaded the entrance inside. These actions and responses are represented separately. The article does not identify injured protesters or report a police dispersal attempt; those facts remain unknown, not “none.”

A Dir.bg search-only preview attributes nine injured police officers to MVR and reports two demonstrators detained, not injured. That differs from Sofia Globe’s four hospitalized officers; the counts, timing and basis are unresolved. The search-only preview says the protest lasted more than six hours and moved to the Health Ministry. Whether this was the same crowd or a separate/connected gathering remains unresolved, so the candidate stays scoped to the Parliament crowd.

Turnout stays attributed: Sofia Globe says “more than 1000”; BTA’s search-only preview says “hundreds”; BNR’s search-only preview says “thousands.” No exact count or lower/upper numerical bounds are derived from these descriptions. The threshold is supported by the opened Sofia Globe report, while estimates and their bases remain a gap.

Dialogue and coding limits

The Sofia Globe article reports PM Kiril Petkov’s conditional offer to receive a delegation after quarantine if protesters sent a qualified medical working group. It does not report an agreed or completed meeting. Search-only Darik and Actualno titles suggest health-minister interaction/MPs meeting; a later BTA search-only preview says a working group formed after the protest met once and ended over disagreement about online broadcasting. The participants and relationship to a formal protest delegation are unverified, so accommodation remains unknown. The event’s physical end time is unknown. Do not merge the Health Ministry location into the Parliament gathering without evidence linking the participants.

Search coverage and source treatment

Searches explicitly covered 1–7, 8–14, 15–21 and 22–31 January; Bulgarian and English protest/strike terms; major cities; and labor, education/student, farming, health, transport, prices, and political/social-restriction themes. BNT, BTA, BNR, local outlets, Sofia Municipality and Parliament appeared in the search record. No other qualifying gathering was identified for the remaining weeks. This is a search result, not proof of absence; the open web and ordinary search-index date filters do not substitute for a Bulgarian press hemeroteca.

Source counts are not confirmation counts. Reuters’ article on Investing.com is one Reuters editorial origin. Separate articles from BNT, BTA and Darik remain grouped by outlet/origin for assessment; syndication or shared reporting is not treated as independent confirmation. 1 current-unit body opens; 0 prior-unit-only. Only the Sofia Globe main article body was read. BTA S002’s exact captured URL passed a fresh manual gate, but one ordinary GET failed at DNS before HTTP. BNT S003’s exact URL returned HTML metadata with zero visible lines. Reuters S007 returned a tool cache miss; its next access gate caught a changing Brazil sibling ledger, so no request was made. These are transport/tool/stability limitations, not publisher denials. Receipts R001–R010 and manual sidecars retain the gate/request history; BTA and BNT were not retried.

The 2 January Reddit thread records expectation/planning, not an observed event. A retrospective country-report search preview claims an education warning strike on 19 January involving over 100,000 workers; targeted contemporary queries did not establish occurrence or onset. It remains a gap and is not coded as an event. Do not date a strike from a retrospective claim alone. A captured search found a NOVA headline for another Vazrazhdane protest on 23 February, which is boundary context only and does not establish continuation of the January event.

Remaining review gaps

- BTA’s “hundreds,” Sofia Globe’s “more than 1000” and BNR’s “thousands” turnout descriptions and estimate bases need review.
- Injury counts for police differ between body-read and search-only accounts. No evidence establishes injuries to protesters; beatings, shootings, killings, protester violence and crowd dispersal remain unknown on this evidence.
- The PM’s conditional offer is not an agreed meeting. Search-only health-minister and BTA working-group leads need verification. The Health Ministry location’s relationship to the Parliament crowd is unresolved.
- The retrospective education-sector warning-strike claim requires contemporary occurrence/onset evidence.
- Non-detection in the other January weeks is limited by search-index coverage; this dossier is not an exhaustive newspaper-archive census.
