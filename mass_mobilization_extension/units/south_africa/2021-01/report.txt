South Africa — January 2021 open-web research
Cutoff: 2026-10-07 inclusive, America/Mexico_City.
Status: researched_open_web_pending_review. All records are provisional candidates or screening decisions; none has an official ID or approval.

Discovery and files: 34 single-query searches (Q001–Q034) cover all four January weeks, national and regional reporting, and labor, education, service delivery, housing, student, transport, COVID-policy, political and migration leads. Searches were also made in Afrikaans, isiZulu and isiXhosa. Source-language verification remained limited; other local languages were not systematically searched. The dossier holds 45 source cards: 28 opened, 12 search-only, 4 blocked and 1 not found. These are cards, not 45 independent confirmations.

Candidate findings:
• SA2101-E001 — 30 January beach-closure actions reported in Cape Town-area sites and Plettenberg Bay; Port Elizabeth was advertised but occurrence is unverified. The Muizenberg estimates conflict (police about 200; organizer 400–500; IOL almost 200); no sites are summed. Police warning/removal is recorded as an attempted dispersal, with success contested. The identified arrest was at Plettenberg Bay only. The later beach reopening is not attributed to the action. Cross-site episode grouping remains pending_unit.
• SA2101-E002 — 27 January Durban surfers opposed the government beach ban; attendance is unknown, so threshold remains pending.
• SA2101-E003 — a KaBokweni youth road blockade in January raised recruitment and college-related state demands. Exact day and crowd count are unknown; violence is not inferred from the headline.
• SA2101-E004 and SA2101-E005 — Humansdorp and other Eastern Cape matric-marker work stoppages involved transport/PPE and COVID-safety issues. Public assembly and qualifying crowd size are not established. The reported 151 Stutterheim markers are workers who stopped work, not a public crowd. Department engagement or removal of positive markers is recorded cautiously as accommodation, not victory.
• SA2101-E006 — an October 2021 TimesLIVE retrospective describes a January 1 electricity-related protest and road blockade at Zamdela, but does not state the event year or crowd size. No January 2021 date is assigned; eligibility remains pending_evidence and arrests remain unknown because the report does not date them to the gathering.

Screening and boundaries: Two records remain excluded. The January Uber Eats action concerned private platform fees and does not establish a qualifying own-state demand or a 50-person public gathering; sources conflict between 21 and 22 January, and no continuous physical action from the separate 18–19 December stoppage is documented. Teacher-union sources establish policy objections, but no qualifying public action is established by the readable reports. No older-onset episode was shown to continue physically into January. The 6 February Fish Hoek action is a later lead with no established uninterrupted link to the January beach action.

Limitations: Search date windows and domain filters often returned out-of-period material. Local-language results yielded little verified reporting. Some pages were search-only or inaccessible, including Citizen HTTP 403 responses, an anti-bot interstitial, SABC and COGTA PDF timeouts, cache misses, and a Business Insider redirect to its homepage; denied access was not retried or bypassed. Open-web coverage is not systematic newspaper-archive coverage and cannot establish absence. Official latest-baseline bytes, universe and additional coding procedure remain unresolved; baseline gate is pending_official_baseline.
