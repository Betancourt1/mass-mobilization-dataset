Bosnia, November 2020 — research in progress

Eleven managed-search attempts are captured with 259 result headers. Seven source cards remain search-only. Six exact public URLs were opened after scoped pre-open assessments, but the response objects were lost by the wrapper before their content could be serialized. Those attempts are logged as capture failures, not denials or no-result responses. No retry was made; no body claim is inferred from them.

Search records surfaced: (1) Otes/Ilidža protest activity following a killing, reported on 21 November, with a possible government migration-policy demand; the date onset, exact demands, and attendance remain unresolved, and the incident may involve a private/conflict grievance; (2) a separate Blažuj camp-relocation protest on 23 November, described only as a smaller group; (3) a Sarajevo medical-student protest on 4 November over clinical instruction, with count/target unknown; and (4–5) separate Brčko hospitality gatherings on 23 and 25 November against District emergency-headquarters restrictions, confirmed by the District Police’s search-result notice but with no per-date count/location. Counts from different dates or places are not pooled.

All five remain provisional and below independent review. Research is still open for Federation/RS/Brčko weekly and sectoral coverage, source bodies, numeric attendance and state targets. No missing report or capture failure is treated as proof that no event occurred. Official baseline remains pending.
