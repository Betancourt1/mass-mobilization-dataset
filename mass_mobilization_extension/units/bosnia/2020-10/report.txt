Bosnia, October 2020 — research in progress

Open-web research has recorded 22 query attempts (443 captured result headers); two duplicate-query attempts are explicitly marked as output-capture failures rather than zero-result searches. The local evidence set includes 12 source cards, 7 opened bodies/pages, 4 pending event candidates, 2 exclusions and 7 open gaps.

The clearest contemporary reports confirm 28 October anti-restriction activity in Banja Luka and Bijeljina, a separate 26 October Bijeljina gathering, and GRAS worker action inside company premises on 16 October. Turnout is not established for the protest candidates; the Banja Luka reports say only “several dozen.” The October 28 parents’ petition in Bijeljina lacks enough evidence to establish a public gathering. A separate GRAS warning strike on 20 October is documented as workplace stoppage, without headcount or a clear government-directed demand, and is kept separate from the 16 October protest. A 7 October UNSA protest was conditionally announced, but no captured follow-up confirms that it occurred. The Brčko report describes 20 proprietors closing individual premises, not a pooled gathering.

Coverage remains incomplete for Federation/Brčko places and for local unions, students, climate/environment, migration/asylum and civil-society sectors. No search failure or missing report is treated as evidence of no event. This dossier remains in progress, pending independent review and the official baseline.
