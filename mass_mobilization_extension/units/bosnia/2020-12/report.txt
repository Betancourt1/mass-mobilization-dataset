Bosnia, December 2020 — research in progress

Thirteen managed-search attempts with 377 result headers are captured. Four source cards are retained; three opened RFE/RL bodies were preceded by exact-target assessments, and one RTRS item remains search-only. Coverage is still incomplete across regions and sectors.

The strongest local event lead is the overnight 23–24 December Lipa/Bihać roadblock: RFE/RL cites police describing several hundred migrants blocking the Bihać–Sarajevo highway. This local estimate supports the 50-person threshold, but no participant statement in the opened report identifies a direct government demand. The candidate therefore remains pending_state_target. The camp fire that preceded the blockade is separately excluded as an incident; its cause is unknown and is not attributed to protesters.

Two separate continuations are recorded. HNK health workers began a general strike on 10 December over a collective agreement and wage coefficient with the HNK Government; an out-of-month 5 January 2021 report documents a public Mostar march by several hundred, but that count does not establish a qualifying December gathering. Separately, Bihać residents were reported to guard Bira against return/relocation of migrants; group size and public-protest character are unresolved. Do not pool either with the Lipa roadblock.

Air-quality, weekly regional, union, student, environmental/climate, migration/asylum and civil-society coverage remain open. No missing report or unreturned result is treated as evidence that no event occurred. Official baseline and independent review remain pending.
