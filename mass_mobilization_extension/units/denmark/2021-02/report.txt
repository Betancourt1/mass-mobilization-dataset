Denmark, February 2021 — open-web first pass completed; coordinator review remains pending. This is a provisional research dossier, not an official dataset update.

Search capture: 10 batched search invocations containing 40 query strings and 292 ordered result headers. Queries included Danish and English terms, event-date windows, Men in Black follow-ups, sector and location coverage. Returned headers are retained as full invocation arrays; the pooled search tool does not map each header to a query term, so no per-term result partition or absence claim is made.

Five date-level candidates are recorded: February 6 Copenhagen Men in Black (~600 per the saved prior-unit AFP summary) and February 27 Copenhagen Men in Black (The Local body reports police estimates of about 600 initially and 1,200 late evening, with eight arrests) are proposed includes. A February 9 Myanmar solidarity gathering and a possible February 13 Men in Black action remain pending evidence. A February 26 Wolt courier protest (~100 in the saved prior-unit Solidaritet summary) remains pending state-target review because the summary describes a private-company pay/bonus dispute. February 6, 13 and 27 are distinct MIB action dates; shared campaign context does not establish one continuous event. Wolt is a separate sector action. No exclusion or continuation is confirmed.

There are 18 source cards. Source metadata records three opened cards: 1 current-unit body opens and 2 prior-unit-only consultations, plus 15 search-only cards. The one current body was read in this unit; two are consultations of previously saved adjacent-month bodies. Four fresh-gated requests across NB-Økonomi and Dagens.dk returned no article body because the web reader supplied only an iframe/cache result and ordinary HTTPS requests failed at DNS; none returned an observed site denial. Baptist.dk body requests were deferred after full-input hashes changed between manual review and request. No copied article or image was retained.

State response is unknown for every candidate except eight arrests reported at the February 27 gathering. Those arrests are not turnout, alleged fireworks/rowdy behavior alone does not establish protester violence or dispersal, and the government extension of restrictions preceded the rally; no concession or physical event end is inferred.

Open gaps: official baseline and detailed coding procedure; systematic local/regional archive coverage and broad coverage outside Copenhagen; MIB campaign/event boundaries; Myanmar action occurrence, turnout and Danish state target; February 13 turnout, demand and target; Wolt state-policy target; and physical end/event-specific state responses. Searches do not establish that no additional actions occurred.
