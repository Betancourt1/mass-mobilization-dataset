Sweden 2021-01

Scope and method
This is provisional open-web research for 1–31 January 2021 (assignment ccode 380, not an official MM code), cutoff 7 October 2026. The unit contains 24 logged searches: general country discovery; Swedish and English searches for each of five January weeks; follow-up checks for identified leads; regional searches for Stockholm, Gothenburg, Malmö and seven additional cities; sector searches for labor, housing, climate, students, care, pandemic restrictions, Palestine, migration and anti-racism; and December/February boundary searches. Each log stores the literal query and the matched title/URL/reference headers captured together. Returned headers are not a total-results count or proof of coverage. Search dates were often ignored; source dates were checked where visible. Web searches do not reproduce LexisNexis or systematic regional archives.

Findings
No candidate meeting both criteria—an in-person gathering of at least 50 and a demand directed to Sweden’s government or its policy—was identified. events.json is therefore empty; this is not a claim that no qualifying protest occurred. continuations.json is empty because no qualifying earlier-onset physical episode was identified. Six exclusion/lead records are retained:
- X001: Gothenburg Navalny solidarity gathering, 23 January; opened GP report says about 20 people outside the Russian consulate, below threshold, with a Russia-directed demand.
- X002: Stockholm Navalny solidarity gathering, 23 January; opened SVT report confirms the gathering and quotes a call for Putin to resign, so the reported target is Russia. SVT gives no count. The original Sveriges Radio report remains unread after an HTTP 403; a secondary page’s approximate number is not independently verified, and no count is attributed to the blocked original.
- X003: Conditional private-care strike notice, later cancelled. Kommunal’s 5 January release covered about 55,000 employees, not attendees; Almega reports the planned 15/22 January steps were cancelled after agreement on 14 January. A 15 January labor roundup still describes planned action, leaving publication timing unresolved. No public gathering or executed action is verified.
- X004: Pågatåg wildcat strike threat scheduled for 4 January; contemporaneous SVT says it was cancelled when Arriva retained the Seko safety representative. The dispute was already reported in December. SVT attributes to Sydsvenskan a report that about 100 employees met on New Year’s Eve to plan a possible strike, but provides no venue or evidence that this was a public gathering. The underlying conflict concerned a private employer, not a government demand.
- X005: 19 January online tenant-organizing meeting against the government’s market-rent proposal. The report says over 150 joined online; future April demonstrations were plans, not January physical attendance.
- X006: 4 January workplace-support gathering. SVT reports about 100 coworkers outside Arriva’s Skåne office before a meeting about the union safety representative. This is recorded separately from the cancelled strike; its target was a private employer, not Sweden’s government. The article does not name the city, organizer or exact end time.

Sources and access
Seventeen source cards are recorded, including opened contemporary reports, a source archive, search-only and blocked leads. The GP article for Gothenburg was retrieved from its exact URL (HTTP 200 redirect to canonical; first 250,000 response bytes inspected in memory). The SVT Stockholm page first returned Cache miss; the exact public URL then failed sandbox DNS, and one approved ordinary HTTPS request returned HTTP 200 with 133,950 bytes; the visible-text excerpt was inspected in memory and not saved. The Sveriges Radio Stockholm URL returned HTTP 403 after its cache miss and one ordinary exact-URL request; no body was read and it was not retried. A related Svenska Dagbladet page remains search-only after Cache miss. The Local archive request returned HTTP 200 after cache/DNS failure; its 143,417-byte response was read in memory, but the archive item was not opened. The Local train report and labor sources were read only to the access limits recorded on their source cards. A retrospective Arbetet URL returned HTTP 403 and remains unread. No article text was saved.

Open limitations
The official latest dataset bytes, universe, schema and additional Coding Procedure remain unresolved at the project baseline gate; no official IDs or approval are assigned. Web discovery is not comprehensive: municipal and regional records, all local media and minority-language coverage were not systematically searched. December/February and 31 January checks did not establish a continuous Swedish episode but cannot prove none existed. The listed source-access, participant, boundary, independence and conflicting-date gaps remain open for coordinator review.
