GAMBIA — FEBRUARY 2021 — RESEARCH HANDOFF

Scope and method

This dossier covers Gambia, 2021-02, under the fixed 2026-10-07 event cutoff. Search discovery used 14 captured web.run search actions, each submitted as a batch of four strings (56 query strings total), across generic and weekly February searches, local and international publishers, court and Three Years Jotna leads, work/health/education/agriculture/fishing and price issues, permit and political-activity context, and targeted localities including Banjul, Serrekunda, Brikama, and Gunjur. English and French searches were used. The captures retain ordered result headers and URLs. The 14 original search actions from the registered task rollout were compared with search_log.jsonl by original event ID, exact query batch, tool, and ordered headers/URLs; there were zero mismatches. The durable capture manifest is search_capture_manifest.json.

The search log contains 14 captured search actions and 337 returned headers. These are search-result headers, not unique events or proof of exhaustive coverage. Sixteen relevant source cards are retained: 14 search_only and two opened. Two current-unit body opens; 0 prior-unit-only reads. Only two article bodies were successfully read. Four exact-URL body attempts ended in tool-layer technical failures: one Timeout (S004) and three Cache misses (S007 twice, S016 once). None returned an HTTP denial or article body. The first S015 preflight detected an input change; no request was made on that mismatched snapshot. Fresh receipts and contemporaneous manual scope reviews are recorded in access_receipts/, with access_receipts/manifest.json inventorying durable receipts and notes.

Evidence and coding result

No candidate met the evidence threshold for entry in events.json. This means no qualifying February event was verified in the material reviewed; it does not mean that no event occurred. No definite February event was established for exclusion, so excluded.json is empty. No episode with a verified pre-February start and a qualifying February continuation was established, so continuations.json is empty. Ambiguous leads remain in sources.jsonl and are itemized in gaps.json rather than being discarded or coded as events.

The opened March 24 The Point article (S005) reports an individual prosecution over alleged speech on February 10 and describes no public crowd, assembly, or organized collective action. The opened September 2 article (S015) documents a nurses’ sit-down strike beginning September 1, outside February; it does not resolve whether any February health-worker action occurred. The S004 court/rearrest report timed out; the S007 opinion and S016 civil-society permit story each returned Cache miss (S007 twice). Search snippets for worker issues, rice prices, a forum, a local alkalorship dispute, COVID-related permits, and UDP activity remain discovery leads and do not establish an eligible gathering.

Limits and follow-up

Coverage is limited to open-web discovery and two bodies. It does not reproduce LexisNexis or systematic local newspaper, radio, print, or regional archive review. Queries were not performed in Mandinka, Wolof, or Pulaar. Geographic, sectoral, publication, and temporal coverage remain uneven; results can miss unindexed or differently worded reports. Four body-request tool failures are not access denials and do not resolve the underlying sources. The official baseline/manual remains pending; local protocol criteria are provisional. No search-count or result-count establishes exhaustiveness.

Coordinator review should focus on the gaps in gaps.json: any public crowd around the Three Years Jotna court episodes; date and execution of the S016 permitted demonstration; February labor action; the scope of any permit suspension or canceled plan; local meeting/forum eligibility; and additional language, locality, and archive coverage. Event start dates, attendance, target, continuity, and outcome remain unknown unless stated in the cited evidence.
