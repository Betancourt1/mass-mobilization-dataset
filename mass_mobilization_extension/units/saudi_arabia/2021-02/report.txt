Candidate leads and unresolved coding
Two candidate records remain pending_evidence. E001 preserves a reported Buraidah/Qassim demonstration lead from search-only snippets dated in early February. Some snippets allege economic hardship, unemployment, taxes or quarantine measures and say “hundreds”; none establishes a physical public meeting, exact event date, attendance threshold, specific demand directed to the Saudi state, organizer, event end or response. Occurrence, scope, state target, turnout and precise dates therefore remain unresolved. All seven state-response fields and protester violence are unknown.

E001 reports overlap. Al Alam and Together for Justice snippets may share an upstream social-media original; Together for Justice’s English and Arabic pages are same-publisher versions. IslamTimes cites social-media posts; Al-Ahed and other snippets may repeat the same police account or source material. These are not treated as independent by default. Watanserb and its archive appear derivative. No body or source-chain provenance was verified.

E002 remains separate: Okaz snippets describe an enforcement encounter at a gathering violating COVID limits, with one variant placing it on a Buraidah commercial street. The two Okaz URLs are variants of one article. The report supplies no protest grievance, crowd total or event date; several people in an enforcement account are not a total turnout estimate. Urdu News explicitly attributes its enforcement account to SPA; that attribution is limited to that report and is not corroboration of E001. Whether E002 was a protest, a public meeting, or the same episode is unresolved.

Exclusion and continuity
One Reddit result (S012) exposes only the title “What happened in Qassim, Saudi Arabia?” and no body or event details. It is excluded as a standalone event record; this does not exclude an underlying gathering it may refer to. No continuation is recorded: the January Saudi dossier contains no matching dated physical event, and the February search leads do not establish an onset or continuous episode crossing a month boundary.
