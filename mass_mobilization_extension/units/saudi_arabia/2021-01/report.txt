Saudi Arabia — January 2021

Status: researched from open web sources, with independent review pending. The unit is 2021-01-01 through 2021-01-31. The fixed research cutoff is 2026-10-07 inclusive, America/Mexico_City. The baseline gate remains pending.

Search method and observed coverage
I ran 16 actual web searches, each logged separately with its actual timestamp and the returned title/URL/reference headers paired in original order. Coverage included Arabic and English queries for all five weekly windows (Jan 1–7, 8–14, 15–21, 22–28, and 29–31); broader month discovery; regional terms for Riyadh, Jeddah, Mecca, Medina, the Eastern Province, Qatif, Dammam, Abha and Jizan; labor, women, education, health and public-sector terms; thematic terms for wages, land, police conduct, prices/tax/fuel, social restrictions, reform and removal/resignation demands; and December 2020–February 2021 boundary checks. Queries are in search_log.jsonl. Fifteen source cards are retained: 5 opened substantive sources, 1 blocked URL, and 9 search-only records. Two other opened page views supplied no substantive article body. Open web search does not reproduce LexisNexis and does not establish exhaustive coverage.

A separate result about Saudi citizens in Washington concerns a foreign-country protest site, not a Saudi domestic gathering.

Continuity and source checks
A secondary Qatif overview describes a conflict phase ending 2020-01-07 and its listed 2020 item as a security operation after fire at a patrol. That armed-conflict chronology is not a January 2021 public protest and was not added as a continuation. An Arabic anniversary page for the Qatif uprising returned a web-tool Internal Error and yielded no readable body; it remains search_only.
The opened ANHRI January and first-quarter monitors explicitly cover Egyptian protests, not Saudi events. The opened U.S. Department of State 2021 human-rights report gives general restrictions on assembly and worker organization but no dated January gathering. ALQST’s 2021 annual summary describes repression and land expropriations, but does not identify a January public protest in the page reviewed. These retrospective/context sources do not prove that no gathering occurred. The Saudi Press Agency year summary returned zero readable lines; the Akhbaar24 2021 retrospective returned only its title and publication date in the page view. Their contents remain search_only for this review.
