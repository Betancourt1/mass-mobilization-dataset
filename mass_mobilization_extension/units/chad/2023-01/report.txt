Chad — January 2023 first-pass research

Scope and method
This dossier covers candidate protest activity beginning in Chad during 2023-01, under the fixed 2026-10-07 inclusive America/Mexico_City cutoff. I used the available May 2015 Mass Mobilization manual in baseline/mm_codebook.pdf and its text extraction, plus the extension protocols. The manual calls for a public gathering of at least 50 people making a demand of the state; it also distinguishes public labor action from negotiation alone. The official dataset baseline and any additional Coding Procedure remain unverified, so this work is provisional.

Eight actual grouped web-search invocations (Q001–Q008) are preserved with native result headers in capture_inputs/ and search_log.jsonl. They cover English, French, and Arabic month terms; French week-by-week windows; labor, education, opposition and civic-action terms; N'Djamena, Moundou, Sarh, Doba and Abéché; local Chadian outlets; and international/regional coverage. There are 212 returned-result headers across these responses; this is not a unique-URL or result-total measure. Searches used open web tools rather than the original LexisNexis procedure. No result count demonstrates completeness.

Candidate leads
CHD202301-E001 is the 12 January press conference by Mouvement Citoyen Top 20 Octobre at Radio Oxygène (S001). The report states the theme was democracy against dynastic rule and announces future sit-ins and other actions in the 23 regions. The conference date and venue are reported, but the city, total attendance, public access, and status as a protest gathering are not. The later actions are announcements only, with no date or execution evidence. Eligibility remains pending_threshold; no planned action was entered as an event.

CHD202301-E002 is a possible 16–18 January teachers’ strike by the SET provincial branch in Doba (S002). The source body could not be retrieved: two opens of the exact URL returned Cache miss after separate fresh access receipts and no HTTP status. This is recorded as a technical failure, not a publisher denial. The search result reports the dates and a demand for 2019–2022 transport costs, but gives no participant or workplace count and does not establish whether the strike became a public protest. It remains pending_evidence. All violence and state-response fields are unknown.

Cross-month and unresolved material
CHD202301-X001 records the October 20, 2022 opposition demonstrations as outside this unit, based on an HRW search result (S004); the January publication is retrospective and does not establish a continuing physical protest. A 3 January Tchadinfos report (S003) mentions a teachers’ strike among disruptions during the first school term but supplies no dates, location, attendance, or demand detail. Its relationship to the Doba lead is unknown; the two are not merged. No continuations are coded because no qualifying episode beginning before January is shown to have continued physically into this month.

Source and coverage limits
Four source cards are retained: two bodies opened within a stated scope (S001 and S003) and two search-only records (S002 and S004). There were 2 current-unit body opens and 0 prior-unit-only consultations. The Doba card has two fresh access receipts, AC002 and AC003; other body-read receipts are AC001 and AC004. Evidence summaries are paraphrases, not reproduced articles. Search-only material is discovery evidence and has not been upgraded to body verification. No systematic press-archive access or searches in every Chadian language were available. The absence of further verified candidates is not evidence that no other events occurred.

Inventory: 8 queries; 4 source cards; 2 provisional candidates; 1 cross-month exclusion; 0 continuations; 6 gaps. No candidate is approved for the official dataset, and no official ID is assigned.
