Central African Republic, January 2023 — first-pass research dossier

Scope and method
This unit covers the Central African Republic during January 2023 only and uses the fixed 2026-10-07 information cutoff. The original Mass Mobilization codebook was read from the coordinator’s baseline copy. It defines a protest as a gathering of at least 50 people making a demand of the home government; a work stoppage alone does not establish a qualifying event. The official-baseline gate remains pending.

Nine grouped open-web searches were captured through the native query-header helper, with 241 ordered result headers in English and French. Searches covered the whole month and weekly windows, Bangui, transport, teachers, FACA retirees, anti-Castel actions and fuel-price grievances. The dossier contains 17 source cards: nine body-read and eight search-only. This is bounded open-web discovery, not a systematic newspaper-archive or LexisNexis search. Search-only leads, cache misses, timeouts and DNS failures are not treated as evidence of absence.

Provisional candidates
E001 is the January 7 mototaxi blockade/strike at the Nsila/Nzila gendarmerie barrier, reported by Journal du Faso and Oubangui Médias. Drivers objected to gendarmerie formalities and fees and called for the barrier/gendarmes to leave. Neither report counts people at one time. Gendarmerie intervention, barrier removal and road reopening do not establish crowd dispersal, accommodation or when the protest ended. The spelling and independence of the two reports remain unresolved.

E002 is the separate January 5 mototaxi sit-in at the same barrier, reported by Oubangui Médias. It remains separate from E001 pending evidence about whether the two dates were distinct gatherings or one episode. Neither action meets a documented 50-person threshold.

E003 is a nationwide primary/secondary teachers’ work stoppage starting January 31 and continuing through February 2. Radio Ndeke Luka reports partial stoppages and school closures; a later Education International statement supplies the three-day date range and broader union demands. None of the accounts documents an executed public assembly or event-specific turnout during this strike. January owns the record by start date; do not duplicate it in February. The later federation’s “massive” wording applies to a series of strikes and is not assigned as this event’s turnout.

E004 is a provisional lead for anti-Castel demonstrations/product-boycott activity in Bangui. ActuNiger’s January 23 article reports activity at several city locations, bottle-breaking and a demand for Castel to leave the country over allegations of financing armed groups. That allegation is not independently verified. The article gives no count at one gathering and does not state a demand to the government. A Hamama search-result snippet suggests about twenty at the brewery in mid-January, but its article body was not accessible and the figure cannot be treated as total attendance across locations. Government targeting, threshold, exact dates and episode boundaries remain unresolved, so the candidate is not proposed as eligible.

E005 is a distinct January 5 Bangui bus/taxi work stoppage over fuel and transport pricing. Search-result excerpts from the January UN Human Rights Division report and Oubangui Médias say drivers stopped work/parked vehicles; they do not document a public assembly or turnout. The UN snippet reports a government fare demand; Oubangui’s snippet says the union disputed authorization and some taxis resumed before noon. The UN PDF returned Cache miss and the Oubangui page timed out, so these details remain search-only. Do not merge this action with the mototaxi barrier protest solely because of the shared date and transport sector.

Exclusions and other leads
X001 is the SOCATRAF work-stoppage announcement for April 3, outside January; the January dates refer to salary arrears, not event dates. X002 is a University of Bangui teacher strike dated January 3, 2022, not 2023. X003 is a Bangui city-hall employee action dated September 26, 2023 in a search-result excerpt; its page body was not accessible, and it is outside this unit.

The Ndjoni Sango Castel page S001 remains search-only under the documented same-host denial history for a different page; the direct article body and copied versions were not requested or retained. The Corbeau News FACA-retiree lead S010 remains unverified after a Cache miss and DNS failure. TV Plus Afrique S004 remains title-only after a Cache miss. Journal de Bangui S005 is also header-only and was not promoted to an event. The January 21 Radio Ndeke Luka article S017 reports a January 20 government meeting after civic organizations threatened action over fuel prices; it documents dialogue and a request for lower prices, not an executed public demonstration. Whether public actions followed remains open.

Review state
The five candidates, three exclusions, 18 gaps and empty continuation list are provisional and await coordinator review and the official baseline gate. Key unresolved fields include the 50-person threshold and episode boundary for the mototaxi actions; any public assembly during the teacher or bus/taxi stoppages; anti-Castel turnout and government targeting; and whether civic groups held demonstrations after the January 20 meeting. No official data update, absence finding or final eligibility approval is made.
