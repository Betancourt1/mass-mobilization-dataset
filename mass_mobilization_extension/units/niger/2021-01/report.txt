Niger, January 2021 — research completed, pending coordinator review

Open-web research did not establish a qualifying January protest. One dated RJRN gathering remains a candidate pending evidence; the January 23 ITN founding session was screened out as organizational activity. Neither the unresolved candidate nor this screen-out establishes that no other protests occurred.

Scope: events beginning 2021-01-01 through 2021-01-31 in Niger, with review of December 2020 and February 2021 sources for episode ownership. The queue’s country code 436 is provisional. Project cutoff: 2026-10-07 inclusive, local America/Mexico_City.

Searches and source access: fifty-six literal open-web searches in French, English, and Hausa-indexed terms were issued in fourteen pooled four-query batches. Coverage included four weekly windows plus sector, geographic, election, local-news, labor/student, and attack-response searches. The tool did not map results to individual queries; each query record preserves that limitation and relevant pooled URLs. Eight source pages were opened and eight remain search-only. Search-only Tournons La Page material identifies Niamey municipal order no. 0006/PDS/VN/SG dated 25 January prohibiting an MNPD march followed by a meeting planned for 27 January. The linked 18,771,676-byte report PDF body could not be read; no source shows that people assembled despite the ban. A search-only Bénin Web TV result reports more than 3,000 at an opposition-coalition meeting at Niamey’s Palais des sports, but frames it as an electoral support appeal. The full article could not be retrieved; the exact day, event form, and any state-directed demand remain unresolved.

Candidate: opened Le Sahel/ONEP coverage reports an RJRN anniversary and youth-exchange meeting in Niamey on 24 January and a request for more youth job creation and training. The article gives no venue, audience access, turnout, or protest format and situates the discussion in election-conflict prevention. E001 remains pending_evidence. An opened Nigerdiaspora reproduction of Le Sahel reports four trade-union confederations met in Niamey on 23 January, read an ITN founding declaration, and signed a charter; this is screened out as organizational activity, not a demonstrated public protest. Its page metadata dates the reproduction 24 January while its footer says 25 January. Opened ANP articles dated 2 and 11 January report election-related statements and an appeal for future mobilization, not an executed assembly. Search-result reports on the 2 January attacks describe official mourning, security deliberation, Assembly statements, and a cohesion forum; they do not report public protest. Search coverage is not proof of absence.

Boundary review: opened Alarme Phone Sahara coverage dated 26 February and opened Amnesty International releases in French and English dated 4 March describe post-runoff protests beginning on 23 February after the 21 February election. They do not report a January onset. Amnesty’s language versions are the same release, not independent corroboration. Searches of December 2020 elections and subsequent January/February coverage established no continuous protest episode crossing into January.
