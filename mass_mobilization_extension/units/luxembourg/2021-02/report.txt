Luxembourg — February 2021

This is a bounded open-web first pass for Luxembourg (provisional ccode 212), with the project cutoff of 2026-10-07 inclusive in America/Mexico_City. The dossier is ready for coordinator review, not an exhaustive event census or an update to the original catalogue. The official baseline remains pending.

Search and source coverage

The log contains 36 actual web-search invocations and 903 ordered result headers/URLs. Some invocations were single-query searches and later calls preserved their original batched queries as one action; the exact invocation and result order were retained. Searches used English, French, German and Luxembourgish, with month/week/date searches and follow-ups on HORECA, COVID restrictions, education/labor, housing/planning, care/health, climate, migration/refugees, agriculture, women’s actions and local/community media. The complete registered-rollout reconciliation matched all 36 original event IDs, exact query strings, clocks, ordered headers and URL arrays to the saved search log.

Eighteen exact-URL source cards were retained: one opened written article and 17 search-only cards. One full accessible French written article was read. The embedded audio/video was not played. The exact RTL Today Esch and Reddit Feb 13 opens returned technical Cache miss results without HTTP status or body; neither is treated as a publisher denial, and neither was retried. Four fresh prerequisite chains for the Le Quotidien Feb 11 lead stopped before any body request because their input/review/preflight prerequisite failed; receipts and no-request outcomes remain in access_receipts. This is an unresolved follow-up, not a denial, and the worker stopped further attempts on that body as directed by the coordinator. Search-result titles and snippets are discovery evidence only, not body verification or turnout proof.

Candidates

Three provisional candidates are recorded. E001 is the full-body report of a fourth HORECA gathering in Luxembourg City. The article says “Saturday” and was published Monday 8 February, so 6 February is a transparent weekday-derived date; the article reports no crowd size, exact venue, explicit state-directed demand, end or event-specific state response. It remains pending evidence.

E002 is the search-only reported HORECA action at Place d’Armes on 11 February. Some search metadata refers to calls for more aid, but no article body was read for this candidate. A pre-event hope for up to 1,000 participants is not actual attendance. The source cards include related RTL variants and other discovery leads; their independence and origin relationships are not assumed. Its body and eligibility gaps, including the unanswered Le Quotidien lead and four failed prerequisite chains, are documented separately.

E003 is the search-only 5 February Esch co-housing/development-plan protest lead. RTL and Wort titles appear to refer to one action, not two. Neither body was read; attendance, execution details, exact demand and intended municipal/state target remain unverified. A technical Cache miss on the RTL URL is not evidence of denial.

No candidate is coded as meeting the threshold and state-demand criteria on current evidence. For all candidates, end date, protester violence and each state-response dimension remain unknown. No numerical attendee bounds or demand categories are inferred from search snippets.

Other leads, continuity and exclusions

The Tagesblatt search title about roughly 300 people protesting COVID measures on a Saturday and a Reddit item dated 13 February are retained as an unresolved possible anti-COVID lead; the actual event date, identity, location and attendance were not verified, so it is not assigned to the month as a candidate. Radio ARA’s planned 1 February march, the planned 9 February education action, an RTL Play Mamer heritage segment, women’s-action program/search leads and a 27 February Reddit lead remain follow-ups, not proven executed events. Searches for late-month HORECA, labor, health/care, housing, migration, climate and agricultural actions did not establish additional body-verified qualifying events; no non-occurrence conclusion is drawn.

No continuation from January or into March is established. January’s HORECA gatherings on the 16th, 23rd and 30th remain separate actions; the February 6 report and February 11 report are also kept separate. Search leads about March 8 women’s actions and March 27 housing are outside this unit absent evidence of a continuous action beginning in February. No confidently excluded event was identified, so excluded.json is empty. No continuous episode starting before February was established, so continuations.json is empty.

Limits and handoff

The searches cover four languages and several week/date and sector windows, but do not replace LexisNexis or systematic local newspaper/archive review. Search-engine non-results cannot establish that no other protests occurred. Some exact article URLs remain unread, publisher/source-origin independence is unresolved, and the official baseline/manual gate is pending. The event records are provisional, preserve unknowns, and require coordinator review before any later integration.
