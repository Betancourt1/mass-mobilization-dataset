Luxembourg — January 2021

This is a bounded open-web first pass for Luxembourg in January 2021 (provisional ccode 212, Europe), with the project cutoff 2026-10-07 inclusive in America/Mexico_City. It is ready for coordinator review, not an exhaustive event census or an update to the original catalogue.

Search and source coverage

Thirty-seven web search queries were recorded across English, French, German, and Luxembourgish, including weekly windows, Luxembourg City, HORECA and other sectors, official sources, and follow-up around identified episodes. The search tool combined query batches and did not expose exact per-query timestamps, result totals, or reliable query-to-URL attribution; those limitations are preserved in the log. Relevant URLs were deduplicated by exact URL, resulting in 22 source cards: 9 opened, 11 search-only, and 2 blocked. The official HTML judgment and PDF were read from cached web-tool content, but the coordinator later received HTTP 404 when retrying both exact URLs over ordinary HTTPS. No bypass was attempted. A Wort page and a Virgule page returned HTTP 403 and remain blocked. Le Quotidien was read through an ordinary HTTPS response by the coordinator; its normal web-tool open timed out. Search-only cards remain provisional, not independent corroboration.

The search included local/contemporary reporting and public official records. Amnesty-related results did not provide a Luxembourg January 2021 episode. Relevant French and German items remain partly excerpt-only or blocked; Luxembourgish-specific search did not surface additional January event evidence. Portuguese-language pages appeared in discovery but were not opened. The open web is not LexisNexis or a systematic newspaper archive, and no completeness claim is made.

Candidates

Six physical-action candidates start in January:

- Jan 9: anti-COVID-measures demonstration between Glacis and Place Guillaume II. RTL’s English and embedded French versions give materially different crowd descriptions (“around 200” versus “several dozen”); no numeric bounds are set, and eligibility remains pending evidence.
- Jan 16: an HORECA protest at Place d’Armes, distinct from the adjacent anti-measures gathering. Le Quotidien reports about 200 owners; RTL confirms the action but gives no count. This is proposed for inclusion, with source-origin independence still to review.
- Jan 16: an anti-measures/anti-mask group at Knuedler, reported as a few/several dozen. It is retained as a pending-evidence nomination. The official court case has a Jan16 charge but a later “at that date” passage under the same case heading describes a March 3 meeting and about 50 then 120; date/event compatibility is unresolved and the March 3 count is not transferred.
- Jan 23: a separate HORECA procession in Ville-Haute. The official judgment establishes it was distinct from the P1 gathering; an approximately 200 crowd estimate is presently only in a Land search excerpt, so evidence remains pending.
- Jan 23: a separate P1-organized gathering in Ville-Haute. The court’s 11–100-person legal category spans both sides of 50 and is not an observed estimate; threshold remains pending. The two police notices are one event, not two. Exact addresses are anonymized.
- Jan 30: HORECA procession from Place Guillaume II to the Chamber of Deputies. RTL reports organizers’ estimate of 350; this is proposed for inclusion, with no independent count or exact end time.

All six end dates are unknown. No state-response field or protester-violence field is coded beyond unknown. Police presence, statutory proceedings, general ministerial comments and silence are not treated as event-specific response evidence.

Exclusions and continuity

Two announcements are retained as exclusions: the Jan16 HORECA demonstration planned by Etienne-Jean Labarrère-Claverie was explicitly canceled before execution, and a separate Jan21 HORECA request has no located proof of occurrence. The canceled action’s early “at least 500” plan and later 2,000–2,500 concern estimate are forecasts, not attendance. It is kept separate from the other Jan16 HORECA event that did occur at Place d’Armes.

No pre-January continuation was established. RTL’s Jan9 story mentions a prior municipal-park protest with around 40 participants but gives no date; searches did not identify it, so it is not assigned to December or January. A Feb11 HORECA demonstration is a distinct later event, not proof of a continuous action beginning in January. Jan16, Jan23 and Jan30 HORECA gatherings remain separate event-level records; no campaign key is assigned.

Outstanding gaps

Key unresolved items are the Jan9 within-story attendance discrepancy; the Jan16 court chronology and anti-measures turnout; the Jan23 P1 threshold and anonymized location; the Jan23 HORECA snippet-only count; the occurrence of the Jan21 request; the date of the prior park gathering; any event-specific state response; and the official baseline/schema/usage gate. The 12 gap records specify follow-up for these issues, source access, language and episode-level review.
