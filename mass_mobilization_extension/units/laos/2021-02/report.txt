Laos, February 2021 — interim research report

Scope and method
This provisional dossier screens public collective action starting in Laos during February 2021. The fixed event cutoff is 2026-10-07. The registered worker rollout is used to preserve search records. Forty-five search actions yielded 819 ordered headers, including a date-windowed search across 1–7, 8–14, 15–21, and 22–28 February. Searches covered English, Lao, Thai, Chinese, and Vietnamese, with general, labor/wage, land/resettlement, election, diaspora, local-media, official, and human-rights queries. Search headers support discovery only. Open-web indexing cannot reproduce the historical LexisNexis process or establish complete coverage; local archive access and independent language review remain limited. No absence claim is made. A final provenance reconciliation matched all 45 original registered rollout captures to the 45 durable search records by original event ID, query, tool, timestamps, result counts and URLs, and ordered headers: all 819 headers matched with zero mismatches, and no searches were rerun.

Current evidence and coding
The strongest February lead is an RFA April retrospective, repeated in an RFA May follow-up, reporting that around 100 cement-plant workers gathered on February 22 outside a private owner’s home to demand unpaid wages. The two stories share one RFA reporting chain. The January Laos dossier has saved the prior full-text read scope for both; this February unit did not request either article. The reporting also describes earlier written complaints to labor offices and the Ministry in early February, but those were separate acts and do not establish the gathering’s addressee. The gathering is provisionally retained in excluded.json under the private-dispute rule, with state-target uncertainty preserved for review. It has not been included in events.json or an official dataset.

Other leads include the RFA Namtha 1 Dam resettlement report (S003), a BHR Centre page that appears in search as a republication of that RFA report (S004), and an RFA article on two critics removed as election candidates (S005). Those three remain search-only. Q044–Q045 also surfaced a GlobalSecurity result with the S005 headline (S008; relationship unverified), an RFA Nam Ngum 4 compensation/detention result (S009), and a FIDH UPR snippet on resettlement (S010). These are discovery leads only: none establishes a February public gathering. S009’s snippet mentions a man reportedly held until February 2021 and an earlier promise not to protest, but the body is unopened and the detention/dispute is not coded as an assembly. The CIVICUS retrospective (S006) is now opened: it discusses the election but reports no Laos February public gathering; the other assemblies and attacks it recounts are outside the country or month, or are not public assemblies. There are no event candidates or continuations established in this interim pass.

Evidence scope
1 current-unit body opens; 2 prior-unit-only source consultations. S006 was opened after a fresh exact-URL receipt, full manual publisher/origin review, and immediate matching 246-path hash check; its full returned article is summarized in access_receipts. For S003, four individually reviewed exact-URL gates were not used for a request because the immediate full-input rechecks found concurrent source-ledger hash changes. No S003 body request was made, and none of the four outcomes was a source denial. No body request is in flight.

Open questions
The main unresolved issue is whether workers directed a demand to the Lao state during the February 22 gathering, rather than only to the private employer. Exact turnout, organizers, precise site, duration, and physical end are unknown. Earlier wage requests are not shown to be public actions, and later mediation/payment do not establish continuation. The Namtha 1 and Nam Ngum 4 resettlement leads, the candidate-removal story, and the possible S008 republication remain search-only. The opened CIVICUS retrospective reports no Laos February gathering but cannot establish that no other gathering occurred. Open-web coverage does not establish no other gatherings occurred. The official baseline and original Coding Procedure remain pending.

This is an interim, open-web candidate dossier for coordinator review only; it is not official approval or dataset integration.
