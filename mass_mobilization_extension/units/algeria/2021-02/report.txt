Episode, threshold and field-specific decisions
E001 is limited to the Central Post Office Square/central Algiers gathering; the source's qualitative thousands figure spans the capital and other cities, so the Algiers-only threshold remains pending. The account explicitly says the central demonstrators dispersed peacefully by evening, supporting the February 22 physical end. The national arrest total is not turnout; the report attributes 26 arrests to Algiers.

E002 and E003 are separate Kherrata (February 16) and Bejaia (February 15) actions. The opened accounts support qualitative hundreds or thousands for those respective local actions, but neither establishes a physical end. The Euronews account attributes attendance at Bejaia to LADDH; its AFP image credit is not treated as independent written reporting. E004 lists multiple wilayas in one report but supplies no per-site count; the sites are not summed into one threshold-qualified crowd.

E005 is an Algiers February 26 rally; the local report's thousands description supports the threshold. Its source-specific reports of batons and tear gas support beating and crowd-dispersal response flags. Those dimensions remain separate from events in other cities. E006 is a distinct Oran gathering: the opened accounts report arrests and forced police dispersal, but neither gives an Oran attendance estimate or explicit physical closure of the whole gathering. Its end date remains null. E007's Bordj Bou Arreridj estimate is internally inconsistent (hundreds and later above 3,000); both descriptions are preserved, without a tighter bound. E008's Constantine account is dated February 25 and has no count. E009's Annaba account is dated February 25 and says participants dispersed around 17:30, supporting its physical end; its target/threshold remain unresolved. E010 and E011 are separate Bejaia and Constantine February 26 records with no city-specific counts. Anadolu's report of no intervention in the capital is not applied to other city sites.

E012–E015 preserve distinct southern labor/housing actions. E012 reports dozens at Touggort, not a definite 50-person minimum; E013 has no Hassi R'Mel count; E014's Ouargla university sit-in has no verified date or count and a pending state target. E015 records the February 9 Ouargla gathering phases at the wilaya-side portal and Mekhadma, with arrests and dispersal attributed to the local accounts, but no crowd total; the four arrests are not attendance. The report does not convert one city's evidence to another site.

E016 is the Touggourt-to-Algiers jobseekers' march. The source says more than a dozen began; it does not establish 50, an exact route/end, or a reliable calendar start. Its phrase “Monday 2 February” conflicts with the 2021 calendar, so start_date remains null and the quoted conflict is preserved. E017 is the February 8 AADL2 sit-in at Béjaïa wilaya headquarters. The “several dozen” description does not establish 50. The current sit-in narrative attributes an explicit refusal by the wali to receive the association through a participant account cited to BRTV; exact within-day timing/sequence is not recorded. An older association grievance is not used as current-response evidence. The reported ongoing action has no physical end.

E018 and E019 are distinct February 7 housing-list actions at Toudja and At Ksila; the linked article gives no count at Toudja and only an uninspected photograph characterization at At Ksila. E020 is the February 5 Taskriout RN9 housing-list road action; the number of apartments and traffic disruption are not turnout. No physical end or 50-person lower bound is recorded for E018–E020.

E021 records an actual Oran gathering at the AADL regional agency earlier in the publication week. The article reports subscriber delegates being received by the regional director, coded as accommodation (a hearing), not fulfillment of their demand. Exact event day, attendance and physical end remain unknown. A separate February 9 sit-in at the wilaya headquarters was announced, not confirmed as held, and is not folded into E021.
