Lesotho — January 2021 (provisional ccode 570)

Status: researched first pass, pending independent review and official-baseline verification. One unresolved source-only candidate is retained because an official March report dates a Tšepong strike vaguely to “sometime in January.” Its physical onset and whether it was a public gathering are not verified. This dossier does not claim that no protest occurred. Research is fixed to the 2026-10-07 inclusive cutoff (America/Mexico_City); later-period reports are used only for boundary checks.

Twelve pooled web-search calls yielded 48 recorded searches. Searches covered January weekly windows; broad English and Sesotho terms; public-service workers, nurses, teachers, students, transport and price issues; local and regional outlets; and December 2020/February 2021 episode boundaries. The search tool did not expose reliable query-by-query attribution, result totals or exact execution times. Earlier records preserve pooled URL sets. Source and episode checks
Fifteen source cards record the reviewed material and access limits. Opened contemporary Lesotho Times reporting and official Government of Lesotho notices for Jan 5/6, Jan 13 and Jan 14 report COVID-19 restrictions, not a protest. A government school-opening notice was also checked. A Nov 6, 2020 youth action in Maseru is outside January and no continuous January phase was found in that source. Searches for civil-servant, transport, price, student and local/regional coverage produced no additional verified January public action in the inspected results; accessible local-language and archive coverage remains incomplete.

The QMMH/Tšepong nurses’ salary-grievance action remains a boundary and date-conflict lead. The official DDPR 10-page report, signed Mar 8, states on PDF viewer p. 7 (printed folio p. 6) that a continuing strike at Tšepong began “sometime in January.” Its p. 2 calls the matter an “alleged” strike; p. 8 says the strike was allegedly led by the Lesotho Nursing Association and involved nursing staff; p. 10 bears the report date. The report gives no January day, public picket/site, simultaneous count or evidence of a public assembly. Contemporary Sunday Express (Feb 24) and SABC (Feb 25) say the nurses’ strike began Feb 1, after a Jan 27 government deadline; a later Africa-Press/Informative Newspaper report says Feb 2. The U.S. State Department search-result excerpt also dates the action Feb 1–Mar 11, but the official page open returned a technical-difficulties/forbidden response, so the report was not read. The March 16 National Assembly order paper asks about a QMMH “go-slow protest currently taking place”; it gives no start date or crowd and does not resolve the conflict. These sources may concern the same action or distinct workplace stages; that remains unresolved. SABC’s “more than 300” figure describes February picketing and is not carried into January. Its report characterizes an interim order as effectively ending the action and says workers were expected back, but does not date the order or independently establish the last observed physical gathering; the candidate end date remains null.
