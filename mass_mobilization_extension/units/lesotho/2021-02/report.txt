Lesotho — February 2021 (provisional first pass)

Scope and method
This is an open-web first pass for the assigned 2021-02 unit. It preserves 16 actual search-tool responses (20 individual query strings, including two batched invocations) and 330 returned headers in search_log.jsonl, with original action IDs, query batches, result order, URLs and available clocks. Searches covered protest/strike terms, QMMH health workers, COLEPE, LFM drivers, public employees, teachers, factories, transport, students, prices, politics, and one Sesotho-language query. Captured search headers/snippets are discovery evidence only.

Ten source cards are retained. Scope counts: 0 current-unit body opens; 6 prior-unit-only consultations (S001–S006; S006 records only a partial/technical access attempt, not report text). At card level, five sources are marked opened from prior dossier records and five remain search-only. No body was requested or opened for this February unit. For the LFM item, a fresh manual-review gate was aborted after the shared phase closed; a later fresh access check deferred because the full corpus changed during scanning. Both no-request outcomes and their receipts are preserved under access_receipts. Search-only summaries remain provisional and are not treated as verified article-body facts.

Findings
One unresolved QMMH/Tšepong health-workers’ strike-and-picketing lead is recorded as E001. Contemporary reports state a February 1 strike start, while the DDPR retrospectively says the continuing strike began sometime in January. The candidate is therefore pending month ownership, and the first public picket date remains unverified. SABC reports more than 300 health workers had been picketing, but no simultaneous count, exact public picket site, or dated first gathering is established. Salary review/parity and outstanding COVID-19 payments/incentives are reported; the relation between the PPP employer dispute and state-directed demand requires review. A court interdict is noted but does not determine the end of physical picketing or map to the seven coded state-response dimensions. Protester violence and all seven dimensions remain unknown.

Two search-only leads are retained as excluded from current qualifying-event coding: an LFM workplace-facilities stoppage (X001), with no established public gathering or own-state target, and COLEPE petition delivery (X002), with no established linked march or 50-person assembly. The results are not claims that no other protest occurred. No additional verified February public event emerged from these searches.

Limits and follow-up
Open-web indexing, archive and local-language coverage are incomplete; no systematic LexisNexis review or exhaustive local-source audit was conducted. Several relevant records remain search-only or the event body was previously consulted only in January. The DDPR-versus-February onset conflict, event ownership, public crowd threshold, exact assembly site and date, physical end date, government-target attribution, excluded leads, and event-specific response/violence all remain open. See gaps.json. The official baseline check is pending; this dossier is a candidate file for human review, not an approved or integrated catalog update.
