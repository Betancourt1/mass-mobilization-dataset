Guatemala, February 2021 — open-web first pass

Scope and method
The dossier records 30 captured search actions containing 38 original query strings and 769 ordered result headers. Searches covered Spanish- and English-language national and regional news, international coverage, weekly/date windows, public-sector and labor/taxi topics, rural electricity disputes, and official or university sources. There are 48 relevant URL cards: 10 body-read sources and 38 search-only leads. The body-read cards retain brief paraphrases, line scopes, outlet/origin metadata, and access receipts. Three source URLs had technical retrieval failures: S004 and S010 each encountered a Cache miss and then a DNS failure before HTTP, while S011 returned a Cache miss. These are not access denials.

This is not a LexisNexis search and cannot establish absence or exhaustive coverage. Search and reporting are concentrated in online Spanish-language sources, with limited local-language and rural coverage. The official baseline and current schema remain pending. The saved May 2015 manual and current protocol inform screening, but the additional Coding Procedure is unavailable. The cutoff remains 2026-10-07; events after the reporting month are used only as boundary leads.

Candidates
E001 is the 18 Feb coordinated CODECA march to Guatemala City, with a body-confirmed demand directed at national authorities and public services. SWI explicitly attributes its report to EFE and says “cientos.” A separately bylined Anadolu account says around 3,000 in the text and nearly 2,000 in a caption; preserve the discrepancy, without exact numeric bounds or a claim of independent eyewitness basis. The candidate is proposed for inclusion pending review.

E002 is the 25 Feb Day of Dignity memorial and walk; the report’s “decenas” does not settle the 50-person threshold. E003 is the 13 Feb girls’ bicycle caravan with state-directed justice and protection demands, but no turnout estimate. E004 is the 24 Feb taxi action against Government Agreement 17-2020; a gathering and one partial road obstruction are reported, but no crowd count or full route completion. E005 is the 23 Feb retired-military roadblocks demanding state compensation, land and housing; no attendance estimate is available. These remain pending threshold.

E006 is the 21 Feb Malacatán road blockade amid an outage. The contemporary report confirms the blockage but names no government demand and does not quote a specific demand to Energuate; keep state targeting unresolved and distinguish the private-distributor grievance from state policy. E007 is a 24 Feb substation occupation described only in a later retrospective, without count or a clear event-specific demand. E008 is the retrospective’s 27 Feb manifestation against Energuate; “thousands” describes affected residents, not protest attendees, and the article does not clearly tie a broader state-management aim to that gathering. E006 and E008 remain pending state target; E007 remains pending evidence.

No exclusions or prior-month continuations are established from the reviewed material. The January dossier’s 14 Jan march is distinct from the Feb 18 CODECA action by organizer and stated demands; shared themes do not establish continuity. No event end is inferred from publication date or month-end.

Open issues
Attendance and event closure are unresolved for E002–E008. Malacatán’s 21, 24 and 27 Feb actions may be separate, coordinated or part of one outage episode; available evidence does not decide this. The Congress item about receiving retired military participants, energy-related official records, Feb 12 palace reports, additional bicycle/feminist coverage, taxi reports and possible USAC occupations remain search-only. None is coded from a search-result title alone. Event-specific state responses and violence remain unknown unless supported by a source; background wartime violence and electricity-related harm are not attributed to protest participants or state response.

The eight candidates and nine gaps are research proposals for coordinator and human review, not approved entries or an update to the original dataset.
