Liberia — January 2021 research unit
Cutoff: 2026-10-07 inclusive (America/Mexico_City)
Status: researched_open_web_pending_review; nonexhaustive first pass

Scope and method
I logged 52 web searches, including broad protest terms, four January weekly windows, local/official/rights outlets, county and city names, student, worker, market, health, land, electricity and policing topics, and December 2020/February 2021 boundary checks. The queries were in English, with one colloquial “palava” variant. Search results from multi-query calls were pooled where the tool did not preserve query-to-result mapping; no result totals are claimed. There was no systematic local-language or LexisNexis coverage, and search silence does not establish that no event occurred. The current official catalogue bytes/universe and the additional CodingProcedure remain unverified; the historical May 2015 codebook is only a provisional reference.

Provisional events
1. Poll workers reportedly protested unpaid stipends at the National Elections Commission. NEC search captions label a Jan14, 2021 protest and mention police/official interaction, but the official page returned HTTP 403. A New Dawn issue returned HTTP 429. No source body established the physical date, one-time crowd, or precise state demand; all response fields remain unknown. Keep the date null and eligibility pending_evidence.
2. A women advocates’ coalition held a press conference in Monrovia before the Jan7 Gbarpolu rerun and asked the government for security and electoral protections for candidate Botoe Kanneh and supporters. FrontPage Africa’s Jan5 article says it happened “Monday”; Jan4 is derived from that weekday and publication date, not printed numerically. Attendance is unreported, so eligibility remains pending_threshold. Because the action concerned one candidate and an election rerun, state-policy demand versus election-only support remains for review. All seven state-response variables remain unknown; an ambiguous follow-up snippet does not establish a response to this gathering.

Exclusions and episode boundaries
- A planned January11 ICOP assembly was explicitly an announcement for Jan11, 2020, outside the unit; execution is not established.
- A Jan11, 2021 student march addressed a private school building dispute, not a state demand. The report says “dozens”; “over 500” described affected students, not attendees.
- A separate Jallah Town electricity protest began Feb8, 2021 and belongs outside January.
No confirmed action beginning before January was found continuing into this unit. December Gbarpolu electoral violence is context for the Jan4 press conference, not proof of a continuing same protest or a response to that conference. Do not merge candidate episodes automatically.

Evidence limits and next work
The 15 source cards include 3 opened bodies, 3 blocked sources, and 9 search-only leads. The blocked sources were the NEC listing (403), New Dawn PDF (429), and Local Voices Liberia article (406); restrictions were respected. Directly read sources support the private-school exclusion, the 2020 ICOP date, and the Jan4 press-conference lead. A later MFWA excerpt cites the same FrontPage Africa report and is not independent corroboration. There are 11 open gaps, including poll-worker date/size/state target/response and episode continuity; the press conference’s attendance, state-target classification, and response link; source independence; language and geographic coverage; and the official-baseline gate. Next work should pursue accessible original reporting on these two leads, add independent attendance evidence if available, and extend county/radio/local-language coverage. This unit is not an official update, exhaustive month, or approval for catalogue inclusion.
