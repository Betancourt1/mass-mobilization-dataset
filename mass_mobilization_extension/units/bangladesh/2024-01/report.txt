Bangladesh — January 2024

Scope and methods: This is an open-web research dossier for the assigned month, not an official MM update. I captured nine actual search responses (123 rendered result headers) across broad English discovery, local-source queries, weekly Jan 1–14/15–31 boundaries, targeted election-period searches, and Bangla-language discovery. One additional actual search invocation was not captured; its exact invocation and loss are preserved in BDG202401-G001. Native request/response clocks, result totals and browser refs were unavailable and remain null. Captured rendered citation tokens/order are retained separately.

Candidates: E001 is a distinct 6 January left-wing rally outside the National Press Club in Dhaka. An opened Al Jazeera article reports about 200 protesters denouncing the election; the domestic election target and 50-person threshold are supported, so this is proposed for inclusion pending independent review. E002 is a separate BNP supporters' march through Shahbagh that called for people to join the election strike. The article describes a “small group” but gives no count; it remains pending the 50-person threshold. The 48-hour stoppage itself is listed as excluded as a non-gathering action without event-specific turnout. E003 is a 7 January election-day street-demonstration lead found in an Al Jazeera search excerpt; the opened page response omitted the relevant live update. It remains pending evidence and threshold.

Continuations and exclusions: No pre-January episode start was established. The election strike/stoppage is excluded as a separate public-gathering candidate; that exclusion does not erase the separately reported gatherings.

Sources and restrictions: Seven source cards are preserved, including an undated Daily Star News gas-price sit-in lead, a Cumilla election-boycott video result without established gathering occurrence, and an announced 14 January lawyers’ protest whose execution is unverified. Two Al Jazeera bodies were opened after fresh exact-URL receipts and contemporaneous manual review; the election-day liveblog body excerpt did not expose its relevant update. AP and an Al Jazeera election retrospective remain discovery-only context. Official dataset version/universe/schema/use terms are pending.

Gaps: Attendance and state response for the small march and election-day lead remain unknown; National Press Club rally organizers are unspecified. Local Bangla discovery also surfaced a Cumilla BNP video dated 1 January without evidence sufficient to establish a public gathering and a 14 January lawyers’ protest call with execution unverified. An 11-party gas-price sit-in surfaced in local discovery, but its “today” date cannot be resolved from the saved excerpt and it is not assigned to January. Search coverage is not exhaustive and does not establish that other events did not occur. Systematic local newspaper archives/LexisNexis and independent Bangla-language review were unavailable.
