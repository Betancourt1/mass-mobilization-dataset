Candidate

One Feb 1 vigil outside the Public Health Ministry in Bissau is retained as GNB202102-E001, pending the 50-person threshold. Two readable bodies identify the vigil and its accountability/health-service demands, one from VOA and one from Lusa (the RTP item is syndication). VOA calls turnout “many”; RTP republishes a Lusa report describing “dozens of young people.” Neither gives a number that establishes at least 50, so participants_min/max remain null and eligibility remains pending_threshold. RTP/Lusa is one reporting origin. The exact CNEWS article that search results describe as reporting more than 100 participants and police force returned Cache miss on two gated requests; those claims are not used as verified evidence.

The Lusa/RTP body states that the about-one-hour vigil was dispersed by police. No further response field is inferred. The earlier death that motivated the vigil is not coded as an event killing; an absent further report is not coded as government ignore. The article attributes commentary to RENAJ vice-president Adama Baldé and the VOA story quotes MCCI president Sana Canté, but no organizer is identified.
