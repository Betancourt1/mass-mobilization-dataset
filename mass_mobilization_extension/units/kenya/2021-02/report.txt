Search coverage: 12 actual captured search actions returned 378 ordered result headers across 279 distinct URLs. Queries covered broad Kenya protest, demonstration and strike terms; weekly February windows; English and limited Swahili; and school, health and labor, digital taxi, railway and eviction, and land/ranch leads. The complete original event IDs, exact batched query strings, ordered headers, URLs, references and observed clocks are in search_log.jsonl. This is an open-web first pass, not exhaustive coverage; it does not reproduce LexisNexis or systematically cover local radio, regional newspaper archives or county vernacular sources.

The 279 source cards include one opened article (KEN202102-S090) and 278 search-only discovery cards. The Mwatate Kenya News Agency article was read in full for its article text. It reports over 300 Maktau farmers demonstrating against threatened eviction and alleged land appropriation connected with the Isanga-Iwishi ranch, marching from Maktau along the Voi–Taveta Highway toward a ranch site. Its 2021-02-22 publication says the event was on Saturday; 2021-02-20 is recorded as a same-report weekday inference, not an independently confirmed date. The article supports an event-specific crowd estimate above 300 but is one uncorroborated account. Police intercepted the march over a missing local permit, and a county commissioner calmed the residents and directed them to land agencies. Because the described dispute concerns a private ranch, the article does not establish an explicit demand directed to the Kenyan state; E003 remains pending_state_target. No physical end, organizer, protester violence or coded response dimension is established. The read scope and receipt are in access_receipts/.

Other Moi Girls and Kisumu railway article bodies were not retrieved.
