Gabon, February 2021 — open-web first-pass research

Scope and method

This dossier covers Gabon, 2021-02, under the assigned ccode 481 and fixed 2026-10-07 cutoff. It records 15 actual web-search invocations and 360 aligned ordered result headers, including the original batched calls. Searches used French and English protest, strike, union and topic terms; follow-up queries covered COVID “casserole” action, teacher/CONASYSED leads, SYNAPEF/Eaux et Forêts, SOGATRA, commerce, bakers, Total and other reported grievances. The open web cannot reproduce LexisNexis and does not establish exhaustive coverage.

The dossier has 66 relevant URL cards: 1 opened article and 65 search-only discovery records (1 current-unit body opens; 0 prior-unit-only body reads). Search titles and snippets are not treated as body evidence. One article was opened after a fresh exact-URL receipt, full saved publisher/origin review and immediate 216-path/hash recheck. Its read scope and receipt are recorded in the source card and access note. No blocked body was retried or copied.

Candidate records

E001 records a February “concert de casseroles” against recent government measures. The opened Gabon Media Time article (19 February, by Henriette Lembet) reports street participation on the second evening, Thursday 18 February, in Libreville, Akanda, Owendo and Port-Gentil and confrontations at PK7, Louis and other neighborhoods. It reports arrests and videos of agents striking detainees with pots; it says shots were heard and people may have died but does not identify the shooters or confirm deaths. The first evening is inferred as 17 February from the article’s “second evening” wording, while the first-night activity remains unclear. No turnout number or site-level lower bound is given, so eligibility is pending the >=50 threshold. No city crowds are added together.

E002 is a SYNAPEF assembly/strike lead retained as pending evidence. Search-result titles alone do not establish a February date, an actual public assembly, attendance, state-directed demands, or relationship between the meeting and the work stoppage.

No continuation with a verified pre-February physical start was established. SOGATRA may have started earlier, but its onset and any qualifying assembly remain unresolved; it is not assigned as a continuation or new event.

Provisional exclusions and follow-up

The exclusion ledger keeps work stoppages and announcements separate from qualifying public gatherings. It includes the CONASYSED school stoppage/release-demand campaign, SOGATRA strike coverage, SNEC-USTM’s announced university-dead day, Total and commerce strike threats, bakery work-stop coverage, Moutouki policy/economic reporting, bar owners awaiting assistance, and a suspended COSYGA strike notice. These are research-stage exclusions from event coding, not claims that no gathering occurred. Each remains linked to follow-up gaps where a public assembly could still be verified.

Major unresolved issues are site-level turnout and organization for the casserole actions; first-night activity and end date; independent confirmation and attribution of police response, shootings and deaths; the execution and turnout of a planned CONASYSED assembly; SYNAPEF event date/public access/turnout; and SOGATRA onset, public assembly, state target and end. Relevant source bodies beyond the one opened article remain unverified.

No MM official IDs or catalogue integration were produced. Candidates remain open for coordinator and human review; no official baseline approval is represented.
