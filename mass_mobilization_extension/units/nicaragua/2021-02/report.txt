Nicaragua — February 2021 research dossier

Scope and method
The assigned unit is Nicaragua, 1–28 February 2021, with the project’s fixed 2026-10-07 event cutoff. I recorded 16 actual batched web-search actions, preserving their original query arrays, action IDs, result order, exact URLs, references, titles and available clocks in search_log.jsonl. Queries covered Spanish and English protest terms, week ranges, opposition/student/labor/rural activity, local outlets (including La Prensa, Confidencial, Artículo 66 and 100% Noticias), international reporting and rights monitors. Search-result counts were not exposed consistently and were not invented. This is open-web discovery, not a LexisNexis-equivalent archive search.

Result
No candidate event beginning in February 2021 was established from the searched material. This means no qualifying event surfaced in the documented searches; it does not establish that no event occurred. events.json and continuations.json are empty. Four surfaced leads are retained in excluded.json with their date/evidence limits: two February 2020 protests in an opened OACNUDH bulletin, a March 8 2021 women’s demonstration that is only a search-result lead and outside the month, and a February 22 UNAN commemorative-event lead that currently shows no protest or state-directed demand.

Evidence and access
There are 17 source cards. One exact public body request was successfully opened: a partial relevant-section read of the OACNUDH Bulletin No. 15 (NI202102-S001). Its title and introduction explicitly date the bulletin to 1 February–31 March 2020. It describes a 3 February 2020 UCA protest and a 25 February 2020 opposition march that was cancelled, followed by a separate picket inside Metrocentro; it gives no picket attendance. This is cross-year evidence only. One exact request to the La Prensa politics landing page (NI202102-S007) returned a tool-level Cache miss; no body was read, no HTTP denial was observed, and no retry or alternate URL was attempted. Its search-only headline about CxL leaders barred from a Matagalpa community remains unresolved. No source body reporting a February 2021 candidate was opened. 1 current-unit body opens; 0 prior-unit-only body reads. The one opened source was a partial 2020 report.

Coverage limits and follow-up
The search surface included local and international web reporting in Spanish and English, but did not systematically cover Indigenous-language media, local radio/television archives, social-media archives, every regional outlet or LexisNexis. Search ranking repeatedly mixed February 2020 results with 2021. The original MM manual is absent from the portable baseline available to this worker, so the documented eligibility rule is used pending official baseline review. See gaps.json for the exact La Prensa access failure, coverage limitations, missing manual and month-boundary follow-up. No exhaustive or protest-free-month claim is made.

Readiness
The dossier is a first-pass research handoff for coordinator review. All seven response variables remain unassigned because no in-month candidate event has supporting evidence; no values are inferred from silence. The status remains pending official baseline and human review.
