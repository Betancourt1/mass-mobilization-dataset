Togo — January 2021

Research is still open for review. Seventeen recorded search batches are documented, including 52 unique source cards. Q015–Q017 each bundled several related probes in one web__run invocation, a deviation from the supplied one-query-per-call instruction. The literal inputs are listed separately in search_log.jsonl, while returned headers remain at combined-call scope because the saved tool output does not associate headers with individual inputs. A fixed query count is not evidence of completeness. Search coverage includes French and English general queries; each weekly window; regional/city and student probes; education/SET, health, agriculture and political leads; explicit Ewe/Eʋegbe and Kabiyè/Kabiye terms; and a December–January Lomé University continuity search. Searchable results and inspected coverage are predominantly French. Across 315 captured search-result headers, 12 entries in Q001, Q003, Q004, Q006, Q007, Q009, Q010, and Q011 have null titles; their per-header notes record the provider’s truncated/incomplete headings or excerpt fragments. Exact titles were not recovered from the stored captures, so the nulls remain rather than being guessed; URLs, references, and header order are unchanged. Separately, one of the 52 source cards (S037) has a null title, with its limitation explained in that card. This is open-web discovery, not an exhaustive search; LexisNexis was unavailable under the protocol.

One candidate is retained: Lome Gazette’s 22 January report says teachers were holding sit-ins at some schools alongside a nationwide 72-hour strike over detained SET colleagues. The text gives no crowd size, no named school, and no evidence that any one gathering reached 50 people. Lome Gazette’s own relative weekday phrases (“since Monday” for the sit-ins and “since yesterday Tuesday” for the strike) conflict with its 22 January date, so onset is recorded only as January. A 25 January Togomatin issue separately documents ministerial monitoring instructions and legal objections around the planned strike but does not verify a gathering or its attendance. Eligibility remains pending_threshold. The relationship between the reported sit-ins and strike is unresolved. Later reports concern announced 25–26 or 28–29 January strike dates, cancellation/suspension and government warnings; they do not verify execution of those later calls and are not merged into the candidate.

Two motivated exclusions are recorded. A 20 January SET/STT office meeting is described as an internal union mediation meeting, not a public gathering; arrest counts do not establish turnout. Separately, late-January announced strike dates lack verified execution, with reports describing cancellation or suspension. No exclusion implies that no other event occurred.

One November 2020 Lomé University activity-cessation decision is retained in continuations.json as an unverified cross-month lead. December reports describe an open-ended decision, but no January continuation, physical gathering or turnout has been verified. February nurse and dockworker actions remain outside the January unit and do not prove continuity.

Access limits include an ILO record returning HTTP 403 and a Dapaong student lead returning HTTP 406 with no body. A 20 February SET chronology lead remains body-unverified: an earlier original-page retrieval exposed page metadata but did not isolate the article body, and a later web-open attempt returned a cache miss. A Dapaong exact-title search later surfaced an indexed 1 December 2016 date for the blocked school-action lead and a similar page on another site. Neither the blocked original nor the similar page was reopened; the date remains a discovery clue, not verified source metadata or event evidence. A numbered official-hosted PDF was readable, but its issue/article date was not established and it is not used to prove January continuity. Explicit Ewe/Eʋegbe and Kabiyè/Kabiye searches yielded no captured local-language articles; that is a search limitation, not a finding of absence. The baseline_gate remains pending_official_baseline. Official comparison, ID assignment, integration, and contribution await baseline resolution; provisional candidate reconciliation, consolidation, and working exports may continue.
