Candidate findings
Four candidates are retained for review. E001 is the Jan 26 solidarity reception at Sheikh Hikmat al-Hijri’s home in Qanawat. Syria Direct’s photo caption and Enab Baladi’s later report date that reception to Jan 26. Al-Araby’s Jan 28 local-source account says people arrived at the home two days earlier and that Damascus intervention was followed by release of the previously detained man and an apology from military-security chief Luay al-Ali. That is the sole explicit event-linked release/apology account used for E001 accommodation; the prior Homs detention is not a protest arrest. Attendance, public-forum status, the reception’s exact demand, and physical endpoint remain unresolved.

E002 is kept separate for Jan 27 Suwayda city gatherings/roundabout activity. North Press and 7al report demands for an apology, and 7al says a Jan 27 delegation comprising the governor, Baath branch secretary, and police commander visited al-Hijri’s headquarters as a de-escalation attempt; it says the effort produced no result, but does not establish whether the delegation held an actual meeting or negotiated. This official visit is distinct from the Sheikh’s reception of solidarity delegations, so E002 accommodation remains unknown. Reports vary qualitatively on crowd scale (“limited”/“several” versus “large crowds”); none gives a count. No endpoint is inferred.

E003 is a Jan 27 report of pro-regime supporters demonstrating in Hasakah and Qamishli against SDF/AANES. E004 is the distinct Jan 31 reported attempted march toward an Asayish checkpoint in Hasakah after a government call opposing the siege policy. Both remain pending_state_target: AANES/Asayish are de facto authorities in contested/fragmented control areas, and the project’s domestic-state rule is not calibrated for this target. Crowd size and endpoints are unknown. Asayish reportedly cut short E003, but it is not coded as a Syrian-state response pending actor review. The Jan 31 Asayish–National Defense firefight and reported armed death are kept separate as combat; no such casualties or violence are transferred to E004.
