United Kingdom — January 2021

Method and coverage
I ran 31 separate open-web queries, one per call. Queries covered the five weekly windows, London, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland and other English cities; English, Welsh, Irish and Scottish Gaelic terms; and selected anti-lockdown, health, housing/rent, environmental, labor and seafood grievances. December 2020 and February 2021 searches checked possible continuity. The log retains 899 returned result headers in their captured order. Blank, truncated or non-title result headers remain null with capture limitations; no title was inferred from a snippet. The 42 source cards include 3 opened bodies, 37 search-only leads and 2 blocked exact URLs. This is a bounded first pass, not an exhaustive search; there was no systematic LexisNexis/newspaper-archive search.

Evidence and coding
The ITV body establishes an anti-lockdown demonstration in Parliament Square on 6 January, beginning at noon, and attributes 21 arrests there to a Metropolitan Police statement. It does not give human turnout. The arrest count does not establish the 50-person threshold. A police dispersal plan and footage of some demonstrators being removed support an attempted/partial dispersal mechanism; the source does not establish that the whole crowd cleared or when the event ended. A separate small gathering outside Westminster Magistrates’ Court is reported at a different site; its exact date, purpose and turnout are not stated, and seven detainees are not counted as attendees or coded as formal arrests.

The opened LBC body dates the StandupX Clapham High Street march to 9 January and reports about 30 participants. It returned to Clapham Common and was dispersed by police; the approximately 30-person estimate is below threshold, so it is recorded in excluded.json. The body reports 16 arrests and 22 fixed-penalty notices, while quoting an earlier Met post with 12 arrests “so far”; both counts are retained as a time/count discrepancy. The same article mentions a separate Bournemouth town-centre protest, but does not give its date, turnout or specific demand. Three arrests and at least seven notices there are response counts, not attendance; this lead remains pending unit and is not merged with Clapham.

The opened Guardian article dates the Westminster seafood-lorry protest to 18 January. Lorries from Scotland and Devon parked near Downing Street with Brexit-paperwork and anti-government slogans. The article gives neither a vehicle count nor a count of active human participants, so the 50-person threshold remains unknown; vehicles are not treated as attendees. The group did not dump fish. A government compensation pledge is mentioned but is not established as a response to this particular protest. A Reuters-attributed interview does not establish that Reuters wrote or syndicated the Guardian article.

Unresolved leads and access
The Cardiff Bay Welsh-language South Wales Police page (S008) returned an exact-URL HTTP 403 with a Cloudflare challenge after default DNS failure; its body was not read and no retry, copy or mirror was used. It may concern an earlier event. The Inkl Edinburgh lead (S034) likewise returned an exact-URL HTTP 403 after default DNS failure; its title header was visibly incomplete and no article text or alternate copy was used. Edinburgh planned-protest, student rent-strike and other search-only leads remain unverified for occurrence, date, domestic state target, qualifying human turnout and physical boundaries. No December-onset continuation or uninterrupted February continuation was established, so continuations.json is empty; this is not evidence that no such event occurred.

All four event records are candidates pending review: two dated actions with unknown human thresholds and two separate site leads with unresolved month ownership. One below-threshold Clapham action is preserved in excluded.json. Eleven specific follow-up gaps document attendance, dates/endings, site separation, source access/independence, student-event status and open-web coverage limits. The unit remains pending the official baseline gate; no official Mass Mobilization IDs or approval are assigned.
