Estonia — February 2021
Research status: open-web first pass, pending coordinator review.

Scope and method
I ran 12 actual batched web searches containing 48 English, Estonian and Russian query strings and captured 323 ordered result headers in CAPTURE-0001 through CAPTURE-0012. Searches included four week ranges across 1–28 February, generic public protest/demonstration/strike terms, Tallinn, Narva and Tartu, Navalny/Immortal Regiment leads, unions and labour disputes, HKScan/Rakvere, agriculture and environmental terms, local-media domains, and official/statistical sources. Captures retain the actual invocation timestamps and ordered headers; result snippets are discovery leads only.

Candidate and adjacent-month lead
One February candidate is retained as pending evidence: search-result text from the KAPO 2021–22 annual review describes a Tallinn gathering supporting Alexei Navalny and a counter-action. The visible result refers to five people in the counter-action photo, not the main gathering, and does not establish total attendance or the >=50 threshold. Search results frame the support around Navalny and Russian political prisoners; they do not show an Estonia-directed demand. The exact day, turnout, event structure, demands and state response therefore remain unknown. The exact Estonian KAPO PDF was opened by the web tool at 09:51:06Z and the registered page reference reports 2,133 lines; the wrapper did not surface the page text to the worker, so no substantive field coding relies on that unseen body. A later gated attempt to query that page reference was deferred after the all-input source fingerprint changed. See the access receipts and notes.

A captured ERR/BNS result identifies a distinct 23 January 2021 Tallinn demonstration supporting Navalny. It predates February and is recorded in excluded.json as outside the unit month. No continuation into February was established.

Coverage and limitations
The search layer found no other event that could be established as an eligible February gathering. This is not evidence that no such event occurred. The web search service is not LexisNexis and does not give systematic newspaper-archive coverage. Local publisher-domain queries yielded no usable contemporary article body in this record. Searches covered English, Estonian and Russian terms and selected regional cities, but not every municipality, outlet, language community or issue vocabulary. Sector searches covered labour, industrial action, agriculture and environment; no monthly official labour-dispute table was opened. Retrospective Eurofound text and a Statistics Estonia monthly questionnaire page remain search-only. Dates, turnout, violence, arrests and other state response are unknown unless specifically stated in the candidate record.

Sources and disposition

All remain search_only because their substantive bodies were not reviewed here; the KAPO PDF has a logged successful page-open event, but body text was not visible to the worker. ERR’s later retrospective result appears to derive from KAPO, so it is not treated as independent confirmation. The January ERR article is a contemporary adjacent-month lead but its body was not opened. No official baseline was changed; the unit remains pending official baseline and human/coordinator review.
