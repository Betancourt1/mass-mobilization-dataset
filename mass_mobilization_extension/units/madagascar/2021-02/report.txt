Scope and result: This dossier covers Madagascar from 1–28 February 2021 under the fixed event cutoff of 2026-10-07, America/Mexico_City. It screens for public gatherings of at least 50 people with demands directed to Madagascar’s government or its policy. The extension remains separate from the integrated catalogue. Seven provisional event candidates are retained, one work-stoppage lead is excluded, and no physical continuation is confirmed. No candidate currently has reliable evidence establishing the 50-person threshold; unknown attendance, event dates, state target, and episode boundaries remain explicit. This is not a claim that no qualifying protest occurred.
