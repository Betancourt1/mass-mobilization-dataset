Mass Mobilization Extension — Argentina, February 2021
Research status: researched_open_web_pending_review
Cutoff: 7 October 2026 inclusive, America/Mexico_City. Research performed 9 October 2026.

Scope and method
I ran 20 actual Spanish- and English-language web searches, Q001–Q020, covering all four February weeks, Buenos Aires, Santa Fe/Rosario, Chubut, Mendoza, Formosa, Jujuy, Tucumán, Salta, Neuquén/Añelo, Córdoba, transport, health, social movements and labor actions. The log preserves 586 actual returned title/URL/reference headers in their original positions. Each query has an ephemeral raw-capture key; raw tool output and article bodies are not stored in the repository. No search total or exhaustive coverage claim is made. Open-web search does not reproduce the dataset's LexisNexis procedure, and result-date filters surfaced out-of-month material.

Search-header capture corrections
Semantic header titles that were body excerpts, numbered provisions, clipped headline fragments, section labels or visual placeholders were set to null with precise ref-specific capture limitations. The reviewed repairs preserve the exact URL/ref order and do not rerun the original queries. Longer legitimate titles remain intact. This is metadata repair, not article-body evidence.

Opened originals: Infobae on the 18 February food/work march (S001); TN on 27F (S046); MULCS on the 4 February anti-megamining action (S025); LA NACION on Formosa roadblocks (S053); and TN on the TUDA boundary (S070). Diario Crónica's exact anti-megamining report (S023) returned HTTP403 after a web Cache miss and a default-sandbox DNS failure. Its body was not read; no retry, mirror, bypass or copy was used. For S025 and S070, one exact-URL ordinary HTTPS request returned HTTP200 after web Cache miss and default DNS exit 2; only short factual metadata and limited text excerpts were retained in transient memory, not article files. Request initiation times were not sampled for several opens, so accessed_at/requested_at remains null where exact time was unavailable.

Candidate findings
Four body-reviewed candidates remain pending the 50-person threshold:
- E001: the 18 February social-organization march from Avellaneda/Puente Pueyrredón to the National Social Development Ministry. The local crowd is uncounted. A leader's 100,000 claim covered more than 60 sites in 24 provinces and is not assigned to the Buenos Aires route. The date is provisionally supported by the date-coded Infobae URL and the body's “this afternoon” wording.
- E002: the 27 February protest campaign over VIP vaccination and government conduct. TN identifies Plaza de Mayo as the epicenter and several Buenos Aires and provincial locations. No site-level count is given; “thousands of complaints” is not an attendance figure. Reported attackers near Olivos were unionists, not identified as state agents.
- E003: the 4 February plurinational anti-megamining action called by Chubut assemblies. MULCS reports a multi-province day and a Buenos Aires march to the National Mining Secretariat, but provides no crowd count and is a participating organization, not independent news corroboration.
- E004: self-organized residents' roadblocks at at least five Formosa-city sites beginning 8 February to demand promised housing modules. LA NACION reports that the provincial state accelerated some deliveries after mobilization began. Housing need and abandoned modules are not protest attendance; grouping of the foci and the 50-person threshold remain unresolved.

Five additional candidates (E005–E009) are search-only leads: the Obelisk social-organization protest, a Castells-led Casa Rosada protest, Clorinda residents' reopening demand, a Jujuy multi-day social protest and a Mendoza Hospital Central labor mobilization. Their bodies, physical dates, demands, attendance and/or own-state targets remain unverified.

Boundary and exclusions
One continuation (C001) records TN's 4 February report that TUDA road protests continued in Buenos Aires. The article says the campaign began in mid-January but does not establish an exact physical start or an end. Its transport-chamber meeting did not include TUDA; truck volumes are not protest attendance. January's February 18 food/work boundary lead points to the distinct February action above; uninterrupted January-to-February assembly is not evidenced. Fishers and Añelo remain unverified February-continuation searches.
One exclusion (X001) records a government-support mobilization scheduled for 1 March; it falls outside this unit and the opened report says the President asked supporters to join remotely.
