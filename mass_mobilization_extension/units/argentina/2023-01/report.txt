Argentina 2023-01 open-web research

Status: first-pass research completed; candidates and open gaps are pending independent review. No official approval or inclusion decision is claimed.

Scope and method

The unit covers Argentina, January 1–31, 2023 (ccode 160; queue 3845). The captured log contains 24 distinct Spanish- and English-language searches with full ordered result headers and query clocks. Discovery sampled each of the four January weeks and targeted national/provincial coverage plus labor and health actions, environmental mobilization, social-assistance protests, and justice campaigns. The pass is not exhaustive; LexisNexis and an authorized official baseline were unavailable.

Body-verified candidate actions

- Jan. 4, Mar del Plata: an Atlanticazo rally against planned offshore oil exploration. A Télam-credited report says about 600 attended; a second local report has closely overlapping language and is not treated as independent confirmation.
- Jan. 18, Dolores: an anniversary gathering near the tribunal and at the municipal amphitheater called for justice in the Fernando Báez Sosa case. A24 reports “miles de personas”; the municipality supplied event logistics, which are not coded as a response to the justice demand. Exact attendance is not reported.
- Jan. 18, Villa Gesell: a separate Le Brique ceremony drew “cientos de personas” and included an explicit call for justice. It is not summed with the Dolores crowd.
- Jan. 18, Mar del Plata: A24 reports a separate mobilization under “Justicia por Fernando Báez Sosa”; attendance remains unknown.
- Jan. 24, Mar del Plata: taxi/remise drivers assembled, blocked roads, and demanded municipal action against transport platforms. “Hundreds” refers to vehicles and “dozens” to drivers; neither establishes at least 50 people. One body-verified report documents assaults by a group of participating drivers; that evidence does not characterize all participants or the movement.
- Jan. 25, CABA: Unidad Piquetera blocked Avenida 9 de Julio/Metrobus over Potenciar Trabajo benefit suspensions and sought a meeting with the Social Development minister. The blockage was lifted that evening without a meeting; no attendance count is reported. This is the verified CABA segment only.

Two Jujuy discovery leads carry Jan. 17 publication/search dates (a Milagro Sala pardon demand and a “Padres del Dolor” justice march); their physical occurrence dates remain unknown. The first page exposed only headline/subhead/date/caption; the second exact-page open returned a technical Cache miss. Both remain pending evidence. A24 briefly mentions a Zárate municipal mobilization in its Jan. 18 anniversary-coverage item; the occurrence date, underlying demand, and crowd size remain unknown, so it stays pending state-target review. Dolores and Villa Gesell turnout wording supports the 50-person threshold, but no numeric lower bounds are coded; both eligibility decisions remain pending coordinator interpretation of the state-target criterion.

Access and source limits

Eight source cards are body-opened, six are search-only after technical Cache-miss failures, and two remain blocked: the exact Noticias Argentinas article returned HTTP 403; the exact El Expreso de Salta URL returned HTTP 429. Neither exact URL was retried. The Noticias Argentinas restriction remains limited to its exact URL. The El Expreso 429 is preserved as an exact-URL rate-limit observation with no body and no publisher-wide inference. Cache misses are recorded as technical retrieval failures, not denials. Manual-review receipts are evidence checks, not general authorization. Search snippets are not treated as article-body verification.

Continuity and remaining review

No continuation record is created. The Atlanticazo is a recurring monthly campaign, but the Jan. 4 rally is a discrete gathering; references to adjacent months do not prove one continuous episode. No national total is inferred by adding local actions. Search results point to possible Chubut health-worker strike activity, San Juan/Mar del Plata/Paraná piquetero actions, and incomplete Salta/Jujuy reporting; public assembly, execution, date, or turnout remains unresolved where noted in gaps.json. The March 17 Jujuy health-worker item is excluded from January only; it is not evidence of no January action.

Review status

The dossier contains 9 agent candidates, 1 out-of-month exclusion, no coded continuations, and 9 focused gaps. Event eligibility is provisional; crowd thresholds, state responses, source independence, local ownership, and the pending official baseline require review. Silence is retained as unknown. This work is a first-pass research handoff and is not an official dataset update.
