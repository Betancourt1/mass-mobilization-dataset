Argentina, October 2020 — open-web research dossier

Scope and method
This dossier covers only Argentina, 1–31 October 2020. It uses the supplied local Mass Mobilization codebook as the coding reference and records unapproved candidates for later scientific review. I ran 56 Spanish- and English-language web queries covering weekly date windows, Buenos Aires and provincial regions, public-sector and university labor, social organizations, Indigenous issues, road blockades, the October 12 Banderazo, Guernica and adjacent-month continuity. Open web is not equivalent to LexisNexis or a systematic provincial newspaper archive. Indigenous-language search was not performed.

The search tool returned shared result sets for several four-query calls without query-level attribution, which is stated on each row. Five query records have empty result arrays because their actual responses were not retained with aligned headers: these are capture losses, not zero-result searches, and their results have not been reconstructed. Search-result titles/snippets are discovery leads only.

Evidence and access
There are 100 source cards: 13 have relevant rendered article text read within a saved scope, and 87 remain search-only. The dossier records 13 current-unit body opens and 0 prior-unit-only source consultations. Access checks are saved under access_receipts/ before each article-body request; decisions were manual_review and did not themselves authorize access. The selected public pages had no recorded access denials. Several web-tool opens returned Internal Error or no body text and were kept as technical failures, not mislabeled as denials.

The Buenos Aires Times account carries mixed TIMES/NA/AFP story credits and a separate AP image credit; Canal 26 similarly contains NA credits with its own byline. Their written-story origins are unresolved, so S005 and S007 are discovery-only and are not used for coding or independent corroboration. Other opened reports are not counted as independent merely because their publishers differ; explicit Télam republication is recorded for S091.

Candidates
- ARG202010_E001, 12 October #12O Banderazo: proposed_include. Infobae and regional El Territorio describe a multi-city opposition demonstration with demands involving COVID restrictions, judicial reform and other federal policies; El Territorio reports thousands at the national action but no venue-specific crowd count. City boundaries and nationally organized event identity remain open.
- ARG202010_E002, 6 October ATE action: pending_threshold. TN confirms a national strike and public march over the government's salary offer. The reported 354 people attended the prior delegates' meeting, not the street action; no crowd estimate was found.
- ARG202010_E003, 7 October social-organization march to the Ministry of Social Development: proposed_include. TN describes a large, multi-organization public march for jobs and state assistance, ending after officials received delegates and an agreement. Exact attendance and the identity of some groups at the site remain unresolved.
- ARG202010_E004, 15–19 October El Foyel occupation: pending_state_target. TN reports about 40 occupants at the start and a court-ordered eviction; later attendance above 50 and a state-policy demand are not established.
- ARG202010_E005, 19 October Route 40 blockade opposing the El Foyel eviction: pending_threshold. TN reports about ten people; policy target and any later crowd increase remain open.
- ARG202010_E006, 19 October protest outside El Bolsón police station seeking release of four detainees: pending_threshold. Diario Río Negro says a group gathered but gives no count. A proposed 20 October Bariloche demonstration was not confirmed.

No candidate received an official Mass Mobilization ID, and none has been independently reviewed or approved. Excluded records are empty because no additional exclusion was confirmed from body-read evidence; partisan-support leads and search-only calls remain unresolved rather than treated as events or as exclusions.

Continuity
ARG202010_C001 records the San Luis agricultural road blockades that began 29 September and were still active on 1 October. Its onset predates this unit; the owner is likely Argentina 2020-09. The protest sought more flexible COVID circulation protocols, but size and closure remain unknown.
ARG202010_C002 records the Guernica housing-related land occupation from 20 July to its 29 October eviction. A24 and a Télam-credited La Nueva report describe about 2,000 families initially and 1,500 remaining in the final census, with provincial housing negotiations and relocation assistance. This belongs to a prior onset month, likely Argentina 2020-07, and is not duplicated as an October event. The prior owner should review whether household counts establish the 50-person rule and whether the demand qualifies as state-policy redress.

Open questions and limits
Attendance remains unquantified for several public actions; household figures are not individual participant counts. The 12O's national grouping and venue-level threshold, ATE's street-crowd threshold, the state target for El Foyel-related actions, the exact Guernica policy demand and the San Luis episode's closure all need review. The Reuters screenocean video result could not be opened. APFDigital, La Revista del Chaco, the official Guernica census page and two Infobae Guernica pages exposed no article body in the web tool; those records remain search-only. Their failures do not establish a site denial. Political support gatherings and the October 17 partisan caravan were not automatically treated as eligible anti-state protests.

Coverage is systematic open-web discovery in two languages across national/provincial, weekly and sector-specific queries, but it is not exhaustive. Missing search results, missing attendance and inaccessible archives are gaps, not evidence that no event occurred. Baseline gate: pending_official_baseline.
