Cameroon — January 2023 first-pass handoff

Scope and cutoff
Assigned unit only: Cameroon, 1–31 January 2023, provisional ccode 471. The research uses the fixed 2026-10-07 inclusive cutoff in America/Mexico_City. The official baseline remains pending; nothing here is an official dataset update.

Search coverage
Eight grouped web-search invocations are preserved in search_log.jsonl and capture_inputs/query_001.json through query_008.json. They cover broad English/French monthly terms; weekly windows; worker/sector terms; Bamenda, Buea, Limbe, Yaoundé and Douala; Eneo/ASEELEC and BLT/SOCOPAO; and a focused follow-up on whether the late-January Martinez Zogo journalist actions were actually held. This is a bounded open-web first pass, not proof of complete monthly coverage.

Records

Candidates
E001 records the reported 4 January National Printing Press strike, with no assembly count or resolved state target. E002 records the 20 January BLT/SOCOPAO workers’ gathering; the reported 800 are workers affected by the dispute, not attendees, and a direct state target is uncertain. E004 is the 23 January Amplitude FM tribute in Yaoundé; Actu Cameroun confirms the tribute and Opale’s search excerpt reports about one hundred journalists, but the body could not be retrieved and the tribute’s own state target remains unresolved. All candidates remain pending and distinct; no official IDs or merges are proposed.

Exclusions and continuity
The ASEELEC members’ meeting is not coded as protest on current evidence. A separate large crowd at the Yaoundé morgue is not coded as a protest without a demand attributed to that crowd. The announced black-clothing action and 26 January march are not treated as executed. The 2025 Sosucam false positive and April 2023 teacher protest announcement remain excluded. A search-only November 2022 Eneo subcontractor strike is retained as possible earlier context for the January ASEELEC meeting, with continuity explicitly unverified.

Limitations and next work

English/French open-web searches do not cover all local-language or print-archive reporting; no exhaustive absence claim is warranted. Follow-up should verify any late-January journalist action, participant counts and state targets, and the possible Eneo continuity.
