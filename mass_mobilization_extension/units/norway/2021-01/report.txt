Research status: Norway, January 2021
Status: Open-web research complete for coordinator review; no official Mass Mobilization inclusion decision.
Evidence cutoff: 2026-10-07 (fixed assignment cutoff). Research work was recorded on 2026-10-08.
Scope: Physical public gatherings in Norway with at least 50 participants and a demand directed to the Norwegian state or its policy.

Search and coverage
The dossier contains 26 literal Norwegian- and English-language web query strings and 35 source cards. Some English/Norwegian query pairs were submitted together and the search service returned a merged result set. The sweep covered weekly dates, local Oslo gatherings, other Norwegian regions and cities, labor and climate leads, COVID restrictions, Belarus/Russia and Palestine solidarity leads, as well as month-boundary sources.
The search identified five separate Røde Luer gathering hypotheses outside the Storting in Oslo: 2, 9, 16, 23 and 30 January. The 2 January event is attributed to a partisan Bullo Tidende report and an organizer invitation; the invitation alone is not evidence of occurrence. The 9 January gathering is supported by a participating speaker's account of giving an appeal and an organizer follow-up thanking attendees. The 16, 23 and 30 January dates are supported by organizer follow-ups thanking attendees at the previous Saturday gathering. These sources describe opposition to Norwegian COVID restrictions and state measures.
Each date remains a separate, unresolved event hypothesis. No source gives a per-date attendance figure or evidence that any one gathering reached 50 participants. Attendees are not pooled across Saturdays, and a recurring organizer or campaign is not treated as an automatic event merger. The 2 January occurrence remains pending further evidence; the later four dates remain unresolved on the threshold because attendance is unquantified. No exact physical dispersal time is established for any date. Protester violence and each of the seven state-response fields remain unknown. The organizer's 23 January relay of an unnamed officer's statement about free speech does not establish a state-response category.

Exclusions and boundary checks
Four leads are recorded as excluded:
- Private child and youth care strikes: employment counts describe workers on strike, not a public assembly; the demand concerned private employers.
- Church-sector strike: a workplace dispute that began in December and continued into January; employee totals are not assembly attendance. A separate 24 December gathering is outside this month and has no count.
- January curfew consultation: 1,429 written submissions, not a physical gathering.
- 26 January Navalny solidarity event: the source describes an online action organized by a Swedish politician, with Norway's prime minister participating; no physical assembly in Norway is reported, and the demand concerns Russian authorities.
A LO Oslo archive lead announcing a Saturday 25 January demonstration is tied by its surrounding archive context to 2020; 25 January 2021 was a Monday. The DNMF political strike result for 28 January is from 2015. Stop Lockdown reports found for November 2020 and March 2021 do not date or size a January action. These boundary sources were not used to infer a January 2021 event.
There are no continuation records. A 17 January 2026 Stavanger police-log result is outside the assignment month and year.

Evidence and review limits
Sources include Norwegian parliamentary, labor, government, organizer, participant, local news and research pages. Organizer and participant accounts are useful for event occurrence and demands but are not independent crowd estimates. The January Røde Luer source network has no independent contemporary turnout estimate in the reviewed sources. Open-web searching is not equivalent to the historical LexisNexis search; absence of a located report does not establish that no event happened. English and Norwegian were searched; no other language or newspaper archive coverage is claimed.
The official baseline files and additional Coding Procedure were not verified. The May 2015 local manual is historical only, so the official-baseline gate remains pending. All event hypotheses are agent-coded for coordinator review and are not official dataset records.
