Congo Brazzaville — 2021-02 first-pass research dossier

This is a provisional research record for the Republic of the Congo (Congo Brazzaville; provisional ccode 484), not an official Mass Mobilization update. The official baseline remains pending.

Search and source scope

The dossier preserves 28 actual open-web search responses, including 459 ordered result headers and both identical general searches as separate executed calls. The pass used four weekly French search windows, broad French and English country-month searches, targeted SOPECO queries, limited Lingala/Kituba/Kikongo probes, regional searches naming Brazzaville, Pointe-Noire, Dolisie, Nkayi and Oyo, sector queries for education, health, transport and students, Amnesty/retrospective material, and a February/March boundary query. Search-result headers were retained as captured; relevant sources were recorded separately. This is a bounded open-web pass, not systematic LexisNexis or local-newspaper archive coverage, and cannot establish that no other protest occurred.

Open questions and coverage limits

The main unresolved questions are whether any public physical action occurred on 15–16 February; its actual start/end, exact target, venue, organizer, turnout and public status; and event-specific protester violence and state responses. Search coverage sampled several major cities and sectors but was not exhaustive. French and English dominated, with limited local-language probes. Some archives and exact story bodies were unavailable. Country-name ambiguity remains explicit: Congo Brazzaville is separate from the Democratic Republic of the Congo/Kinshasa, and generic “Congo” results were not assigned without country evidence. The unit remains provisional, open-web-only and pending independent review and the official baseline gate.
