Malawi — February 2021 research (provisional; open-web first pass)

Scope and method
This dossier covers events whose onset may belong to 1–28 February 2021 under the extension’s public-gathering criteria. The fixed project cutoff remains 2026-10-07. The 26 original search actions and their exact ordered headers are captured in search_log.jsonl; the final reconciliation compared every registered-rollout action ID, query, tool, result-header order, URLs and observed clocks with those rows. The capture contains 518 result headers. Queries include four weekly windows, broad protest/demonstration/strike/march searches, local and regional publishers, official-source discovery, health and midwifery, teachers and schools, transport, sex workers, business/policy topics, episode-specific follow-ups, and English/Chichewa terms. This is open-web discovery, not a reproduction of LexisNexis or an exhaustive census.

Candidate events
Three candidates remain for coordinator review. MWI202102-E001 is a proposed inclusion: one Nyasa Times report estimates “over 500” unemployed Community Midwifery Assistants on a Lilongwe march on Feb 12 seeking government employment and an interface with officials. The article provides no exact upper bound, physical end, formal organizer or state response; no independent body corroboration was read. The demand is provisionally mapped to labor_wage_dispute because the available taxonomy has no separate government-employment category.

MWI202102-E002 records the Nation report of Chiwoko Primary School learners protesting in the streets against the teachers’ strike. The article gives no date or count for that action; the separate Chichiri learner’s comment is not assigned to the Chiwoko group. Month placement, the 50-person threshold and a demand to Malawi’s government are unresolved.

MWI202102-E003 records only a possible teachers’ preparatory meeting reported in the saved summary for the Nyasa Times article consulted from the January dossier. The summary says police dispersed teachers at a Lilongwe meeting while they prepared demonstrations over a government Covid-risk allowance. Exact date, venue, attendance and whether the meeting itself was a qualifying public protest remain unresolved; it is not conflated with the later school strike.

Search-only leads for a nationwide teacher work stoppage, a conditional student demonstration over school closure, Dowa learners, disturbances in Neno/Lilongwe, and the Lake Chilwa fishermen/police shooting remain gaps. Headlines alone do not establish event dates, attendance, state-policy demands or protest character. No exclusions are recorded because the opened evidence does not resolve those leads conclusively outside scope.
