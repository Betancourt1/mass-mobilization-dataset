Source review and provisional candidates
Three source bodies were opened: CNDH’s 2021 human-rights annual report (S021), the public excerpt of TelQuel’s 12 February Fnideq article (S007), and Alyaoum24’s 20 February Rabat report (S004). Forty other source cards remain search-only.

E001 — Fnideq, 5 February. CNDH describes a gathering over economic and social hardship after the Bab Sebta crossing closure and COVID-19 effects, a demand for economic alternatives, dispersal by local authorities and subsequent arrests/prosecution. It gives no turnout figure. Four people prosecuted and the annual regional count of demonstrations are not attendance measures. CNDH calls the gathering peaceful, while TelQuel later characterizes the preceding action as ending in an “émeute”; this discrepancy remains unresolved. E001 stays pending_threshold.

E002 — Fnideq, 12 February. TelQuel’s public text describes a second sit-in at the main square/area around the Mohammed V mosque and says hundreds gathered to demand reopening of the Bab Sebta crossing. This supports at least 50 but gives no exact or bounded count. The excerpt is incomplete behind a subscription prompt; it does not establish the physical end or an event-specific state response. The brief mention of disturbances is not attributed to protesters. E002 remains proposed_include, pending coordinator review.

E003 — Rabat, 20 February. Alyaoum24 reports dozens of activists gathered at Bab al-Had square for a Social Front sit-in commemorating the 20 February Movement; authorities prevented the sit-in. The report says security pushed demonstrators and some activists said they were kicked. “Dozens” does not establish 50 or a numeric range, and the broader Moroccan Association for Human Rights demands statement is not attributed to those present. The report gives no physical end time. E003 stays pending_threshold; the reported kicking remains attributed, not independently confirmed in the excerpt.
