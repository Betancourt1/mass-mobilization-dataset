Coverage and method
Recorded 22 literal English, French and Arabic/Tifinagh queries covering general protest terms, weekly windows, health and education/labor sectors, named cities, postal workers, hammam professionals and one exploratory Tifinagh/Amazigh form. Search results were often pooled across queries; the service did not attribute each returned URL to its exact query. The logs preserve each literal query and disclose this limitation. Source cards preserve returned URLs, titles and web references. No search outcome is treated as evidence that no protest occurred.

Forty-seven relevant source cards are retained: ten opened pages/PDFs, thirty-five search-only leads and two blocked pages. Directly read sources include a local Arabic weekly, a local Arabic news article, a human-rights organization report, an online business outlet and Hespress reports. Ordinary HTTPS recovery attempts to twelve pages that had Cache-miss errors, including official and local postal-strike/picket leads for Oujda, Agadir and Casablanca, failed locally before HTTP response due DNS resolution failure; the RifDia page and a Hespress report about the Jan 26 Inezgane teachers’ march returned HTTP 403 and were not retried. The annual CNDH aggregate (13,471 gatherings across all of 2021) is contextual only and was not allocated to January or used as event attendance.

Preliminary records
Fourteen event candidates are retained. The strongest occurrence evidence includes coordinated contract-teacher gatherings on 26 January in Casablanca and Inezgane; 27 January health-worker sit-ins reported at 92 sites; unemployed-graduate actions in Fez, Nador and Midar; a Sidi Bennour worker picket that continued as a sit-in; and other local labor actions, including a separately reported Jan 26 school security/cleaning workers’ action in Marrakech. AMDH’s appeal for state intervention in that passage is not clearly attributed to the protesters, so target eligibility remains pending. The 92 figure is sites, not people. “Hundreds” for teachers appears only in an unopened search result and is not treated as a numerical count. Local attendance is absent for most records, so thresholds remain pending or unresolved. A second health-worker report gives the literal date “Wednesday 21 January 2021,” which conflicts with the calendar and may duplicate the directly reported Wednesday 27 January actions; it remains search-only, month-precision and pending evidence, with no corrected date or automatic merge.

Two exclusions are retained: an ENSA course boycott/official meeting with no public gathering documented in the opened article, and a Peugeot-Citroën Kenitra factory work stoppage. The source says the governor visited and mediated talks, but documents no public gathering; this remains distinct from Khenifra. One Amanour strike/sit-in is recorded as a continuation because AMDH says it had reached day 360 on 14 January; its exact onset and owning month are unknown. A possible Karama transport-worker gathering remains pending because the machine-rendered AMDH text does not clarify who gathered or whether it was a public action. The separate Marrakech workers’ action remains pending on state-target attribution, attendance threshold, exact site and physical end.

Open questions
A further end-of-month sweep found an opened report of a planned Jan 21 sit-in by hammam professionals outside the Casablanca-Settat Wilaya; authorities prohibited it in advance. Mixed search snippets say both that it was planned/banned and that owners decided to demonstrate on Jan 21, but no opened source confirms that a crowd assembled, so E013 remains pending evidence with no actual start date. A separate opened report dated Dec 17, 2020 documents the fifth day of a Jbel Aouam mine strike/sit-in; it is not January evidence, and whether it continued into January is unresolved.
