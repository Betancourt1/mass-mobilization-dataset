January candidates
Three candidates are proposed for review. POL202101-E001 is the Jan20 Warsaw Women’s Strike protest. The opened Onet report supports a several-hundred crowd, state-policy grievances, snow thrown at police, police dispersal calls/cordon/gas, and one detention. The next-day RPO note preserves detainees’ attributed brutality complaints and the police’s lawful-action account; no specific beating is established. Physical departure is unknown.

POL202101-E002 is the Jan27 coordinated Women’s Strike action reported at multiple sites. The opened Onet live report gives more than 100 in Szczecin and separate approximate or qualitative figures for other cities; no crowd totals are combined. Police dispersal calls at 19:01 support a yes for the Warsaw-site crowd-dispersal response under the manual’s warning-as-attempt rule. This does not establish successful dispersal or response at other sites. The national physical end remains unknown.

POL202101-E003 is the separately announced Jan28 multi-city action. Onet reports actual activity in multiple cities; an official police response estimates roughly 1,300 at peak in Warsaw, while Onet describes hundreds in Szczecin. These are site-specific, not conflicting estimates of one crowd, and are not summed. The police response and contemporaneous reporting document warnings/cordons, arrests with time-specific attributed counts, and reports of protester conduct. RPO’s inquiry and the attached KSP response are related official records, not independent corroboration. An attributed arm-jerking allegation and reported uses of force do not establish a beating. Warsaw was still reported active at 22:33; physical end and national close remain unknown.

POL202101-E004 preserves the Jan18 KOD free-courts lead. KOD’s calendar reports Kielce and related multi-city pickets; an opened contemporary Łódź article confirms a local picket. Kielce/Białystok source leads include advance announcements, and the Białystok article was blocked. No executed site has a supported attendance count in the captured bodies; the 50-person threshold and local-versus-national coding unit remain unresolved, so the candidate is pending_unit.

POL202101-E005 preserves a Jan14 KOD candle-heart commemoration in Kielce with an appeal about beginning a criminal trial. The source gives no turnout and does not establish that the ceremony was a qualifying public protest; it remains pending_evidence.

Exclusion and prior-onset continuation
POL202101-X001 records Jan23 demonstrations in Warsaw and Kraków supporting Alexei Navalny. The opened Polish Radio article reports a dozen or so (kilkunastu) in Warsaw and about 100 in Kraków, without combining those sites. The stated demands concern release of Russian political prisoners, an end to repression in Russia, and peaceful assembly rights there; no demand to the Polish government is reported. They are excluded as foreign_target_only.

POL202101-C001 is a Dec31 Warsaw protest against the New Year movement restriction, owned provisionally by poland_2020-12 rather than January. Contemporary OKO.press records the same gathering still at Rondo de Gaulle’a at midnight Jan1, physically crossing the month boundary. The report gives no crowd count, so the 50-person threshold is unresolved. It does not establish a later physical end. A separate Żoliborz New Year activity in the same report is not merged or used for turnout or ending evidence. The ObyPomoc PDF search lead mentioning more than six hours could not be opened and does not set the recorded end.

Boundary and coding limits
February boundary results include a Feb1 ESKA article describing planned recurring Monday actions and search-only leads to Feb1 city reports. These do not establish physical continuity from Jan27–28 or an exact national close. Jan20, Jan27, and Jan28 physical endings remain unknown; the Warsaw Jan28 report still active at 22:33 does not prove a nationwide end. Multi-city coordination is recorded without automatic aggregation or splitting; the official event-unit procedure and baseline remain pending.
