Equatorial Guinea, February 2021 — open-web research report

Scope and method
This dossier covers February 1–28, 2021, with event ownership based on onset. Ten actual batched web-search invocations are captured in search_log.jsonl and capture-history/actual/; the batches contain 40 query strings and 239 ordered result headers. Searches used Spanish, English, French and Portuguese terms, including four February weekly windows and month-boundary searches. Topics included demonstrations, protests, strikes, workers, students, graduates, CPDS and civil society, with relevant local and official domains. Search-result text was used for discovery only. This is a limited open-web pass, not an exhaustive survey or a claim that no protest occurred.

Sources and access
Twenty-eight source cards are retained. All are search_only; 0 current-unit body opens and 1 prior-unit-only body consultation are recorded. The prior consultation is January source EQG202101-S071, a selective read of the same Europa Press URL used by February source S001. It describes CPDS’s demand for the prime minister to resign in a statement about a government-convened employment-information meeting with graduates; this is not a new February body read or an independent second source.

Current body attempts on Europa Press S001 returned a web-tool Cache miss. A stale S001 access check failed its immediate input fingerprint check, so no request followed that receipt. Attempts on AhoraEG S012 returned a DNS resolution failure via ordinary HTTPS and then a web-tool Cache miss. An ordinary HTTPS attempt on Radio Macuto S002 failed DNS resolution. None returned an HTTP denial or article body, and none is coded as a denial. Exact-URL receipts and full saved publisher/origin reviews are under access_receipts/. The other historical and contextual cards were retained as search-only without body requests.

Findings and decisions
No February event is sufficiently established for events.json. EQG202102-X001 preserves the reported government-convened graduates’ meeting as an unresolved exclusion/lead: search results report more than 1,000 attendees, but do not establish that the attendees protested or made a qualifying demand. The reported CPDS demand for the prime minister’s resignation may be a separate action. This is not a finding that no protest occurred. The other five exclusions preserve historical search leads dated or described as 2014–2017, including UNGE scholarship mobilization, the Rebola cultural-center dispute and a PDGE-aligned demonstration. Their search-only turnout, violence and response claims are unverified and do not establish February 2021 continuity. No continuation is confirmed. GECOMSA wage-arrears reports are retained as a pre-February labor lead; no February strike or public gathering is established by the search material.

Gaps and review state
Seven explicit gaps cover meeting eligibility and date, CPDS relationship, GECOMSA continuity/action, unreadable source bodies, language and local-media coverage, unavailable LexisNexis/official baseline review, unknown turnout/closure/violence/state response, and regional/sector chronology. Search-result counts are not turnout evidence. If the meeting is later shown to be a protest, turnout threshold, date, demands, violence, state response and closure require separate source-backed coding. The original cutoff and codebook are unchanged; baseline_gate remains pending_official_baseline. This dossier is pending coordinator and human review.
