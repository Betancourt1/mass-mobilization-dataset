SWITZERLAND — JANUARY 2021
Documented open-web first pass; coordinator review pending

Cutoff and scope
The event cutoff is 2026-10-07 inclusive, America/Mexico_City. Searches and reads for this unit were actually conducted on 2026-10-09. No source card has a publication date later than the cutoff; search-result headers are retained as returned, including unrelated or later-dated results that were not coded as evidence.

The first pass includes 38 logged searches: weekly January searches in German, French, Italian and English; targeted checks for Bern, Geneva, Central Switzerland, Ticino and Grisons; sector checks for health, labor, agriculture, transport, students, housing and environmental actions; and December/February boundary checks. No Romansh-specific search or closed-news-archive review was conducted. Local and rural source coverage is limited. This is not exhaustive and is not an official MM update.

Candidate records
None is approved for the official catalogue. The proposed and pending records are:

- E006 — 2 January, Bern Bundesplatz, anti-COVID-measures gathering. Nau and a republication credited to Kantonspolizei Bern say several dozen people gathered; about 25 remained after police ordered departure. The initial crowd is not shown to have reached 50, so eligibility is pending_threshold. Police attempted to disperse the gathering; formal arrest status is unknown.
- E002 — 9 January, coordinated Central Switzerland rallies in Schwyz, Altdorf, Stans and Sarnen. Local police estimates are about 400, 220, 300 and 200 respectively; Keystone-SDA reports over 1,000 combined, while speakers cited by Blick claimed 1,300. The Schwyz count and four-site total are kept separate. The one-versus-multiple-site unit decision is pending; the aggregate extremes are reported estimates, not statistical bounds.
- E004 — 23 January, Bern Bundesplatz, about 100 people against government COVID restrictions. Police ordered people to leave and checked people who remained; some were followed and checked elsewhere. The report's “stopped/held and reported” wording and citations do not establish formal arrests. The final physical endpoint is unknown.
- E003 — 30 January, Zurich walk against COVID measures, several hundred at Münsterhof. Police blocked the route, made announcements, and participants left in groups and regrouped at Limmatquai/Niederdorf for checks and further orders. Four people taken to a station for inquiries are not coded as arrested. The police operation's end shortly before 16:00 does not establish the protest's final endpoint.
- E007 — Geneva lead found in the 20 minutes 30 January archive index. This remains pending_evidence only. The archive/publication label is not coded as a physical start date; location is only a headline label, and no attendance, demand category, or state response is inferred. It is separate from Geneva's documented 26 February gathering.

Exclusions and unresolved source leads
E001 retains the announced private-business reopening initiative; no executed public gathering of at least 50 was documented. E005 retains the announced “white coats” window display and brief hospital work abstention; neither establishes an assembled public crowd of at least 50, and execution was not independently confirmed. A separate 12 January Ticinolive headline about possible restaurateur and medical-worker protests is preserved as blocked source S080, not merged with either exclusion; its exact page returned HTTP 403 and was not retried.

No verified episode beginning before January and continuing into it was found, so continuations.json is empty. February reports, including the Geneva 26 February event and later Zurich actions, remain outside this unit; no automatic cross-month merge is made.

Coverage and limitations
The unit contains 80 source cards: 28 opened, 46 search_only, 5 blocked and 1 not_found. Search-result false positives included older and later protests. Some relevant article pages were inaccessible or returned only an index; no LexisNexis or equivalent closed-news-archive review was performed. Eight follow-up gaps are listed in gaps.json, including crowd thresholds, event-unit scope, final event endpoints, arrest-status wording, inaccessible reports, and the limits of the open-web pass. The current official baseline and additional Coding Procedure remain coordinator-controlled; baseline_gate is pending_official_baseline.
