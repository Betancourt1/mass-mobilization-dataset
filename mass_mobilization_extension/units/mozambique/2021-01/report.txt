Mozambique — January 2021 (provisional ccode 541)

This is an open-web research checkpoint for coordinator review, not an exhaustive census or an official dataset update. The pass saved 74 literal Portuguese- and English-language searches and 25 source cards. Coverage included broad protest/strike terms, weekly date windows, Maputo and regional-city probes, local outlets, public-service/health/teacher/transport/labor sectors, land/water grievances, COVID restrictions and before/after-month leads. Search results were noisy, often returning 2024–2025 electoral protests, 2022 transport/labor actions, other countries, armed conflict or older history.

Query provenance is uneven and documented. Q001–Q047 retain their literal strings but their earlier pooled URL outputs were lost from the work record; these lines have null returned_urls and are not empty-result findings. Q048–Q051 preserve a visible pooled 25-URL union without per-query attribution. Q052–Q061 retain visible individual city-query URL unions. Q062–Q074 were run and saved individually; the visible URL/title/ref rows were saved, but result returns for Q062, Q063, Q066, Q069 and Q072–Q074 were visibly truncated, so omitted rows are not inferred to be empty. Q072–Q074 reran weekly windows for 1–7, 8–14 and 15–21 January; the 22–31 January output remains the earlier lost-mapping Q044 and was not rerun. A query count does not establish coverage.

No public assembly meeting the documented threshold of at least 50 people and a demand directed to Mozambique’s state or policy was established in this pass. The candidate array is empty; this means “not found in this pass,” not “none occurred.” No continuation into January was established. Large Cabo Delgado displaced/affected-population figures are not demonstrator turnout, and armed-conflict incidents are not protest events.

One prior-month cargo-transporters’ action remains in excluded.json as a boundary lead because reporting dates the gathering to 2 December 2020. Rádio Moçambique and VOA report employment-related grievances, including wages, recognition, contracts and social security, largely in relation to employers. VOA also reports one driver objecting to fines for mechanical faults; this could imply a public-policy grievance, but the authority and exact target of that complaint are unresolved. The January ownership decision rests on the documented December date; the reported 19 Nampula detentions do not establish total turnout, and an organizer’s intention to continue does not establish a January occurrence or prove that the broader dispute ended.

Opened material includes reports on January COVID restrictions and a Palma return-to-work order, UN/WFP and UNRIC humanitarian reporting, a bounded portion of ACLED Cabo Ligado’s January conflict-monitoring report, the O País 1 January and 13 January daily archives, December trucker reports from Rádio Moçambique and VOA Português, two Nampula traffic-light/utility-dispute reports, and DW’s report on individual water collection amid Nampula scarcity. The O País 13 January archive’s visible list includes COVID, armed-conflict, tourism and street-vending-policy items, not a protest item; an archive listing does not prove absence. Nampula coverage describes an official dispute/press briefing and individual water collection, not a public assembly. MMO credits O País on the traffic-light story, so those pages are not independent confirmation. Search-only results from Quelimane, Gaza, Maxixe and Niassa describe party meetings/outreach or an official decree briefing rather than protest; pages that cache-missed remain search_only. Displacement sources and conflict monitoring are retained only as context.

Access and scope limits remain material. Portuguese and English were used, but Changana, Sena, Makhuwa/Emakhuwa, Nyanja and other regional-language radio, transcript and print coverage was not systematically reviewed. Open-web search is not equivalent to LexisNexis. Exact request/access times were not captured; executed_at and accessed_at remain null, and recorded_at identifies when metadata was saved.
