Methods and coverage
I ran 25 public web searches and parsed 511 returned result headers, preserving each query, timestamp, title/URL/reference ordering, and title-capture limitations in search_log.jsonl. Header count is not a coverage measure. Searches used Vietnamese and English. They included broad January searches; January 1–7, 8–14, 15–18, 19–21, and 22–31 windows; labor/strike and collective-work-stoppage terms; land and environmental grievances; COVID-related impacts; BOT/public-service complaints; central-region city names; industrial-province names; and December 2020 and February 2021 boundary searches. Geographic terms included Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang, Hue, Quang Nam, Binh Duong, and Dong Nai. This is non-exhaustive open-web research, not an equivalent of LexisNexis or a province-by-province review of local and regional archives. There was no independent Vietnamese-language review.

Four follow-up originals were added from saved headers and opened after a fresh full metadata-only publisher/story-denial scan before each request. Each scan parsed 165 saved unit source files, 18,828 source cards, and 2,333 coordinator source checks with zero parse errors; no matching denial record was found for those publishers/stories. The saved raw search keys for Q001, Q006, and Q007 were not recoverable in the current tool session; the saved result headers remained available, no search was rerun, and this recovery limit is recorded in the relevant source cards and log notes.

Findings
No source reviewed establishes a public gathering of at least 50 people in Vietnam during January 2021 with a demand directed to the domestic state or its policy. The unit therefore contains no event candidate, exclusion, or continuation record. Those empty arrays mean no qualifying case was established in this research; they do not mean no protest occurred.

Five source cards are retained. S001 is an opened VOV article with no captured publication date; it reports 2020 labor-dispute totals and a forecast of possible Tết disputes, not a January 2021 gathering. S002 is a Dân trí article dated 2022-01-11 reporting an annual total of 105 2021 stoppages and saying they occurred mainly before and after Tết Tân Sửu; it gives no January-specific cases or count. Its three search references are to the same article, not independent sources. S003 is a Human Rights Pulse article dated 2020-10-23 reporting a hunger strike by detained activists that began 2020-09-05; it does not show a public January 2021 gathering or continuity into January. S004 is a Thanh Niên article dated 2020-01-20 about stoppages that year, outside the unit. S005 is a VietnamNet article dated 2011-02-26 about an earlier Hanoi factory strike, outside the unit; its page credits VIR, but the full-text relationship was not verified. These contextual and out-of-period sources are not event records.
