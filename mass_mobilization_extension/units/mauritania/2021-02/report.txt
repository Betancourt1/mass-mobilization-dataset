Mauritania — February 2021 first-pass research dossier

Scope and method
This is a candidate dossier for unit mauritania_2021-02, provisional ccode 435, with the fixed cutoff of 2026-10-07. It records an open-web first pass, not exhaustive national coverage, official coding, or integration into the legacy catalogue. I captured 43 literal web-search actions and 803 ordered result headers from the registered worker rollout, including one intact batched Arabic/French invocation. A final event-ID/query/header-order comparison found all 43 actions represented exactly in search_log.jsonl; no search was rerun to reconstruct a capture.

Queries included Arabic, French and English month-level and weekly terms and targeted labor/non-teacher, teacher/student, roads, Tifirit, Chami, Koylal/Feralla land conflict, AIDS-secretariat employees, miners, geologists, ports/fisheries and other sectors. Captured locations include Nouakchott, Tifirit, Chami, Koylal/Afir Lal/M’Bagne, Aioun and the Nouakchott–Akjoujt corridor. Searches do not establish complete coverage across Mauritania’s regions or all local-language variants. Hassaniya/local-register terminology was not systematically assessed. LexisNexis and equivalent newspaper archives were not available as a systematic search method; no exhaustive claim is made.
