Netherlands — February 2021 research report (open-web first pass)

Scope and method
The unit covers protest assemblies beginning 1–28 February 2021, under the provisional Netherlands assignment (ccode 210) and fixed cutoff of 7 October 2026 (America/Mexico_City). Thirteen Dutch- and English-language open-web search actions produced 420 ordered result headers and 34 source cards: 9 with captured body text, 24 search-only cards, and 1 URL-specific HTTP 429. There were 24 exact-URL body attempts across 20 cards; 9 returned article content and 15 returned no article body. Repeated attempts had separate fresh access checks and manual reviews. All ordered headers and exact tool references are retained in search_log.jsonl; the original registered-rollout extraction was compared action-by-action (13/13, 420 headers, no differences). This is a researched open-web first pass, not exhaustive coverage and not a reproduction of LexisNexis All News.

Coverage included weekly COVID/curfew demonstrations, local Amsterdam and Maastricht reports, the February Strike commemoration, Schiphol climate action, and Extinction Rebellion’s Lenterebellie climate campaign. Searches do not establish complete national or regional coverage. Labor, education, health and other sectoral protests, local archives, and deeper local reporting remain uneven. Headers and proposed plans remain discovery evidence unless a source body is captured; arrests, vehicles, event publication dates and nearby disorder are not turnout or event facts by themselves.

Candidate assemblies
E001 is the 6 February Extinction Rebellion protest at Schiphol. NL Times describes action at several airport locations against aviation’s contribution to the climate crisis and reports seven arrests; NOS, explicitly working with NH Nieuws, describes a permitted Plaza demonstration of several dozen and arrests in the departure hall. “Several dozen” does not establish 50 people, total attendance across locations is unknown, and a specific government-directed policy demand is not demonstrated. Keep distinct locations and arrests separate; eligibility remains pending state-target and threshold review.

E002 is the 7 February Maastricht Vrijthof demonstration against COVID measures. NL Times reports about 200 people. Police warnings about distancing preceded movement from the square around 15:15 and an emergency ordinance. A separate same-day Amsterdam group is described as a handful and is excluded as below threshold (X001); the inaccessible Amsterdam local report does not add facts.

E003 is the 14 February Amsterdam Museumplein lead. AT5 reports “dozens” demonstrating against COVID measures, but that qualitative count does not show whether 50 attended. The report says the gathering was calm at publication. A security-risk designation was associated with earlier violence, not evidence of violence or dispersal at this gathering. The count remains pending.

E004 and E005 are physically separate 21 February gatherings. NOS reports about 1,000 at Amsterdam Museumplein opposing COVID measures; police called for distancing and then dispersed the crowd, with one arrest reported. At Leeuwarden near the Fries Museum, NOS reports several hundred in a mixed group and identifies lockdown and curfew opponents, but does not state a shared demand, quantify that subset, or establish the gathering’s final end. The candidates remain separate; E005 remains pending state-target determination.

E006 is Extinction Rebellion’s nationally coordinated Lenterebellie kickoff on 26 February. NOS reports about 80 at Amsterdam Overtoom and several dozen at Rotterdam Erasmusbrug, where police forced the group off the bridge; XR later estimates about 80 in Rotterdam and alleges police pushed, kicked or struck participants. NOS reports differing arrest counts, and Utrecht arrest totals are not attendance. A separate MediaTV local Rotterdam lead returned Cache miss and adds no body evidence. Keep estimates and allegations attributed, do not sum local counts into a national total, and leave the one-event versus separate local-assembly coding decision for review. The 27 February actions and planned Tilburg demonstration are not counted from the prospective press release alone.

E007 is a possible 28 February Museumplein demonstration appearing in NH Nieuws and Amsterdams Dagblad search leads. Both exact URLs returned Cache miss twice across fresh-gated attempts; bodies, attendance, demand, response and even execution remain unverified. It is retained as a pending-evidence lead, not an established event.

X002 records an AT5 February search lead whose opened story actually dates the Museumplein gathering to 24 January. It is excluded as outside the month; none of its approximately 1,500 attendees, COVID demand, emergency order or police dispersal is transferred to February. January dossier episodes were consulted only to test boundaries. Repeated weekly branding and the February 21 report of a future March 14 finale do not establish physical continuation. No continuation is coded.

Evidence limits and follow-up
Thirteen open gaps retain the unresolved 14 February threshold; Leeuwarden’s shared demand and eligible subgroup; physical endings; Schiphol threshold and state target; Lenterebellie event-unit choice, local attendance/arrest discrepancies and attributed police-force allegations; inaccessible 28 February and 25 February bodies; verification of prospective 26–27 February actions; and open-web/archive coverage. The source-specific technical Cache misses and HTTP 400s are not treated as publisher denials. The 25 February Eerste Kamer URL produced an HTTP 429 and remains blocked at that exact URL only; no retry or publisher-wide hold is inferred. The local May 2015 Clark–Regan manual was consulted, while the latest official baseline and supplemental Coding Procedure remain unverified; baseline approval is pending coordinator review.

This dossier preserves candidate, exclusion, continuation, source, access and gap distinctions for independent review. It does not update the integrated catalogue or constitute official approval.
