Netherlands — January 2021 research report (open-web first pass)

Scope and method
The unit covers events beginning 1–31 January 2021; the provisional assignment gives ccode 210. The fixed dataset cutoff remains 7 October 2026 (America/Mexico_City). This first pass persisted 14 Dutch- and English-language open-web queries and 84 source cards (20 opened, 64 search-only), identifying 18 local candidate gatherings, one below-threshold announced gathering and 19 follow-up gaps. It is not an exhaustive search and does not reproduce the specified LexisNexis procedure. Coverage is concentrated on COVID and curfew activity; labor, education, health, environment and some regional coverage remain incomplete. Search-result counts are not event counts or independent corroborations.

Proposed inclusions
E001 is the 17 January Amsterdam Museumplein gathering against COVID measures. Reports describe well over a thousand attendees or an official estimate near 2,000; 143 arrests and water-cannon dispersal after warnings. Reported violence is attributed to a subset. A DutchNews story published on 18 January contains contradictory weekday wording (“Monday’s” and Sunday); other opened reports identify Sunday 17 January. The square was cleared, but people remained on nearby streets; the physical end of the wider gathering is unknown.

E014 is the 10 January Hilversum Marktplein protest against lockdown and COVID measures. RTL/ANP reports hundreds; an NH Nieuws search excerpt gives approximately 400–500. The city called on participants to leave and the square was later empty, supporting attempted dispersal. One arrest is reported, but its reason is unspecified.

E002 and E003 are separate 24 January demonstrations in Eindhoven and Amsterdam against government COVID measures. NOS reports hundreds in both cities. Amsterdam’s police estimate was about 1,500 with 190 arrests; an emergency order and police action cleared Museumplein, though the wider physical end is not established. In Eindhoven the demonstration was ended and hundreds later returned after an initial clearance. NRC quotes ANP that police struck back with batons, supporting beatings yes. Later looting and property destruction are treated separately from the state-directed assembly; violence is attributed only to reported subsets.

E008 is the 23 January Stein curfew-opposition gathering. NRC reports about 100 youths protesting the curfew; NOS describes dozens and the mayor disputed the demonstration label. NRC reports fireworks thrown at police, while NOS does not specify the target; violence is attributed to a subset, not the whole crowd. Fourteen arrests are reported for the gathering or disorder; participant overlap and physical end remain unresolved.

E017 is the 24 January Breda Tuinzigt gathering called against the curfew and Rutte. An opened 2022 retrospective report says about 70 people gathered; an official court search excerpt says 40–50 were in the area around 20:20. The estimate and time-specific count discrepancy are preserved. The report describes later fires, barricades and private-property damage, followed by an emergency order and riot-police intervention. Those later acts are not assigned to every attendee; protester violence against the state and the gathering’s exact physical end remain unknown.

E018 is a separate evening Helmond De Braak lead on 24 January. The retrospective report estimates about 50 people gathered amid signals about continuing riots and says riot police drove the group apart. It does not establish a state-directed demand by this crowd; attendance is near the threshold and demand remains pending. Keep it separate from X001, the announced afternoon protest near the stadium where only a handful appeared.

E015 and E016 are separate 31 January demonstrations against cabinet COVID measures. NOS reports roughly 600 at Amsterdam Museumplein, with an earlier municipal estimate near 200; police cleared the square but demonstrators remained nearby. Thirty arrests on Valeriusplein are not attributable to attendees from the opened evidence. At Apeldoorn Zwitsalterrein, NOS reports about 400 attendees who left peacefully around 16:00; no attempt to disperse them is documented, so crowd_dispersal remains unknown. The nearby/citywide arrests are not linked to attendees. A previously announced organizer had canceled, so the actual gathering’s organizer remains unknown; four citywide arrests are not linked to attendees.

Unresolved January candidates
E004 Enschede is described as “dozens” demonstrating against COVID measures, below a clear threshold determination. E005 Tilburg, E006 Venlo and E007 The Hague remain pending evidence because the available reports do not establish both a qualifying crowd and a specific state-policy demand. E009 Urk has “dozens” of youths reported separately from about 100 cars and a nearby test-site fire; vehicles do not count as attendees, and the fire is not automatically attributed to the gathering. E010 Rotterdam is proposed for inclusion: NRC reports about 100 demonstrators against COVID measures before curfew, while RTL’s around-50 figure is the later group remaining outside. NRC says police broke up the remaining short demonstration on 23 January; the exact end time is not reported. E011 Den Helder and E012 Amersfoort are described as curfew demonstrations without crowd counts. E013 Amsterdam Westerpark had about 350 anti-fascism demonstrators, but the opened report does not establish a direct demand to Dutch state authorities. These remain pending rather than being silently excluded.

X001, the announced curfew protest near the Helmond stadium on 24 January, is excluded from candidates because a contemporary report says only a handful attended, below the 50-person threshold. No protest is inferred from criminal disorder, arson or property damage alone.

Episode boundaries, response fields and limitations
The 17, 24 and 31 January Museumplein gatherings remain separate candidates; February reporting about weekly Museumplein actions is a continuity lead, not proof that separate dates form one event. A 20 December Dam pop-up protest outside the month was reported as about 15 people and is not linked to a January assembly. No continuation record is added because continuity is not established.

All 18 candidates retain the seven state-response fields: accommodation, arrests, beatings, crowd dispersal, ignore, killings and shootings. Unknown values remain unknown where evidence is silent or attribution is unclear. A baton formation is not by itself a beating; the Eindhoven baton-strike report is explicit. Water cannon or tear gas is not gunfire. City-level crowd counts are kept separate from national arrest totals and vehicles. Physical end dates remain unknown where sources report only an emptied square, later calm, police departure or an early phase; none is inferred from silence.

Final metadata audit repaired a duplicate source identifier: s072 is restored to the February RTL search-only result, and the opened 20 December NH Nieuws article now has source ID s080. The previous incorrect mapping copied NH Nieuws content onto the RTL URL; it was corrected by restoring the RTL card as search-only and assigning the opened NH Nieuws card s080. No event claim relied on the incorrect mapping. A prior status.updated_at value was temporarily entered as 20:35:00Z before that time had occurred; it was repaired at the actual clock reading 20:20:33Z, and subsequent checkpoint times were taken from the clock. Exact web request times were not exposed and remain null.

The official baseline and Coding Procedure gate remain unresolved. Open gaps cover count conflicts, state-target framing, event ownership, physical endings, response attribution, access limits, independent-source overlap, geography, search method and contested evidence. This report records a researched first pass pending coordinator review, not official approval or an update to the integrated catalogue.
