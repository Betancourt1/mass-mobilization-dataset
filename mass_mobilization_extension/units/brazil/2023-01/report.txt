Brazil — January 2023 first-pass research

Scope and method
This is a bounded, open-web first pass for Brazil (ccode 140), 1–31 January 2023. Eight actual search responses were captured in order: one broad English query; Portuguese broad/month and Jan 9 episode queries; Jan 8/9 response; Jan 1–7 encampment/road/refinery search; grouped Jan 15–21 and Jan 22–31 windows with labor, Indigenous and student terms; and selected regional-city searches. The saved log contains 115 aligned result headers and 52 unique source cards. The pass includes contemporary local reporting, an organizer report/announcement and a retrospective Folha article reproducing selected ABIN alerts. It does not claim an exhaustive census, recreate LexisNexis, or establish that an event did not happen because it was not found.

12 current-unit body opens were read; 0 prior-unit-only consultations were made. Read scopes and access-receipt paths are recorded on each source card. Selected URLs were opened based on their potential to establish dates, physical attendance, domestic target, boundaries or continuity; not all surfaced result URLs were opened. Six ordinary non-AP direct-open attempts returned cache misses/internal errors without an HTTP status: S015, S022, S027, S034, S036 and S047. This is not a site denial, and their search-result text was not used as body evidence. Manual exact-URL/publisher/origin review preceded body attempts; `manual_review` was not treated as permission.

Access/provenance incident
Search responses BRA23Q002 and BRA23Q003 incidentally surfaced AP article text. No AP body was requested or opened. No AP article text was retained, cited, copied or used in event coding. S009, S010, S011 and S025 remain discovery metadata only and are marked search_only with this incident recorded.

Candidate events
- Jan 8, Brasília: a march from the pre-existing Army HQ camp to the Three Powers buildings and occupation. Sources describe thousands; SSP/DF estimated 3,000 near the camp before the march, not inside the buildings. Entry/vandalism, arrests and clearance are documented. Candidate E001 remains pending_unit because the event’s boundary and owner relative to the pre-month campaign are unresolved.
- Jan 9, São Paulo: the Paulista Avenue march demanded punishment, no amnesty and accountability. Folha reports no police estimate and attributes “more than 50,000” to Guilherme Boulos. Candidate E002 is proposed_include with that attribution preserved. The separate earlier USP Law School gathering of about 800 is E003 pending_unit while its relation to the later march remains unresolved.
- Jan 9, Manaus: approximately 50 people were reported near Isaac Sabbá refinery access at 07:00 and absent roughly an hour later. The threshold is borderline and the source describes blocking fuel distribution without a specific domestic-government demand. E004 is pending_state_target. Reuters is a written contribution to S050, so the report is not an independent Reuters confirmation.
- Jan 16, Porto Alegre: an Ajuris-organized democracy gathering is documented, with no attendee estimate; E005 remains pending_threshold.
- Jan 25, Porto Alegre: Brasil de Fato describes thousands at the World Social Forum opening march. Interviews report varied demands; E006 remains pending_state_target. The cycle-delivery-worker subgroup’s platform dispute is not generalized to the march.

One exclusion records the reported Brasília (three people) and São Paulo (about ten) gatherings around the Jan 11 pro-Bolsonaro call as below 50. It does not treat selected locations as a national census. A continuation record preserves the pre-month post-election encampment/campaign and related road actions; its exact owner, unity and end remain unresolved. Jan 9 camp clearances do not prove the broader campaign ended.

Limits and review state
The candidate records preserve all seven state-response dimensions; unreported dimensions remain unknown. Gaps retain attendance, domestic-government target, monthly ownership, closure, event separation, source access and response questions. Body-open statuses indicate a read scope, not independent corroboration or completeness. Reuters is explicitly credited in S050; organizer reports are identified as such. The official baseline and version gate remain pending. All event eligibility is provisional, coordinator and independent review remain outstanding, and no official dataset row has been changed.
