Malaysia — January 2021 research checkpoint
Recorded: 2026-10-08 17:24:00 UTC

Scope and method
This pass used 64 web queries in English and Malay, with additional Chinese and Tamil terms. Search dimensions included weekly windows, workers and unions, emergency-related mobilization, geography (Sabah, Sarawak, Penang, Johor, Kelantan and Kuala Lumpur), sectors, local/regional outlets, planned or cancelled gatherings, and December/February boundaries. Relevant returned URLs were retained in the query log; pooled results could not be attributed reliably to a single query. Exact search/open times were not captured, so executed_at and accessed_at are null; recorded_at marks this checkpoint only. The work used open-web search, not a systematic LexisNexis or newspaper-archive review.

Findings
No January-onset public assembly meeting the evidence threshold was established in this pass. A targeted follow-up surfaced additional local-language reports about later or undated emergency/Undi18 gatherings a January archive index and a 2020 Tamil rally lead, but snippets/open failures do not establish January event occurrence or continuity. The January 14 #BantahDaruratMuhyiddin item was described as an online campaign by a CSO coalition, without physical gathering, venue or attendance documented in sources read. UMNO’s January 30–31 General Assembly was an internal party event announced as postponed before its planned dates, not a protest. Accordingly, events.json and continuations.json are empty. These research arrays do not assert that no protest occurred.

Boundary and context checks
December 2020 and February 2021 checks did not establish an episode continuing through January. Later March Undi18 reports are outside the month and do not document January continuity. Emergency and movement-control reporting is policy context only; restrictions and lack of indexed reports are not evidence of absence.

Counts at checkpoint: queries 64; sources 21; candidates 0; exclusions 2; continuations 0; gaps 7.

Limits
Local-language and regional archive coverage is incomplete. Some pages remained search-only after reader cache misses, without an established site denial; several local-language boundary leads have unconfirmed dates. Search does not reproduce systematic newspaper archives. Source independence is limited for the online-campaign reports. Official baseline and current coding procedure remain pending. See gaps.json for follow-up.

Status
Research is still being checked; this is not an official dataset update or an absence determination.
