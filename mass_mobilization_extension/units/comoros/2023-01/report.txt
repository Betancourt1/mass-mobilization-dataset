Comoros — January 2023 research report

Status: researched_open_web_pending_review. Fixed research cutoff: 2026-10-07, inclusive, America/Mexico_City. Queries and capture work were completed on 2026-10-10. Candidate events and sources are limited to the assigned January 2023 unit; later query results are preserved in capture files without being treated as January evidence. The May 2015 codebook in baseline/mm_codebook.txt was consulted. The additional Mass Mobilization Coding Procedure remains unavailable, and the official baseline, universe and current schema remain unverified.

Method and coverage

The unit contains 12 captured native search actions (search_log.jsonl and capture_inputs/COM202301-Q001–Q012.json). They cover general country-month searches in French, English and Arabic; four weekly French windows; student, teacher and market/labor sectors; Moroni, Mvouni, Mutsamudu and Iconi/Anjouan leads; regional/local press; event-specific follow-up; one keyword-level Shikomori search; and official/human-rights searches. The 12 captures preserve 315 native result headers and exact raw URLs, with snippets omitted by the project helper. Grouped calls are counted as one query each. Search volume does not establish completeness.

Five current-unit pages/issues were opened and all five now have explicit saved read scopes: three news-article bodies (S002, S006, S010), the Comores Infos January archive listing (S005), and selected portions of La Gazette issue 4306 (S008). The archive and newspaper issue have explicit read limitations; their full linked stories/issue were not read. S002 and S010 are from the same outlet/author and are not independent corroboration. S005 is an archive listing, not the linked articles. S008 is a 1 February issue: its municipal press-conference report does not document the Volo Volo gathering; a separate page describes the Mutsamudu labor action. The Africa-Press teacher and tax stories (S004, S009, S012) remain blocked by the inherited publisher hold; no body or syndication copy was used. Eurecanews and Al-Fajr student reports (S001, S003), and the Al-Fajr archive (S007), were not recovered after Cache miss/inaccessible results and exact-URL DNS failures before HTTP; these are technical failures, not denials. Habariza (S011) remains search-only because its “©CMM” origin is unresolved. The U.S. State Department annual report (S013) is search-only, later context and not a January event record.

Retained candidates

COM202301-E001 — Mvouni student strike/demonstration, 2 January. Masiwa reports the strike; search-only Eurecanews and Al-Fajr results call it a campus demonstration. Grievances concern representation, student health-mutual governance and campus conditions. No attendance count establishes 50. The university’s roughly 6,000 students are not protest attendance. The 9 January gendarmerie and ministry delegations are not coded as a separate public event without assembly evidence. Eligibility: pending_threshold.

COM202301-E002 — Volo Volo sellers, 30 January. Comores Infos reports sellers stopped work and women vendors gathered outside Moroni city hall against municipal market-fee changes. No attendance estimate is given. Keep seller and municipal accounts of the fee rules attributed and unresolved; stalls, sellers affected and stoppage duration are not assembly counts. Eligibility: pending_threshold.

COM202301-E003 — Mutsamudu port truck drivers and dockers. The 1 February Gazette issue says the action had continued for about 20 days, placing its start in January only at month precision. It quotes wage/employment/health demands and a call to the government, but does not establish a public assembly, participant count or state-policy relationship. Staffing figures are not attendance; the planned discussions are not a confirmed response. Eligibility: pending_state_target.

COM202301-E004 — opposition-front press conference in Iconi, reported for 26 January. Search-only evidence attributes a call for common election rules and a boycott to the Front commun. No attendance count or public-rally format is established; the report’s “©CMM” origin is unresolved and the original codebook excludes political rallies. Eligibility: pending_evidence.

One teacher work-stoppage lead is retained in excluded.json (COM202301-X001) because available evidence documents an announcement and school absence, not a qualifying public assembly or a verified crowd of 50. This is not a claim that the stoppage did not occur. No episode is classified as a continuation from before January. The Mutsamudu strike is reported as still active on 1 February, but its post-month closure remains a gap.
