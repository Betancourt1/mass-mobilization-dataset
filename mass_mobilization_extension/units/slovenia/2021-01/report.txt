Slovenia — January 2021

Status: researched_open_web_pending_review. These are candidate records for coordinator review, not official dataset rows. The official baseline version, universe, and additional Coding Procedure remain unverified.

Scope and search coverage
The assigned event window is 1–31 January 2021 under provisional ccode 349. Twenty-four one-query web searches covered general English/Slovenian protest and strike terms, each January week, Ljubljana and regional cities, the January 11 police-union strike, Friday actions and their December/February physical boundaries, and school-closure actions. Targeted follow-up examined Rog and the January 13 Maske dol event. Search engines returned date noise and did not expose total result counts. Query count records work performed; open-web searches do not reproduce LexisNexis or establish exhaustive coverage.

Candidates
- E001 — 13 January, Trg republike, Ljubljana. Večer reports more than 100 people protesting government decrees and demanding political change. Nova24TV separately reports the event, an approximate 14:00 start, Maske dol coordinator Anica Bidar, police dispersal calls and most attendees leaving after Bidar's call; it gives no count and references social-media/RTV material. The threshold estimate therefore remains with Večer alone. Police dispersal orders/attempted removal are coded yes; the report does not establish arrests or other response dimensions.
- E003 — 19 January Rog eviction-related action, pending_unit. Regional Obala reports several dozen people at the site earlier and around 200 at a protest by 18:00; the groups are not added. The morning eviction intervention and the evening crowd have unresolved physical continuity. Ten detentions, one police-reported injury, activist claims of three serious injuries and an MP's truncheon allegation refer to the morning intervention and are preserved as context, not assigned to the later crowd's response fields. The evening crowd's physical end remains unknown.
- E002 — 22 January Rog march, pending_unit. Reports describe several hundred people, with separate estimates of around 500 from police, 500–600 in 24ur/STA, and roughly 1,000 attributed to TV Slovenija through Svet24. Delo's reporter also says several hundred. These are retained with their attributions and are not summed or counted as independent confirmations. The march raised municipal and autonomous-space demands; police route blocks, tear gas, one detention and dispersal around 20:00 are reported. 24ur's displayed 15:37 time conflicts with its 18:00 start and later outcomes.
- E005 — 30 January Trbovlje school-closure protest, pending_unit. 24ur reports around 200 people, identifies a local residents' Facebook group as stating the reopening demand, and describes the event as peaceful. Its physical end and relation to the following day's actions are unknown.
- E004 — 31 January school-closure actions in several cities, pending_unit. Reported counts include several hundred in Koper, about 100 in Ljubljana, 50 in Maribor, 200 in Kranj and 100 in Celje; other reports use different Koper/Portorož scopes and counts. City estimates are not summed. Some Zasavje accounts report dispersal requests without establishing successful clearing. A search-only Publishwall repost names Koper and other local sites but does not verify a shared organizer or single event.
- E006 — 8 January Friday actions, pending_threshold. Delo describes individual walks and unquantified additional presence at Trg republike. No source provides a qualifying crowd count; individual walks, vehicles and an unquantified square presence are not combined.

One exclusion records the 11 January police-union workplace strike. Forecast workforce participation is not public turnout, and no qualifying public assembly was established. No older-onset continuations are recorded. December vehicle actions and February small-group activity do not establish continuous physical presence into or across January. Searches did not verify January 15 or January 29 Friday gatherings.
