Experimental research extension; not an official Mass Mobilization update.

Scope and decision rules
This dossier covers only February 2021-start episodes for Belgium (provisional code 211), under the fixed event cutoff of 2026-10-07 inclusive, America/Mexico_City. A candidate requires a public meeting of at least 50 people and a demand directed to Belgium’s government or its policy. The month identifies episode onset; it does not split a continuous occupation. Uncertain ownership, threshold, state target, response and physical end remain unresolved. The official baseline gate is pending.

Candidate
One candidate is recorded: belgium_2021-02_E001, Reclaim the Night in Saint-Gilles, on February 13. The Brussels Times (S008) says the police zone estimated around 80 and described the gathering as peaceful; BX1 (S011), credited to Belga, reports about 100 activists. Both exceed the threshold, but the reports are not treated as independent turnout confirmation. They locate the action at Parvis de Saint-Gilles versus Place Marie Janson. BX1 attributes to Feminists Fight Back concerns including gendered violence, harassment, support for sex workers, femicides, abortion law and police violence. The safety demand is evident, but neither opened account identifies a clear Belgian government recipient or remedy, so eligibility remains pending_state_target. The two accounts also differ on police containment/intervention; administrative arrests are reported, but dispersal remains unknown. No exact event end is established.
