Cuba, January 2023 — bounded first-pass research dossier
Cutoff: 2026-10-07 inclusive. No official approval or baseline validation.

Coverage and evidence
Ten grouped native search invocations were captured with their actual execution and response-capture timestamps and all 281 returned headers. Discovery used English and Spanish general and weekly searches, selected Cuban independent-media and human-rights sources, and regional/service-specific searches including Santiago de Cuba and Havana. The capture is a bounded open-web pass, not a complete archive or event census.

Two scoped article reads were made: elTOQUE’s retrospective article and the Observatorio Cubano de Conflictos (OCC) January report. Both reads are limited to the passages recorded in their source cards; linked pages and page furniture were not reviewed. The elTOQUE entries initially surfaced as January leads are under its explicit 2024 section and are preserved as four out-of-year exclusions. OCC reports 651 January actions, including 393 spontaneous events and 258 actions associated with five campaigns. Its definitions include digital and individual activity; these aggregate counts do not establish physical meetings or attendance. The OCC article describes few street demonstrations and one Santiago de Cuba community confrontation with police after arrests of a food seller and other merchants. It supplies no day, count, articulated demand, or event-specific police response. This is retained as one candidate pending the 50-person threshold; no numerical bounds are inferred.

Diario de Cuba’s search-result text summarizes the same OCC report. Two fresh-receipt exact-URL attempts returned reader Internal Error, and neither attempt exposed an HTTP status, so its body remains unread and it is not independent episode confirmation. An EFE Marcha de las Antorchas result was retained as an unresolved search lead: a fresh exact-URL request after manual review returned HTTP 429 and no body. Headers also surfaced a reported solo Pedro Albert walk and a Damas de Blanco Sunday detention story, but neither was opened or coded. All access attempts, receipts, native query captures and limitations remain in this dossier.

One event record is pending threshold and evidence review; it is not an approved Mass Mobilization event. Four excluded leads are dated in January 2024, outside this unit. The continuation list is empty. Nine follow-up gaps cover event size, target/demands, dates, response, source independence, baseline, spatial/archival coverage, retrieval outcomes and unverified search leads. The report makes no claim that no other protests occurred.

Counts: 10 grouped searches; 281 captured result headers; 7 source cards (2 opened, 5 search-only); 1 candidate pending threshold; 4 exclusions; 0 continuations; 9 gaps; 0 official approvals.
