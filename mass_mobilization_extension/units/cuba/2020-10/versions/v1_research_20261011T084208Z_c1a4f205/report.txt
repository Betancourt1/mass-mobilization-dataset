Cuba — October 2020

Scope and method
Research covered event dates 1–31 October 2020. Searches were run in Spanish with English terms embedded in the initial generic searches, then four week-window searches and focused queries for San Isidro, economic-liberties calls, provincial/local grievances and September continuity. The recorded searches surfaced Havana and scattered provincial reports, but much of the local result set was either an announcement, a restriction/detention account without assembly counts, or a possible 2019 archive result. Only managed open-web search and page opens were used. Search ran after the fixed event cutoff of 7 October 2026 (America/Mexico_City); actual query clocks and native model are not observable. Two current-unit body opens; zero prior-unit-only cards. These bounded searches verify zero eligible candidates, not that no mass gathering occurred.

Evidence and decisions
Two items were excluded on limited evidence: a San Isidro action planned for 10 October appears obstructed without a crowd count, and the 31 October economic call has a named individual action but no documented 50+ assembly. The national call named many sites; its local outcomes remain unresolved. A search-indexed Santa Clara newsletter snippet appears to refer to a 2019 issue despite an October 2020 upload path and is not coded as a 2020 event.

Open gaps
The most material gap is actual attendance/outcome at the announced October sites. Regional source coverage and September-to-October episode linkage remain unresolved. Nothing here establishes an exhaustive national count or substitutes for a later official baseline review.
