Angola — January 2022 (unit angola_2022-01; provisional ccode 540)

Research used 13 captured web-search invocations with 13 original query strings and 304 result headers. Searches covered general Portuguese and English terms; localized Portuguese terms; weekly windows for 1–7, 10–16, 17–23 and 24–31 January; Luanda and provincial/regional terms; taxi, education, health, judicial and public-worker topics; and December 2021/February 2022 boundaries. Nine current-unit body opens were saved; there were 0 prior-unit-only consultations. Open web is not equivalent to the codebook’s LexisNexis procedure and is not exhaustive. Portuguese reporting dominates; English searches supplement it. No systematic Kimbundu, Umbundu, Kikongo, Chokwe or all-province archive coverage was completed.

Two January candidates are retained. E001 is the 10–12 January taxi-association stoppage and street blockade in Luanda, organized by ANATA, ATA and ATL. Street pickets/blockades are reported, but nobody counts a qualifying gathering. More than 40,000 describes taxi drivers/operators, not protest attendance; thousands of pedestrians were commuters. E001 remains pending_threshold. Arrest accounts differ in timing and attribution: BANTUMEN relays a police statement of 17 detained, CIAM/Jornal de Angola relays an association president’s claim of more than 100, and the interior minister later reports 102 suspects detained. CIAM is explicitly sourced to Jornal de Angola. Vandalism attribution is contested.

E002 is the 23 January Luanda gathering following a fatal police shooting. Novo Jornal’s later passage says hundreds, while its lead says groups of dozens; VOA supplies no count. Reports describe tire burning, property/bus damage and an assault on the officer, followed by police reinforcements reopening the road. E002 remains pending_state_target because no explicit demand or slogan is reported. The fatal shot preceded the gathering; it is not coded as a protest-response killing/shooting, and the victim is not established as a demonstrator. Mutamba and Via Expressa location references remain partly unresolved.

Three exclusions preserve strike-only education reports and the out-of-window February student-march lead. No confirmed continuation episode starting outside January was established, so continuations.json is empty.
