Angola, December 2020 — open-web research dossier

Scope and method
Research was limited to unit angola_2020-12. The supplied Mass Mobilization manual and worker protocol were used with the authorized 2020Q4 overrides. Searches used a public web-search service, not LexisNexis “All News.” The log contains 24 query records. Portuguese and English queries covered the country and month, week/date windows, province names, protest/march/strike terms, the Dec. 10 and Dec. 19 episodes, labor/health sectors, and possible January continuity. The tool exposed rendered title/URL/reference/order headers but not native request metadata or clocks; timestamps remain null. Five query-log entries document an initial shared or lost response and explicitly do not represent zero results. Search result snippets were used only for discovery and are labeled search_only.

Findings
Four candidate records are retained. E001 is a Dec. 10 Luanda civic march with a reported crowd of hundreds, demands about corruption, cost of living, local elections and jobs; opened reporting describes it as peaceful, and an organizer says some participants were detained. E002 is a Dec. 19 Luanda march protesting the Constitutional Court’s refusal to legalize PRA-JA; an opened Lusa-attributed report gives a near-600 estimate, a route from Santa Ana cemetery to Maianga and says police blocked access to the court while securing the march. It remains pending review of the political-rally exclusion and the date discrepancy among syndicated copies. E003 is a Dec. 17 Supreme Court employee strike over salary disparity; public-gathering status and size are unknown. E004 is a Sonangol-linked former-worker vigil outside the company headquarters; the source describes labor/decree demands but supplies no attendance count and only month-level onset is retained. E003 and E004 remain pending threshold review.

The Dec. 18 report announced a SINMEA doctors’ demonstration for Dec. 19. No accessible follow-up confirming execution or at least 50 participants was found; it is preserved in excluded.json as an unverified announcement, not a claim that it did not occur. No verified continuation from a prior-month event was found. The Dec. 19 PRA-JA report announced further provincial marches in January, but searches found no evidence that the same gathering continued across the month boundary.

Sources and limits
There are 13 relevant source cards: five opened body reads, six search_only records and two blocked URLs. Club-K and Ver Angola exact Lusa-copy URLs returned HTTP 403 and their access actions stopped; the Esquerda pre-event URL returned HTTP 502, treated as a technical failure. A separately accessible Novo Jornal Lusa-attributed account was read; cross-published versions are not independent confirmation. Every opened body attempt was preceded by an exact-URL access receipt and manual publisher/origin review. Current receipt checks for S001, S002, S003, S010 and S011 return manual_review; current exact checks for S005 and S006 record hold after their observed HTTP 403. Receipts are in access_receipts/.

Coverage remains incomplete: open-web search is not a newspaper archive, local reporting availability varied, and no full search of all provincial outlets was possible. No event excluded by search absence is coded as zero activity. Ccode remains null because no original country-code mapping was supplied with the assignment. All event judgments are candidates for coordinator review; nothing is official or independently approved.

Structural validation: research.py validate-unit exited 0 with no structural issues; research.py summarize-unit also exited 0 and agreed on all counts. These checks establish record consistency only, not factual or official approval.

Source-scope inventory: 5 current-unit body opens; 0 prior-unit-only. All five opened cards have recorded partial read scopes.
