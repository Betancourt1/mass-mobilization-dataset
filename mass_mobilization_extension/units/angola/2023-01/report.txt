Angola, January 2023 — worker research report

Scope and coverage
This is a worker-prepared country-month dossier for Angola (ccode 540), January 2023. It contains 24 captured searches and 503 ordered search-result headers. Discovery included Portuguese and English queries, four weekly January windows, national and provincial sweeps, and sector searches spanning labor/retail, education, transport, justice and civic/political actions. Luanda produced the clearest event-level reporting. Benguela, Huambo, Cabinda, Malanje and Namibe were included in regional discovery; the results do not establish that no qualifying events occurred elsewhere. There is no quota or completeness claim.

Candidate actions
E001 is the reported PRA-JA march from Largo da ENDE toward the Magnólia complex on 28 January. O País (S003) and Isto É Notícia (S025) both rely on the same PRA-JA communiqué, so they are not independent corroboration. The retrospective USDOS account republished by ecoi (S029) describes a peaceful political march that day, reports four National Assembly members, police dispersal with tear gas and batons, and 17 injuries, but does not identify its underlying source or state the march’s demand. It does not specify whether participants were struck by batons or how each injury occurred, so beatings remains unknown. No source supplies total attendance. The 17 injuries and four legislators are not turnout; eligibility remains pending_state_target, with the 50-person threshold also unknown. S019 is not used as evidence because it explicitly credits held source S001 (DW África).

E002 is a separate January 28 Luanda gathering for political-prisoner release and better treatment of women street vendors, with a planned march stopped before it proceeded. Folha 8 (S028) reports the gathering, demands, police action and arrests; S029 describes a second action hours later but gives no underlying source for the passage. No total attendance is reported, and detainee counts are not turnout. The state/policy demand is documented, but eligibility remains pending_threshold. E001 and E002 remain distinct because their reported demands and sequence differ.

Exclusion and other leads
EX001 preserves the Shoprite strike/protest, but it is excluded because the reported demands target a private employer and the article’s “more than 70%” has no workforce denominator and is not an assembly count. Its announced three-day period is not treated as a confirmed end. Search-only discovery for BIOCOM and MATRA in Malanje describes workplace dispute procedures, not a public assembly. Other leads crossing the unit boundary or lacking execution evidence were not coded as January events, including a taxi-action result dated January 2022, a justice-worker action in February, TPA rallies in June, and an April university-student solidarity-march search lead whose date/execution was not established from saved search metadata. A teacher-strike January lead traces to an earlier suspended phase; no executed January restart was verified.

Access and limitations
There are 31 source cards: 6 opened, 19 search-only and 6 blocked. Saved scoped holds stopped body access for DW (S001), Observador (S002, S005), HRW (S004, S009), and the same Amnesty report in English (S031). The HRW and Amnesty stops preserve prior publisher-wide/manual holds; the English Amnesty URL received no exact access check or body request, and no Angola-specific HRW 403 is claimed. The Isto É Notícia article S019 was opened after a fresh manual_review receipt; its body disclosed “*Com DW África*” and the written-origin relation to S001, so it is preserved only as a derivative and is not corroboration. All public body reads were preceded by unique exact-URL receipts and a manual scope review when the tool returned manual_review.

Ownership and review status
Events and eligibility values are worker proposals, not official inclusions. The coordinator controls reconciliation, review, consolidation and any eventual disposition. The official baseline gate remains pending. Seven explicit gaps are recorded in gaps.json, including attendance, demand/target, source independence, provincial coverage and saved source holds. No repository files were materialized by this worker; the dossier is ready for coordinator review in its temporary mirror.
