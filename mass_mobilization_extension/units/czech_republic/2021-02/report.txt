Czech Republic — February 2021
Research handoff for coordinator review; this dossier is a provisional open-web research product, not an official dataset update.

Coverage and method
Captured 19 actual query responses with 452 ordered result headers in search_log.jsonl. Searches covered the full month and weekly date windows in Czech and English, with follow-ups for discovered actions and labor, climate, and local/regional leads. CAPTURE-0019 preserved the final search batch without rerunning it. Sources remain separate by URL and agency; no candidate is assigned an official Mass Mobilization ID. Searches do not reproduce LexisNexis or systematic newspaper archives, and this is not an exhaustive search.

4 current-unit body opens; 0 prior-unit-only consultations. Four body-read cards have usable source text: S003, S008, S012 and S015. One additional physical body response for S011/TN Nova is quarantined as historical non-evidence pending an independent scoped audit; it contributes no candidate facts or eligibility. No full article text was retained and no article images or videos were inspected. There are 21 source cards; all other cards remain search-only or contain only search/technical access history.

Candidates
Six provisional candidate records are in events.json. E001 (Brno-Medlánky, 13 February) and E004 (Mratín, 20 February) are proposed inclusions based on contemporary reports estimating about 200 and around 150 attendees respectively. E002 is the 13 February Prague Masopust drive: about 300 cars were reported, not people, so the 50-person threshold remains pending. E003 is the 14 February Valentine action series: search leads report a Prague march and an organizer plan for 15 walks, but execution, sites, count and unitization remain pending. E005 is a 21 February Prague rally from search-only leads with an estimated 300; bodies could not be retrieved. E006 is the 22 February Wenceslas Square action called “Generální stávka”; the report says “several dozen” attended, which does not resolve the threshold. The later group of roughly twenty is not the total turnout.

Continuity, exclusions, and uncertainty
No confirmed physical episode beginning before February and continuing into this month was found in the captured material; continuations.json is empty. E006’s report announces a return on Tuesday morning, 23 February, but no follow-up event is confirmed. Three screening exclusions preserve a Russian-government-targeted Navalny protest lead, a Chcípl PES business noncompliance statement that does not describe a gathering, and a 7 March action outside the month. The Brno City PDF and a climate-activism story remain leads with unknown event details/dates, not candidates.

Access and provenance
The usable body reads are source-scoped and do not convert silence into an absence of violence or response. S003 is a ČTK report carried by iROZHLAS. S008 is a ČTK-carried Novinky report and its vehicle count is not attendance. E003 and E005 source-origin independence remains unresolved. R008 and R009 record Cache miss failures, and R011 records a Unicode decoding error; none is a site-denial finding. R010 supports the compliant S008 body read. R012 is a pre-request gate with a failed post-note corpus hash recheck; no request was made. R013 and R015 followed with fresh stable receipts and successful immediate whole-input checks. R016 stopped before a new request after identifying a prior Cache miss for the exact Brno PDF URL. CAPTURE-0019 recorded a web search-layer “Blocked by robots.txt” notice scoped to a site:denik.cz query; no exact S002 URL request was made and no subdomain denial is inferred. Coordination manual notes also preserve the separately reported rfi.fr search-layer restriction from a Djibouti query without applying it to Czech sources.

Access receipt inventory: 16 unique R001–R016 receipts and 16 saved manual review notes are listed in access_receipts/manifest.json; the manifest records SHA-256 for every file.

The dossier remains open to human/coordinator review for event unitization, threshold, source independence, event continuity, state-response coding, inaccessible local records, and broader archive/language coverage. “No candidate found” is not evidence that no protest occurred.
