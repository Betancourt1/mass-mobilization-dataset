Searches used broad Jamaica protest/demonstration/strike terms, weekly date windows, local newspapers and radio, official material, and labor, transportation, and health-worker queries. Its search-only excerpt does not establish a dated collective public action, public gathering, turnout, or government-directed demand, so it is recorded as a lead rather than a candidate event.
