Canada — January 2023 first-pass dossier

Status: researched_open_web_pending_review. This is a bounded open-web pass, not an exhaustive month census or an official dataset update.

Search and capture. Fourteen original web-search invocations (Q001–Q014) are captured with the native helper and stored in capture_inputs/. Each invocation contained four search strings, for 56 strings and 440 returned result headers. Q014 was recovered after the initial freeze from its exact completed event in the public rollout during a read-only completeness check; it was not rerun. It is appended under the next available query ID to preserve all earlier capture IDs and records. Searches covered Canada-wide English and French terms, all four January week blocks, provincial and city terms, Ottawa’s convoy-anniversary events, healthcare and labor actions in Quebec, and climate, housing, Indigenous, and affordability topics. The result-header count is not a count of unique articles or protests.

Source review. Sixteen exact-URL cards are present: eight opened bodies, seven search-only cards, and one URL whose body fetch returned only a cache-miss error. CityNews says some Canadian Press material was used; its reported 200-person estimate is attributed to a CityNews reporter. The Ottawa Lookout relays the 200+ figure from a councillor/CTV and is not a separate count.
