Kosovo — January 2021 (queue 85; provisional ccode 341)

Scope and method
This is an open-web research pass for public gatherings whose physical onset may fall in January 2021. Searches covered general and weekly windows and event/sector leads in Albanian, Serbian and English, followed by targeted date, demand, attendance, response and boundary searches. The search service pooled results across batches, with no per-query result totals or reliable query-to-URL attribution; the log records actual queries and places pooled URLs once per batch. Four earliest queries have no captured execution timestamp, and earlier handoff calls lacked recoverable exact query text. These limitations are recorded rather than reconstructed. Search snippets are leads, not verified body evidence. The official baseline remains pending; open-web searching does not reproduce LexisNexis, and no exhaustive-coverage or absence claim is made.

Candidate records
1. 12 Jan, Prishtina: opened KALLXO and CIVICUS accounts report a PSD action at the Central police station criticizing unequal COVID-19 fine enforcement. KALLXO says 16 activists were taken to the station; that is not attendance. A search-only follow-up attributes an estimate of about 20 to a PSD spokesperson, but it was not opened. No evidence establishes 50 participants. The reported detentions are coded as arrests; conflicting police and PSD accounts of aggression/force remain attributed and unresolved. Pending threshold.
2. Health workers’ march in North Mitrovica: KoSSev, a Serbian government office report crediting Tanjug, and Deutsche Welle search snippets report a march on 25 Jan and hundreds/several hundred participants. No article body was opened. Therefore 25 Jan is a discovery lead only: actual start remains null, all state-response fields remain unknown, and eligibility is pending evidence. Snippets describe a letter to Kosovo’s Basic Prosecutor’s Office, while a KoSSev snippet says the workers’ director looked to Serbian state health institutions. Preserve both explicit target leads; do not infer ownership from nationality.
3. Crowd near Kurti and Osmani’s visit in North Mitrovica: snippets report a gathering near the Mikro settlement on 30 Jan, with “dozens/several dozen,” and conflicting incident accounts. No body source was opened, so actual onset remains null, threshold is unestablished, state responses remain unknown, and eligibility is pending evidence. Election-campaign context and “Here is Serbia” chants do not by themselves establish a demand directed to Kosovo state policy.
4. Kosovo Police Union: a search-only KOHA report describes a possible future protest over allowances. No executed assembly was found. This remains a follow-up lead only, with null onset and pending evidence.

Exclusions and month boundaries
A PSD “Recycle Bin” action is reported in the broader CIVICUS window and dated 19 Jan by search-only local results; available accounts target an electoral rival/candidate, not an established government policy. A cystic-fibrosis association protest outside the Ministry of Health is reported by CIVICUS in the same 26 Nov 2020–20 Jan 2021 window, but its exact occurrence date is unspecified. The linked KOHA page’s 7 Dec 2020 publication date is not the event date; January onset is unestablished and the lead is retained with null date. A reported June 11 church protest is out of month according to an opened 2021 incident compilation. A separate Gjakova occupational-medicine specialists’ protest in Pristina is reported for 18 Feb; it has different organizers and grievances from the North Mitrovica health-workers lead and is not merged.
A FrontOnline search result suggests a 7 Nov 2020 gastronomy protest, while CIVICUS says the association continuously protested sometime in its broad window ending 20 Jan. The exact assembly dates and physical continuity are unverified, so no continuation with a known start date is recorded. CIVICUS also reports that part of the Prime Minister’s Office staff went on strike during the broad reporting window; a linked KOHA page was inaccessible and a Reporteri page remained search-only. The date, public-assembly form and attendance are unresolved, so it is retained as a gap rather than assigned to January.

Coverage, access and remaining gaps
The pass includes 40 logged queries, 34 source cards, four January candidate/follow-up records, four exclusions, no date-verified continuation and 14 open gaps. Three source bodies were opened: KALLXO, CIVICUS and the Government of Serbia Office for Kosovo and Metohija incident compilation. Most candidate-specific reports remain search-only. KoSSev, KOHA and Reporteri pages returned Cache miss; one KoSSev Albanian route returned 403; two ordinary HTTPS retrieval attempts after cache misses failed at DNS resolution. A Deutsche Welle domain search was blocked by robots.txt. No restrictions were bypassed, and no full article text was retained. Search-only reports about the Jan 25 health workers and Jan 30 crowd remain pending evidence, including date and state-target ownership. Other key gaps are the Jan 12 crowd threshold and disputed force accounts; whether the Police Union announced action was carried out; and physical dates/continuity for gastronomy, the PM Office strike and the cystic-fibrosis protest.

Resources and tooling
No server, watcher, browser, container or background process was started. Two finite ordinary HTTPS GET attempts to public article URLs after web Cache miss exited with DNS-resolution errors; neither left a running process. Web searches and opens were finite foreground calls. Existing portable CLI/protocol covers persistence and validation; no new skill or CLI is warranted because remaining effort is source access and evidence judgment, not repeatable mechanics.
