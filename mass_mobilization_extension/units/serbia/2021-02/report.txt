Scope and method
This dossier preserves 16 batched open-web search captures: 59 exact query strings and 471 ordered result headers. The searches include separate weekly windows for 1–7, 8–14, 15–21, and 22–28 February, in English and Serbian Latin/Cyrillic, plus follow-up searches for freelancer taxation, hospitality/event workers, musicians and photographers, RGZ, Presidency protests, labor, environmental issues, national and regional news, official records, and human-rights reporting. Search logs retain original queries, capture clocks, references, titles, URLs, and result order. Search ranking is not comprehensive.

One source body was reviewed under its recorded source-specific gate: Peoples Dispatch S001, readable lines 19–63. The 21 February article interviews an internet-workers association president about the ongoing freelancer campaign and government negotiations, but does not date or locate a distinct February gathering, provide attendance, or tie a state response to a dated in-month assembly. It therefore does not establish physical continuation of January E002.

Six unresolved protest lead clusters are preserved in events.json for follow-up: RGZ outside the Government/Presidency (E001); an Oslobođenje Presidency protest (E002); a Kosovo-policy Presidency protest lead (E003); hospitality-worker protest headlines (E004); an event-industry/hospitality protest headline outside the Government (E005); and a multi-sector musicians/photographers/hospitality lead (E006). They are candidates for verification, not six confirmed events. All have unknown dates, attendance, threshold, demands, protester violence, and all seven state-response dimensions. They remain pending_evidence, and no event has been proposed for inclusion.

The multi-sector lead includes an undated RTV BN headline. Separate Naslovi and Vesti archive search results mention 14 March 2021, but these discovery hints do not establish a physical event date, prove they refer to the same episode as RTV BN, or establish independent source origins. The lead remains unresolved rather than definitely excluded from February. The Danas RGZ trailing-slash URLs and the two Presidency headline URL variants are separate source records but are not counted as separate events.

S007 attempt 1 returned an internal Cache miss with no HTTP status or body, a technical retrieval failure and not a publisher denial. Attempt 2 stopped before any body request because its new manual review was incomplete when the shared phase closed. These outcomes are preserved at access_receipts and do not support blocked status. No source request was made after the phase closed.

One source-level exclusion records that S001 alone does not establish a February physical continuation of January's freelancer gathering. continuations.json is empty because no dated February continuation of a January-started physical assembly is evidenced. excluded.json and gaps.json preserve the unresolved continuity, event-date, threshold, target/demand, source-relationship, response, baseline, language, regional-archive, and open-web/LexisNexis limitations. A search failure to identify a verified event is not evidence that no event occurred.

Coverage and limitations
The captured searches use Serbian Latin and Cyrillic alongside English and national/regional news, official and human-rights sources. They include broad national discovery and sector-focused follow-up but do not provide systematic LexisNexis-equivalent coverage or comprehensive local/regional archive access. Publication dates, URL path dates, and archive labels were not treated as physical event dates. Headlines were retained as search-only leads and not as body evidence. No exhaustiveness or zero-event claim is made.

Source inventory: 25 unique source cards (1 opened, 24 search_only, 0 blocked, 0 not_found). Search attempts and source outcomes are preserved in access_receipts. Candidate and source records remain provisional pending independent review and coordinator baseline reconciliation.
