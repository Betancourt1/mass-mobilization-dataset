China — January 2023 (ccode 710)
Open-web research handoff; all records remain candidates pending coordinator review.

Method and scope

A bounded first pass used 13 grouped search invocations (Q001–Q013), containing 50 English and simplified-Chinese queries and 362 captured result headers. Search topics covered general January protest activity; early/mid/late-month and weekly periods; labor, wages, hospital staff, bank depositors, fireworks restrictions, and Tesla; and region/local names in Henan, Chongqing, Shaanxi, Jiangsu, Jiangxi, and Hebei. Query inputs and result headers are preserved under capture_inputs. Search breadth does not establish completeness.

Five exact public article URLs were opened after fresh access receipts and manual exact-URL/current-unit/canonical-origin review: S001, S002, S004, S005, and S006. The articles were read only within their recorded line scopes; summaries and limitations were retained, not full article text. Four additional cards are search-only: S003 is an exact trailing-slash variant of S002, S007 is a Qianxi page dated 2025 rather than 2023, and S008–S009 describe Tesla owners protesting private-company price cuts. No AP or AfricaPress body was opened or used. The fixed source cutoff is 2026-10-07 inclusive.

Candidate records

- E001: January 1 Henan rural-bank depositor travel/march toward Zhengzhou and a reported regulator petition. It is linked to a campaign described as beginning in April 2022, but the highway and regulator actions may be distinct episodes. Attendance is uncounted; RFA says it could not independently verify the online material. Eligibility is pending episode-unit review; the state demand and 50-person threshold remain open.
- E002: January 2 Luyi, Henan fireworks-ban crowd at Hongdaoyuan Square, reported by RFA. Police statement reports six arrests; the crowd allegedly blocked and overturned a police vehicle. No attendance number is given, so the 50-person threshold is pending. The Nanjing crowd RFA mentions occurred on December 31 and is not established as a protest or January continuation.
- E003: January 7 Zhongyuan Huiji factory-worker protest in Chongqing over dismissals and wages. An RFA caption attributed to Reuters says “hundreds”; workers' representatives reportedly negotiated with district-government representatives. The main reported grievances name the employer and labor agencies; whether participants demanded state action is unresolved.
- E004: January 11 Yancheng hospital nurses' collective strike over long hours and unpaid overtime, mentioned briefly in NTD. The source gives no participant count or public-gathering details; eligibility is pending evidence.
- E005: January 16 Luochuan hospital employees reportedly gathered over unpaid social/medical insurance. NTD says a large group and describes police attempting dispersal, but its “300+” figure concerns affected workers, not attendance. Attendance threshold and state target remain unresolved; a police-beating allegation is not independently verified.

Cross-month context and exclusions

The January 1 bank-depositor action is linked in its notes to the broader 2022 rural-bank campaign; sources describe a withdrawal crisis beginning in April 2022 and cite a July 2022 Zhengzhou protest. No specific physical gathering is established as continuing into January, so continuations.json is empty and no cross-month episode is merged.

Three leads are retained as exclusions: X001 is a Qianxi County wage protest dated January 6, 2025, not 2023; X002 concerns Tesla's private-company price cuts, with no government/policy demand evident in the search headers; X003 is a Wuning loader-at-gate wage account whose January 15 date belongs to a social-media post, not a verified physical action, and whose approximate 100 refers to affected workers rather than attendees. Wuning remains a follow-up gap if an original dated post/video is found.

Limits and follow-up

All five event records remain nonofficial, open-web candidates. None is coded as an approved update. Several have unresolved attendance, state-target, event-unit, end-date, or source-verification questions. The bank articles rely on online videos/social posts and RFA explicitly disclaims independent verification; NTD's hospital and wage leads are based on social material; and the Reuters source behind RFA's Chongqing caption was not opened. Search results identified broader January–March China Dissent Monitor material and reports of February health-insurance demonstrations, but this handoff does not treat quarterly aggregates or out-of-month events as January candidates.

Coverage was bilingual in English and simplified Chinese but not exhaustive by province, platform, or source language. The first pass did not use LexisNexis or a systematic newspaper/archive collection. Search-result omission is not evidence that no event occurred. The official Mass Mobilization baseline, version, universe, and licensing remain pending coordinator verification.
