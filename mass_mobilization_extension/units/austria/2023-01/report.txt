Search and coverage
A first pass captured 19 German- and English-language search invocations, preserving returned-result order, raw URLs, references and titles. Queries covered Austria-wide protest, demonstration, strike, climate and energy-price terms and city/province leads including Vienna, Linz, Innsbruck, Graz, Salzburg, Klagenfurt and Bregenz. Body research used accessible national and regional reports. Verified reporting is concentrated in Vienna; coverage by province, language archive and sector is uneven. Open-web search does not reproduce LexisNexis or licensed newspaper archives, and the 19 queries do not establish exhaustive coverage. Search-only items remain leads, not verified event evidence.

The 14 January attendance estimates refer only to Vienna and are kept distinct from the German event.

Continuity and access
Kurier reporting places Letzte Generation actions on weekdays from 9 to 13 January and reports aggregate arrests, complaints and police deployment; these totals are not attendance and do not establish one continuous assembly. A 25 January report describes a planned February wave; a 13 February report confirms a later, distinct action of about 15 activists plus seven supporters, below the threshold. December-to-January continuity remains unresolved: a search lead for a possible 19 December Innsbruck action led to a page whose body retrieval had a technical cache miss; the search result is not coded as event evidence. The January 25 Der Standard article was denied by robots.txt at its exact URL; no publisher-wide hold was inferred. The Press Nachrichten lead also had a cache miss and a missing original pre-request receipt after a filename collision; the later receipt is explicitly not substituted for it.

Other precise ends, event-specific response, protester violence and daily episode boundaries remain unknown where sources do not establish them. Reporter and organizer estimates are attributed and not treated as independent confirmations. The baseline/manual, licensed archive, source-independence, provincial/sector coverage and exact December/January episode questions remain open.
