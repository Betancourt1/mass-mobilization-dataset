Scope and method
Research covered reported activity dated 1–28 February 2021. The preserved search log contains 32 actual native web searches and 639 ordered result headers; three searches returned zero headers. The searches included four week windows (1–7, 8–14, 15–21, and 22–28 February), broad Singapore protest/demonstration/march/strike/riot searches, event-specific searches about the Myanmar Embassy assembly and police investigation, migrant-worker and wage queries, official and rights-source searches, international publication queries, and targeted Yangon/location checks. Header totals describe the native tool output, not event coverage.

Search terms were used in English, Chinese, Malay, Tamil, and Burmese. Geographic focus was Singapore, with event/location checks relating to the Myanmar Embassy in Singapore, the Singapore Embassy in Yangon, and Ang Mo Kio worker leads. Sectors included general public protest, labor/migrant-worker grievances, and Myanmar-related political solidarity/planned demonstrations. Searches included Singapore Police Force, CNA, Straits Times, TODAY, The Online Citizen, Berita Harian/Mediacorp, Mothership, FIDH, rights organizations, and international outlets. No systematic January-to-March week-by-week continuity sweep was captured; the January Singapore dossier was consulted locally to distinguish its already-owned event.

Finding and provisional disposition
One event lead is retained as a candidate: an official police release says officers were alerted at about 3:45pm on 10 February to three men allegedly protesting outside the Myanmar Embassy to show support for the people of Myanmar. The release identifies two Japanese men and one Indonesian man and says investigations were ongoing. It does not state the full attendance or a specific demand directed at Singapore's government or policy. Three identified men are recorded as a lower bound only; total attendance and the at-least-50 threshold remain unknown. The target and demand also remain unresolved, so the candidate is pending evidence. No own-state or foreign-state-only determination is inferred from the embassy location or police permit rule.

The police account does not establish an organizer, the end of the assembly, or any of the seven coded response values. Investigation is not coded as arrest, and no value is inferred from silence. Protester violence is unknown. No independent contemporary report body was opened, so this is one official account rather than corroboration.

Two location/planning leads are retained in excluded.json rather than treated as Singapore events: search-only results about the 5 February warning/plans do not establish an executed gathering, and a search-only title places a protest outside Singapore's embassy in Yangon, which is outside Singapore. Other Myanmar-related boycott/protest results have uncertain location and remain a gap. The January 26 MOE assembly belongs to the January unit and is unrelated by date, issue, and location; it is not a February continuation. The continuity record is empty because no episode beginning before February was identified for this unit, not because absence was proved.
