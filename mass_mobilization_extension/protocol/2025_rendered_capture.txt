2025 rendered search capture adjustment
Owner authorization: sessions/2025/rendered_capture_owner_authorization.txt.
This adjustment applies to 2025 work in this isolated branch. Other-year protocols
and publisher restrictions are unchanged.

Preserve the exact available invocation response before parsing it. Store the
verbatim returned string, or JSON serialization of the actual tool object, in
the invoking month's private_reading/search/ with a unique filename and SHA256.
These private search responses are local evidence and excluded from portable
exports. Preserve existing temporary responses there when available, matching
recorded hashes; preserve gaps for responses already lost. Never reconstruct
inaccessible rollout files, repeat a query to replace missing capture, invent
native fields, or claim the parsed display is a native event-header payload.

Use capture-search for the actual whole invocation. Header records may contain
only visibly exposed title, exact raw URL and ref plus explicitly derived
order. Keep unexposed metadata null or absent. Batched response belongs to the
invocation as a whole; never guess result partitions. Preserve a pooled call
once with explicit consultation references in other month reports. Empty arrays
for unavailable captured headers must explicitly say unavailable, not no results.

Add capture_provenance describing rendered output, private relative path/hash,
missing native fields and any early losses. Local clocks observed immediately
before/after calls are local clocks, distinct from unknown native timestamps.
Record search, reading, waits, repair and closure intervals prospectively;
retain unmeasured historical intervals as unknown.

Search leads remain search_only and pending_evidence. Before each publisher
body request, obtain a fresh stable access-check receipt in the invoking unit,
inspect inherited canonical and current 2025 metadata and manually review exact
URL, publisher aliases and written-story origins. Hold/defer/unknown relevant
external restriction means no body request. The inventory and no-match never
grant access. Do not download denied/paywalled/anti-bot content.

Source-backed coding, month-onset ownership, threshold, domestic state target,
seven response variables, uncertainty, independent review and official approval
gates remain unchanged. This adjustment authorizes a faithful available-output
capture method, not source access, factual approval or official inclusion.
