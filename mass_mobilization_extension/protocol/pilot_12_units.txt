Pilot protocol: twelve country-month outputs, 2023 Q1
Authorization recorded in ACCEPTANCE.txt. This is a staged proposal, activated only after the current eight-unit batch is locally committed and separately postverified, followed by an independently audited boundary and live ownership/reservation check.

Selected units: Guatemala, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau and Guyana; January, February and March 2023. Preserve the canonical country/month unit IDs from coverage_matrix.csv. Never assign a completed or already-owned unit. Country codes: Guatemala 90, Guinea 438, Guinea-Bissau 404, Guyana 110. Exactly twelve separate monthly dossiers are required.

Grouping comparison: one researcher handles all three selected months in Guatemala; another handles all three in Guinea. Each maintains separate monthly assignment, status, events, continuations, exclusions, gaps, search_log, sources and report files. Guinea-Bissau and Guyana each have a separate researcher per month. This creates two quarterly-context roles and six monthly roles. Every event belongs to its onset month; later month evidence is a continuation or explicitly cited consultation. Context shared across months is not independent corroboration. Neither quarter nor month workers may write shared planning, review or aggregate files.

All researcher and auditor children request gpt-6-luna with high reasoning and fork_turns none when the tool requires it for an override. Keep coordinator settings unchanged. Record requested settings and tool acceptance separately from independent runtime observation; if no authorized effective-model getter exists, report that limitation. Never substitute a model or effort. Run roles in rolling waves with at most eight active children total, including independent auditors; 12 units does not mean 12 simultaneous workers. Capacity limits determine launch order, not output count. Reserve an auditor slot when practical.

Before activation, the owner inventories all active physical draft roots, including unfinished siblings, in an explicit complete registry. Bind registry and source ledger bytes. Use safe named root containment for external drafts. Every current canonical unit ledger, review/source_checks.jsonl and registered draft ledger must be readable and stable. Changed, removed, new, unreadable or ambiguous inputs invalidate affected prior review. A hash cache reuses file dispositions only. Each new URL/action still requires fresh stable complete inputs and a manual scoped review. Preserve exact denial, publisher hold, original-story relationship, retrieval failure and uncertainty. Do not open publisher bodies after a denial or unreviewed hold. The helper never grants access.

Early handoff: validate the whole monthly dossier as soon as a researcher has finished coding. Confirm accepted terminal status and every consolidator field, including open_questions, before independent review. Run structural checks against the intended export and inspect source aliases/history. The helper flags review requirements; it is advisory and cannot replace the existing portable-text exporter or copyright review. Keep originals byte-identical and preserve authored scientific judgments/URLs.

Close completed work in independently auditable waves where repository dependencies allow. End reading after the current bounded request, save originals, confirm no in-flight requests from every registered writer, freeze the complete inventory, and use reviewed deterministic closure scripts. No arbitrary waiting interval substitutes for acknowledgments and matching hashes. The owner controls consolidation, local commit and a separate postcommit audit. Every implementation/dossier edit after approval requires a fresh independent audit of the exact diff and operation. Do not push, publish or open a PR.

Derive checkpoints from the live coverage matrix, actual monthly assignment/status files and the committed tree. Keep inherited years separate. Report committed, prepared and active units distinctly; never call planned skeletons completed or count the same inherited unit twice.

Metrics: see pilot_metrics.txt. The pilot ends only when all 12 dossiers have an independently reviewed local commit and separate postverification, or when a concrete decision/access/ownership blocker is preserved and reported. Source denials alone create scientific gaps and do not imply that the pilot must halt. A coordinator's unresolved instruction conflict or unavailable audit is an activation blocker.
