Azerbaijan, 2020-10 — research dossier
Status: researched_open_web_pending_review. No candidate meeting the inclusion rule was established; this does not mean no protest occurred.

Coverage: 40 submitted web searches in English, Azerbaijani and Russian, including broad discovery, weekly October windows, regional/local and sector-specific terms, war-support queries, and continuity checks into 1–7 November. Seven aggregate responses were captured as shared envelopes repeated across each query in the call, not independent result sets. Twelve query responses were not retained; their exact query strings and explicit output-loss records are in search_log.jsonl, with headers unknown (not no results). Search timestamps were not exposed. Search-engine date filtering was not assumed reliable.

The search surfaced October 2019 Baku opposition and women’s-rights protests repeatedly; dates in the underlying result descriptions place them outside this unit. For October 2020, the principal in-country lead was a 6 October youth action at Azneft Square supporting the Azerbaijani army. The opened Media.az report names no demand on Azerbaijan’s government and gives no attendance estimate, so it is excluded for target and its threshold remains unknown. A separate Oxu.az search result describes the same action, but opening it returned a technical Internal Error; it remains search_only and is not counted as independent confirmation.

Other opened leads were outside this unit: a 1 October army-support demonstration in Tabriz, Iran; an 18 October rally organized by an Azerbaijani cultural center in Munich; and a 26 October march by Azerbaijanis in Bielefeld. The two Germany events addressed Armenian attacks and international audiences rather than Azerbaijan’s own state. A Reddit result alleging a 31 October Russian-resident rally was opened and found tagged HUMOR with no evidence of a real gathering. These are documented in excluded.json or gaps.json.

Body scope: five pages opened and read (Media.az, Trend.az Tabriz, Azerbaijan.az Munich, Trend.az Bielefeld, Reddit false lead); one article remained search_only after tool error. Body reads were partial to the rendered article/post text and did not establish crowd sizes for the support/diaspora leads. Fresh exact-URL access checks for the five successful opens returned manual_review with no saved restriction match; that result was not treated as automatic permission. Receipts are under access_receipts/. No access workaround was used.

Limits and open gaps: no LexisNexis or equivalent newspaper archive, no verified official baseline/codebook schema/country code, incomplete local and regional coverage, query-date behavior uncertain, 12 unretained query result payloads, and no basis for declaring the month exhaustively covered. Full access inventory and source independence are not established. Review the false leads, exclusions, source boundaries and gaps before any downstream use.
