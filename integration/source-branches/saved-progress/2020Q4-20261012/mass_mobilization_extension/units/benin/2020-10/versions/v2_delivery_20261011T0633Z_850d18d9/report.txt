Benin 2020-10: bounded open-web research handoff

Historical evidence cutoff: 2026-10-07. New research and page opens were conducted on 2026-10-11 UTC; individual managed-tool call times are not exposed. No LexisNexis or equivalent archive was used. This is not exhaustive and makes no claim that no other gatherings occurred.

Coverage: 23 French/English query submissions in all (seven v1, 16 new v2) across weekly date windows and targeted Cotonou, Parakou, Djougou, Bantè and Savè, plus sector and September-onset searches. Batched outputs retain shared headers without query-specific attribution. Exact-page opens were preceded by URL-specific local assessments; captures retain provenance and bounded paraphrases, not full article text.

Findings: two separate local candidates, both pending the ≥50 simultaneous-participant threshold. BEN202010_E001 is the Cotonou 31 October market-women march; reports disagree on its starting point and no Cotonou attendance count is available. BEN202010_E002 is the Parakou march: Daabaaru reports the stadium-to-Place-Tabéra route, organizer Marie-Reine Yankpè’s group, democracy/electoral-inclusion demands, and receipt of a declaration by a municipal secretary. A Le Parakois search-result excerpt reports about 100 participants, but its exact page returned a technical error and was not retried; the number is not body-verified and is not treated as an established count. No figures from different towns are combined.

The 29 October La Matinal report says the mayor exceptionally lifted the 28 October Cotonou ban for the 31 October women’s action and that the prefect asked for Republican Police coordination; it does not establish actual on-site enforcement. An 8 October Parakou traders’ work stoppage is retained as an exclusion lead because no public assembly or count is reported.

Remaining limits: independent per-site attendance, Cotonou route, organizer-name equivalence, exact signed municipal act/enforcement, other early/mid-October actions, and September onset. Baseline remains pending; independent scientific review is pending. No acceptance or verification is claimed.
