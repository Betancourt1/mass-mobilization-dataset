Burundi, October 2020. This is a bounded first-pass open-web search, not a claim that no protest activity occurred. The defined evidence cutoff is 2026-10-07, but all actual searches and source access in this package occurred on 2026-10-11, after that cutoff. Four search batches used English, French and Kirundi wording: broad demonstrations/strikes and regional terms; Iwacu/COSOME and opposition/union queries; a Regideso labor lead; and September/January neighboring-month context. Searches named Bujumbura, Gitega, Ngozi, Muyinga, Karusi, Bubanza and other locations; source/sector coverage remains uneven. The only article body opened was SOS Médias Burundi’s 27 October report on four union confederations’ press conference about the public-service census. It documents representatives’ press conference and demands to the government, but does not establish an outdoor/public gathering or 50+ audience. It is excluded from event rows without asserting zero attendance. Search results also surfaced a Regideso strike warning dated October 2014; this is a temporal false positive, not October 2020 evidence. September and January queries did not establish a neighboring onset or continuation. No attendance is transferred across dates or sites. No fixed search quota was imposed. Official baseline comparison, country-code mapping, and independent scientific review remain pending; this is not a verified or officially approved MM observation.
