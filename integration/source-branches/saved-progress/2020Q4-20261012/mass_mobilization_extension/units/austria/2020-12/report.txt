Austria, December 2020 — open-web research handoff

Scope and method
This dossier covers only public mobilization in Austria whose start falls in December 2020. I used German- and English-language general, weekly-window, local/regional, and event-led searches, then checked surfaced events against contemporaneous press, organizer pages, student records, and an official report. The formal inventory has 15 captured query-response records, preserving returned URL headers and result metadata. An earlier small set of exploratory web searches was not durably captured per-query; its merged output is unattributed and was not used as evidence. The 15-record count is the auditable inventory, not a claim that no other searches occurred.

Open-web search cannot reproduce LexisNexis or establish exhaustive coverage. Search-result dates can be inaccurate, and some relevant pages were only discoverable in search results. Search-only material is labeled as such and supports pending candidates only. One official annual-report PDF URL was checked exactly and manually reviewed; the web open tool said the URL was inaccessible through that tool, so the body was not read and no retry was made. No saved denial, login, or paywall was observed. The research manual/codebook copy in baseline was consulted; official baseline ccode mapping is still pending. Austria’s adjacent November dossier is only a blank template, and no January Austria unit was present in the assignment package; no cross-month continuation was established. Known December event records do not establish a continuing multi-month action.

Candidates
Eight candidates remain for independent review. Four have evidence supporting proposed inclusion: the 26 December Graz Covid-measures gathering; the 10 December Vienna demonstration against a planned Afghanistan charter deportation; the 12 December Vienna student march against the University Act amendment; and the 13 December Salzburg Covid-measures demonstration. Each has one or more material unknowns, including participant bounds, source independence, organizer detail, or state response. The 11 December Vienna FightFor1Point5 event remains pending because the local physical action is documented but a direct Austrian state/policy target is not. Three candidates remain threshold-pending: the 15 December Rossauer Lände blockaders, where 23 people are counted but total gathering attendance is not; a separately described later march toward the Interior Ministry, for which no count is available; and the late-December Salzburg Residenzplatz basic-rights demonstration. The official Ombudsman Board result gives no exact day, size, organizer, or specific target for that last event; it is kept separate from the 13 December Salzburg event.

No absence claim is made. An empty excluded list means no confidently classifiable exclusion was retained from this evidence pass, not that there were no other protests. Continuations are empty because no candidate is evidenced to have started before December or continued into January. Unknown protester violence and state-response fields remain unknown rather than negative.

Coverage and follow-up
Sources span ORF regional reporting, Der Standard, an opened contemporaneous Austrian chronology, organizer material, a university-student report, an FFF event page, and one search-only Ombudsman Board result. Some press accounts rely on the same police count or agency material and are not treated as independent observations. The event set is necessarily provisional pending the official baseline, human review, more local/sectoral archive searches, exact turnout for threshold-pending cases, end dates, and event-specific state-response evidence. No official IDs or final event approvals have been assigned.
