Egypt — October 2020 (new version, open-web research; review pending)

Scope and method: English and Arabic managed web searches queried broad protest/strike terms, early and later October reporting, Obour factories, labor unions, regional and sector terms, student/health/education events, housing-demolition coverage and the adjacent September boundary. Eight actual query captures are in search_log.jsonl; raw managed results are linked in the isolated workroot. Body requests were preceded by exact-URL local checks and unit-local assessments. The web tool exposes no native request clock; captures retain that as unknown. The browser/runtime identity was not observable; requested settings remain gpt-6-luna/high only.

Candidate E001 is an Oct 5 sit-in by Labor University/Cultural Foundation staff at its Cairo headquarters. Mada Masr's Oct 11 reporting gives a gathering estimate of 700–1,500 people, distinct from the much larger nationwide employee pool, over unpaid salaries and a requested institutional transfer. Workers appealed to state officials. Start is supported; end and later response remain open. The previously reported late-September letters/vigils are not assumed to be this event.

Candidate E002 records Oct 12 reports about multiple union and insurance-company protests against the Ministry of Public Business Sector draft HR regulation. Sources do not list exact sites or give count per gathering; this remains pending unitization and threshold and is kept separate from E001.

Aggregate monitors (ANHRI Q4, DARB first-half October) broaden context and sector/geography but do not establish every event date, size or separate gathering. A September-demolition protest search lead is excluded from October because its reported dates are in September.

Limits/gaps: weekly region-specific non-labor searches remain incomplete; LexisNexis was not available; search-result absence is not evidence of no event. See gaps.json. Both candidates are proposed for independent scientific review; no official baseline validation or acceptance is claimed.
