Angola, October 2020 — open-web research, pending review

Scope and method
This dossier covers only Angola, 1–31 October 2020. I ran 16 web searches and captured 347 rendered result headers in search_log.jsonl. Searches used Portuguese and English, weekly date windows (1–7, 8–14, 15–21, 22–31), cross-month continuity terms, and targeted searches on unemployment, cost of living, municipal elections, corruption, police response, Luanda and Huambo. Search outputs were captured as rendered-only headers; native response metadata and query clocks were unavailable. Search snippets were used only for discovery and are marked search_only.

Seven current-unit body opens; zero prior-unit-only opens. Seventeen unique relevant source cards are saved: 7 opened, 9 search_only and 1 blocked. Each body attempt was preceded by an exact-URL access-check receipt in access_receipts/. The provided source_checks ledger contains one unrelated Albania 403 record; exact-URL scans for the sources used here returned manual_review with no matching saved hold. The October 3 Observador result remains search-only because the inherited historical Observador scope ledger is omitted. Ver Angola's October 26 page returned HTTP 403; Esquerda returned HTTP 502, while News24 and DW body opens failed in the managed tool without an HTTP status. Those technical failures are not treated as access denials.

The original codebook was not supplied at baseline/mm_codebook.txt. Documented protocol categories are used provisionally, and the study's ccode is null because no original mapping was present. The work does not reproduce articles or rely on long quotations.

Candidates
Three October-start candidates are retained. E001 is the 3 October Luanda march demanding the presidential chief of staff's removal; Euronews/LUSA reports about 100 people, so the 50-person threshold is supported. Its demand-category mapping remains provisional without the original manual.

E002 is the 24 October protest action reported in Luanda and Huambo against precarious living conditions and for municipal elections, with unemployment also reported in Luanda. Occurrence and state response are documented, but attendance is unresolved: arrest totals include bystanders, while an approximately 2,000 estimate appears only in a search excerpt. Reports conflict over detentions and deaths. Protester violence and police gunfire are reported, but killing attribution remains unknown.

E003 is a search-only candidate for a 26 October courthouse gathering calling for detainees' release. The excerpt says several dozen young people, which does not establish 50; the article body returned HTTP 403. It remains pending threshold and evidence.

No pre-October action was established as a continuous episode into this month. A separate protest was planned for 11 November, outside this unit; full neighboring-month ownership review remains open.

Out-of-scope results
One exclusion records false-positive October 2019 calls and a 15 October 2019 march returned by 2020 searches. The cited page and linked references identify 2019, not this unit's year.

Coverage and gaps
Searches found no other October episode that could be coded from the retrieved material, but this is not evidence that no other events occurred. Portuguese and English coverage is incomplete; no systematic searches in Kimbundu, Umbundu or other Angolan languages or provincial media archives were completed. Open-web search does not reproduce LexisNexis, and date filters may be ignored. The October 24 crowd threshold and provincial grouping, the courthouse gathering threshold, alleged deaths and exact state response, the missing codebook/ccode, the omitted historical Observador ledger, and neighboring-month event boundaries require review. Full details and follow-up actions are in gaps.json.
