Nineteen actual English/Bulgarian queries are logged (536 rendered headers; some combined queries share unattributed headers; native totals and query clocks were not exposed), including four Bulgarian weekly windows, Sofia/region follow-up, taxi/transport and conductor searches, and a January boundary/continuity search.
