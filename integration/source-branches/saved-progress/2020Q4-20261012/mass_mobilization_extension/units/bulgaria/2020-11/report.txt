Bulgaria — November 2020

Scope and method
Research covers Bulgaria, 1–30 November 2020. Fixed data cutoff: 7 October 2026, America/Mexico_City; actual tool work was 11 October 2026 UTC. Ten actual English/Bulgarian public-web queries are logged (283 rendered headers; combined response headers can be shared/unattributed and total result counts were not exposed), including four Bulgarian weekly windows and targeted police-wage, taxi/transport, Varna workplace and regional follow-ups. Coverage includes Sofia, Varna, Ruse/other regional searches and police/firefighter, taxi/bus, public-transport-conductor and restaurant-worker sectors. 8 current-unit body opens; 3 prior-unit-only consultations. Eight body-read source cards represent seven unique URLs because one BNT card duplicates the same exact page. One BNT English open timed out. A BTA target returned actual HTTP 429 Too Many Requests; the action stopped with no retry or bypass. No LexisNexis-equivalent archive or native result totals were available.

Candidates
Six date-specific candidates are recorded, all unresolved. The Nov 8 national police/firefighter salary rally and Nov 29 second national rally are executed but uncounted; the union membership figure is not turnout. A Nov 25 transport-carrier/taxi assembly, with taxi action continuing on Nov 26, is physically documented; its threshold is unproved. A distinct anti-lockdown meeting near the Council of Ministers was described as “tens,” which does not establish 50. A Varna conductors’ protest linked to Nov 23 and a separate restaurant-worker action dated Nov 27 are reported in later December sources; both lack attendee counts. For conductors, the state-policy target versus municipal transit employer issue is unresolved. The 270 layoff notices are not protest attendance. The police rallies share salary demands with October action, but no automatic event merger is made.

Exclusions and follow-up
The police tent exhibit and workplace siren/light actions were reported as plans; execution of a qualifying public gathering was not established. The separately reported anti-restrictions and transport assemblies physically overlapped later, but their groups and demands are kept distinct.

Sources and gaps
Fifteen source cards: 11 status-opened, 3 search-only and 1 blocked by the actual BTA rate limit. Eight current-unit body-read cards (seven unique target URLs) and three prior-unit-only cross-month evidence consultations. Technical timeouts are not denials. Candidate decisions and named next steps are in events.json and gaps.json.

Coverage is bounded open-web research; missing search hits or unavailable records do not establish absence of protest.
