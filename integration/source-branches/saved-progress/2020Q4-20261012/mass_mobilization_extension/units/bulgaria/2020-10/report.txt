Bulgaria — October 2020

Scope and method
Research covers Bulgaria, 1–31 October 2020. The fixed data cutoff is 7 October 2026, America/Mexico_City; searches were executed 11 October 2026 UTC. Thirteen unique captured English/Bulgarian generic, local, weekly-window and event follow-up queries were recorded (385 rendered result headers across the log; combined responses sometimes share unattributed headers and totals were not exposed). Four Bulgarian weekly date windows were queried. Regional leads included Sofia, Ruse, Plovdiv, Varna and Smolyan; sector follow-up included police/firefighters and public demonstrations. Four source cards have body-read scopes: Mediapool on Oct 3, BNT on Oct 16, Dnes announcement on Oct 13, and bTV report on Oct 21. The Dnes article is a plan, not execution proof. BNR exact opens returned timeout/502; Smolyan.bgvesti was unavailable in the web tool; no retries. No LexisNexis-equivalent archive or native search totals were available. Search results are not exhaustive.

Candidates
Four date-specific candidates are recorded. The Sofia Oct 3 Grand National Uprising was described as thousands, directed at the government and chief prosecutor, and peaceful; it is proposed for review. A separate Ruse Danube Bridge road action on the same date lacks its own count and a separately stated demand, so it remains pending. The Oct 16 fifth national uprising in Sofia is physically documented, but attendee threshold is unknown; BNT reports objects thrown at police. The Oct 21 Varna police/gendarmerie/firefighter action is reported as over 150 people seeking up to a 30% Interior Ministry pay increase, and is proposed for review. End dates and state responses remain unknown unless specifically documented.

Other leads and gaps
A local Plovdiv search result describes only a few dozen at a recurring action and remains an excluded low-size lead; its body was not accessed. The July-start anti-government campaign provides context but is not treated as an event on every campaign day. Regional Ruse participants are not pooled with Sofia. The police wage action could relate to November rallies, but common issue alone does not prove one continuous episode.
