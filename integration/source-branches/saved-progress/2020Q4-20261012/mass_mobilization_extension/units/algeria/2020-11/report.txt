Algeria — November 2020

Scope and method
Research covered Algeria, November 2020 only. Thirty-five individually attributed public-web queries were run in English, French and Arabic, including weekly windows and focused searches for referendum demonstrations, Béjaïa/Bouira/Seddouk activity, education/labor actions, targeted municipality searches and continuity. Each actual response was captured as rendered result headers with aligned URLs and refs. Query clocks and result totals were not exposed. Open-web search does not reproduce the codebook's LexisNexis workflow; no systematic LexisNexis, full local archive or Amazigh-language search was available. These searches do not establish completeness.

Candidates
Seven candidates are recorded: November 1 actions in Béjaïa city, Bechloul, Haizer, Ath Laziz, Kherrata, and reported opposition actions in Tizi Ouzou Province; and a November 25 UNPEF-affiliated teachers' sit-in in Algiers. All remain pending. Attendance of at least 50 is unestablished for Béjaïa, Bechloul, Haizer, Ath Laziz and the teacher sit-in. Kherrata and Tizi Ouzou also lack verified evidence of an actual qualifying gathering. Béjaïa and Bechloul have only qualitative “several dozen/dozens” descriptions. The Bechloul account reports ballot-box damage, tear gas, rubber bullets and arrests; whether the bullets establish a shooting response remains open. The teachers' report gives neither venue nor size. Unknown response fields have not been inferred from silence.

Other leads and exclusions
A Seddouk collective announced a November 22 general strike and march, but targeted follow-up did not establish execution. A demonstration in Canada and an announced Paris march are outside Algeria. A Radio Algérienne result concerns Sahrawi towns outside Algeria and does not establish a demand on Algeria's state. October 31 Béjaïa and Tizi Ouzou marches/unrest are continuity questions, not a November continuation. A planned action is not coded as an executed gathering.

Sources and access
The source ledger has 13 cards: 5 opened, 7 search-only, 1 blocked. Opened sources were read through the public web tool and summarized with limited scope; article text is not reproduced. Le Soir d'Algérie displayed a subscriber/login wall after its preview. AP and Facebook affected/ambiguous-origin holds were not opened; AP's fresh check returned manual review/no match, which is not permission. VAVA attributes some details to Liberté; independence among local reports remains unresolved.
