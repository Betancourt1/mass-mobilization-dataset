Armenia 2020-12 — reconciled open-web dossier (versioned V1)

Scope and dates

This is a versioned reconciliation of the already assigned Armenia December 2020 dossier. The project’s fixed information/event cutoff is 2026-10-07, America/Mexico_City. All query, access, and capture actions in this work session occurred on 2026-10-11 UTC, after that reference cutoff; actual source publication dates are recorded per source. The record makes that timing explicit and does not invent native tool clocks. Later sources are used only for stated retrospective/coverage purposes, not to backdate event facts.

The version retains the original dossier records and adds targeted discovery on event boundaries, local/regional actions, official police archives, and human-rights oversight. The log has 33 submitted-query records and 532 captured result headers. The original 21 records include repeated headers from shared, unattributed concurrent envelopes; those repetitions are not independent queries/results. Eleven additional targeted queries were captured individually after those records. One earlier 15-Dec query was captured late as q033 from three relevant headers still visible in its actual tool response; its native clock, total, and omitted headers are unknown, and its appended log position is not its submission order. Captured header counts are not unique-page counts, search-result totals, or an exhaustiveness measure.

The search used English, Armenian, and Russian terms; broad and weekly-date discovery from the parent pass; then separate Armenian-language local searches for Gyumri, Syunik/Goris, and Vanadzor, plus targeted police-site, legacy Ombudsman, Ombudsman and civil-society queries. The added source mix includes regional press (Aravot, Azatutyun/RFE-RL, NEWS.am, Yerkir), local investigative press (Hetq), the state police archive and Human Rights Defender site, and Helsinki Citizens’ Assembly Vanadzor (HCAV). No Lexis-Nexis or systematically complete local archive was available.

Evidence added and effect on candidates

There are 78 source cards: 17 opened, 60 search-only, and 1 target not found. Five new sources yielded rendered body text; one exact Ombudsman report URL fell through to a generic page-not-found response. The six official-police exact-URL body requests failed at the managed browser layer (timeouts or one 502); a single same-exact-URL retry of the Dec 4–7 summary also timed out. There was no HTTP denial, and no alternate URL or workaround was used. Those official results remain search-only and do not establish bulletin content.

For 14 December Gyumri, the opened Aravot report adds the short Sayat-Nova closure and movement through central streets and attributes a no-police-clash statement to an ARF local representative. It mentions a few motorists joining but provides no total simultaneous attendance. Candidate E008 remains pending threshold; that partial description cannot establish either ≥50 or <50.

For the 21 December Syunik action, opened Azatutyun/RFE-RL coverage places the documented roadblock at Goris and reports that protesters said they would not let the Prime Minister enter the province. The report discusses border delimitation and local security concerns, but gives no headcount and no clearly articulated policy-change demand by that Goris crowd. Separate Kapan/Meghri results are not added into a pooled crowd. E011 remains pending evidence and threshold.

For 22 December Vanadzor, two Armenian-language search results repeat the Vanadzor–Yerevan route blockade and resignation demand. They provide no participant number, so E013 remains pending threshold. A road blockade is not itself evidence of a qualifying 50-person public gathering.

For 24 December, opened Hetq coverage says police had detained 69 people outside the government headquarters by 1 p.m. and reports a few minor scuffles while police cordoned the building. Arrests are not total attendance; the article does not identify who initiated the scuffles. E014 remains pending threshold; violence attribution remains unknown.

For 15 December, opened NEWS.am coverage states that a short rally at French Square followed the March of Dignity and that this ended the day’s opposition demonstration. This makes E009 (procession) and E010 (square rally) a likely same-day event sequence, rather than two independent events. UIC’s 2,700–2,800 count remains bounded to French Square at 18:55; it is not a count of all people along the procession route. The versioned boundary file asks the independent reviewer to settle the final representation, keeps the existing eligibility states unchanged, and prevents double counting or transfer of the square total to route participants.

The opened HCAV page summarizes media monitoring: 192 police-violation cases recorded for 2020, including 101 peaceful-assembly-rights cases. These annual aggregate figures are not event-specific; they are not assigned to any candidate. A targeted police-site search found a Dec 17 police response concerning a News.am Dec 1 force allegation and daily-summary result headers for Dec 4–7, Dec 9–10, Dec 18–21, and Dec 28–29, but exact body opens failed. A targeted Dec 22–25 police query did not surface a matching dated release. An Ombudsman 2020 annual-report PDF result could not be fetched at the exact URL, and the targeted legacy Ombudsman query returned older, unrelated material. No event-specific rights-monitoring record was verified.
