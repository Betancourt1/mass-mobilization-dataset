Unit armenia_2020-12 — open-web research dossier

Scope and method

This dossier covers Armenia, December 2020 only. The evidence search used the managed public web search/open tools and 21 actually submitted queries: broad English, Armenian and Russian searches; weekly windows; city/province, police, labor/student and human-rights terms; and one post-month continuity query for the Republic Square tent. Search output did not provide native totals or clocks. Several concurrent submissions returned shared envelopes; 347 saved headers include duplicated, partially retained headers whose query attribution is uncertain. Those are not independent result sets or unique results. Search dates were not reliable filters, so publication/event dates were checked in saved metadata and, where allowed, opened bodies. The structured query ledger preserves query strings, header order/references/URLs, observed shared-output limitations, and unknown native clocks.

The ledger contains 65 source cards: 12 marked opened and 53 search-only. Twelve cards record a body-read scope, but S001 is an accidental restricted-origin open whose only retained observation is the AP byline; its content was not used. Eleven other bodies were reviewed with fresh source-specific access checks. Body reads were partial/rendered passages, as detailed on each card; no full copyrighted article body was retained. Search-only items were not treated as body evidence. Access-check receipts are in access_receipts/. Several early checks were deferred when a concurrent ledger parse was unstable; fresh checks succeeded after the ledger stabilized, and the initial defers remain in receipts.

Candidate findings

Fifteen dated candidate records are preserved without claiming all meet the manual threshold. Proposed include records have qualifying crowd evidence: thousands at the 5 December Yerevan rally (S002), a couple hundred in central Yerevan on 8 December (S030), several hundred outside the government building on 10 December (S008), 2,700–2,800 in French Square at 18:55 on 15 December (S025), and thousands at Republic Square on 22 December (S011). The 9 December Parliament gathering is confirmed but lacks an attendance estimate in allowed opened evidence. The 15 December March of Dignity and French Square count are held as separate candidates pending evidence linking procession and counted assembly. Candidates for 1, 3, 11, 14, 21, 22 (Vanadzor route), 24 and 25 December remain below/uncertain threshold or unresolved in location, size, linkage, or demand; arrest counts were not used as crowd totals. No simultaneous crowd totals were pooled across cities. State response and protester violence remain unknown unless a source reported a specific incident.

Two continuations are recorded: the pre-December resignation campaign, whose first qualifying assembly date is unknown (C001), and the Republic Square tent/sit-in begun 22 December and reported later as dismantled around two months afterward, with exact end date unknown (C002). Neither continuation is converted into an invented onset/end date. No exclusion is asserted (excluded.json is empty because no candidate was conclusively excluded on the available record). All ccode fields remain null pending verified baseline/code mapping.

Coverage and unresolved limits

The 21 gaps cover threshold counts and event linkage, campaign onset, city-specific actions, response/violence, independent-origin checks, missing local archives/police/ombudsman and event-specific rights documentation, and limits of the web search. In particular, no official baseline/schema/version or code mapping was supplied, so this file cannot claim official compatibility or scientific completeness. Search output is a discovery aid, not a systematic census. Some local reports may repeat a single written origin. A cross-month NEWS.am search result informs only provisional tent continuity.

Access incidents and source-origin limits

S001 (Guardian) was opened accidentally after a manual_review result. On inspecting the returned page, the AP byline was noticed immediately; exact response clock is unavailable. Only origin metadata was checked, no factual content was retained or used, and the AP restriction’s exact inherited scope remains pending. No variants were reopened. S012 (Panorama) was also accidentally opened without a fresh access-check; the response exposed only its title/publisher and zero body lines. No content was available or used and there was no retry. These incidents are documented as gaps, not evidence. S015/PBS-AP was not opened; S057/LA Times has unresolved written origin and is not counted as independent corroboration.
