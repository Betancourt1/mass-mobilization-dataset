Methods and coverage
Research has used open-web discovery and opened Turkish- and English-language reporting and organizational statements. Searches have emphasized the Boğaziçi rector appointment protests, Ankara support actions, the Jan 21 campus and Kadıköy actions, labor, and public-health workers. Some provincial and sector searches, December/February continuity checks and remaining event-boundary work are still open. Search-result excerpts remain discovery leads unless a body or primary document was read. The available open-web method is not equivalent to LexisNexis; coverage is uneven and no absence is inferred from search results. The current official MM dataset/codebook universe and compatible export procedure remain unverified, so all records are local candidates under the pending baseline gate.
