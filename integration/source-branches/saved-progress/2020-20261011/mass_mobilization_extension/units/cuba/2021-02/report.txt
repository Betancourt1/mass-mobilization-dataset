Cuba — February 2021 country-month research
Unit: cuba_2021-02
Scope: first-pass, open-web research; candidate records only, pending coordinator review and the official baseline.

Search and coverage
Eight actual batched web-search invocations were captured in search_log.jsonl, preserving 31 actual query clauses and 237 ordered returned URL/header entries. Searches covered generic English and Spanish terms, weekly February windows, Caibarién and the reported fine, Camagüey and leafleting, and worker/protest queries. The ledger preserves all returned headers and original refs, including unrelated, empty, and search-only leads. Query and result counts describe captured tool calls, not unique sources or events.

Sixteen source cards are retained. The three permitted full-text reads were S002 ADN Cuba, S005 Periódico Cubano, and S014 CiberCuba, each under a per-attempt stable exact-URL receipt, saved manual publisher/origin note, and immediate corpus path/hash check. Each card records the actual body scope and remaining limits. No body read was restricted to an earlier month. S008 remains search-only: the web reader returned a one-line tool-access error with no body, and a separately gated ordinary HTTPS request failed DNS before HTTP. Neither outcome is an observed publisher denial. No Cuba source denial was observed.

Two candidates remain unresolved. E001 concerns the Caibarién candy seller fined 2,000 pesos: the two opened reports describe a February 27 seller-side gathering and a February 28 municipal-office action, both with qualitative “dozens” estimates. ADN Cuba attributes the fine withdrawal to the office action; do not transfer that accommodation to the February 27 gathering unless the episode boundary is established. Neither account proves 50 actual demonstrators. E002 concerns leafleting at a Camagüey MLC-store queue on February 27: CiberCuba reports several arrests and unspecified objections by people in the queue, but gives no crowd total or exact leaflet text. Arrests do not establish turnout, and the government-directed demand remains pending. CIVICUS and other Camagüey results are retained as search-only leads, not confirmation.

One legal/petition item concerning the January 27 Ministry of Culture gathering is excluded as a February physical assembly (X001). No confirmed physical continuation from January into February is recorded. The empty continuations file preserves that distinction; prior-month activity and later reporting are not treated as an assembly continuing across the month. No February candidate is treated as an official Mass Mobilization update.

Coverage and unresolved evidence
The open-web searches do not replicate LexisNexis and are not exhaustive. Local/regional archives, official records, radio-origin links, and rights-organization coverage remain uneven. See gaps.json for the attendance threshold, event dates and boundaries, state target and response, source origins, access, closure, and archive gaps.
