It shares a tentative episode key with E006 and is `pending_unit`: a KMP source says the Southern Luzon caravan was camped at CHR and planned to join the Mendiola march, but the precise physical episode boundary and overlap with the wider Manila rally remain for review. The Jan. 22 Cebu action remains separate pending evidence of national coordination.
- E005: Jan. 25 Batasan Complex anti-charter protest lead, based on a later secondary timeline and its RMN Facebook citation. The post’s body/date/turnout were not exposed; an ordinary request returned HTTP 200 HTML but no event text was extractable. This remains `pending_evidence`, not a verified gathering.
- E006: Jan. 20–21 KASAMA-TK/Southern Luzon farmers’ protest caravan. A KMP organizer report says more than 100 farmers participated on Jan. 20 and describes actions at Camp Aguinaldo, DILG, and the Philippine Coconut Authority. A second KMP post calls Jan. 21 the caravan’s second day, reports farmers from at least 20 landholdings at DAR, says a planned face-to-face dialogue was canceled in favor of an online schedule, and notes a camp at CHR. The 20 landholdings are not an attendance count; the Jan. 20 estimate is organizer-origin and not independently corroborated. S032 displays Jan. 20/21 page headers and S034 displays Jan. 21/29 headers; these may be original/update cues, but their roles are unclear, so event dates follow the post bodies. The Jan. 22 Mendiola linkage is unresolved, so E006 is `pending_unit`; the Jan. 20–22 episode end and state responses remain unknown.

E001–E003 remain `pending_threshold`; E005 is `pending_evidence`. E004 and E006 are `pending_unit` because the possible multi-day caravan/Manila linkage affects distinct-event counting.
