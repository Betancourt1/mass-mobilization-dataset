Guinea, February 2021 — open-web research dossier; pending coordinator review.

Scope and method

The unit covers the Republic of Guinea in February 2021. Search terms were run in French and English across weekly date ranges, named towns, protest/strike terminology, and electricity, water, education, labor, health, transport and displacement leads. Captured material includes Q001–Q018: 18 saved search-response/capture records (Q001–Q017 batched responses and Q018 one targeted follow-up), with 466 ordered result headers retained. The captured work covers Conakry and regional reports including Kissidougou, Faranah, Kankan, Fria, Kamsar and Boké, but does not establish exhaustive national or sector coverage. Search output was used only to discover sources. No event count or absence claim is inferred from the number of results.

The unit distinguishes the Republic of Guinea from Guinea-Bissau and Equatorial Guinea. No diaspora action was accepted as a Guinea-targeted event. No official event baseline or LexisNexis comparison was completed; baseline approval remains pending. Q004’s RFI-targeted search response reported robots.txt blocking. No RFI article body was requested. An ordinary open of the exact Kankan Orange Money article URL returned only Cache miss; that technical retrieval failure is not a denial or a body read.

Sources and access

There are 22 source cards: four opened and 18 search-only. Body reads covered the Kissidougou event account (S002), Faranah event account (S008), EDG institutional page (S011), and Fria bakery account (S015). S011 reproduces a 2019 decree that establishes EDG as a public company under named ministerial supervision; it is institutional context, not evidence of local operations or a particular 2021 outage. S006 remains search-only after the cache-miss attempt. The source cards preserve article-level limits, receipts and manual reviews. Search-only accounts were not treated as independent confirmations. No exact body denial was observed; no publisher hold was overridden.

Candidates and screening

Three local event candidates are retained separately. E001 is a February 22 Kissidougou gathering over water and electricity service failures; it reports tear-gas dispersal and local official engagement, but no count, physical end or route. Its 50-person threshold remains pending. S011 documents EDG’s public-company status; SEG status and both utilities’ local responsibility need follow-up. E002 is a February 24 Faranah electricity protest. Its report describes road barriers and two groups, but no protester count; delayed vehicles are not attendees. EDG’s role in that outage, group coordination and the physical end remain unresolved. E003 is the February 12–15 Fria bakery stoppage over an official loaf-price instruction. The source reports an official meeting and price increase, but no number of bakeries/workers or public assembly; the 50-person threshold remains pending. The January Fria lead does not establish continuity. E001, E002 and E003 are separate local candidates.

One search lead is recorded as an exclusion from current coding: a pharmacist strike notice without evidence it was executed (X001). This screening decision does not establish that no collective action occurred. The Kankan/Kamsar Orange Money, Boké union-station, school-boycott, Conakry demolition/eviction, and January Fria leads remain follow-up gaps rather than asserted events. No continuation is recorded because the school and Fria cross-month leads do not establish a known outside-month event start or a continuous episode.

Counts at handoff: 18 saved search-response records; 466 ordered result headers; 22 source cards (4 opened, 18 search-only); 3 candidates; 1 exclusion; 0 continuations; 16 gaps. Eligibility remains pending review, and the unit is not an official dataset update.
