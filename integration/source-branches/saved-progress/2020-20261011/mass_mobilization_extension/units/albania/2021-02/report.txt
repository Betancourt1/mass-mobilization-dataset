FEBRUARY CANDIDATE

E001 records a February 16 protest in Shkodër by residents affected by flooding, before Prime Minister Edi Rama arrived for a COVID-19 vaccination launch. Euronews Albania reports that Bërdicë residents protested at the city entrance and asked the Prime Minister and government to attend to flooded homes and farmland and lack of assistance. Balkanweb separately reports that residents of flood-affected Nënshkodër protested before Rama arrived. The reports do not establish that both describe the identical assembled group or have independent written origins.

Neither opened report supplies an attendance count. Euronews's roughly 350 hectares of flooded land is not a crowd figure, and neither report establishes 50 physically assembled people; E001 therefore remains pending_threshold. Euronews locates the action at Shkodër's entrance, while Balkanweb gives no precise site. Both are dated February 16; neither establishes a physical end. Balkanweb notes police nearby and says protesters were scattered because gatherings were not permitted, but it does not describe a specific order to disperse or physical clearing. No protester violence or event-specific state response is established; all seven response values remain unknown. A March 3 Ministry of Defense search result concerns flood conditions and continuing support, not the protest, and does not establish its end.
