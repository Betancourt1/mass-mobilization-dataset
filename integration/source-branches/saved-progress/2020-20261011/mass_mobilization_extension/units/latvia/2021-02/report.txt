Latvia — February 2021
Unit: latvia_2021-02; provisional ccode 367; Europe; queue order 243; fixed event cutoff 2026-10-07 inclusive (America/Mexico_City).
Status: researched_open_web_pending_review. This is a provisional open-web first pass, not an official update or approval.

Coverage and method
Twelve actual batched web-search actions were captured from the registered worker rollout: 284 ordered result headers and URLs, including repeated, off-month and irrelevant results. Queries covered English and Latvian general protest terms, Riga/picket searches, weekly February windows, and hairdresser/beauty-sector restrictions and demands. Results were captured as returned; the 284 headers are not a total-events estimate. Thirteen relevant URL cards are retained. Five article bodies were opened and read (S001, S002, S005, S007, S012); the other eight remain search-only.

Findings
No candidate is established on current evidence as a public gathering of at least 50 people making a demand of the Latvian state. E001 remains pending evidence: LSM’s opened Feb. 22 article says LKKA planned to consider demanding Health Minister Pavļuts’s resignation at a 17:00 annual meeting. It does not report a venue, meeting mode, public access, attendance, decision or street action. A search-only LETA/TV3 result later says the meeting took place and most association members supported the conditional demand, but it supplies no number and its body was not retrieved. The 9,000 figure in LSM refers to registered beauty providers, not meeting attendees. The event status and threshold are therefore unresolved.

The opened AFP account of “haircuts on ice” describes dispersed forest and frozen-lake appointments, one client at a time, and gives no collective turnout or activity onset; its Feb. 18 publication date is not used as event start. The opened LA.LV report describes councilor Ieva Brante’s individual haircut video on Feb. 6. Riga’s municipality reports Belarus solidarity building illumination on Feb. 7, with no public assembly and a foreign-state target. The opened LSM Feb. 12 report describes court and competition-policy advocacy by beauty-sector associations, not a street gathering. Four non-events/leads are kept in exclusions, while E001 is retained separately because its public-meeting status remains unresolved.

Source access and uncertainty
S003 (BNS/TV3.lt), S009 (VS.lv/LETA) and S013 (TV3.lv/LETA) remain search-only after documented cache/status or DNS failures. No explicit access denial was observed. Search snippets, petition signatures, association membership/workforce figures, and article publication dates are not treated as attendance or event-onset evidence. Protester violence and all state-response dimensions remain unknown for E001; no protest gathering is established from which to code them. No continuation is assigned because no cross-month protest event with a supported start was found; the onset of the dispersed haircut activity remains unknown.

Limitations and next review
Open-web search is not LexisNexis and is not exhaustive of Latvian, regional, municipal or archived coverage. Review E001’s publicness, mode, venue, actual attendance and demand outcome; retain all unknowns until evidence is available. The official baseline and coordinator review remain pending.
