El Salvador, February 2021 — open-web first pass completed; coordinator and independent review remain pending. This is a provisional research dossier, not an official dataset update.

Search capture: 17 actual search invocations contained 65 submitted query strings (64 distinct; one exact duplicate was retained as its own actual invocation) and 458 ordered result headers. Search terms were in Spanish and English and covered general protest/strike terms, weekly/date windows, the 9F anniversary, unions and municipal workers, culture employees, water rights, pensions, Florenzi workers, education, and regional locations. Batched result arrays are preserved exactly; returned headers cannot be assigned to individual query terms and do not establish absence. The audit-restored Q017 response preserves one original four-query batch and 23 ordered headers; its event-relevant topics are already represented by S001, S012/S021, S013 and S016, so no source or candidate judgment changed. Header 8 is an uncarded ARPAS year-end roundup; its title does not identify a February action, so it remains a discovery header without a new source card or candidate.

There are 22 relevant source cards: 4 cards with current-unit article-body scopes and 18 search-only cards. Four current-unit body opens and 0 prior-unit-only consultations are recorded. Twelve actual page-request attempts produced four opened article bodies and eight reader/DNS failures; six additional receipt checks did not lead to a body request. None of the technical failures is an observed site denial. The receipt manifest inventories each attempt and saved manual note; a receipt alone is not permission.

Two event candidates remain pending review. E001 is the February 22 water-right caravan in San Salvador. Gato Encerrado reports a 09:10 route to party offices with letters seeking water-right legislation, but gives no crowd count, route end or government response. E002 is the February 11-12 Culture-union stoppage over delayed food-card payments. Gato Encerrado reports public signs outside the National Palace and an explicit ministerial nonresponse, but gives no turnout; the 1,100-plus ministry workforce is not attendance. Public-action and threshold eligibility remain unresolved. `ignore=yes` records only the article’s explicit statement that the minister did not address the demands and remains for response-code review. The reported end is the unions’ withdrawal at midday Feb. 12, not a broader campaign end.

Two exclusions are retained. X001 is an actual February 9, 2022 second-anniversary 9F gathering, outside this event-start month; the article’s “decenas” does not establish 50 attendees. X002 is a February 10, 2021 pro-government union gathering outside the Assembly opposing a proposal to remove President Bukele. The report says around 40 people attended, below the threshold; 40 unions represented is not a crowd count. Support for the president or generic labor-law rhetoric is not treated as automatic eligibility. No continuations are confirmed.

Unresolved leads include 9F student mobilization, the February 3 union march and other municipal/union actions, Florenzi-related demonstrations and the No+AFP pension movement. Body access for several exact URLs ended in Cache miss, timeout or ordinary HTTPS DNS failure; those are technical failures rather than denials. Florenzi and ASTRAM/STISSS episode boundaries need January comparison, and the No+AFP exact-story host variant remains unresolved. Do not treat unread search headers as event evidence or independent corroboration.

Coverage is non-exhaustive. Searches used Spanish and English, not a systematic Indigenous/local-language corpus; coverage outside San Salvador, local/regional archives, LexisNexis-equivalent retrieval, and event-specific follow-up are incomplete. The official current baseline/universe/schema/licence and additional original coding procedure remain pending. Preserve unknowns and the fixed 2026-10-07 cutoff; no official IDs or integration were made.
