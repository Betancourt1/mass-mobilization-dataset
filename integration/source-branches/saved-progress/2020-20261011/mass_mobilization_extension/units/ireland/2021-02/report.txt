Ireland — February 2021 bounded first-pass report

Scope and method

This dossier covers public gatherings in Ireland with an onset in February 2021 under the fixed 2026-10-07 research cutoff. Seven actual batched web-search actions were executed and durably captured, comprising 28 query strings and 239 ordered result headers. Queries covered Ireland-wide and regional demonstrations, strikes and workers, Dublin anti-lockdown actions, Cork and other cities, specific February dates, Irish-language terms, Debenhams pickets and the Cork Topshop/Opera Lane lead. Date-window queries returned out-of-window and current results, including 2026 pages; search dates are therefore unreliable, and the result set cannot establish that an event did not occur. Search capture preserves exact queries, ordered URL/title headers, clocks and original action IDs, but no snippets are used as occurrence evidence.

Two source bodies were read after fresh exact-URL receipts, saved manual publisher/origin reviews and immediate full-input hash checks: An Garda Síochána (S001) and BreakingNews.ie (S005). Both concern Dublin on 27 February. The access workflow was not used to open the DW lead S002 because a saved publisher-wide hold applies. RTÉ source S014 duplicates an exact URL already recorded as HTTP 403 in Ireland January S004; no retry or alternate route was attempted. Search-only leads remain search-only. The saved source set contains 18 cards.

Candidate event

E001 is a local Dublin city-centre anti-lockdown assembly on 27 February. BreakingNews reports “hundreds” gathering to protest current Covid-19 restrictions, which supports the >=50 threshold and the broad `social_restrictions` category; it supplies no precise crowd count, named organizer or detailed platform. Garda reports that people began assembling in the Grafton Street area at about 1:30 p.m. and later calls the gathering a protest. BreakingNews says it was due at 2 p.m., reports movement to the GPO, and describes fireworks and clashes. The Garda release reports 23 arrests and three injured Garda personnel. The injuries are not protester casualties, arrests are not turnout, and the official account does not show that all participants engaged in violence. Arrests are coded yes; accommodation, beatings, crowd dispersal, ignore, killings and shootings remain unknown. Event end is unknown. E001 is proposed for coordinator review, not official approval.

Other leads and exclusions

The only Cork Topshop/Opera Lane lead is the unopened Oireachtas source S015 summarized as a planned 23 February protest. It is kept out of observed events because execution and threshold are unverified; the specific lead is preserved in X001 and G006. S014 is a blocked Debenhams discovery URL and cannot verify a Cork picket, its turnout, a campaign onset, or continuity. No Debenhams continuation is coded without confirmed start/continuity evidence; S018 is a Reddit discovery card, not turnout evidence. S012 is a search-only Irish Times lead about a separate 17 March Herbert Park action, outside February; no relationship to E001 is established. The out-of-month record is a boundary check, not a February event.

Coverage, gaps and status

The searches found plausible event-related leads but do not provide exhaustive national or regional coverage. Key gaps concern an independent attendance estimate, detailed demands and organizer, physical end, event/episode boundaries, and corroboration of the state-response account. The Cork Debenhams and Topshop leads need accessible source-level follow-up; the exact RTÉ 403 and the DW publisher hold remain respected. No candidate has been merged with another episode or entered in the official MM dataset. All eight required dossier files are present. `validate-unit` passed with no issues; `summarize-unit` reported 7 action rows, 239 headers, 18 source cards, 1 event, 1 excluded lead, 0 continuations and 8 gaps. `baseline_gate` remains pending_official_baseline.
