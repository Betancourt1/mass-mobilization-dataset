Djibouti — February 2021
Research status: researched_open_web_pending_review. This dossier is a provisional open-web research record, not an official Mass Mobilization update.

Scope and method
Research was limited to Djibouti and February 2021. Twelve actual batched web searches were captured in search_log.jsonl, preserving each exact query, ordered original result headers and empty/non-empty response state; 274 result headers were recorded, and the search provider exposed no total-result counts. Queries used English, French, Arabic and Somali terms and covered broad protest/strike language, weekly date windows, official and local media, rights organizations, sectors, regions and the January-to-February boundary. The search log retains all 12 invocations. Open-web searching does not reproduce LexisNexis or systematic press archives, so this work is not exhaustive.

Source inventory
Eight relevant URL cards are recorded: 1 opened current-unit body, 1 prior-unit-only consultation, and 7 search_only cards. The exact S004 ARDHD/RFI URL was body-read in the January unit as DJ202101-S007, but it is search_only in this February dossier and supplies January boundary context only. This technical failure is not a site denial. No retry or alternate copy was attempted. Source counts are metadata-based; same-origin copies are not independent corroboration.

Candidate and exclusions

Its actual start date, attendance, demand, demand recipient, organizer, state response, protester conduct and physical end remain unknown. The approximate arrest count in search text is not turnout. No confirmed February event is proposed for inclusion.

Four exclusions retain the December 2020 incumbent-candidacy rally, the title-only Paris demonstration outside Djibouti, the undated Dikhil electoral-support title, and a La Nation candidacy-filing title that does not describe an assembly. The CIVICUS and WardheerNews search-only titles remain undated or ambiguous and are documented as gaps rather than assigned to February. No continuation is recorded: searches did not establish an ongoing physical episode from January, and a recurring campaign is not treated as one continuous assembly.

Unresolved gaps and limits
Eleven gaps document the S003 event/date and threshold uncertainty, own-state demand and organizer, event-specific state response and end, January boundary continuity, undated or ambiguous source leads, the narrowly scoped RFI search-layer restriction, open-web/archives/language limits, and the pending official baseline and manual review. The Q012 rfi.fr robots response is scoped to that exact search query; it is not a direct-page denial and does not apply to either ARDHD URL. Searches that produced no relevant confirmation do not show that no protest occurred.
