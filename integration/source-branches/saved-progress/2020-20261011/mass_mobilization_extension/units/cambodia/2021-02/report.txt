Scope and method

This dossier covers 1–28 February 2021 under the fixed 7 October 2026 event cutoff. It is a provisional extension record, separate from the integrated catalogue. The official baseline and coding-manual review remain pending.

The first pass logged 21 actual English- and Khmer-language searches with 514 exact ordered result headers. Coverage included broad protest terms, four English weekly windows, four Khmer weekly windows, land and forest disputes, labor and garment work, activist releases, Kandal Province, and a date-specific February 23 search. The exact arrays were reconciled against the original exposed tool events without repeating searches; the prior parsed rows for S008–S021 are preserved in the capture history and replaced with the original ordered fields, refs, titles, URLs and exposed clocks. Q001’s recovered 24-header array matches the parent-provided SHA-256 and the final extractor snapshot. Search-result snippets remain discovery only and were not used as event evidence. This is not an exhaustive survey of local outlets, archives or every province, and it does not establish event absence.

Body scopes are recorded for RFA S001 (article lines 31–77), the selected Sithi timeline page S007 (displayed entries dated 1 March through 5 February; linked articles not opened), and CamboJA S013 (article lines 38–67). Other search-only release, labor, forest and human-rights leads do not establish additional qualifying gatherings.

Provisional candidate findings

E001 records CamboJA’s report of a 16 February village protest in Prek Lavea, Ksach Kandal district, Kandal Province, where villagers burned tires during a land dispute. The article reports 11 arrests but no protester count. Its ten dispute families are not attendance, and the “dozens” reported refer to police. The body does not establish an explicit government-directed demand at the protest, so the state target remains pending; threshold, end date and the relevance of a separate police-violence allegation also remain unresolved. Reported tire burning is coded as protester violence; arrests are recorded separately as state response.

E002 records RFA’s report of solidarity gatherings on 23 February in separate communities across 11 provinces. RFA reports 10–20 people at each location and banners seeking dropped charges against six activists; speakers also asked government to address local disputes and end intimidation and arrests. The site ranges remain separate: no campaign-wide or simultaneous local attendance total is assigned. Whether this coordinated multi-sited campaign is one dataset unit or separate local assemblies remains pending_unit, alongside the local threshold question. RFA could not reach government spokespeople; this is not coded as ignore. No physical end or completed follow-up is established.

Two separate leads are excluded: a follow-on Kandal police-headquarters protest by 11 people, with no exact date given, is below the threshold; head-shaving by detained activists on 23 February is an in-custody action, not a public assembly. No January-onset continuation was substantiated. Candidate counts and coding remain provisional, not official inclusion decisions.
