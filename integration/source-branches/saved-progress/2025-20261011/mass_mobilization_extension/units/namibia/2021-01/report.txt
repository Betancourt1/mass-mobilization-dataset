Findings and unit boundaries

Three provisional event records are retained. E001 concerns a Windhoek unemployed-teacher gathering reported for 21 January, with demands on government/education policy including teacher-pupil policy and recruitment; its post-event count is “dozens,” so attendance of at least 50 is unproven. E003 concerns a Windhoek gathering dated 19 January in a 2 February follow-up, described as “scores” and associated with a petition to the prime minister. These are competing or possibly separate dates; the research does not resolve whether two gatherings occurred or one date is erroneous. Petition delivery does not establish an official audience or response to either specific gathering. Both teacher records remain pending threshold and date/episode review.

Neither the station gathering nor the later Yetu crowd has a separate attendance count, and at least 50 at the station demand is unproven. Rubber-bullet fire and dispersal are reported for the later Yetu phase; the sources do not establish that these were responses to the earlier station demand. All seven candidate-level state-response variables remain unknown. The relationship of the state-directed station phase to the December-started private wage dispute is unresolved.

The NAFAU–Shoprite wage strike began 23 December 2020 and continued into January. Informanté reports public actions at Walvis Bay Dunes Mall and intervention in Keetmanshoop and Tsumeb. The 15 January Namibian Sun search result reports community members joining striking workers to close the Tsumeb branch on a Tuesday morning; the 20 January Afrikaans Republikein search result says workers protested the previous Wednesday. These may be separate actions/phases or reflect differing dates; no single verified Tsumeb date or local turnout is assigned. Available Shoprite demands target the private employer. Store closure/access disputes, police presence, rubber-bullet reporting and court proceedings do not by themselves establish a demand to the Namibian state. These private-target public leads remain excluded from January MM candidates and linked to the December-owned continuation. Available sources disagree on strike end (22 January versus an agreement in the last week of January); this does not establish the physical end of each local gathering. Do not use nationwide workforce or strike counts as local attendance.
