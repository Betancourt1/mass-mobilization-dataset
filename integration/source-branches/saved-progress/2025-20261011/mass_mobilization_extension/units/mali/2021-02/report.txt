Mali, February 2021 — research handoff

Scope and method

This is a candidate dossier for Mali's territorial February 2021 events through the fixed 2026-10-07 cutoff. The assigned provisional C-code is 432; the official baseline gate is still pending. I ran 30 captured searches in French and English, including weekly date windows, follow-up queries, regional and sector searches (students, teachers, health workers, police unions, bakers, market actors and transporters), continuity checks, and exploratory Bamanankan/Bambara terminology queries. Earlier interim totals of 665 and 637 headers conflicted. Reconciliation against the registered rollout's actual search outputs shows exactly 30 `web.search` actions and 638 ordered result headers, matching all 30 persisted query records one by one. The 638 count supersedes both interim totals.

Search results support three unresolved February gathering candidates: the 8 February IPR/IFRA Katibougou student action (E001), the M5-RFP meeting in Bamako on 21 February (E002), and Gao demonstrations against the governor's curfew beginning the night of 21 February (E003). Each remains `pending_evidence`; the search snippets and headers do not establish the required 50-person threshold, and no candidate has been approved. The M5-RFP search results disagree qualitatively about attendance. The Katibougou >1,000-student headline has unresolved date and episode relation and was not applied to E001. The Gao search result lacks a crowd estimate and an end date. State-response and violence fields remain unknown unless direct evidence is obtained.

Exclusions preserve separate work stoppages and closures that have no captured evidence of public assembly: bakers, public-school teachers, Kayes health workers, transporters, market closures, Sotramas and an AEEM strike/internal association lead. A Mali-consulate protest in Bagnolet/Paris is excluded from this territorial Mali unit despite its reported Mali-directed NINA-card grievance; its physical-country ownership lead is reserved for coordinator review. Other exclusions retain an unconfirmed police-union date lead, a future M5-RFP gathering plan, a political-proposal report with no assembly evidence, and a separate Katibougou attendance headline whose event date/relation is unresolved. The continuity array is empty: targeted searches found no verified physical continuation across the month boundary, and January events were not merged automatically.

The dossier has 40 source cards; all are search-only. Exactly four body-request invocations occurred in the rollout: three `web.open` calls (Studio Tamani, AMAP and iCimali) and one ordinary HTTPS GET for AMAP. All failed technically: three web-tool Cache misses and one DNS resolution failure before HTTP. No body content was received, no site denial was observed and no article body was read. Saved attempt records reconcile to all four actual invocations. The initial Studio Tamani request gate stopped before network access when source-input hashes changed. A later apparent Kibaru open was only present in a conditional exec input: its pre-request gate found two changed ledgers and exited before invoking web.open. A fresh Kibaru gate was also stopped when the input-comparison command failed before comparing hashes. These are recorded no-request deferrals, not denials or body reads. A separate phase-path lookup also made no network request. The exact receipts, notes, failures and no-request deferrals are preserved in `access_receipts/`; none authorizes future access. Any future request requires a fresh exact-URL receipt, full matching manual publisher/origin review, saved-note read-back/hash and immediate stable recheck of the current complete source-input set.

Limitations and next work

Open-web discovery is not equivalent to LexisNexis or the historical manual expansion. No exhaustive claim is made. The Bambara/Bamanankan searches were exploratory; they do not substitute for a fluent local-language review or local archive coverage. Search-result snippets are discovery leads, not body-verified evidence. Obtain contemporaneous article bodies and independent reporting for the three candidates, resolve crowd sizes and event endpoints, review local-language and regional archives, and resolve official baseline/manual availability before any consolidated coding decision. No event has been integrated into the original catalogue or represented as an official update.
