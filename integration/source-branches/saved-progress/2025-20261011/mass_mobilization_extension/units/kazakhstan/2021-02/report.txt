Kazakhstan — February 2021 — queue 237, ccode 705

Status: open-web research prepared for coordinator review. This is a provisional extension dossier, not an official update. The official baseline/manual comparison remains pending.

Method and scope
Nine actual batched web-search actions are captured from the registered rollout. Each action contained four query strings, for 36 query strings total; the nine captured result sets contain 257 ordered headers. Queries used English, Russian and Kazakh phrasing, broad February terms, weekly windows, city names, labor issues, the Feb. 28 opposition rallies, Shymkent bus drivers and Nur-Sultan crane operators. Actual original event IDs, query batches and ordered headers were compared against the saved search log; all nine matched exactly. Search headers are discovery evidence only.

Four exact URLs were opened after fresh receipts, saved manual publisher/origin review and immediate full-input hash rechecks: RFE/RL and the Alliance Tirek February monitor on the Feb. 28 rallies; Azattyq's retrospective crane-operators report; and Inbusiness.kz on the Shymkent Bus work stoppage. Each card records request clock, receipt, review note and read scope. No full article body was retained; embedded media and linked articles were not inspected. A further captured KTK local-TV lead was not readable: the exact web open returned a one-line Internal Error, and a separately gated direct HTTPS attempt failed DNS before HTTP. This is a technical retrieval gap, not a denial, and no body evidence was used. The RFE/RL item credits Reuters reporting; no independent confirmation is inferred from that attribution or from other outlets. One local exact-URL gate check for the Tirek target initially failed closed due to a typo in the comparison string; no web request was made on that attempt. The target was then copied from its receipt, all inputs were rechecked, and the documented request proceeded.

Candidate events
Three candidate records are retained: a Feb. 28 Oral rally with approximate attendance above 50 and political/land-policy demands (proposed include); the crane-operators labor campaign reported to begin Feb. 12 (pending attendance threshold); and the Feb. 3 Shymkent Bus work stoppage (pending clear state target and proof of a qualifying public assembly/turnout). City and workforce totals are not treated as event attendance. Attendance, event ends and most response variables remain unknown where the reviewed text does not establish them.

Exclusions and continuations
The Feb. 28 Atyrau gathering (~30) and Almaty Sports Palace gathering (~40) are excluded below threshold; detainees are not counted as turnout. Planned Semey and Aktobe rallies are excluded because the reports say people were detained before a group assembled. The Nur-Sultan memorial gathering (~13) has no clear protest demand in the read report. The Kentau transformer-plant action is excluded as an employer-only wage dispute with no state demand reported. No cross-month continuation is confirmed; a search-only oil-worker lead needs follow-up before any event or continuation coding.
