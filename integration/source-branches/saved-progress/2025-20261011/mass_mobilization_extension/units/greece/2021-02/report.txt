Greece, February 2021 — researched open-web candidates; pending human and official-baseline review

Scope and method

This dossier covers Greece, February 1–28, 2021, under the fixed October 7, 2026 cutoff. The discovery pass saved 31 original search actions and 829 ordered result headers. Queries used English and Greek, with general searches, weekly date windows, and targeted searches for student/education-law actions, solidarity actions around Dimitris Koufontinas, COVID/lockdown demonstrations, health workers, artists, farmers, and regional cities including Athens, Thessaloniki, Evosmos, Larissa, Patras, Ioannina, and Volos. The search log preserves each original action, including batched queries and result URLs.

This is an open-web discovery pass, not a census. It does not reproduce LexisNexis or a systematic Greek press archive. Local and regional reporting, additional languages, and primary records remain unevenly covered. The original coding manual and verified official baseline are absent from the portable handoff; baseline compatibility remains pending.

Evidence and candidates

One proposed candidate is recorded: E001, a distinct February 23 courthouse gathering in Thessaloniki against a new campus-security law. A short Euronews report credited to AP says more than 5,000 students and others gathered outside a courthouse. That reported lower bound clears the 50-person threshold, but it is not an exact count. AP attribution means an AP republication would not provide independent corroboration. The report places 31 arrests and an unspecified violent incident at a separate February 22 demonstration; neither is coded as a February 23 event response. Although the February 23 gathering is described as banned under pandemic restrictions, the report does not describe event-specific enforcement. All seven state-response fields and protester violence therefore remain unknown. Exact courthouse, organizer, physical start/end, and independent confirmation remain open questions.

The source ledger contains 89 relevant cards: one opened article with a recorded body scope and 88 search-only leads. A physical page request for S024 first returned only site navigation and no article text; a later separately gated request returned the complete short article body relevant to E001. Two other S024 preflights were stopped after the immediate full-input check detected changed hashes, and no request followed either stale check. Three documented S005 requests for an Athens police page ended in reader-cache or DNS/tool failures without an HTTP response or article text. These are retrieval failures, not source denials; further S005 attempts were stopped for this pass. Across the recorded exact-URL page attempts, one article body was usable, one response contained only a navigation shell, and three S005 requests returned no body. Other saved access receipts for S036 and S049 document preflights, not body reads. No source denial is recorded in this dossier.

Unresolved leads and accounting

The remaining search-only material includes possible distinct student gatherings and a university occupation, Koufontinas solidarity actions, anti-lockdown events in Athens, Thessaloniki and Evosmos, arts-sector demonstrations, and healthcare, farming, and worker/student solidarity actions. Search headers alone do not establish that an announced action occurred, its date or attendance, a public gathering rather than an occupation/intervention/strike, or any response. The ten gaps preserve these questions and the missing manual/baseline, source-access, local-archive, continuity, ownership, and event-boundary work. `excluded.json` and `continuations.json` are empty because this pass did not establish a specific nonqualifying event or a February episode that began earlier; this does not mean no excluded or continuing actions occurred.

The dossier has 31 search actions, 89 source cards, one proposed candidate, zero excluded candidates, zero continuations, and ten open gaps. Source scope is 1 current-unit body opens and 0 prior-unit-only consultation cards. This research remains a reviewable experimental extension, not an official update or integration.
