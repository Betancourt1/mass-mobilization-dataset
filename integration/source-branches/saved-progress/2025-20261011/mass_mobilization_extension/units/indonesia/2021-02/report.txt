Indonesia, February 2021 — provisional open-web dossier

This is an experimental, reviewable research dossier, not an official Mass Mobilization update or an exhaustive event catalogue. The fixed event cutoff is 2026-10-07, America/Mexico_City. The latest official baseline remains unverified; original dataset IDs have not been assigned.

The search log preserves 27 actual invocations and 462 ordered result headers, including English and Bahasa Indonesia general queries, weekly/date windows, media-domain checks and episode follow-ups. A final comparison against the registered rollout matched every actual action ID, query batch, ordered header, URL, reference, title and clock. Batched responses remain pooled. One Kompas-domain query reported a robots.txt block but exposed no URL for that target; no blocked URL was invented. Search output is discovery metadata, not event confirmation.

Thirty relevant source cards are recorded (2 current-unit body opens, 0 prior-unit-only consultations): two public institutional pages were opened under fresh exact-URL receipts and full matching-corpus/manual review, and 28 sources remain search-only. Both reads are scoped partial reads. The Kediri DPRD report describes a local PMII action against the Omnibus Law with a qualitative “hundreds” crowd and council reception; it is a provisional proposed include pending independent review. Banda Aceh Kesbangpol reports 20 people at a GAH protest, which is retained in exclusions as below the threshold. One IDN Times open returned a technical Cache miss; it is not an access denial. Read scopes, paraphrases, receipt and manual-review hashes are preserved in body_observations.jsonl and access_receipts/; full article bodies are not stored.

Fourteen candidate records remain: the Kediri action is a provisional proposed include; 13 other leads are pending additional evidence. They concern health workers, nightlife workers, small businesses, labor groups, Papuan students, social-assistance activists, local youth, students and farmers. Search-only discovery does not settle physical turnout, exact event dates, state targets, event ownership or continuity. Five additional items are retained as provisional exclusions, including foreign-government targets, an out-of-country gathering and an awareness action without an evidenced state demand. The Banda Aceh action is one additional source-verified below-threshold exclusion. No confirmed continuation is assigned; the GAH report mentions earlier actions without exact dates.

The search surface does not reproduce LexisNexis and includes date and relevance noise. Geographic, sectoral and archive coverage remains uneven; failed or noisy searches do not establish that no other events occurred. Priority follow-up is independent reporting for the proposed Kediri event, attendance and own-state demands for the remaining candidates, pre/post-month continuity checks, and event-specific verification of protester violence and each state-response dimension. UBL, North Sumatra SPN, student interpellation and Pancoran results remain unresolved date/boundary leads rather than February-coded events.

All candidate records remain pending coordinator review; the proposed include is not an official coding decision. The status counts below cover this dossier only.
