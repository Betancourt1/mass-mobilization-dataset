Angola 2025-04 — first open-web pass complete; pending independent review

Scope and method
This dossier covers Angola in April 2025 only. The pass used pooled public web searches in Portuguese and English, including country-month and event-specific phrasing, local and international news, student/labour/strike terms, and retrospective follow-up. Every actual search invocation is captured once with the complete ordered visible header list; batched result-to-query partitions were not exposed and were not inferred. Exact returned strings and request inputs remain in private_reading/search. Search-result excerpts, including incidental long DW text in a search response, are leads only and were not used as verified body evidence. Search access does not reproduce LexisNexis; neither exhaustive coverage nor absence is claimed.

Finding
One April 26 Luanda MEA student gathering/march is retained as a proposed-inclusion candidate, angola_2025-04_E001. Opened Primeiro Impacto reports the event and an MEA-attributed 150 detentions, not a crowd count. Opened Folha 8 says more than 50 MEA members were gathering, supporting the threshold as an attributed lower-bound claim; it is not an exact maximum and remains subject to human review. Folha 8 also reports public-school demands, criticism of the Angolan government, arrests and alleged beatings. RTP’s April 24 article is an advance notice with the planned place/time/route; it is not execution evidence. The candidate records arrests and beatings as reported, while participant violence, dispersal, shootings, killings, event end and official police response remain unknown. The education demand is left unmapped because the supplied category list has no education-specific category.
