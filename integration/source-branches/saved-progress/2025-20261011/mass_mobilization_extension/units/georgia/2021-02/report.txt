Georgia February 2021 research report
Unit: georgia_2021-02
Fixed event cutoff: 2021-02-01 through 2021-02-28; country-month only.

Method and coverage
I ran 24 actual web-search actions in English, Georgian, and Russian, covering national opposition activity, pandemic restrictions, environmental opposition, labor, students, and local actions. The durable search log contains 676 exact ordered result headers with original refs and captured clocks. A complete comparison to the registered rollout matched every actual action by original event ID, exact query batch, returned URL array, and ordered headers. Q024 is a late-recovered actual search: its original 11:34:15 UTC clock and response are retained; it was assigned the next unused ID rather than renumbering earlier records. The open-web search is targeted, not systematic LexisNexis research. Local archives, language variants, and event-to-event continuity remain incomplete.

The dossier contains 105 source cards. Search-only cards are leads, not body evidence or independent corroboration. Four source bodies were successfully returned and reviewed: HRC’s 19-page monitoring PDF (partial review through PDF page 5 and part of page 6 / tool lines L0–L186), Georgia Today on Tkibuli miners (lines L20–41), Civil Georgia on the February 19 Parliament tent confrontation (lines L33–49), and Civil Georgia on the February 28 Kutaisi rally (lines L33–72). Three news articles were reviewed; one additional successful read was a human-rights monitoring PDF. One exact Publika body request returned a reader Cache miss. A separate On.ge open call used a malformed encoded URL and returned no page; a later exact receipt was stopped when the immediate full-input recheck caught a live source hash change in Honduras. These tool outcomes are retained as access failures/preflights, not site denials. No body text was copied into the dossier.

Candidate events
GEO202102-E001 — Parliament protest-camp rallies, February 19–20. HRC and Civil Georgia establish the tent action and report about 20 initial detentions plus five later detentions. Detentions do not establish attendance. The >=50 threshold, exact participant demand, organizer, physical end, and whether February 19–20 were a continuous event remain unknown. Civil Georgia’s “physical confrontation” is not attributed to protesters. Eligibility remains pending_threshold; all unsupported response and violence fields remain unknown.

GEO202102-E002 — February 28 rally in downtown Kutaisi against the Namakhvani HPP project. Civil Georgia reports “thousands,” a government-directed demand to revoke the project permission, and related calls for ENKA to leave the Rioni Gorge and for ownership/construction orders to be annulled. This supports the threshold qualitatively and the land/resource demand category; no exact crowd count, physical end, or event-specific state response is reported in the reviewed passage. Eligibility is proposed_include for independent review only; the record is not human-approved or an official MM event. Keep this rally separate from the continuing camp.

Continuation and exclusion
C001 records the pre-February Rioni Valley camp/vigil. HRC’s day-106 reference on February 8 and Civil Georgia’s report of rotating camp shifts for over 100 days by February 28 establish continued campaign activity, not an exact camp start/end or uninterrupted occupation. The February 7 HRC count of about 500 remains attached only to that gathering; it is not reused as the February 28 Kutaisi rally count.

X001 excludes the February 11 Tkibuli miners’ underground refusal over January wages. Georgia Today reports miners refusing to leave their workplace over an employer wage decision and describes a company commission. The article gives no worker count and does not establish a public assembly or own-state demand. Employer action is not state accommodation; keep private Saknakhshiri pay demands and underground refusal separate from public gathering activity.

Open leads and unresolved gaps
Search-only leads include the February 1 student-status protest at the Government Administration (Publika); an On.ge student-status action with an unclear date; February 6–7 curfew-defiance activity in Tbilisi and Batumi; a February 21 Social Explosions restrictions action; a three-city fuel-price protest; Adjara and Kakheti local actions; Dream City/Batumi housing; a Batumi opposition rally; and post-raid/February 26 opposition activity. A News Georgia search header dates a separate Tbilisi tuition-fee student protest to January 27, so it is retained as an adjacent-month lead, not a February event. Each remains a lead until event date, public assembly, threshold, state target, boundaries, and response are verified from exact article bodies.

The unit is a research handoff, not a complete national event census. Search coverage and access remain open-web limited, the official baseline gate is pending, and neither candidate has final coordinator review. The visible candidate records preserve unknown values, distinguish attendance from arrests and workers, and retain source provenance and access history for the next review.
