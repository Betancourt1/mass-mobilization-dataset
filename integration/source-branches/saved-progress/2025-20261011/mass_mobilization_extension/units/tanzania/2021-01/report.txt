It describes complaints about the stand’s condition, space, use, and fees, and a request to city leadership. It does not say that a public group physically assembled or give a human turnout count. The figure of more than 3,000 refers to vehicles in a fee calculation, not participants. Its end, response fields, and demand-category mapping remain unresolved. No inclusion is proposed.
