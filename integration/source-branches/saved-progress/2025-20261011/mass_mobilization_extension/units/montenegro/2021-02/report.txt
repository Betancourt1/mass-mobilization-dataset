Montenegro, 2021-02 — open-web research report (pending review)

Scope and method
The fixed event cutoff is 2026-10-07. This pass recorded 18 actual web-search responses, containing 27 constituent query strings and 384 ordered result headers. Searches covered English and Montenegrin/Serbian Latin and Cyrillic terms, date windows, Tuzi/Podgorica and other localities, and labor and pensioner leads. Each response was captured from the registered rollout and compared against the original action metadata; no search was rerun for bookkeeping. Open-web indexing is not equivalent to LexisNexis or a complete local press archive, so the searches do not establish exhaustive coverage.

Candidate event
E001 is the Tuzi residents’ 25 February road block opposing additional COVID-19 measures that closed local hospitality venues. RFE/RL (S002), Antena M (S003), and Anadolu Agency (S006) describe residents gathering with Mayor Nik Đeljošaj and blocking the road. Antena M’s read scope covers the morning action and the mayor’s request for talks; RFE/RL reports that residents withdrew and traffic normalized late that afternoon. No source provides a headcount or a bounded estimate that establishes at least 50 participants, so the candidate remains pending threshold. The reports come from three outlets, but independent reporting has not been established; Anadolu’s public page exposes only an excerpt.

The reports support a demand concerning Montenegro’s own health restrictions. The mayor’s claim that Tuzi was being discriminated against or retaliated against is attributed and unverified. RFE/RL reports a meeting with the deputy prime minister and health minister, coded as accommodation because dialogue occurred; no policy concession or meeting outcome is established. Three reported arrests concern hospitality operators, not identified demonstrators. Arrests of demonstrators, injuries, beatings, forceful dispersal, protester violence, shootings, and killings remain unknown.

Other leads
Two notice-only labor leads remain unresolved rather than treated as completed protests: X001, former Vektra Boka workers’ proposed 11 February gathering, and X002, a proposed Bauxite Mine workers’ gathering on 25 February. Search-result excerpts indicate postponement or non-permission, but do not establish whether either group later assembled; proposed headcounts are not turnout.

C001 continues the former KAP workers’ severance-law campaign first reported in September 2020. February 2, 5, 12, 19, and 26 entries come from an advance-notification register. Occurrence and attendance are unverified; an ambiguous table string is not interpreted as a participant count. The January dossier records the same episode key and is not used to transfer turnout between dates.

Access and remaining gaps
The dossier contains 22 source cards: three with scoped page content and 19 search-only. S002 and S003 were read within recorded visible-page scopes; S006 is excerpt-limited. Two exact-URL attempts for S004 returned technical Cache miss errors with no article body. These are not observed publisher denials. Five dependent requests were aborted or not issued because prerequisites or phase timing did not hold; their no-request records are retained. The receipt manifest inventories durable receipts, notes, outcomes, and no-request records.

Seven gaps remain, including the E001 turnout threshold and event-specific coercion/violence attribution, source independence and partial access, KAP occurrence/threshold, follow-up for both proposed labor gatherings, uneven language/archive coverage, and official baseline/manual compatibility. These open-web findings are research candidates only; eligibility review, official-baseline verification, and human/linguistic review remain pending. No absence claim follows from unlocated events.
