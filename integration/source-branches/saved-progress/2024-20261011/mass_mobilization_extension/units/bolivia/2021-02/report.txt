Bolivia — February 2021 research dossier

Scope and status
This is an open-web candidate dossier for Bolivia (ccode 145), 1–28 February 2021. It is not an official dataset update. The official baseline and country-specific inclusion mapping are pending, and all candidate rows remain subject to coordinator review. Research covered 27 captured search actions and 685 ordered result headers; the results were used for discovery, not as standalone event proof. Thirty-six source cards are saved: 16 have recorded body reads and 20 remain search-only. Searches used Spanish and English, with Aymara- and Quechua-name queries; regional coverage is selective and not exhaustive.

The initial Q020 capture failed after the search response because TextEncoder was unavailable. The original exact query array, 25 ordered headers and URLs, and start/completion clocks were recovered from rollout event exec-ccc060bd-ce8c-46aa-9464-e1e1dcf3f988 without repeating the query. Partial Q001, Q019, Q021–Q023, Q025 and Q027 header captures were also restored from their original search actions. Capture history is retained in search_log.jsonl and G001.

Candidate register

- E001 — 22 February, La Paz: a march from Adepcoca/Villa Fátima toward Plaza Murillo by coca growers, medical workers and urban teachers against Law 1359 and related demands. ERBOL reports police tear gas and clearing, and says protesters threw objects; the latter is a single-source attribution. No crowd count is available. Pending threshold.
- E002 — 23 February, central La Paz: a separately dated coalition mobilization against the health law. ANF reports police gas dispersal and relays a Unitel-attributed officer injury report, but the injury is not assigned to a particular gathering. No crowd count. Pending threshold.
- E003 — 23 February, central La Paz: Normal Simón Bolívar applicants protested alleged admissions irregularities. ANF treats this as distinct from the health-law coalition action. No count; whether the grievance fits the domestic state-target rule needs review. Pending threshold and state-target review.
- E004 — 24 February, Cochabamba, Santa Cruz and Oruro: health-worker marches against the law. Los Tiempos reports no per-city attendance; its 95% Oruro figure is strike adherence, not crowd size. Multi-location boundaries are unresolved; do not pool. Pending threshold, with effective unit review pending.
- E005 — 25 February, several unspecified cities: health workers’ “blockade of the thousand corners.” The article gives no site list or gathering count. Pending threshold, with effective unit review pending.
- E006 — 26 February, La Paz: doctors marched to the Health Ministry. Los Tiempos describes “some hundreds,” qualitatively above 50, but provides no exact count or interval. Proposed include pending coordinator review; physical end is unknown.
- E007 — 26 February, Cochabamba: a health-sector march with no attendance count. Do not transfer the La Paz estimate to this gathering. Pending threshold; physical end is unknown.
- E008 — from 22 February, soy-producer road blockades in Santa Cruz: sources name Pailón and other producer municipalities, but give no simultaneous crowd count. On 26 February Red Uno reports partial lifts while the Friendship Bridge remained blocked. A 25 February eju.tv report credited to Unitel records an intention to lift that evening, not an observed closure. Pending threshold; physical end is unknown. Government contact is reported, but a policy concession is not verified.

The captured same-day end_date fields for E001–E007 preserve their original date coding; the opened sources do not establish physical cessation. Apply an effective end of unknown pending coordinator review. Each candidate has a distinct episode_key so shared issue, strike call or date does not merge separate physical gatherings.

Other records and cross-month boundary
X001 records the nationally announced 1 February medical strike as cancelled after an agreement; it does not establish a public assembly. Sirmes Oruro separately announced a local 1 February strike, but execution remains unknown. The relevant January item is bolivia_2021-01_E010, a strike announcement, and its ownership remains under review. A prior draft called it C001; that label was wrong because January C001 is the unrelated Vinto landfill closure blockade. February C002 remains the record for the 1 March announced strike/march call. Law 1359 was promulgated on 17 February; a new health-sector strike call from 18 February precedes documented marches from 22 February. The first ten-day strike’s announced final day was 28 February. C002 records the 1 March call as an announcement only: its original date fields are retained in original_boundary_fields, while the physical assembly start is unknown. Neither the January sequence nor the 1 March call proves a continuous episode or a February street-action end.

Evidence and limits
Opened reporting supports the specific dates, locations and demands above. Reports do not supply attendance counts for most gatherings, so thresholds remain pending. Police dispersal is coded only where reported; violence and injuries remain source-attributed and uncorroborated where noted. State-response fields stay unknown when the sources do not establish the response. E003’s domestic-state target and E008’s accommodation coding require review; talks/contact do not establish a concession.

The evidence base includes opened ERBOL, ANF, Los Tiempos, Red Uno, Defensoría, La Patria, Tarija200 and Correo del Sur articles. Search headers alone were not treated as body evidence. The source cards had previously referenced check1 receipt IDs for S017, S018 and S019, but no matching receipt JSON was retrievable from this unit’s access_receipts directory or the targeted temporary-file scan; whether those original artifacts ever persisted is unknown. The IDs and this limitation are retained in each card’s missing_receipt_artifacts history. Only existing check2+ receipts are now cited as active references, and they do not retroactively establish authorization for earlier body reads. Q004–Q007 date filters were unreliable and returned stale/off-window material. S032 remained search-only; S034 republishes El Deber, S036 republishes Unitel, and S031 repeats a URL used in the January dossier. Do not count those as independent confirmations. G002–G008 list the substantive unresolved thresholds, event boundaries, regional coverage and source-lineage checks.
