Macedonia — January 2021 research report
Status: researched_open_web_pending_review
Cutoff: 2026-10-07 inclusive (CDMX). Research completed: 2026-10-08 16:54 UTC.
This is an experimental evidence unit, not an official MM update.

Method and coverage
A bounded open-web first pass used 39 English, Macedonian and Albanian queries. It included broad searches; weekly windows for Jan 1–7, 8–14, 15–21, 22–28 and 29–31; follow-up queries for Monstra, students, health workers, accountants, matura and the Tursko Rudari mini-hydropower project; city leads including Skopje, Tetovo, Kumanovo, Bitola and Probistip; and local news, official policy/traffic notices, Helsinki Committee reports and CIVICUS Monitor. The eight weekly/late-month searches do not provide equivalent LexisNexis coverage. The search tool pooled returned URLs across some multi-query calls, so no per-query attribution or invented result totals are supplied. Exact execution times not captured for later batches are recorded with recorded_at rather than estimated.

Sources are 37 URL cards, not 37 independent confirmations. The Albanian and Macedonian January reports and March retrospective are from the same Helsinki Committee. MIA-origin stories republished by Kanal 5 are labeled. Searches and opened items were checked for event date versus publication date, attendance versus workforce/class totals, and announcement versus execution. Web-reader cache misses and initial sandbox DNS errors were not treated as publisher denials. The same public URLs were read only over ordinary certificate-verified HTTPS where authorized. Republika links S015/S018 returned HTTP404; Sloboden Pechat S017 and the Alsat traffic notice S037 returned HTTP403. The Jan20 Kanal5/MIA body had already been opened in the web reader, although the coordinator later received HTTP403 from an ordinary HTTPS request. Telegrami S036 yielded search-result text only; the first HTTPS 200 response was not text-extracted, and a later request returned HTTP404. No bypass was attempted and no full protected article was stored.

Candidate review
- E001, Jan29 Monstra-case march in Skopje: proposed_include, pending review. Portalb reports “several thousand”; Euronews Albania reports “hundreds”; the Helsinki Committee does not give a count. Preserve qualitative disagreement; both reported estimates exceed 50, but there is no exact count or numeric interval. The march sought prosecutor Fatime Fetai’s dismissal and a different judicial/prosecutorial outcome, alongside appeals to international actors. Portalb explicitly says the protest ended and participants departed at about 14:30; police then withdrew armored vehicles. Police cordon and shock bombs support crowd_dispersal=yes; some participants throwing stones/objects and reported property damage support protester_violence=yes. Shock bombs are not coded as shootings. Arrests, beatings, accommodation, ignore, killings and shootings remain unknown. A Helsinki Committee reference to several hours of road blockage is not converted to a duration.
- E002, Jan20 Philosophy students outside the Education Ministry: pending_threshold. MIA-attributed Kanal5 reporting gives the date, place and demand to withdraw the draft primary-education concept and hold public debate; no crowd estimate or physical end.
- E003, Jan26 health workers at 8 September Hospital: pending_threshold. CIVICUS reports a protest over promised COVID reimbursements/bonuses; the linked Republika page returned 404, and no attendance estimate was recovered.
- E004, Jan26 accountants outside Government: pending_threshold. Open 24.mk and CIVICUS confirm a protest seeking deadline extensions and a new accounting-work law; no crowd count or physical end. An unanswered letter from the prior week is not coded as protest-time ignore.
- E005, Jan27 Tursko Rudari residents’ road block over a mini-hydro project: pending_state_target. Open MIA-attributed Kanal5 reporting confirms the physical action and describes it as peaceful. Attendance is unknown; the direct government addressee is not explicit. The mayor’s permit information and a company interaction are not coded as state accommodation.
- E006, high-school examination-related public-action lead: pending_evidence, start/end null. A Jan27 search result mentions partial street protests in Skopje and Kumanovo. CIVICUS discusses student street activity and an egg-throwing incident at the Education Ministry without dating them. It is unresolved whether these were one or multiple events, whether any began in January, and whether they relate to the matura class boycott. Counts of boycotting students or cities are not crowd counts.
- E007, electricity/VAT protest at the Government courtyard: pending_evidence, start/end null. Telegrami search-result metadata dates the article to Jan14 and its headline/excerpt reports a protest over reducing VAT on electricity bills and police removal of protesters. The article body was not obtained, so publication date is not used as physical start and response remains unknown.

Excluded and episode boundary
X001 records the Jan21 Kumanovo online-class boycott over reducing state-matura subjects. It is nonattendance, not an established public assembly; school participation is not crowd size. Possible street actions are retained separately as E006 pending evidence.
C001 records the Feb26 Monstra protest as a subsequent-month separate-episode boundary. CIVICUS and the Helsinki Committee distinguish that post-verdict protest and its clashes/arrests from Jan29. No Feb26 response is transferred to E001. No prior-month physical continuation into January was established in this first pass.

Open gaps
Ten gap records cover threshold evidence, response attribution, physical end, student event dates and episode boundaries, state-target classification for Tursko Rudari, source access/language limitations, and open-web versus LexisNexis and official-baseline limitations. The candidate set is not exhaustive; failure to find or open an item is not evidence that no event occurred.
