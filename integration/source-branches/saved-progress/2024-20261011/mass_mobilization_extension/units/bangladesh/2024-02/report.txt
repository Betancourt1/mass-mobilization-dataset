Bangladesh — February 2024

Scope and methods: This is a pending-review open-web dossier, not an official MM update. Fifteen captured actual English/Bangla general, weekly, local, regional, sector and targeted searches produced 214 rendered headers. Native request clocks, result totals and refs were not exposed; no counts or clocks are inferred. Search results are discovery only unless the relevant body was read under a fresh exact-URL gate and manual source review.

Candidate E001 is the 28 February Ganatantra Mancha rally at the National Press Club followed by a march toward the Secretariat, stopped near Zero Point. Dhaka Tribune and The Daily Star body reports substantiate the event, domestic price/governance demands, police baton charges and unspecified detentions. Neither establishes total attendance. Injury claims differ and police disputed the higher claim; injuries are not attendance. Eligibility remains pending the 50-person threshold. The Financial Express body was opened after a stable gate/manual review but explicitly credited bdnews24.com; after that origin was discovered, the source was marked dependent, its estimates withheld, and it was excluded from independent corroboration. A metadata-only gate was run after updating that source card; it is not retroactive authorization.

Candidate E002 is a 25 February Viqarunnisa student/guardian protest lead from a search excerpt about school sexual harassment. The state target and attendance are unresolved; no body was opened. No verified continuations or exclusions were recorded.

Gaps and limits: Three source-access states are opened and one remains search-only. English/Bangla general, weekly, local, regional and sector queries found these leads but do not exhaust coverage. Systematic newspaper archives/LexisNexis, independent Bangla review and official dataset version/universe/schema/use terms remain unavailable or pending. No claim is made that no other protests occurred.
