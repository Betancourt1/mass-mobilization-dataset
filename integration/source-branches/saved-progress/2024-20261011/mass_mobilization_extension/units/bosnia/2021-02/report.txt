Candidate findings and boundaries

E001 — Kozarac residents’ February 8 public protest. RSE and RTRS report the gathering and demand to move the Banja Luka–Prijedor route; RTRS estimates around one hundred attendees. RSE’s March 3 report explicitly identifies the February 8 protest and says the route would be shifted because of residents’ complaints. Accommodation is coded yes as an official response announcement; actual implementation is not verified. Arrests, beatings, dispersal, ignore, killings and shootings remain unknown. The physical end is unknown. RSE also mentions earlier protests several months before February; their dates, ownership and relation to February 8 remain unresolved, so the February event is not merged with that older campaign.

E002 — Breza residents’ February 15 gathering outside RMU Breza. RSE reports more than one hundred residents gathered in support of the miners; 101 is recorded only as a derived integer lower bound. The crowd’s own state target is unclear, so eligibility remains pending_state_target. The article’s separate report that the miners’ agreement ended the workplace strike is not transferred to the residents’ gathering; all seven response fields remain unknown. The gathering has no reported physical end.

The mining actions are retained separately as X001–X005 scope/execution boundaries. RSE dates Zenica’s initial production stoppage to February 8; Zenica and Breza general strikes and one-hour warning strikes at five other mines began February 12. The opened reporting does not establish a public assembly or turnout for those workplace actions. The 20 miners reported in a hunger strike are a subset, not crowd attendance. A proposed Sarajevo protest outside the Federation Government was conditional on the dispute remaining unresolved. Its execution is unverified; the February 15 agreement does not prove cancellation, and the Breza gathering does not establish a Sarajevo event.

E003 — APF union representatives’ February 15 gathering outside the Federation Government in Sarajevo. Opened reports establish a public gathering and a direct request for government action over the blocked agency account, unpaid wages/contributions, health insurance and operating resources. Attendance is not reported, so eligibility remains pending_threshold. The 25 employees and 25 affected families are not turnout. The separate APF warning strike is X006; one report calls February 15 its fifth day and describes a planned 30-day duration, but neither statement establishes exact onset or actual end. Response fields for the gathering remain unknown.

C002 — The HNK health-workers’ strike that began December 10 appears in a February 4 report as unresolved. Its February 8 negotiation deadline is not an end date. This is recorded as a prior-onset labor-campaign continuation; the report does not establish a February public assembly. The Lipa January protest is not carried into February because the January dossier records its food refusal ending January 5 and this month’s searches produced no February continuation evidence.
