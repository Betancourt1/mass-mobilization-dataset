Armenia — March 2024 open-web research

Methods and coverage: 14 actual queries were preserved with exact invocation objects and exact rendered outputs. Queries used English, Armenian and Russian generic protest/demo/strike/march terms, half-month windows, refugee-aid and regional/sector searches, and episode-specific before/after searches. Parsed result headers were checked against preserved outputs; all parsed headers matched, and two empty local-outlet searches retained the actual empty-result message. Native event IDs, refs, clocks, order and result totals are unavailable and remain null. March-specific searches often returned later April–June protests. Open web differs from LexisNexis and does not establish exhaustive coverage.

Findings: One March 20 rally candidate is proposed for review. An opened contemporaneous Azatutyun/RFE-RL article states that hundreds of Nagorno-Karabakh refugees demonstrated at Liberty Square in Yerevan, organized by Karabakh civic groups and public figures. They demanded expanded Armenian government aid, higher/non-repayable housing support, continuation of rent/utility compensation, and international security guarantees for collective return. “Hundreds” clearly clears the 50-person threshold qualitatively; no exact number is assigned. Demands are provisionally coded as political_behavior_process. The article does not report the rally’s end, protester violence or state response, which remain unknown.

A photo-caption result, March 27 context-story result, and March 21/22 Aravot.am and Oragark search results may relate to the same rally but remain search-only and are not counted as independent corroboration. The Aravot and Oragark results mention housing-funding demands; written origins and independence are unresolved. “Azatutyun / RFE/RL Armenian Service” is the publisher/service label; the opened report’s underlying written origin is unknown. No later or earlier continuous action was confirmed. Another search result described a three-person armed attempt to storm a police station on March 24; it is retained as an excluded non-gathering lead, not a protest candidate.

Sources and gaps: Six source cards, one opened article and five search-only results. Gaps cover response, episode boundaries, source independence, additional geography/sectors and the open-web/LexisNexis difference.

Baseline status remains pending official baseline. Candidate remains pending independent human review; no official observation or ID has been assigned.
