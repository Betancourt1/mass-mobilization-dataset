Lithuania — January 2021 (ccode 368, Europe)

Scope and method

This is a bounded first-pass open-web search for public protests with at least 50 people and demands directed at the Lithuanian state or its policy. The assigned month is January 1–31, 2021; the research cutoff is 2026-10-07 inclusive, America/Mexico_City. Search was performed on 2026-10-08. The dossier contains 73 query records, covering broad English and Lithuanian terms, four weekly Lithuanian date windows, Vilnius/Kaunas/Klaipėda and regional leads, COVID restrictions, labor, teachers, health workers, students, farmers, transport workers, unions, and Russian, Polish, and Belarusian-language searches. Follow-up included January 12 Lukiškės candle-lighting, official and local memorial material, and December/February boundaries. Literal Q053–Q073 strings were recovered from read-only actual worker tool inputs; each record preserves its call ID, recorded tool-call boundary, per-call input SHA-256, and recovery-file SHA-256. These boundary timestamps are not exact request execution times, so executed_at is null for the recovered records. Multi-query results were pooled and cannot be attributed query-by-query; no result totals or mapping are invented.

Findings

One physical-action lead is retained as LTU202101-E001: a retrospective account says people went to Lukiškės Square late Tuesday, January 12, to light candles. Eligibility is pending evidence. The account says “many people,” describes arrivals alone or in pairs, and lists several towns without separate concurrent counts. It does not establish 50 people at Vilnius or at any single location, and no photographs were counted. A January 9 call from Nacionalinis susivienijimas’s Vilnius branch asks for candles at Lukiškės Square, frames the square’s status as a political issue, and also asks people not to gather. That prospective appeal does not establish that the people later described attended because of the policy demand. No demand category is assigned; the link between the physical lead, attendance threshold and state target remains open. No end date is inferred from the last description. Do not treat the action automatically as a sit-in or as a protest solely because a political organization issued a call.

Five leads are retained in excluded.json: the January 23 Navalny solidarity picket outside the Russian Embassy (foreign-state target); January 13 local memorial/fire program leads; a separate home/window candle initiative (no qualifying public assembly or state demand established); the January 4 emergency-powers statement; and the judicial-reform statement (document dated January 12 on its page, described as distributed January 16 in the bulletin). Institution counts in the home/window report are not people counts. The Navalny picket was executed and lasted about an hour; sources report about 100, while 15min’s account internally varies between “several dozen” and “about one hundred.” LRT and TV3/BNS share BNS material and are not independent confirmations. The reported detention concerns a person described as disrupting the picket, not a documented response to its demand.

The State Data Agency page says no strikes were organized in 2021, while its detailed strike series covers 2015–2019; this narrows formal-strike leads only. It does not support an absence claim for demonstrations. A February 5 small-business protest and a March 20 anti-lockdown rally were checked as out-of-period events; no source links them to an event that began in January. Continuations are empty.

Sources and limits

Twenty-two source cards are retained: 12 opened and 10 search-only. They include contemporaneous reports, official context, organization statements, memorial material, event announcements, retrospective context and date-control leads. Some outlets and reports share BNS or Respublika material; no syndication is presented as independent corroboration. The January bulletin is retrospective and partisan. The official Seimas program, Vilniaus Diena announcement, Pro Patria report, and a local memorial logistics page remain search-only or inaccessible through the reader. Same-public-URL HTTPS recovery for two cache misses failed before HTTP because local DNS resolution failed; no protected content was bypassed. Ordinary opens succeeded for the official January 9 call and the January 2022 date-control page.

The open-web method does not reproduce LexisNexis or the historical newspaper selection. Results were not comprehensive; regional/local-language coverage and newspaper archives were uneven. The current official baseline and additional coding procedure remain pending. Attendance, demand attribution, the candidate’s end, and the January 13 memorial lead remain unresolved where evidence is absent. This dossier records a candidate for coordinator review; it makes no official update, absence coding, or publication proposal.
