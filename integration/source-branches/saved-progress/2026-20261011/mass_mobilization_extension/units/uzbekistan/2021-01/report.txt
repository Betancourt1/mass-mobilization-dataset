Research status: researched_open_web_pending_review. Open-web research has reached a documented first-pass checkpoint; all findings remain provisional and non-exhaustive pending review.

Methods and coverage
Twenty-five web searches and 204 source cards are saved (15 opened and 189 search-only). Search paths include Uzbek, Russian and English queries covering named incidents, utilities, workers and regions. The searches have surfaced reporting from Namangan, Kashkadarya, Fergana, Jizzakh, Khorezm and Surkhandarya, plus additional undated local road-blockade leads. Broad weekly searches cover Jan. 1–7, Jan. 8–14, Jan. 15–21 and Jan. 22–31; discovery remains incomplete across local outlets, regions, sectors and December–February physical continuity. The open web does not reproduce LexisNexis. The official dataset bytes/universe and additional Coding Procedure remain unresolved under the coordinator’s baseline gate. Uzbek and Russian materials have not received independent human language review.

Provisional January candidates
E001 — Namangan drivers at Chorsu Square, January 13. Ozodlik reports at least 50 drivers gathered near the city administration over methane-station queues. A driver described a spontaneous gathering with no formulated demands and said participants dispersed after one or two hours. The state-target question remains unresolved. Mediazona’s search result attributes its account to Ozodlik and is not independent corroboration. The reported January 10 queue death and January 11 vehicle fire are separate prior incidents, not responses to this gathering.

E002 — Qarshi residents opposing a city plan affecting Buyuk Turon parkland, January 13. The opened Oxus account describes about 200 residents, supporting the size threshold qualitatively without exact bounds. It reports opposition to a city-government plan for an industrial zone. Kun.uz’s ecology report concerns the site and project, not the protest. The exact gathering site, organizer, physical end and event-specific response remain unknown. A separate November 2020 Buyuk Turon action remains distinct pending episode review.

E003 — Altyaryk refinery workers’ repeated after-work actions, reported from January 10. Labour Mission’s headline describes hundreds; exact attendance bounds are unknown. Labour Mission is a distinct report but attributes the worker’s main protest account to Ozodlik, so it is not independent corroboration. Eurasianet separately interviewed a company spokesperson and a worker but explicitly attributes the protest chronology and footage to Ozodlik. RFE/RL retrospective S030 describes the action as lasting more than a week; that qualitative duration does not establish daily physical continuity or a precise final gathering date. The joint-venture ownership does not establish a demand to Uzbekistan’s government. Workforce totals are not turnout.

E004 — Paxtakor residents, mostly women, block a highway on January 11 over household gas and electricity interruptions. RFE/RL reports local officials met with the group and secured an immediate one-ton coal delivery; it also reports officials asked them to unblock the road. Tashkent Times reports officials promised gas provision within a week, whose fulfillment is unverified. Ozodlik later reports five five-day detentions and four warnings among nine people prosecuted; this is not an attendance count. One detainee alleged a beating in custody and regional police denied force, so the beating response remains unknown. Crowd size and demand-category fit remain unresolved.

E005 — Cholish residents block the Cholish–Urgench road on January 19 over gas and electricity shortages. Ozodlik reports no crowd count, organizer, addressee or physical end. One participant’s later statement that he and neighbors had not faced pressure does not establish absence of any state response. The 50-person threshold and demand-category fit remain unresolved.
