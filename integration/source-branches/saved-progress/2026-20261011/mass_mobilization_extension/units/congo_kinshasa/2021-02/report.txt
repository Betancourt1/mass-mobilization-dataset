Congo Kinshasa (DRC) — February 2021 first-pass research

Coverage

The pass logged 24 actual search invocations with 378 ordered result headers. Searches covered French national and regional reporting, weekly windows across February, the final January/early March boundaries, and probes in Lingala and Swahili; the targeted Lingala “botomboki” search returned no headers. Geographic queries included Kinshasa, Goma, Bukavu, Lubumbashi, Kisangani, Mbuji-Mayi, Tshikapa, Matadi, Beni, Kananga, Kindu and Gbadolite. Sector searches covered labor actions by teachers, health workers, university staff and transport workers, plus security/policing, governor and local-official disputes, fuel/cost issues and education. Direct bodies were not pursued for every search lead; LexisNexis was unavailable, so this is an open-web first pass rather than a completeness claim.

A provenance correction found ten receipt-backed historical attempts without a contemporaneous saved manual-review note: S001#1, S003#1, S007#1, S008#1, S016#1, S020#1, S021#1 and S023#1–#3. Their receipts remain preserved, but a receipt’s “manual_review” decision does not establish that the required human publisher/origin review was saved. No post-hoc note or retroactive authorization was created. The supplied body outputs from S003, S007, S016, S020 and S021 are quarantined as non-evidence. S001, S008 and S023 returned no article body, so those attempt outputs remain process history; separate search metadata remains discovery-only.

Six raw worker candidate IDs are preserved. The coordinator’s current decisions mark E001 (S016/S020) and E002 (S007) as historical discovery leads with effective eligibility pending_evidence; all event fields from their quarantined body summaries are cleared in the coordinator override. Do not use the raw events.json rows as operative evidence. E003, the Union Sacrée-affiliated Kananga march on 27 February, retains police and organizer estimates of 3,000 and about 5,000 and an explicit election-process demand; its later policy outcome is unknown. E004 (ECIDE, Kananga, Feb 10), E005 (judicial defenders, Kananga, Feb 25) and E006 (Lucha, Kananga, Feb 12) remain below/awaiting the attendance threshold because available compliant reporting gives no qualifying count.

For E006, InfosLive and Actualite.cd describe the same Lucha action in Kananga on 12 February, demanding the city mayor’s departure over insecurity. Actualite.cd reports two seriously injured militants, but no turnout; injuries do not establish crowd size. Its account contains conflicting claims about bayonet injuries and alleged stone-throwing. Police prevention of the mayor-banned march supports crowd_dispersal=yes; arrests, beatings, injury causation, protester violence and physical end remain unresolved. The separate Feb 10 ECIDE action and announced civic-forces march are not merged into E006.

Three exclusions are preserved: an out-of-period Goma transport strike in 2022; the February anti-Karega protest directed only at Rwanda’s ambassador; and a Matadi support-march lead whose title identifies no grievance or DRC-government demand. The Matadi classification remains provisional because its article body was inaccessible. These exclusions do not rely on any of the quarantined bodies.

Open gaps include the Zando lead’s occurrence, event date and threshold because S003’s body synopsis is quarantined and S001 returned no body; the M14/Gbadolite occurrence/date and a blocked Dépêche URL; the UOS Beni lead’s occurrence, date, publicness, state-policy target and turnout because S021’s body is quarantined; E001/E002’s unresolved event fields under coordinator quarantine; attendance and report lineage for the Feb 10 ECIDE and Feb 25 judicial-defender marches; the provisional Matadi lead; E006’s turnout and S013 relationship; search-only Lucha reporting later in February; a search-only youth-march announcement and report; and the March 9 Kananga Lucha sit-in follow-up. Search-only metadata remains discovery, not verified body evidence.

Boundary and source handling

The March 9 follow-up is outside February and is retained only as a possible continuity lead. The Les Dépêches de Brazzaville/Le Courrier de Kinshasa host presents a Congo-Brazzaville identity, while the result describes Kananga in the Democratic Republic of the Congo; these are distinct country contexts. AfricaPress publisher-wide holds were reviewed and respected: no AfricaPress body or same-story copy was requested. No third parties were contacted and no candidate was added to the integrated catalog. Official baseline reconciliation remains pending; all candidates require independent review.
