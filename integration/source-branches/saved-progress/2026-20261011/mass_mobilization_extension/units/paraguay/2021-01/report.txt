Paraguay, January 2021 — open-web research record

Scope and cutoff

This unit covers public mobilizations physically beginning in Paraguay from 1 through 31 January 2021. Research used the assigned provisional ccode 150 and the fixed inclusive cutoff of 7 October 2026 (America/Mexico_City). The official current Mass Mobilization files, universe, and supplementary Coding Procedure remain unverified; the baseline gate is pending.

Search method and coverage

The log contains 21 single-query web searches: broad English and Spanish discovery; Spanish windows for 1–7, 8–14, 15–21, and 22–31 January; a December-to-January continuity search; ABC Color; IPS resident doctors; Encarnación merchant follow-up; campesino/Indigenous issues; teachers, workers, transport, and municipal sectors; one limited Guarani-form search; regional-city searches; exact follow-up for the Itaipú and PLRA leads; a January–February boundary search; and an Agencia IP query. All visible returned title/URL/reference headers are stored in search_log.jsonl. No total-result counts or exhaustive coverage are claimed. Exact execution times for Q010–Q021 were not sampled and are null; Q001–Q009 have recorded timestamps.

Searches returned 27 relevant source cards: 21 opened, five search-only, and one explicitly blocked. The search-only items remain limited to their returned text. ABC Color’s Jan 20 Encarnación follow-up initially returned Cache miss; a default in-sandbox exact-URL request failed DNS (exit 1), then one ordinary HTTPS request to that same URL returned HTTP 200 (exit 0), reading a bounded 79,979 bytes. The article confirms a march and a decision to maintain a vigilia, but no attendee count, physical end, or state response. The same outlet/reporter supplied the preceding announcement, so the two are temporal stages, not independent outlet corroboration. The January 6 ABC article on the IPS strike was blocked by robots.txt and not retried. An older March campesino article returned Cache miss and a permitted exact-URL request, but its bounded preview did not expose body text. No persistent servers, watchers, containers, or other temporary resources were started; all local reads, writes, and validation processes were finite.

January candidate records

Six candidate records remain in events.json; none is approved as included. E001 is the confirmed 20 January march by Encarnación merchants and commerce employees for a subsidy bill; a sizable group is mentioned but no number proves the 50-person threshold, and the article says a bridge-access vigil was planned without documenting its duration or end. E002 is the 31 January PLRA supporters’ escrache at a TSJE minister’s residence; the source says eggs and toilet paper were thrown at the house, which is preserved as property-directed conduct, while violence coding, threshold, party-internal versus state-process target, end, and state response remain unresolved. E003 preserves the ABC Color-reported 4 January assembly and planned strike as pending_evidence: occurrence of the assembly is reported, but venue, public/open status, and attendance are not established. It remains separate from E004 while their relationship is unresolved and is not counted as an additional crowd or as an included event. E004 records actual IPS resident/contracted-doctor mobilizations outside Hospital Central on 3–5 January, with null physical start and pending_unit eligibility because the relation to pre-January staggered stoppages is unresolved. E005 records one Partido Paraguay Pyahura mobilization at two locations, Itaipú in Asunción and Plaza Libertad in Villarrica; the article’s 28 January header conflicts with its “yesterday Thursday” wording, so date precision is month, and turnout is unreported. E006 is a separate announced Itaipú 2023 Causa Nacional action at Mburuvicha Róga; no source reviewed confirms execution, so it remains pending_evidence.

All six event records have unknown state-response dimensions unless the evidence states otherwise; silence is not coded as a negative response. No reviewed candidate has reported turnout sufficient to establish 50 participants. Search-only text is not used as proof of execution.

Continuations, unresolved leads, and exclusions

C001 preserves the older IPS staggered-stoppage context already reported on 31 December. It does not claim continuous physical presence from December through the January 3–5 gatherings; C001 and E004 remain linked by an unresolved episode-boundary question. The distinct 4 January assembly/strike-plan report is retained in events.json as E003 with pending_evidence status, not as an included event or a definite exclusion: public access and attendance are unproven, and its relation to E004 and C001 is unknown. X001 is the sole definite exclusion: a CIVICUS account describes armed-civilian threats/attacks on Rosarino families, not a public gathering, and contains an unresolved 9 January 2020/2021 date conflict.

Boundary searches found separately announced February actions and later border-worker blockades in Encarnación; no evidence reviewed establishes physical continuity from the January actions. Similar sector, city, or demand alone is not used to merge episodes.

Limitations and next review

Open-web searching does not reproduce the original LexisNexis frame or provide exhaustive newspaper coverage. The official baseline and supplemental Coding Procedure remain pending. Turnout, physical end, and event-specific state response are frequently missing; E001, E002, and E005 remain pending_threshold, E003 and E006 pending_evidence, and E004 pending_unit. For any continuation, review exact episode boundaries before assigning monthly ownership. Review all event and unresolved-lead records against the verified original codebook and sources before coding.
