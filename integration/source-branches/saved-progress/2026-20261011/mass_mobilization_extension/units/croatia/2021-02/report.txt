Candidate decisions

Opened Index text reports “thousands,” fair-compensation/economic-policy demands and a call for Economy Minister Ćorić to resign. The count is qualitative and has no numeric bounds. The crowd beginning to leave shortly after noon is not a verified end. Two additional source previews are retained as search-only and not treated as independent body confirmation. Proposed inclusion is pending official baseline and coordinator review.

Organizer, physical end, protester violence and event-specific state response remain unknown. Proposed inclusion is pending baseline and coordinator review.

HR202102-E003: the February20 Brodotrogir workers’ protest lead. Search previews report unpaid-wage demands and about200 participants; the attendance report is HINA-attributed and no source body was retrieved. The record remains pending_evidence, and its relation to January’s unresolved Brodotrogir candidate is not established.

Student gatherings at Sava/Jarun are described by search headlines as parties, while an Index headline suggests possible protest symbolism; both bodies remain unread. A Hazud announcement calls for a February25 gathering but does not prove it occurred. These are explicit leads/exclusions with gaps, not settled absence findings.
