Montenegro, 2021-01 — open-web research report (pending review)

Scope and method
I searched English and Montenegrin/Serbian in Latin and Cyrillic, using month-wide and weekly date windows, city names, labor-sector terms, general protest terms and adjacent-month follow-up. The retained search log contains 39 literal records: 31 web searches and eight text searches within the opened monitoring report. A pre-compaction query block was conducted but its literal lines and raw mapping were not retained, and was not reconstructed. Pooled outputs do not map each URL to an individual query; exact result totals and execution timestamps were not exposed. This was open-web research, not LexisNexis or an equivalent archive. For sources marked opened, the exact access request time was not captured, so accessed_at is null; search-log recorded_at values are save times only. No persistent browser or server process was started.

Candidate
E001 is a Jan17 Bar Pamti anniversary gathering at the Bar gymnasium courtyard, with a reported demand about relocating the kindergarten and restoring the schoolyard. Dnevne's search-result text gives approximate attendance of about fifty; it is not an exact count or a lower bound. The substantive contemporary Dnevne and Vijesti pages could not be opened by this worker. The opened Institut Alternativa report says RTCG's Jan17 report used images from the previous year's assembly; those images are not evidence of 2021 attendance. E001 remains pending_evidence, and all seven state-response fields remain unknown. The reported ministerial meeting and promise are preserved as unverified search-result claims.

Excluded items and unresolved first-pass lead
The Jan18 Podgorica musicians' action is excluded because opened contemporary Vijesti reports an organizer estimate of 25 and the opened Institut annex lists up to 25, below threshold, despite a demand about the domestic COVID live-music measure. Jan6 Pljevlja was an Orthodox observance whose notice said the public would not be invited and identifies no protest demand. Jan23 and Jan31 Navalny solidarity gatherings targeted Russian authorities, not Montenegro; Anadolu reports about twenty for Jan31 while a local search-only result says several dozen. Four records are categorical exclusions. The Jan28 Cetinje report is the fifth record in excluded.json, retained as an unresolved, unqualified first-pass lead for follow-up, not as a categorical exclusion: Dan’s search result says “small group” but gives no count or clear policy demand. This wording does not establish attendance below 50, and the search-only result does not establish or disprove that an assembly occurred; the article could not be opened because the request returned Cache miss.

Continuations and unresolved leads
C001 preserves the KAP pensioners' recurring severance-law campaign from at least Sep4, 2020. Its Jan22 and Jan29 entries in the Institut annex are advance notifications, not confirmed attendance; no January turnout is assigned. Former Bauxite workers' January coverage suggests negotiation or a gathering whose authorization was refused, but no public action is verified. Education-union sources document labor demands and dialogue, not a January public assembly.

Coverage limits
Institut Alternativa reports 20 public assemblies in January in aggregate, 15 unannounced and two simultaneous. Those counts do not identify eligible MM events, and its annex lists advance notices rather than confirmed occurrences. Its media analysis also identifies prior-year images in RTCG's Jan17 Bar report. Searches spanned several cities and sectors, but unannounced events, local archive gaps, access limits and episode boundaries remain unresolved. “Not found” in this pass does not mean “did not occur.” The official baseline and the additional original Coding Procedure remain pending.
