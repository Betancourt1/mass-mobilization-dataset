Findings
Two contemporary Odienné reports describe secondary-school disruption on Tuesday 23 February and an initial Monday pupil action tied to classroom shortage at Lycée 2 (CIV202102-S001 and S002). Both mention disorder spreading among schools; Abidjanpeople describes stone throwing, pupils in the streets and police containment, while its quoted principal says the classroom issue had been resolved Monday. Neither gives an attendee count or clearly says the complaint was directed to the national government or a state policy. The KOACI article also contains a conflicting Toussaint-holiday sentence. Keep likely Monday 22 February as a discovery lead only; do not assign canonical onset or February ownership until the contradiction is clarified. Do not infer >=50 turnout from enrollment, school closure or “majority” language.
