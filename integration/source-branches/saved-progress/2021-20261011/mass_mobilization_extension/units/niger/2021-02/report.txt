Scope and method
This first-pass research covers Niger in February 2021 under the fixed 2026-10-07 cutoff and the May 2015 Clark–Regan manual available in baseline/mm_codebook.txt. Search discovery is logged in search_log.jsonl: 18 captured search actions/batches and 485 ordered result headers, including the four-query CAPTURE-0018 batch. Queries used French and English terms and examined local/regional reporting, international news, official, election-observer, and human-rights sources. Search headers/snippets are discovery evidence only. This open-web work is not equivalent to LexisNexis, systematic newspaper archives, or complete local-language/radio coverage; no completeness claim is made.

Candidate and evidence status
One candidate is retained: niger_2021-02_E001, post-runoff election-result protests reported from 23 February in Niamey and other towns. The initial onset and multi-location framing come from prior-unit consultations S001–S003; their English/French Amnesty versions are one release, not independent corroboration. One current-unit body read, S005, is a Reuters dispatch republished by La Tribune. It reports Ousmane supporters demonstrating in Niamey on 25 February for a third consecutive day, consistent with (but not itself stating) a 23 February start. It describes small groups without a count, so the >=50 threshold remains unresolved. It reports stones thrown at police and fires attributed to some protesters; police used tear gas, and the article mentions multiple arrests after prior-day demonstrations without giving a count or specific gathering assignment. S005 connects the action to the result dispute but does not document a demand made by a defined gathering. Search-only S076–S077 snippets describe campaign-level result-suspension statements; their bodies are unread and no gathering link is established. Local-versus-national boundaries, organizers, physical end, full location list, and other event-level responses remain unknown. The candidate remains pending_threshold and is not an approved event.
