Papua New Guinea — February 2021
Queue 272 | Provisional ccode 910 | Open-web research pending review

Methods and coverage

The registered rollout produced 22 actual search actions (Q001–Q022) and 371 ordered result headers. The captured log preserves original event IDs, query strings, result order, URLs, references, and native execution/capture clocks; Q015 was one original batched action with two queries. The search set includes country/month and weekly windows, English and the “Papua Niugini” country-name form, event-specific Wafi-Golpu and Sumgilbar follow-ups, student-police and labor leads, political/no-confidence terms, and local/international outlet searches. Searches were not rerun for bookkeeping.

Four article bodies were read after individual fresh exact-URL receipts, manual publisher/origin/restriction review, byte-identical note readback, and the full-input/phase gate: S002, S003, S004, and S005. S001 remains search-only after a gated web-reader Cache miss and a separately gated exact-URL HTTPS attempt failed DNS before HTTP; neither attempt produced article content or an observed access denial. Physical article-body reads: 4. Search-only sources: 1.

The open web is not equivalent to LexisNexis. Tok Pisin terms beyond the country-name variant and additional local archives were not searched. No claim of exhaustive coverage or of no event is supported.

Findings

One pending candidate is recorded. S004 reports that Morobe Governor Ginson Saonu organized a public forum at Sir Ignatius Kilage Stadium on 10 February. Attending communities opposed deep-sea tailings placement and supported Saonu’s call for alternatives. Saonu directed a 14-day ultimatum to the Prime Minister and National Executive Council to reverse the environmental permit and threatened an injunction if it expired. S005 reports a Lae forum on the Wafi-Golpu issue the prior week and may describe the same gathering; it supplies no exact day or numerical attendee count and is not counted as a second event. No source gives a headcount. Eligibility remains pending_threshold. The local May 2015 codebook’s broad political behavior/process category covers policy choices by leaders, so E001 uses that category pending review of the current supplemental procedure. Protester violence and all seven state-response dimensions remain unknown.

The Sumgilbar reports S002 and S003 document February letters and a 26 February exploration-license withdrawal, not a February public gathering. The dated Warden hearings and Tokain village forum occurred in 2020. Their combined hearing attendance figure cannot be assigned to either hearing individually or to February, and the sources do not establish continuous physical action into February. The student-police lead S001 is provisionally excluded for no reported protest or state-directed demand in the search snippet; its body remains inaccessible. Hela conflict and later nurses’ sit-in search results do not establish a February protest event and are not imported into this unit.

Gaps and review status

The main unresolved questions are the Wafi-Golpu forum’s >=50 turnout, its precise locality and end boundary, whether S005 is the same forum as S004, and event-specific protester/state response. S001’s body is unavailable for technical reasons, not an observed denial. The Sumgilbar body sources identify no February public gathering. The local May 2015 codebook is available and its broad political behavior/process category is applied to E001’s policy demand; the latest official baseline and supplemental Coding Procedure remain unverified, so this dossier is pending human/coordinator review and is not an official update.
