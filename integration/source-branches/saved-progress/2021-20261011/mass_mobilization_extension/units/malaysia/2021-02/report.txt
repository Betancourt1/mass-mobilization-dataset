Malaysia — February 2021 provisional extension dossier

Scope and method

This is a first-pass, open-web search of Malaysia for events whose onset may fall in February 2021. It used 28 actual captured search actions (Q001–Q028), with 727 ordered result headers retained from the registered rollout. The queries included English, Malay and Chinese terms; national news domains and Chinese-language outlets; four weekly windows for all of February; and targeted searches for labor, health workers, land/farm issues, students, prices/cost of living, migration/refugees, and emergency/Parliament politics. Query capture was reconciled one-to-one to the actual rollout: all 28 action IDs, exact query batches, ordered URLs and full header objects matched, with no omissions or mismatches. Searches and captures are in search_log.jsonl.

Sources and screening

Thirteen relevant source cards are retained in sources.jsonl. Two February J&T reports (S001, S002) were opened under separate fresh exact-URL receipts and saved publisher/origin reviews. Malaysiakini describes a 4 February parcel-handling incident inside J&T’s Perak sorting centre and a company explanation about bonuses. Bernama relays the Human Resources Ministry’s investigation, attributing the incident to workload and bonus/commission changes. Neither report gives a total worker or crowd count or establishes an in-person public gathering of at least 50 or a demand from participants to the Malaysian government. The reports concern a workplace/private-employer dispute; the ministry’s investigation and advice are not evidence of a protester-directed demand or state concession. The incident is retained as an evidence-based exclusion, with the possibility of reconsideration if additional evidence establishes public assembly and state-directed demand.

The remaining eleven cards are search-only. They preserve leads for online migrant-rights advocacy; an earlier January J&T stoppage; March actions concerning the emergency ordinance, Undi18, and migrant deportations; and a February 2 court challenge announced in an online press conference. The March items belong outside this unit. The court filing/online press conference does not establish a physical protest. The reported 1,086 deported migrants is not an attendance count. A small Myanmar-junta vigil is directed at a foreign government and search-reported as below 50. A search result identifies #MigranJugaManusia as an online action, not a physical assembly.

No February event currently satisfies the documented provisional criteria of a public gathering of at least 50 people and a demand directed to Malaysia’s government or its policy. events.json is therefore empty; this means no qualifying event was found in this search pass, not that none occurred. excluded.json preserves nine screening decisions and boundary leads. No episode beginning before February was shown to continue into this month, so continuations.json is empty. The January 6 J&T Pontian lead is recorded as an earlier, different-location workplace episode; its search result says 49 workers and a mediated resolution, but its official PDF was not opened. No PDF body request was made. One gate closed before a request; a later receipt became unstable when a shared source-card input changed during manual review, and a subsequent manual-review command failed before saving a note. No body request followed either failure; a new exact receipt and saved/read-back manual note are required before any future request.

Limitations and review

Open-web search is not LexisNexis and does not substitute for systematic regional press or news archives. Searches covered English, Malay and Chinese, but did not systematically cover Tamil-language and every regional/local outlet. Search engines can omit and misdate stories, and syndication and independence are not fully resolved. Search-only snippets remain provisional; no exact participant total is inferred from a workforce, deportee total, or symbolic display. Month assignment is based on reported event onset when available, not publication or search date. No continuation, event ending, police response, violence, or state accommodation is inferred from silence.

The official baseline/manual remains pending at coordinator level (baseline_gate=pending_official_baseline). All records are provisional and await coordinator/human review. The saved access receipts and manual reviews are in access_receipts/; AR002 and AR003 document the two completed article opens. AR004–AR006 document JPPM gate attempts; none authorized or preceded a PDF request. AR005 failed the full-input manual recheck and AR006 has no saved manual note, so neither is a usable body gate.
