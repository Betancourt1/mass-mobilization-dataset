Scope and method
The unit covers 1–31 January 2021. I logged 22 separate open-web searches in English, Portuguese, Tetum/Tetun-style spellings, and Indonesian. Searches covered all four January weekly windows, Dili and the district names Baucau, Ermera, Covalima and Manatuto, and student, veteran/pension, worker/union, farmer/land and public-policy terms. I also searched December 2020 and February 2021 for possible physical continuity, and queried TATOLI, Timor Post, Tempo Timor, Lusa and Suara Timor Lorosae. Search result title, URL and reference headers are preserved in search_log.jsonl; the tool exposed no overall result totals.

Limitations and follow-up
Open-web searching does not reproduce LexisNexis or a systematic newspaper archive. Results often ignored the requested dates, and district or sector queries do not establish complete coverage. The Tetum, Portuguese and Indonesian searches do not replace fluent review of local-language archives; Suara Timor Lorosae's named-site query returned no result headers. More local reporting, archive and language review is needed before claiming comprehensive coverage.

All seven state-response dimensions remain uncoded because no qualifying event was established. No official Mass Mobilization ID or approval is claimed. Baseline version, universe, current schema and additional Coding Procedure remain unresolved centrally.
