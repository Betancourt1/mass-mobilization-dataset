Candidates
E001 records the reported protest by salon, barbershop and health-club owners against COVID-19 closures on 6 February. The date is a timezone-based inference from the page timestamp and “yesterday” wording. The article says “dozens” but gives no numeric count, so E001 remains pending_threshold. Security prevented the initial gathering outside the National Assembly; owners moved to the Kuwait Lawyers Society. The report says the prime minister agreed to reconsider the measures and that a committee meeting was planned, but the final outcome and exact relation to the protest are unresolved. No organizer, exact event end or event-specific violence is established.

E002 preserves a separately announced Salons Union sit-in planned for 5 February at the National Assembly. A prior-unit body read records the announcement, but execution and turnout were not verified. An Al-Anba search header says “Saturday”; the unread body and intended date could describe another plan. E002 is pending_evidence and is not merged with E001.
