Chad — February 2021 first-pass research

Scope and method

This is an open-web first pass for Chad, provisional ccode 483, February 1–28, 2021. It preserves the existing January-to-February handoff and keeps this dossier pending human review and an official baseline check. The captured log has 12 one-query search calls and 314 returned headers. The searches covered four weekly windows (Feb 1–7, 8–14, 15–21 and 22–28), broader opposition and election terms, French and Arabic queries, regional city terms, public-sector/teacher/student/health-worker topics, and retrospective accounts. Exact queries, original refs, titles, URLs and order are retained in search_log.jsonl; sources.jsonl keeps distinct source cards and source lineage. A full capture-history comparison matched all 12 saved query records to the actual extracted captures, including every stored field, full ordered header arrays, aligned URL arrays, timestamps, original event IDs and per-file hashes; only the documented CAPTURE-to-CHAD query ID mapping differs. All 314 headers matched, no response was empty or batched, and Q002 was repaired from its exact stored capture without rerunning. This is not exhaustive coverage.

Results and provisional coding

Five event records are retained for review. The Feb 6 N’Djamena entry has a reported group of at least 31 at one point plus additional smaller, unquantified district gatherings; total attendance and the 50-person threshold are unresolved. A separate Doba Feb 6 lead reports arrests but no turnout. A Moundou conviction report is kept only as an unverified assembly lead; convictions do not establish turnout or even a defined public gathering. The TLP retrospective describes a Feb 13 N’Djamena march across several neighborhoods and a Feb 15 MNCT-led march. Neither has a turnout estimate, and the arrest/injury figures are not headcounts. All five remain pending; no official event is proposed for inclusion.

The announced Feb 15 sit-in outside French targets remains an announcement-only exclusion because no searched source confirms execution. The Feb 28 Yaya Dillo residence raid is retained as political-security context, not a public assembly. The national public-sector strike that began Jan 11 is recorded as a possible continuation from the January dossier: the search result reports a Feb 6 union suspension, but residual school absences leave physical end uncertain, and no separate February public gathering connected to the strike is established.

Language, geography, sectors, retrospective coverage and boundaries

French coverage included weekly, election, protest and regional searches for N’Djamena, Moundou, Doba, Sarh and Abéché. Arabic queries covered February opposition protests and labor/teacher action. No speaker-reviewed Chadian Arabic transliteration or minority-language coverage was established. Sector terms covered teachers, students, health workers and public employees; the clearest labor lead crosses from January, and unrelated-year hits were excluded. The TLP retrospective covers several February dates and later months; its Feb 6, 13 and 15 paragraphs cite Amnesty, RFI and HRW respectively. Mosaïque Guinée’s Feb 13 result is a reproduction of RFI, not independent corroboration. March campaign reporting is not imported as a February event or end date.

Access and limits

Bodies read: the French Amnesty Feb 9 release, the local Ndjamena Hebdo report, and the TLP retrospective PDF (methodology and relevant Feb 6/13/15 passages only). No full copyrighted body was retained. The exact TV5 URL returned HTTP 403; Le Pays and Ialtchad exact opens returned cache misses, with no retry or copy. HRW body/copies were held under an explicit publisher-wide 403 restriction. The exact BeInChad article was not requested because Chad January already records an exact HTTP 406 denial. Every actual body attempt has a fresh exact-URL preflight receipt and contemporaneous full-ledger publisher/origin review in access_receipts/. HRW and BeInChad body requests were stopped before retrieval due to recorded holds. Captured search-result headers remain discovery metadata where a body was not read.

Unknown turnout, physical event endings, site boundaries and unreported response dimensions remain null/unknown. The dossier does not claim that no other events occurred; French/Arabic open-web coverage, regional archives, minority-language reporting and official baseline checks remain gaps.
