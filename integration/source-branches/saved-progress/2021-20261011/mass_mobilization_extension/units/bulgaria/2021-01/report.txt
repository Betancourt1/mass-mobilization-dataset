Method and coverage
Executed 36 actual queries in English and Bulgarian Cyrillic. The search log retains every query and its returned URLs, including irrelevant and empty results. General country/month searches were followed by weekly windows, topic and episode searches, regional searches and December 2020/February 2021 boundary searches. Topics included anti-government and judicial-reform actions, restaurant/hospitality restrictions and compensation, arts/education, labor/health, water, environment, agriculture and transport. Regional terms included Sofia, Plovdiv, Varna, Burgas, Ruse, Vidin and Vratsa.
Search date operators repeatedly returned other years; literal month/date phrases also produced unrelated results. Queries do not establish exhaustive coverage. Open-web access does not reproduce LexisNexis or systematic local newspaper archives. No additional verified protest in a search means that none was found, not that none occurred. Bulgarian-language passages were read and paraphrased; no independent human linguistic review is claimed.
The first four queries were one combined request. Its request-start time was recorded, but exact per-query engine time and result-to-query association were unavailable. Later queries record an actual pre-request timestamp; exact internal engine execution remains unknown. Recorded-at timestamps are separate.
Sources were opened through the web tool where possible. Tool internal/cache errors were sometimes recovered through ordinary public HTTPS. Actual HTTP403 denials from bTV/OFFNews were respected. Source cards state whether the article, a short passage, only a headline or only search results were assessed. No full articles were stored. The curated 82 source cards retain relevant evidence, boundary/discovery leads and methodological context; other returned URLs remain in the search log. A source-card total does not count independent confirmations.

Continuations and exclusions
C001 records the anti-government/chief-prosecutor campaign begun July 9,2020 and January observations. Campaign longevity does not automatically make every assembly a single continuous event or justify new monthly starts. Possible owner: bulgaria_2020-07. No campaign turnout or responses were copied to January.
C002 records a New Year gathering reported as welcoming 2021 outside government buildings. December 31 announcement supplied a scheduled 21h start; physical start/end remain unknown. Possible owner: bulgaria_2020-12. No invented January 1 start.
Two January 23 Navalny-release gatherings in Sofia and Varna are excluded because demands targeted Russia. No Russian arrests elsewhere were coded as Bulgarian state responses.

Gaps and coding limits
Seventeen gap records cover baseline, search method, participants, domestic state target, action units, cross-month ownership, unknown dates, state responses, access, source independence, language, coverage and contested allegations.
All candidate physical ends remain null/unknown. Publication/update dates, planned times, deadlines, reopening dates and a march start are not physical end dates.
All seven response categories remain yes/no_explicit/unknown. Positive original coding is limited to the January 15 reception, January 16 SOLI reception and January 13 Mezdra arrests. Silence is never no_explicit or ignore. No victory is inferred from reception, and prior/future meetings are not transferred.
BNR/NOVA/ministry reprints are identified where known. Agency-linked reports are not independent confirmations.
These are research candidates, not official MM updates, human-approved rows or a consolidated dataset.
