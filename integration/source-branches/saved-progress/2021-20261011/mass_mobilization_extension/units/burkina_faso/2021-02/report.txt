Burkina Faso, February 2021 — first-pass research dossier

Scope and method
This unit records 42 actual web-search invocations and 805 ordered result headers. Searches included French and English general queries, four weekly windows within February, Burkina Faso localities and regions, transport, customs, education, public-sector unions, and retrospective reporting. The exact response arrays were re-extracted from the registered search-session history and compared with the 42 saved query rows: all 42 matched in order, with 805 headers and zero mismatches. The comparison artifact and SHA-256 are recorded in status.json and captures/search/capture-history/final-response-comparison-20261010.json. Earlier partial capture bytes and repair history remain under captures/search/capture-history/.

Coverage is limited to open-web search and the saved results. It does not reproduce LexisNexis or systematic newspaper archives, and it is not exhaustive. Queries and discovery snippets were used to identify leads, not as body evidence. Thirty source cards remain search-only; four articles were opened and their actual read scope is listed in sources.jsonl; one card is blocked. Four current-unit body opens and zero prior-unit-only consultations are represented in the source metadata. No official baseline decision has been made; baseline_gate remains pending_official_baseline. No fluent independent review of all locally used languages is documented.
