Search result headers remain discovery leads only. Open web search does not reproduce LexisNexis or systematic newspaper archives, so this search does not establish comprehensive coverage.

Candidates

PAN202102-E001 — SENNIAF abuse-accountability protest, 16 February. La Prensa S004 dates the gathering to the prior afternoon and describes “hundreds” outside SENNIAF in Bella Vista; TVN S005 identifies the 16 February action as the second consecutive day. The qualitative count clears the 50-person threshold without a numeric range. Protesters sought justice and action concerning abuse in children’s shelters, directed to SENNIAF, MIDES and the Presidency. TVN says police watched; it mentions burned tires but does not identify who lit them. Protester violence remains unknown. Police watching is provisionally coded as inaction/ignore under the consulted 2015 manual, pending review against the current procedure. No named organizer, exact ending, or agreement is established. Eligibility: proposed inclusion, pending independent review. Whether this is a distinct day or part of a longer action remains unresolved.

PAN202102-E002 — SENNIAF protest, 17 February. La Prensa S006 calls this the third consecutive day outside SENNIAF. The article reports pepper spray, protesters’ attempt to topple security barriers, and retreat from the site around 5:45 p.m.; students left toward the University of Panama, but this report does not establish a gathering there. Protesters demanded justice, names of responsible shelter staff, information on funding, and removal of senior MIDES/SENNIAF officials. Attendance is unreported, so the 50-person threshold remains unestablished. Crowd dispersal is coded yes; whether the barrier attempt meets the protester-violence definition and whether the removal demand has a distinct category remain for review. Eligibility: pending threshold; no organizer, arrests, or later destination action is established.

PAN202102-E003 — SENNIAF gathering, 19 February. The Defensoría notice S012 says its monitors departed while a small group remained and reports three detainees (two Panamanian adults and one Nicaraguan). The small group remaining later in the day is not total attendance. The consulted passage supplies no demand or organizer, and no total count; threshold and state-directed demand remain unestablished. Arrests are coded yes. The same passage noted a contemporaneous search for a protester whose whereabouts were unknown at that time; this is not a present-day missing-person claim. Eligibility: pending evidence.

Do not automatically merge the 16, 17 and 19 February candidate records. Sources describe consecutive/repeated days, but the start, continuity, boundaries, and relationship among site-specific gatherings are unresolved. No verified post-February continuation or prior-month continuation was established; continuations.json remains empty.
