Rwanda — February 2021

Scope and method
This dossier covers Rwanda in February 2021, provisional code 517, under the fixed 7 October 2026 cutoff. The captured log contains 18 native search actions, 72 query terms and 433 ordered result headers. Queries covered general and weekly English searches; Kinyarwanda and French terms plus a limited Swahili-term check; local outlets; official, international and rights-report domains; Gakoni/Gatsibo; student, labor, transport, health, teacher, farmer/vendor and land leads; and January/March boundaries. The search capture preserves the original batched terms, ordered headers, URLs, references and clocks. It is open-web discovery, not a reproduction of LexisNexis or a district-by-district source audit.

Finding
One Gakoni Adventist College episode is retained as rwanda_2021-02_E001. KT Press (9 February) dates a property-damaging student strike to Sunday 7 February and reports students refusing classes on Monday 8 February. MUHAZI YACU (10 February, Kinyarwanda) reports the school’s 9 February closure and quotes an unnamed student describing a condition that students not resume until a peer was released and returned. These are distinct reported action dates; the available read scopes do not establish one continuous physical gathering or its physical end.

Eligibility remains pending evidence. Neither report establishes a public meeting of 50 or more people or a simultaneous turnout number. The reports’ reference to all students, 16 arrests, school enrollment of 372 (including 288 refugees), and the MUHAZI YACU photo caption “15 students” are not protest turnout. The reported return/release condition may have been directed to school management; a demand to school management versus a competent Rwanda state authority (including local police) or government policy is not established, and no listed category maps unambiguously to the school-level condition.

The trigger accounts conflict. KT Press attributes the suspended student’s removal to repeated dormitory departures to drink; MUHAZI YACU describes different alleged conduct and a COVID-rules briefing. Both mention Gatsibo Mayor Richard Gasana, leaving a shared official-source dependency. KT Press reports stone throwing and property damage; this is coded as reported protester violence, not as a claim about all students. Sixteen arrests are the only coded state response; other response dimensions remain unknown. S006, a 26 February KT Press follow-up from the same publisher/byline, reports the Education Minister’s reopening announcement and prosecution of arrested students. It adds a later response, not independent event corroboration; reopening is not coded as accommodation to the students’ demand.
