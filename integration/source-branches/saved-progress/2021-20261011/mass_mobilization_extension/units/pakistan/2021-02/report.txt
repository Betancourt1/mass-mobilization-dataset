Pakistan, February 2021 — first-pass open-web dossier

Scope and method

This provisional country-month dossier follows the fixed 2026-10-07 event cutoff. It records 36 actual search actions (Q001–Q036) and 1,107 ordered result headers. A full parity audit matched every registered-rollout action ID, exact query batch, action clock and ordered header/ref/title/URL to search_log.jsonl. The Q035–Q036 leads have been reconciled into sources.jsonl. Search discovery does not reproduce LexisNexis or provide a complete census; no exhaustive-coverage claim is made. English discovery and some Urdu reporting are represented, while systematic Pashto, Balochi, Sindhi, Punjabi and local archive coverage remains limited.

The ledger contains 80 curated source cards: 5 bodies were successfully opened and 75 cards remain search-only. Five event records are drafted: PAK202102-E001 (Feb. 10 federal employee pay protest) and E002 (Feb. 22 missing-persons sit-in ending) are proposed inclusions; E003 (missing-persons camp/march) and E004 (Feb. 8 lawyers’ protest) remain below a documented attendance threshold; E005 (Feb. 7 PTM rally) remains pending attendance and a specific state-directed policy demand. These are provisional agent-coded candidates for independent review, not official dataset updates. No exclusion is yet supported by reviewed body evidence. PAK202102-C001 records the prior PIMS MTI Ordinance strike context, begun in November 2020 and owned by the earlier unit; it is not merged into the February pay protest.

Body evidence and access outcomes

The five successful reads are PAK-S124 (Dawn, Feb. 10 federal employee pay protest), S131 (Reuters/Dawn, missing-persons sit-in ending), S134 (Voicepk, missing-persons camp/march), S141 (Geo, Feb. 8 lawyers’ protest) and S195 (The News, PTM rally). Their per-source notes preserve exact read scopes, source-level paraphrases, request completion clocks and receipts under access_receipts/. Location-level crowd figures in S124 are not pooled; arrests, tear gas, rubber bullets, batons, protester stone throwing and ongoing talks are coded only to the body’s stated scope. S131’s endpoint after a prime-ministerial assurance does not supply an unknown start date. S134 may overlap with S131 but shared issue and city do not establish that the same people occupied one continuous site. S141 and S195 still lack qualifying crowd evidence; S195 also lacks a specific state-directed policy demand.

Fresh exact-URL attempts for S132, S135, S152, S155 and S185, plus S130 attempts 01 and 03, returned the web tool’s Cache miss. S193 could not be accessed through the tool. Those are technical retrieval outcomes, not HTTP denials. S130 attempts 02 and 04 are preserved as no-request gate outcomes; neither was a request. No Pakistan source-card denial was observed in these access records. Do not infer source unavailability beyond the exact attempts recorded.

Q035–Q036 added seven search-only cards (S197–S203) for WAPDA workers, a TLP call-off report, Karachi University teachers, JUI-F-related Islamabad/Murree Road protests, and Feb. 10 employee coverage. Ten existing source cards have updated query/ref provenance. These headers establish discovery leads only; they do not establish an executed gathering, date, turnout, demand, target or state response.
