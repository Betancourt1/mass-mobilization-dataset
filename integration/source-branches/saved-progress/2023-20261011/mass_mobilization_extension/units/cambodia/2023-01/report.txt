Method and coverage
Nine grouped web.run search invocations were captured in search_log.jsonl, with 256 aligned result headers. Discovery covered Cambodia January protest/strike terms in English and Khmer, four weekly date windows, workers and NagaWorld, a Smart Shirt labor dispute, and Indigenous/land-policy issues. Searches included selected local-language phrases but were not systematic across Khmer sources or provinces. Header counts describe returned discovery items, not event coverage or independent confirmation. No LexisNexis-equivalent archive search was available. No claim of exhaustive coverage or of no additional events is made.

January candidate leads
E001 is a search-only Smart Shirt Garments lead in Kampong Speu dated January 25 by VOD previews. The previews report workers protesting a wage deduction tied to Lunar New Year and blocking National Road 41; they attribute a Labor Department intervention request to a union president. The VOD English body returned an anti-bot verification challenge and was not read; the Khmer companion was not opened. The reported turnout and same-day resolution are unverified previews, and the Khmer page is not independent corroboration. The immediate dispute was with a private employer, so state-target eligibility remains pending.

E002 is an opened VOD report of a January 25 Phnom Penh forum organized by the Cambodia Indigenous Peoples Organization. It reports around 100 Indigenous community members and requests for input into draft Forestry and Protected Areas laws. The gathering is described as a consultation forum; whether it meets the original MM event definition remains pending the missing official codebook. The attendance estimate has one opened news source. An IPS Voice statement could not be read after two technical cache misses. An ODC announcement was partially read; its attendance wording is ambiguous and its relation to the VOD-reported forum is unconfirmed.

No other January event is assigned in this first pass. excluded.json is empty because no additional eligible-looking event was reviewed and rejected; this is not an absence finding.

It is not a new January event in this dossier; possible owner is cambodia_2021-12.
