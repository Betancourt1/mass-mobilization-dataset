January candidates

Two provisional UGTA gathering records are retained, both pending_threshold. E001 records a gathering at UGTA headquarters over two government draft laws on union activity and collective labor disputes/strike rights. Its date is deliberately month-only because S015 implies 24 January, S017’s unread search result says Wednesday, and S014 prints an internally inconsistent “Wednesday 26 January.” No opened source supplies attendee turnout; the count of organizational structures is not a count of people.

E002 records the reported Saturday 28 January gathering on the Maison du Peuple esplanade in Algiers, against the same draft laws. Anadolu and Le Matin d’Algérie confirm occurrence, date, place, organizers and policy target. Neither provides a numeric crowd count; “large mobilization” is qualitative. La Sentinelle’s S016 search-only result claims hundreds, but its body was not retrievable after fresh checks, so the claim does not establish the >=50 threshold. The earlier HQ gathering’s relationship to this action is unresolved; a shared policy campaign does not establish a single continuous event.

For both candidates, the seven state-response fields, protester violence and physical end remain unknown. The January 23 doctoral-holder sit-in is only announced in opened reporting; execution is not verified.
