Romania, January 2021 — research report

Status: researched_open_web_pending_review. This is a nonexhaustive open-web first pass. Candidate IDs and proposed eligibility are local research decisions for coordinator review, not official MM observations or human double coding.

Scope and baseline: Romania, January 1–31, 2021, under the assigned fixed October 7, 2026 inclusive cutoff in America/Mexico_City. The project has a historical May 2015 manual copy, but its current official data-file universe, latest schema/cutoff and additional Coding Procedure remain unresolved in the baseline. No LexisNexis access is available, so open-web discovery does not reproduce the original search protocol.

Search coverage: 15 literal searches capture 340 returned title/URL/reference headers; the search interface exposed no total result counts. Searches include broad country/month discovery; Jan. 1–7, Jan. 8–14, Jan. 15–21 and Jan. 22–31 windows; Romanian terms; labor, public-service, health, pension, political and union leads; local/regional follow-up; and an explicit Jan./Feb. boundary check. The February check found later staged Cartel ALFA/PUBLISIND activity and a separate Jan. 31 AUR gathering. The result total is a log of captured headers, not a coverage measure. Regional/local reporting, source indexing and web retrieval vary; no absence or completeness claim is made.

Threshold-supported candidates: Radio România Actualități (RRA) reports nearly 100 PUBLISIND members at Constanța Prefecture on Jan. 20 (E015) and about 100 Cartel ALFA participants at the same prefecture Jan. 21 (E016); G4Media reports approximately 100 Cartel ALFA unionists at Cotroceni Jan. 18 (E008); Adevărul reports approximately 100 participants at Piața Victoriei Jan. 24 (E012); RRA reports more than 100 at the Finance Ministry Jan. 26 (E011); Satu Mare Prefecture’s 2021 activity report gives around 60 union members at the Administrative Palace Jan. 27 (E004); RRA reports approximately 100 in Târgu Jiu Jan. 29 (E017) and about 100 outside the Labour Ministry Jan. 29 (E020); and a Jan. 30 AUR demonstration followed by a march toward Piața Victoriei and Matei Balș, with G4Media estimating about 100, AP via Euronews describing hundreds, and an unopened AGERPRES result saying approximately 200 (E001). These are nine proposed inclusions. E020’s RRA report says the protest lasted two hours, supporting an end on Jan. 29 but no clock endpoints. The Jan. 30 and Jan. 31 AUR gatherings remain separate; no continuous occupation is established.

Other body-confirmed gatherings: RRA confirms Jan. 20 Cartel ALFA representatives outside the Finance Ministry (E014), but gives no attendance; the Jan. 26 Finance count does not transfer. RRA also confirms PUBLISIND pickets in Prahova and Mureș on Jan. 20 (E022 and E003), without site-specific counts. The nearly 100 estimate in that article applies only to Constanța. Zi de Zi describes the Mureș gathering as “dozens,” which does not establish 50. The Jan. 29 police-union gathering outside the Interior Ministry (E021) sought salary-law compliance and ministry reform, but the speaker's aspirational comparison with 100 or future tens of thousands is not a reliable attendance estimate. RRA describes the distinct Jan. 29 Galați pensioner crowd as “dozens” (E018), not a demonstrated 50+.

The Feb. 1 CNSLR-Frăția press-review PDF reproduces a report of a separate Jan. 31 gathering of “several dozen” outside the Health Ministry (E023), seeking health-system reform and accountability after the Matei Balș fire. A direct search found the Ziare.com original, but its body was not accessible after a Cache miss and one exact request returned HTTP 403; the opened press-review reproduction is secondary and not independent corroboration. The report also states that AUR leader George Simion met with Health Minister Vlad Voiculescu on Jan. 31. This same-day meeting is coded as accommodation, with uncertain linkage to the crowd because the report does not explicitly say it was arranged in response to the gathering. Gendarme presence, identity checks and attempted entry do not establish arrest or dispersal. The deaths at the hospital fire are antecedent to the protests, not protest casualties.

Plans and lower-confidence leads: The Jan. 19 PUBLISIND/BNS statement schedules Jan. 20 actions at the Justice Ministry and the Constanța, Prahova and Mureș prefectures, with 100 planned at each site. RRA subsequently confirms Constanța, Prahova and Mureș; the Justice Ministry remains plan-only (E019), and the scheduled count is never transferred as turnout. Organizer-only Jan. 27 pensioner actions retain the announced date in notes, not as a confirmed physical start (E013). The Jan. 20 Finance picket (E014) is no longer schedule-only because RRA confirms its occurrence, but threshold remains unknown. Baia Mare (E005) is a campaign departure confirmed retrospectively, not a verified 50-person rally at the planned Maramureș Prefecture venue. The Jan. 14 Botoșani and Târgoviște leads (E009–E010) rely on search-only evidence; their dates are provisional and their bodies were not read. No scheduled day is treated as a physical start when only an organizer plan establishes it: E013 and the Justice Ministry E019 have null start dates. Other pending-threshold actions include PUBLISIND's Jan. 13 multi-site gathering (E002), the Jan. 14 health-worker gathering in Bucharest (E006), Jan. 15 Zalău (E007), and the Jan. 20/29 actions above whose turnout is absent or below the demonstrated threshold.

Campaign and episode boundaries: PUBLISIND sources describe physical actions beginning Dec. 31, followed by separately dated January gatherings; C001 records the older onset. No continuous occupation is established. Cartel ALFA's organizer program describes staged actions across January and February, and its retrospective lists later city and institutional stops. This campaign horizon is not evidence that any particular January gathering physically continued into February or ended on the last announced date. The Jan. 20 and Jan. 21 Constanța actions remain separate by date and organizer. On Jan. 29, the counted Cartel ALFA gathering is only located to Târgu Jiu; an earlier RRA item says a Gorj Prefecture picket was planned that day, but the opened execution report does not confirm that exact venue. The Jan. 27 pensioner and Jan. 29 Cartel ALFA Târgu Jiu leads are distinct.

State response and endings: Each of the seven response dimensions is independently represented. E023 accommodation is yes for the reported same-day leader/minister meeting, with the episode link uncertain; accommodation is unknown for the other 22 candidates. Arrests, beatings, crowd dispersal, ignore, killings and shootings remain unknown for all 23. The Jan. 30 route is described as a gathering followed by a march, not a confirmed physical end. E020 has a reported two-hour duration with a known end day. Other physical end dates remain unknown; future calendars, planned handovers, last publication times and campaign horizons are not substituted for dissolution or departure evidence.
