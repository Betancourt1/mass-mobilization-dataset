Candidate CZ202301-E001
The January 21 rally and march led by Ladislav Vrabel and ČR na 1. místě began at 13:00 on Wenceslas Square and proceeded through central Prague to Strakova Academy, the Office of Government. iROZHLAS says Vrabel ended it after 16:30 that day. Reports document criticism of government policy and calls for the government to resign, alongside criticism of presidential candidate Petr Pavel; the event is therefore not described solely as electoral support. Police estimates are qualitative (“several hundred”), and the organizer claimed “thousands.” No numeric bounds were invented. The repeated police estimate is not independent corroboration. State response and protester violence remain unknown: a person was reportedly detained over alleged November online conduct, with no link to the January event established; two paint throwers’ affiliation is unknown.

Proposed exclusion CZ202301-X001
The January 8 Prague Castle gathering is explicitly pre-election candidate support. The opened ČTK reports identify the civic association Milion Chvilek and supporters of multiple candidates, but do not establish the codebook definition of an excluded political rally: two or more political parties demonstrating for their positions in association with upcoming elections. The Castle Administration’s recommendation to move the event and the organizers’ public-space dispute leave a possible assembly-right/state-target question unresolved. This is a provisional worker exclusion pending coordinator review of both issues; S006 and S007 share ČTK origin.
