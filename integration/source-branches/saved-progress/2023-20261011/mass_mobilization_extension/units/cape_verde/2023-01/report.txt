Cape Verde — January 2023 research unit

Scope and method
This is a bounded open-web research pass for Cape Verde (provisional ccode 402), 1–31 January 2023, using the documented extension protocol and fixed 2026-10-07 cutoff. Nine grouped native-web queries were captured, with 182 returned result headers and nine relevant source cards. The searches covered general and weekly date windows, Portuguese and selected Cape Verdean Creole wording, local and national media, event-specific follow-up, strike and island leads. Three source cards have readable body text in this unit (S001, S003, S006); six are search-only. Each attempted source-body fetch has its own saved access receipt. This pass did not use a systematic press archive or reproduce LexisNexis, and it is not exhaustive. Search indexing produced unrelated geographic/topic results; local-language and regional coverage remain uneven. No claim is made that no other events occurred.

Candidates
E001 — Praia march against current government policies, 5 January. One opened same-day ASEMANA report (S001) says the march began around 16:30, moved through central Praia to the Government Palace and National Assembly, and continued at the Assembly until about 18:00. It identifies the civic-associations network as organizer, describes thousands qualitatively, records governance, services, employment and labor demands, and calls the event peaceful and orderly. The repeated qualitative crowd wording supports a proposed threshold inclusion under the protocol, but no numeric range is encoded. This remains single-origin evidence without independent corroboration. Security presence and a blocked Assembly entrance do not by themselves establish a listed dispersal or other response; those fields remain unknown.

E002 — Praia municipal-guard strike and planned concentration, reported for 23 January. Two A Nação results (S002, S004) are search-only and share one publisher. Snippets report a planned three-day strike and a concentration at Praça Alexandre Albuquerque, with disputes over fines, promotion/progression, rules and uniforms. Exact-URL body attempts returned cache misses before HTTP, which are technical retrieval failures rather than denials. Actual strike execution, public assembly, attendance, full demands and response are unverified. Workforce size is not used as crowd size; eligibility remains pending evidence.

E003 — Marxa Cabral 2023, planned for 20 January. Santiago Magazine’s SM/Lusa preview (S003) anticipates “dozens” and includes a political complaint about the treatment of Cabral’s legacy in schools. A separate pre-event BUALA article (S006) describes a four-day program culminating in a Praia street march and says Sal activity was planned simultaneously; it frames the event around resistance, democracy, recognition and critique of political elites. Neither account confirms that the march occurred, establishes actual attendance or a 50-person threshold, or resolves the domestic state target and Praia/Sal unit boundary. The candidate remains pending evidence. Search-only catalog leads S007 and S008 may be mirrors; neither produced readable document content or independent event confirmation.

Exclusions and continuations
X001 records the announced Praia municipal-employees demonstration reportedly suspended after an understanding. Its article body and any rescheduled date were not verified (S005); the exclusion means no actual assembly was established in the captured evidence, not that one did not occur. X002 records the SEFI workers’ announced strike notice (S009); the snippet describes an apparent private-employer dispute and no qualifying public assembly or state-policy target. No continuation beginning before January was evidenced. The January 5 report describes one event ending around 18:00; similar slogans found for an August 2022 campaign do not establish continuous physical action into January. Annual recurrence of the Cabral march is not itself a continuous episode.

Unresolved issues
The official Mass Mobilization codebook/version was not reviewed in this unit; the baseline gate remains pending. Open questions include independent corroboration and exact crowd bounds for E001; closure and response coding for E001; actual strike/concentration occurrence and public attendance for E002; execution, attendance, specific state target, dates and cross-island episode scope for E003; the suspended demonstration’s outcome; and whether any further public action surrounded the SEFI labor notice. Cache misses and tool errors are recorded as retrieval failures, not publisher denials. The ten gap records preserve these limits and concrete follow-up.
