Assignment and cutoff
Country: Chile. Provisional code: 155. Unit: 2023-01. Cutoff: 2026-10-07 inclusive (America/Mexico_City). No official Mass Mobilization IDs or approval are assigned here. The catalogue and MM extension remain separate.

Coverage and capture
This bounded Spanish-first/English follow-up used eight grouped native search actions and captured all eight original result arrays (243 result headers) in search_log.jsonl; one missing local Q003 artifact was recovered from its original native search action without replaying the query. Queries covered general January protest/strike terms, four weekly windows, labor/health/students/Mapuche, selected Chilean regions, security demonstrations in Tarapacá, the Dominga ministerial vote, Plaza Baquedano leads, and labor/official-search terms. The searches do not establish national completeness.

Nine exact article-body opens were completed with per-URL receipts and source/origin review: BioBioChile (S001), BioBioChileTV (S002), 24horas (S005), Puranoticia (S010), Civicus (S013), La Tercera (S014), The Guardian (S015), Meganoticias (S019), and Epicentro Chile (S020). Four other exact body attempts returned technical/tool access failures without an HTTP denial: Cooperativa S003 (URL inaccessible through the tool), Meganoticias S006 (Cache miss), and the Radio Angelina/Camila variants S008/S009 (Cache miss). They remain search_only. Staffing levels, traffic diversions, vehicle/Metro disruption, search previews and numbers of workers across cities were not substituted for public crowd counts. No AP or AfricaPress body was opened. No 2021 Chile event was merged into a 2023 physical action.

Candidate actions
E001: On 30 January, residents marched from Alto Hospicio toward Iquique to demand security after a series of homicides. 24horas reports >2,000 people began the march and a letter was delivered to the Presidential Delegation; BioBioChileTV reports >300 at Plaza Belén before others joined and describes a later meeting with the delegate about a security table. Both reports clear the 50-person threshold, but their attributed estimates differ and are not a numeric range. The meeting is coded as state accommodation; no policy concession is claimed.

E002: On 18 January, opposing pro- and anti-Dominga groups demonstrated outside the Environment Ministry during the ministerial vote. La Tercera reports >50 combined across groups separated by about 10 metres; The Guardian confirms both sides without a count. The pro side emphasized jobs/development and project approval; the anti side emphasized fisheries/biodiversity and project rejection. Combined attendance does not establish a 50-person threshold for either side individually, so E002 remains pending threshold and event-boundary review. The committee decision is contemporaneous, but the sources do not establish it as a response to the street demonstration.

Unresolved physical-action leads
E003 records search-preview evidence of a 17 January CONFUSAM health-worker action in several cities; no site reaches a documented crowd threshold and both exact article-body attempts failed technically. E004 and E005 keep the 30 January 06:00 Route 16 blockade and 29 January neighborhood cacerolazos separate from the 10:00 march; neither has a crowd count. E006 documents a 13 January Baquedano gathering with an unstated issue and size. E007 and E008 separately record 20 January demonstrations at Plaza Baquedano and Plaza Ñuñoa, also without counts or stated demands. These are pending evidence, not zero-participation or absence findings.

The opened Epicentro account confirms a SAG work stoppage at Paso Los Libertadores began 1 January and ended 2 January after a negotiating table was established. Its reported 15 workers on site and 40 needed are staffing figures, and no public gathering is described, so the lead is in excluded.json. The official January legal-strike PDF was discovered but not reviewed.
