Candidates currently recorded: 2
E001 is Eko straža’s 10 January clean-air protest in Belgrade, from Slavija to the Government building. Contemporary reports put attendance above 50 but conflict: Nova.rs reports a security estimate of about 1,000 and higher participant/organizer claims; Balkan Green Energy News reports several thousand, including 2,000–3,000 in the procession. No exact bounds are assigned. Organizers demanded domestic clean-air and environmental policy action. The program and symbolic delivery are documented, but no participant departure/dissolution is; the physical end remains unknown. The seven state-response fields remain unknown except ignore=yes: a 19 February Eko straža open letter identifies its author as part of the group that organized the 10 January protest and says the Prime Minister had not answered the submitted demands after 30 days. This is an organizer-attributed nonresponse claim, not independently corroborated; the letter does not give the exact submission date or a receipt, so no delivery date or government receipt is presumed. It also reports public presidential remarks that did not address the demands. A separate gap preserves these uncertainties.
