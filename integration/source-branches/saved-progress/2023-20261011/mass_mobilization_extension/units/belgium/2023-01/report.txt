Belgium — January 2023 (ccode 211)

First-pass open-web research dossier. The nine event records are candidates for coordinator review; none is an official Mass Mobilization update. Six out-of-scope or boundary actions are retained in excluded.json. No pre-month continuations were evidenced.

Method and scope

Captured 45 original explicit searches with 1,128 ordered result headers, preserving result metadata and URLs. Queries covered English, Dutch, French and German; monthly and weekly windows; Brussels, Flanders, Wallonia, Ostend, Mechelen, Liège and other regions; labor, care, prisons, police, firefighters, climate, asylum, farmers and named-episode follow-ups; local/union/official sources and retrospective leads. Search results/snippets were used for discovery only. Candidate source cards identify actual body reads, partial reads, search-only leads, denials and technical failures. Fresh exact-URL access receipts and manual canonical/sibling publisher and explicit written-origin checks were recorded before body attempts.

Coverage remains limited to open web. This is not a LexisNexis-equivalent search and does not support a completeness or “no event occurred” claim. The official baseline and current reuse terms are unverified. The consulted 2015 manual gives the 50-person domestic-state-policy criterion, exclusions, episode/date, participant, demand and response rules, but points to an unavailable supplementary Coding Procedure.

Candidate inventory

1. 31 January national non-profit care-sector demonstration, Brussels — proposed include. Police estimate 18,500; unions 22,500. BRUZZ credits Belga; BX1 reports demands without an independent count. Physical ending unknown.
2. 27 January firefighters’ union action, Arts-Loi / Rue de la Loi — pending threshold. BX1 headline says 200, its body describes about 50 initially plus arrivals; Brussels Times reports a delegation met the Brussels Minister-President. Attendance is unresolved; meeting coded accommodation only.
3. 22 January Olivier Vandecasteele release rally, Brussels — proposed include, with domestic-target interpretation flagged for review because the requested release was by Iran and Belgian officials were asked to exert pressure. Reports place attendance around 1,000 to 1,500.
4. 9 January Ostend municipal workers’ action — pending threshold. A secondary report’s approximately 400 figure combines strikers and protesters; it is not assigned as public-rally attendance.
5. 11 January police-union rally at Federal Police headquarters — proposed include. Anadolu reports about 300; union archive corroborates date/site but not attendance.
6. 23 January police-union action at Bozar / Open VLD reception — proposed include. Union archive says several hundred; kept distinct from 11 January.
7. 15 January Sourour Abouda justice gathering — proposed include. VICE reports more than 100; the earlier custody death is not coded as protest violence or killings. Possible separate 12 January Stuut gathering remains unresolved.
8. 11 January prison-union gathering outside Groen headquarters — pending threshold. VRT’s 70–80 is a forecast; BRUZZ reports the gathering without actual count. The strike itself is excluded as a strike-only action.
9. 14 January farmers’ protest at the N-VA New Year reception entrance in Mechelen — pending threshold. Belga confirms farmers/tractors and opposition to the nitrogen agreement; protester count is absent. Party reception attendees are kept separate.
