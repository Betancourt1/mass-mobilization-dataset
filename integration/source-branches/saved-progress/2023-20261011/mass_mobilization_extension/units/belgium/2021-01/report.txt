Method and results
47 logged queries in French, Dutch and English, plus a German Eupen/regional check. Broad month searches, weekly date terms, Flanders/Wallonia city searches, sector searches and December/February boundaries were followed by episode checks. Search operators sometimes produced other years/countries. Query results are logged at batch level because exact per-query URL provenance was not exposed in combined responses; three relevant URLs whose batch mapping was not retained are explicitly logged as full-session discovery. Exact execution/access timestamps were not captured: null timestamps and separate recorded_at checkpoints are intentional.

70 source cards:17 opened,48 search-only,5 blocked. Opened text comprises contemporary public broadcasters/local news, an organizer report and announcement, plus retrospective/rights material explicitly distinguished. AP/AFP/Belga and repeated movement/police claims do not count automatically as independent corroboration.

Important boundaries

Jan 31's 488 administrative arrests concern associated prevention operations at three stations and suspected future attendees. Original arrests=yes preserves that operation-level association; arresting any of the 150 physical Mont des Arts participants is unknown and requires coordinator event-scope review.
No event ending was inferred from last publication, detention date or month boundary. Jan 24 was a postponed December 5 planned gathering, not a proven continuous December occupation. No confirmed pre-January continuation was established: continuations.json is empty, with historical Arlon ZAD/prison-family campaign leads retained in gaps.

Three triage exclusions retain Namur Assange solidarity (foreign target/search-only 7 people), December 5 Eupen beauty protest (outside month/search-only 40), and an explicitly February 5 Charleroi horeca announcement. Exclusions are discovery triage proposals, not verified claims of absence.
