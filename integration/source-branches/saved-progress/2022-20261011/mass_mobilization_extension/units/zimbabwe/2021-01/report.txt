Zimbabwe — January 2021 research dossier
Unit: 1–31 January 2021; provisional country code 552. Stage-one research is ready for coordinator review, not official inclusion.

Method and coverage
Fifteen open-web queries are recorded. Q001 and Q002–Q005 provide general and weekly January searches. Additional searches cover ZINASU/student activity, Shona and Ndebele terms, health workers, teachers, mining/industrial labor, December 2020, February 1–7, and a rural/provincial sweep naming Mutare, Gweru, Masvingo and Chiredzi. Q015 captured 28 result headers. Shona/Ndebele query returns were predominantly English; no useful local-language body was captured. Harare, Bulawayo, Hwange and several provincial leads appear, but there was no systematic province-by-province or rural archive review. Search-result totals are unavailable and query counts are not a completeness measure.

The searches used open web tools rather than LexisNexis and do not reproduce its historical newspaper coverage. The official dataset’s latest bytes/universe and the additional Coding Procedure remain unavailable in the project baseline; baseline_gate remains pending_official_baseline. No official MM IDs, final eligibility, human double-coding, or absence claim is made.

Candidate
E001 is a 25 January 2021 ZINASU protest at Harare Magistrate Court over judicial capture, based on the Zimbabwe Human Rights NGO Forum’s retrospective June 2022 report, S001 (p.44, lines 1526–1535). The report says police violently disrupted the protest and arrested/detained its leaders. It gives no participant count, does not establish at least 50 attendees, and does not state when the gathering physically ended or whether the whole crowd was cleared. E001 remains pending_threshold, with unknown end date and a pending independent review.

Other leads and disposition
S031’s opened NewsDay page reports Bulawayo City Council workers returned after a week-long strike that began 13 January, but gives no public assembly, picket, or striker count. Its 3,600 figure is the council workforce, not participants. The article body credits ZimLive, so S031 and the denied exact ZimLive S043 are one written-origin lead, not independent corroboration. No BCC event candidate was created.

S005, a Zimbabwe Human Rights NGO Forum report hosted by Kubatana, says nurses at Sally Mugabe Hospital struck on 7 January and sought government-provided PPE, but describes no public assembly or participant count. It also reports police dispersing informal workers at Glenview on 6 January and using tear gas to displace vendors/traders/public at Chigovanyika Shopping Centre on 7 January; the passages supply no attendance or explicit protest demand. These remain leads, not qualifying event candidates. The report states ZPP gathered its national reports; this attribution is preserved and is not treated as separate corroboration.

CITE bodies show S050 was a planned ARTUZ labour withdrawal for 15–26 November 2021, and S051 describes a Chaba mine sit-in beginning 4 November 2021. Both are outside this unit. Search-only ZimLive bail reports S057–S058 refer to an earlier Masvingo teachers’ salary protest involving Sheila Chisirimunhu, but do not establish its physical date, turnout, or boundary. ZimLive body/copy holds were respected; no January event or dated continuation was created. No dated previous-onset continuation was established, so continuations.json is empty. No event exclusion was recorded; uncertain leads remain in gaps rather than being represented as confirmed events.

Boundary evidence
Q011 searched December 2020 and Q012 searched February 1–7. S044, a December monitoring page, was recovered by ordinary HTTPS after web Cache miss and default DNS failure (HTTP 200, process exit 0). A limited parser screened 33 extracted text items for mobility terms and found none. That limited screen is not evidence that December protest activity or continuity was absent; G011 remains open.

S007, a Zimbabwe Situation-hosted report, was recovered through one exact ordinary HTTPS fallback (HTTP 200, process exit 0). Its title says 25 January–5 February, while the introduction says 29 January–5 February. The body identifies ZPP/CSU data and ZADHR excerpts. It reports a separate 1 February MDC Alliance youth flash demonstration outside the Ministry of Justice for release of Job Sikhala and Allan Moyo; no turnout is given. S001 also notes a separate post-month action, but the reviewed sources do not reconcile whether the February 1 accounts describe different gatherings. Neither establishes E001’s physical endpoint. February reports are not merged into this January candidate.
