Albania, January 2022 — open-web research dossier

Status: first-pass research completed; candidates remain pending coordinator/human review. Fixed cutoff: 2026-10-07 America/Mexico_City. Unit ccode is the provisional-plan value 339. This dossier is separate from the integrated protest catalogue and does not assign official Mass Mobilization IDs.

Methods and coverage

Fourteen actual web searches (Q001–Q014) were captured with complete returned-result arrays, references, raw URLs, title order and observed clocks where available. Search paths included broad English and Albanian protest terms; Jan. 1–7, 15–21 and 22–31 windows; targeted 5 Maji, Skavica, Reporter.al, education/labor/teachers, regional place names (Durrës, Vlorë, Shkodër, Dibër), and December/February boundary follow-up. Q003’s Jan. 1–7 query also returned a Jan. 8 event; dates were checked against sources rather than inferred from query windows. Q014 targeted the Jan. 19 5 Maji episode.

Searches prioritized relevant local and national reporting and official follow-up. The source ledger holds 47 relevant URL cards, including inaccessible/search-only records. Opened reporting covers the Guardian/AP and Evropa e Lirë accounts of Jan. 8; 5 Maji reporting for Jan. 19, 27, 29 and 31 and Feb. 6/10 follow-up; the Jan. 19 Skavica opinion item; the Jan. 24 school-heating action; and Jan. 31 Durrës residents. Coverage is weighted toward Tirana; Q011 also searched several regional cities but did not provide comprehensive provincial press coverage.

No systematic LexisNexis or equivalent newspaper archive was available. The open-web results are not exhaustive and a lack of a validated result is not evidence that no protest occurred. Local-language queries and result pages helped identify items, but some Albanian text was summarized through translated tool output.

Candidate events

Eight leads are preserved in events.json. Seven have body-read event reporting, but none has verified turnout of at least 50. The Jan. 19 5 Maji lead is pending evidence because a Feb. 6 follow-up calls that the campaign’s first protest while contemporaneous reporting describes demolitions and resident resistance without establishing a public assembly or its size. Jan. 27, 29 and 31 5 Maji actions are individually reported public gatherings with unknown attendance; their shared campaign relationship and physical end remain open. The Jan. 31 report’s 430 homes is not protest turnout; the Feb. 6 “close to 500 homes” and Feb. 10 figure of 51 signed contracts are also not attendance figures.

Other leads include a Jan. 19 Skavica protest against the hydropower project (one opinion source, no count), a Jan. 24 Qëndresa Qytetare protest for school heating (no count), a Jan. 31 Durrës protest by more than 20 families (not individual attendance), and a Jan. 31 electricity-affordability protest lead based only on search-result discovery because the exact Citizens Channel URL returned HTTP 403. All are pending threshold or evidence.

One exclusion concerns the Jan. 8 Democratic Party headquarters action. Body-read accounts report hundreds and a three-hour conflict with arrests and dispersal, but identify the immediate aim as control of the opposition party and its leadership, not a specific demand on government policy. Future anti-government rhetoric by Berisha was not used to convert that intra-party conflict into a state-directed protest. A Dec. 20, 2021 anti-Open Balkan/Vučić action is excluded as pre-month with no evidence of continuation into January. Announced teachers’ actions are retained as an execution-evidence exclusion: sources establish plans but not that the planned stoppage/protest occurred; search-only cancellation reports are not treated as confirmed.

Continuity and boundaries

S027 mentions a broader anti-Skavica movement beginning in mid-2021 and a Nov. 14 Peshkopi protest; this is recorded as earlier campaign context, not as a continuous gathering or January-owned event. The Jan. 19 Skavica protest is a separate dated candidate. The 5 Maji campaign has post-month reporting on Feb. 6 and an official PM meeting on Feb. 10, but no January event’s physical end is established and the meeting is not assigned to a specific Jan. date.

Access and evidence limits

Every opened body has a fresh exact-URL access receipt in access_receipts/. S015 returned an anti-bot challenge and S028 returned HTTP 403; neither was bypassed. Cache misses affected S016, S017, S034–S037, S040, S042 and S044. Cache miss is recorded as technical failure, not denial. S033’s Reporter.al article excerpt ended mid-sentence. Search snippets remain discovery only. Guardian’s Jan. 8 story is explicitly AP-origin; no AP republication is counted as independent confirmation. Matching Dosja and Telegraf headlines for the Durrës report remain unresolved.

All seven state-response dimensions and protester violence remain unknown unless explicitly supported in a source. Police urging dispersal is recorded for Jan. 29 and Jan. 31; five residents were reported taken to a station on Jan. 31, but the wording does not establish formal arrest status. The Jan. 19 demolition/police operation is not transferred wholesale to a qualifying public gathering. No killings, shootings, beatings, accommodation or ignore value is inferred from silence.

Review readiness

This is an open-web research dossier, not an official baseline or final inclusion decision. The principal unresolved decisions are threshold for every lead; whether Jan. 19 5 Maji was a qualifying public gathering; whether Jan. 19/27/29/31 5 Maji are separate events or phases of a broader campaign; the end/continuity of that campaign; the owner/location and size of the Skavica action; and limited regional/sector coverage. Official baseline gate remains pending.
