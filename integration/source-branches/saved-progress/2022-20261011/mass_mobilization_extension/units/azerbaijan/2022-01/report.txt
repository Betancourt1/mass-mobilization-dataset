METHOD AND COVERAGE
Thirteen query invocations were captured as Q001–Q013 with 340 aligned returned-result headers. Grouped calls retain the complete original action and unpartitioned returned array; no per-query result totals were invented. Searches included general English, Azerbaijani and Russian protest, demonstration, strike, rally and march terms; English and Azerbaijani weekly windows; regional names (including Baku, Ganja, Sumqayit, Shaki, Quba and Lankaran); worker, education/wage, farm/land, price, prisoner and opposition leads; regional outlets (including Turan, JAMnews, OC Media and IRFS); and December 2021 / February 2022 boundary probes. Search snippets were used for discovery only.

The open-web approach does not reproduce LexisNexis or systematic newspaper coverage. No systematic local newspaper/archive search or independent native-language review was completed. Rural/local reporting, minority-language coverage and non-indexed sources remain weakly covered. Search silence is not evidence of absence.

X002 is the Ministry of Energy’s official memorial ceremony for the same anniversary, not a citizen protest (S005). Historical 1988–1990 protests and victims do not become January 2022 events.

X003 records the Baku rally for Mehman Huseynov’s release on 19 January 2019, outside this year (S003). It does not describe a public gathering of 50 or more, and the page metadata did not provide a publication date or year for those dates.

C001 routes the Saleh Rustamov release protest, dated in HRW’s caption to 1 December 2021, to the December 2021 owner. The captured January boundary searches did not find evidence of continuation into January. The event is not duplicated here.
