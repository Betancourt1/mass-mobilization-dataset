Scope and methods
This dossier concerns public protest candidates beginning in Afghanistan between 2021-01-01 and 2021-01-31. It follows relevant episode evidence into December 2020 and February/March 2021 and retains wrong-period discovery leads separately. It does not constitute an official MM update or an exhaustive census.

The checkpoint records 60 exact queries in 17 batches. Searches covered generic protest/demonstration/strike terms, Dari and Pashto terms, weekly/date windows, local and international media, salary/teacher activity, regional leads, violence, and retrospective human-rights records. The search service returned results for query batches rather than exposing reliable per-query hit counts. returned_urls therefore preserves each batch's union of relevant URLs with this limitation stated. Date operators did not reliably restrict results; weekly queries cannot establish week-by-week completeness. No results totals were invented.

The source register retains 55 relevant URLs: 16 opened, 4 blocked, 35 search_only. Opened status can mean partial article access; see each source's limitations. All publication dates are preserved only when known. The original May 2015 manual was opened at the educational mirror linked in the recovered protocol (S053); it is not evidence that the newest official codebook or MM baseline was acquired. The linked AIHRC original, a Killid page, a Pashto 8AM page, and a Hazara International repost remained inaccessible. News reports of an investigation are distinguished from reading its original report.

Candidate findings
E001: Nimroz, January 10. Opened contemporary sources describe residents protesting civilian deaths in an Afghan government airstrike. Anadolu's “dozens” does not prove at least 50. Eligibility pending_threshold. The earlier airstrike casualties are not coded as killings of protesters. Precise Zaranj geography remains partly search-only.

E002: Kabul, January 16. A January 18 KabulNow report explicitly dates a salary-equality gathering to January 16. No qualifying attendance is supplied. Eligibility pending_threshold.

E003: Kapisa, January 21. The opened Dari Pajhwok report explicitly states 300 teachers gathered at Guldan crossroads in the provincial center to demand government salary equality. Eligibility proposed_include, pending independent review. The English and Dari reports are the same agency/author, not two independent confirmations.

E004: Behsud, January 29. Hasht-e Subh's February 9 account of an AIHRC investigation describes about 350–400 residents outside Hesa-e-Awal district office. The inquiry account supports a domestic-state protest over the security deployment, negotiation, arrests, direct police shooting, and 11 civilian deaths. It describes an initially unarmed gathering and later stones/attempted mounted-gun use; protester_violence yes refers to that later action. Eligibility proposed_include, pending independent review. Earlier state accounts and later reporting differ on target, armed participation, casualties, and dates. None is silently reconciled. A January 12 convoy appointment is distinct from the January 29 protest; ecoi metadata and retrospective January 30 dating need checking against originals. Suspension/prosecutor referral is not a conviction or proven protest victory.

E005: Kabul, January 30. Pajhwok describes ACAA supporters before parliament demanding government salary equality and conditioning budget approval. No qualifying attendance. Eligibility pending_threshold.

E006: Behsud, January 31. A Dari report quotes a resistance-front spokesman alleging firing toward women and children gathered to see detainees. Whether this was a new public protest with an express domestic demand, whether 50 attended, and whether it belongs to the January 29 episode remain unresolved. Eligibility pending_unit; allegation not promoted into verified shooting coding. The subsequent armed confrontation is distinct from the civilian-gathering candidate.
