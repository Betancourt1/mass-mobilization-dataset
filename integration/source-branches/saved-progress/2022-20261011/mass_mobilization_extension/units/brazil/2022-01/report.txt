Brazil, January 2022 (unit brazil_2022-01) — first-pass research handoff

Method and scope
Captured 44 actual web-search invocations immediately after use, with 1063 returned result headers; the invocation count is not a search-result total. Queries used Portuguese terms and covered federal wages, transport fares, municipal health and labor, education/student activity, COVID/vaccine policy, cultural/transport policy, and racial-justice leads. Geographic leads included Brasília, Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo, Recife/Pernambuco, João Pessoa/Paraíba, Juazeiro do Norte/Ceará and Porto Alegre, with national federal-worker actions. This is open-web discovery, not systematic LexisNexis or paid-hemeroteca coverage. Search snippets remain discovery evidence.

Candidate events
1. Federal public servants’ wage-adjustment demonstration at the Central Bank — 18 Jan, Central Bank, Brasília. UOL body reports about 300 by the Central Bank’s estimate; a Poder360 search-result excerpt reports about 250 attributed to organizers. The demand was federal wage recomposition. Only this site’s count is used; planned Ministry of Economy actions and other cities are not included. Eligibility proposed_include; end and state responses unknown.
2. Protest against the proposed SuperVia rail-fare increase at Central do Brasil — 25 Jan, Central do Brasil, Rio. A Verdade and Repórter Popular separately say “centenas”/hundreds. AGETRANSP/CD Deliberação 1.223/21, reproduced on LegisWeb, authorized up to R$7 beginning 2 Feb; official search snippets say this tariff was not practiced and the R$5 fare continued. This supports a state-regulated policy target, though the protest reports do not name AGETRANSP as addressee. The event ended the same date (15:00–19:00 per Repórter Popular); service interruption/restart is not used as its end. Eligibility proposed_include, for coordinator review. The phrase “gás de borracha” is retained as the source’s attribution; no specific weapon, projectile, gunshot, injury or crowd-dispersal action is inferred.
3. Municipal doctors’ protest outside São Paulo City Hall — 19 Jan. Search-result-only reports say an approximate 100 doctors protested outside City Hall over hiring/work conditions; the CUT article body was not obtained. Retained as pending_evidence, separate from the enjoined strike.
