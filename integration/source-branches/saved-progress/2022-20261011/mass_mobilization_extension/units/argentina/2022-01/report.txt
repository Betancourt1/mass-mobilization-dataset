Argentina — January 2022 country-month research dossier

Unit: argentina_2022-01 (ccode 160). Research cutoff: 2026-10-07, America/Mexico_City. Status: researched_open_web_pending_review; official baseline remains pending.

Method and coverage

Twelve actual search invocations (Q001–Q012) are preserved as whole original responses: 45 original query strings and 380 aligned result headers. Searches covered general Spanish and English protest terms, four January weekly windows, local/regional results, agriculture and Armstrong, coastal/environmental mobilization, IMF/debt, social-organization road blockades, power outages, labor/sector queries, and December 2021/February 2022 boundaries. Search results remain discovery-only. Relevant contemporary Spanish- and English-language reports were opened after exact-URL preflight and manual review; one official ENRE page was read as context, not event response evidence. Source-origin and syndication claims remain limited to explicit attribution.

Coverage is broad but non-exhaustive. Open web is not LexisNexis “All News”; no full provincial newspaper/union/municipal archive was systematically reviewed. Search invocations contained multiple queries; their original result arrays were preserved whole, without assigning individual results to guessed subqueries. Local and provincial coverage outside the identified coastal, Santa Fe, Buenos Aires/AMBA and La Plata areas remains uncertain. Relevant access failures and unread search-only leads are recorded in sources.jsonl; the HTTP 402 Clarín page was not bypassed, and Cache miss responses were not treated as denials.

Candidate inventory

Eight January leads are retained: two proposed inclusions, two pending threshold and four pending evidence.

• E001 — 4 January Atlanticazo at Mar del Plata: police estimate reported by LA NACION exceeds 3,000; AFP and 0223 describe thousands. Proposed include for this site. Other coastal city reports are not assigned the Mar del Plata count; national event scope remains open.
• E002 — 8 January Armstrong self-convened agricultural producers’ assembly: LA NACION reports more than 500 and opposition to national export restrictions. Proposed include. Other agricultural source leads were search-only/cache-missed, and one similarly titled Infocampo tractorazo was an unrelated 2008 event.
• E003 — 26 January social-organization blockades at several Buenos Aires metropolitan sites: six hours, with explicit government-policy demands; no attendee count. Pending threshold and multi-site unit review.
• E004 — 27 January march from the Obelisk to the Economy Ministry against IMF payments: date, demands and route are supported by LA NACION and Reuters Connect; neither body provides turnout. Pending threshold.
• E005 — 5 January Boedo/Garay outage blockade: dated local action, but area-wide “hundreds” cannot be assigned to the single site; demands appear mainly directed at private utilities, and a state-policy target is not established. Pending evidence.
• E006 — 26–27 January Arana outage blockade: public action is reported; attendance, government target and physical end are unknown. Pending evidence.
• E007 — Mataderos/General Paz outage piquete: search-only lead; body Cache miss, actual onset date (publication date is not substituted), and threshold unverified. Pending evidence.
• E008 — “First piquete of the year” on Avenida 9 de Julio: body blocked by HTTP 402; exact date, turnout and demands are unverified. Pending evidence.

Excluded/out-of-month records separately preserve the 2008 Armstrong tractorazo, the February 8 IMF march, a search-only “1F” February lead and the Parque Pereyra outage complaints without evidence of a public gathering. The February 8 date corrects a preliminary Jan25 inference from S024; Reuters and Anadolu reports support the February boundary reading. S024, S032 and S033 have not been used as January event evidence.

One possible continuation lead from 31 December 2021 records a TN caption of a San Telmo outage protest. No count, qualifying state target, January continuation or closure is established, so it is not a January event. No January candidate has a confirmed event-specific state response; all seven response fields and protester-violence values remain unknown unless directly evidenced. General ENRE Resolution 8/2022 is not linked to any specific protest.

Open gaps

The 11 records in gaps.json cover method/exhaustiveness, regional coverage, Atlanticazo scope, Armstrong count/source access, Jan26/27 turnout, outage-event thresholds and target, blocked/cache-missed sources, Dec/Jan continuity, and event-specific responses/end dates. In particular, residents affected by outages, consumers, workers, or multi-site “hundreds” are not equated with 50 protesters at a named site. Planned events and search snippets are not treated as completed actions. Dates outside January are not merged into this unit.
