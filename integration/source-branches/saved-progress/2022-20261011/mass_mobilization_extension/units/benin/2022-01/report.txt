Benin, January 2022 — research handoff

Scope and method
The assigned unit is Benin, 2022-01 only. I recorded 17 actual search invocations in search_log.jsonl, including full query arrays and ordered result headers/URLs. Searches were organized by the four weeks of January and supplemented with French/English terms for protest, march, strike, sit-in, meeting, worker/student/health-sector action, and place names including Cotonou, Porto-Novo, Abomey-Calavi, Ouidah, Parakou, Natitingou, Djougou, Kandi, Bohicon and Lokossa. I also searched political/opposition, human-rights/security, market/transport and regional themes. A discovery attempt named Fon, Yoruba, Bariba, Dendi and Fulfulde; no relevant local-language record surfaced. One early search-capture call was delayed until the next capture invocation; both actual invocations were subsequently captured.

Findings
One January candidate remains pending: E001, the 28 January union press breakfast in Cotonou. The opened Banouto report S001 records demands on wage revision, rising prices and taxes, but gives no attendee count or public-access evidence and does not establish that a protest took place. The Bourse du Travail venue and reference to six union centres come only from the search excerpt for S005. Eligibility remains pending_threshold; there is no claim that a qualifying 50-person public protest occurred.

The Le Béninois Libéré COTEB/Parakou result S007 is an unresolved lead only. Its search excerpt associates former-worker payments with political intervention but does not show a strike or public gathering. The source returned Cache miss, followed by DNS failure on one ordinary exact-URL request. No candidate was created from the headline.

X001 excludes the 29 January Kogandji/Savalou police operation and armed clash: the opened S004 account contains no public protest or domestic policy demand; the reported deaths are attributed to the police director and remain uncorroborated. Search-only Amnesty S008 also describes resistance to an arrest, not a government-policy demonstration. X002 is a Nigeria/Edo State false positive, not the Republic of Benin. X003 is the official 10 January Vodun celebration at Ouidah, not a protest.

Boundary and continuations
No event starting before January was identified in the available searches; continuations.json is empty. Later cost-of-living actions are visible in search-only sources: a planned/held union meeting on 18 February (S010–S012) and a CSTB meeting on 25 February (S013). They are post-month actions, not continuations of the Jan 28 press event. A CSTB communiqué about January salary deductions was published in February (S014); the search excerpt describes a communiqué rather than a public gathering.

Access, limits and review
S001 and S004 were opened after fresh, unique access receipts and stable manual checks of canonical access evidence and every registered physical 2022 draft source ledger. S002 returned HTTP 403 and was not retried. S003, S005 and S007 had technical Cache miss/DNS failures; no denial was observed for those URLs, and only one ordinary HTTPS attempt to each exact URL followed a fresh check. No alternate URL, host, AMP route or publisher alias was used. Access receipts are saved under access_receipts/.
