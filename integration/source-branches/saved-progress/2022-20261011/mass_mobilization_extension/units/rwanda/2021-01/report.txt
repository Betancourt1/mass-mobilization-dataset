Rwanda — January 2021 research report

Scope and method
The assigned unit is Rwanda, 1–31 January 2021, using provisional code 517 and the fixed event cutoff 7 October 2026 (America/Mexico_City). I ran 17 separate open-web searches, with one query per call, and preserved each literal query, execution time, returned URL/title/reference headers, and limits in search_log.jsonl. Searches covered four weekly date windows, broad event terms, French, Kinyarwanda terms (imyigaragambyo/kwigaragambya), local outlets including Kigali Today, IGIHE, and The New Times, official records, retrospective human-rights reporting, and December/February boundaries. Search-engine date filters and result ordering were unreliable; returned-result counts are not coverage measures.

Findings
No January 2021 gathering is verified. events.json retains one source-only motorbike-taxi gathering lead as pending_evidence because a U.S. Department of State search result says “January 13” but omits the year, and the report is titled for 2022. The direct official page returned “Exception: forbidden.” I did not retry or open an alternate copy; a search-result reprint is retained only as dependent discovery, not independent evidence. The snippet’s “groups” and “significant” do not establish the 50-person threshold; locality, year, and physical boundaries remain unknown. Its “peaceful” and police-engagement/dispersal claims were not body-verified, so violence and all seven state-response dimensions remain unknown. It is not presented as a verified January 2021 event.

Two out-of-period leads are documented in excluded.json. A Kigali Today Kinyarwanda article dated 9 February 2021 describes Gatsibo students refusing classes around 6–8 February; the reported 16 arrests and school enrollment of 372 are not turnout counts, and a demand to the national government is unresolved. A UK Home Office country note reproduces an ACLED entry dated 15 April 2020 about a Gashora refugee-camp protest over COVID-related living conditions and police intervention. A separate Wikipedia search excerpt may describe the same older episode, but that match is unverified. No evidence establishes either lead as a continuation into January, so continuations.json is empty.

Sources and limits
The dossier has 8 unique source cards: 3 opened, 1 blocked, and 4 search-only. Opened material includes the local February report, the UK Home Office retrospective note, and UNHCR’s January 2021 operational update. UNHCR reports 316 evacuees at Gashora but is not comprehensive protest monitoring; its silence does not establish no protest. One matching FLN-hosted copy of the Home Office note is retained under its earliest source ID with both discovery references and is marked dependent, not independent corroboration.

The work is open-web research, not a LexisNexis-equivalent search. Local-language retrieval and translation may be incomplete, and the Kinyarwanda summary has not received independent linguistic review. The official latest dataset bytes/universe and additional Coding Procedure remain unresolved; baseline_gate stays pending_official_baseline. No official ID, final approval, absence claim, or exhaustiveness claim is made.
