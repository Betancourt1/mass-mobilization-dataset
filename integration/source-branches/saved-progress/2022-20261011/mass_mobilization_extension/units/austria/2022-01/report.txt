Visual checks of physical pages 10, 11, 14, 16, 18, 19 and 20 confirmed row/date/place/count alignment for cited matches. Examples include two Jan 19 Linz rows (20 and 1,400), three Jan 30 Salzburg rows (250, 250, 2,500), two Jan 22 Graz rows (200, 13,500), Jan 9 Innsbruck (6,000), Jan 20 Bregenz (1,000), and Jan 22 Bregenz (5,000). These are place/date-level official approximate counts, not event-level response data. They are not assumed independent of police counts carried by ORF. The p.20 text explicitly says Vienna had 546 notified assemblies but authorities could not identify COVID-related or actually held events, and did not perform a retrospective manual evaluation. State totals and notified counts are not assigned to individual gatherings.

Ministry matches are labeled as likely or possible where the table lacks assembly identifiers. Separate Graz gatherings, same-day Salzburg events, Jan 20 Vienna gatherings and the Jan 29 merged Vienna march remain granular candidates with pending decisions rather than being merged from common city/date.
