Cameroon, January 2022 — first open-web research pass

Status: researched_open_web_pending_review. The official baseline and supplemental original coding procedure remain unverified; this dossier is provisional and does not assign official event IDs or update the baseline.

Coverage: 21 captured web-search invocations (487 returned result headers, including repeats and irrelevant results), with batched queries, in English and French. Weekly windows explicitly covered 1–7, 8–14, 15–21, and 22–31 January, alongside full-month and year searches. Queries covered Douala, Yaoundé, Bamenda, Buea, Garoua and other regional terms; political/opposition marches, Anglophone-region civic activity, students and teachers, labor and transport, rights/prisoners, prices, land, and December/February boundaries. Search results were retained progressively in search_log.jsonl. Thirteen source cards preserve one opened report and twelve search-only items, including boundary or non-protest leads. Open-web searches cannot substitute for LexisNexis, local print/radio archives, or local-language searches.

Finding: no actual January public protest gathering meeting the dataset threshold was verified in this first pass, so events.json remains empty. The opened Actu Cameroun article, dated 9 January, explicitly says the SDF march planned for 8 January in Douala did not occur after prohibitions by sub-prefects. It reports police surrounding organizer Jean Michel Nintcheu’s home and a security deployment, but does not report a public crowd; those facts are not coded as a crowd-directed response. The article gives inconsistent Douala arrondissement lists in its lead/body (1, 2, 3) and quoted communiqué (1, 3, 5). It says the organizer intended a new schedule, but no later occurrence was found.

The SDF Peace Week announcement for 4–9 January is a separate planned sequence of proposed demonstrations, meetings, conferences/debates and visits. The SDF page remained search-only after two CacheMiss tool responses; these were technical retrieval failures, not HTTP denials or publisher restrictions. No evidence here confirms which announced activities occurred. A later Africa-Press result may be a republication; origin and execution are unverified. Keep the prohibited march and announced Peace Week separate.

Excluded leads are narrowly limited: (1) the scheduled SDF march explicitly reported not to have occurred; (2) the separate unverified Peace Week plan; (3) an Amnesty detainee-release advocacy report with no new gathering surfaced; (4) the 24 January Olembé sports-spectator crowd disaster; (5) a Garoua CAN football-support mobilization without an identified protest demand; and (6) a later report of a Bambui funeral event, not a protest. These are not claims that no other gatherings existed. An undated Cameroon News Agency headline about a CDC/Buea Council staff strike remains an unresolved date lead rather than a January candidate.

Boundary review: search results about the teacher sit-down strike place its start on 21 February 2022 and student street marches in March. No January onset or continuation was verified; preserve those later-unit records in their own months. No qualifying December continuation or January event extending into February was verified.
