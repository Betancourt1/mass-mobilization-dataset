Search coverage

The draft search log contains Q001–Q018, 344 returned headers in 18 whole-response records. Searches include broad English/French/Arabic queries, weekly French sweeps for 1–7, 8–14, 15–21 and 22–31 January, Arabic second-half coverage, and targeted searches for contract teachers, court registrars, taxi drivers and tax staff. Q018 added a regional Kabylie query in French and a Taqbaylit phrase; its actual full 28-header response is preserved without splitting the multi-query result. Local/regional discoveries include Annaba (Sarih), Kabylie (Liberté), and Algiers/Mila/Ferjioua (Echorouk). December and February boundary evidence includes the 30 December court-registrar sit-in (S011), the 2 February taxi sit-in (S006), and the 7 February substitute/contract-teacher sit-in (S001). Boundary sources are not treated as January actions unless a January episode is separately established.

The source ledger contains 13 cards: 12 opened and 1 search-only. Opened reporting is in Arabic and French, mainly from Ennahar, Echorouk, Sarih and Liberté; the retrospective Open Data Tank catalogue page is used only for scope context. El Watan’s tax-strike article (S003) remains search-only after a web-tool 502 and ordinary DNS-resolution failure; neither is a publisher denial. The lawyers’ Liberté report (S012) confirms an indefinite nationwide court boycott from 13 January through at least 16 January, but reports no public assembly or turnout. The local baker report (S013) confirms a Tizi Ouzou work stoppage and bread-supply impact, but no gathering. The linked Open Data Tank dataset and codebook were not retrieved. No LexisNexis search or official baseline comparison was performed.

Candidate and episode decisions

C001 retains reported court-registrar strike/sit-in activity across multiple courts. January activity is reported, but exact onset, cross-site coordination, threshold, and the relationship between the 30 December one-day Sidi M’hamed sit-in and early-January activity remain uncertain. The 3 January “third day” wording suggests 1 January only if literal. The 2 January item’s relative Sunday/Thursday language has an uncertain publication-date anchor. This is pending evidence, not a confirmed January inclusion.

C002 records the 23 January taxi-driver sit-in outside the Algiers Transport Directorate over a disputed route authorization. The source confirms a public, state-directed gathering but gives no turnout; it remains pending the 50-person threshold. The 19–20 January stoppage is kept separate, and the February 2 gathering does not prove the January sit-in continued.

Six other records are excluded from the event candidate list with provenance: a contract-teacher action announced for 16 January but not verified as occurring; the tax employees’ 5 January stoppage; the national tax strike reported for 18–20 January; taxi drivers’ 19–20 January work stoppage; the Tizi Ouzou bakers’ work stoppage; and the UNOA nationwide lawyer strike/court boycott. For the stoppages and court boycott, the opened accounts do not establish a public gathering and an attendance threshold. Tax-sector workforce size, Annaba’s 96% strike response, lawyer boycott percentages and lawyer workforce counts are not gathering counts. The January 16 teacher article documents a call, not execution. No continuation is recorded because sources do not establish uninterrupted physical continuity across the month boundary.

Open gaps include participant thresholds for both candidates; exact court-registrar onset, scope, and close; the taxi sit-in’s duration and later administrative response; incomplete language/region coverage; the El Watan body access failure; and largely unreported state-response and violence fields. Search silence is not coded as absence of protest or response. The status record is synchronized with the saved files: 18 queries, 13 sources, 2 candidates, 6 exclusions, 0 continuations and 7 gaps. Human review and the official baseline gate remain pending.
