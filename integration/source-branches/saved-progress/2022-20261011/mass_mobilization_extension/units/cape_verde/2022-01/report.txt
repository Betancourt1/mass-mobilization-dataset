Cape Verde — January 2022

Status: first-pass open-web research; pending coordinator review and the source-ledger stability window. This is not an official update.

Scope and method
- 43 actual web-search invocations were captured immediately in search_log.jsonl, retaining the returned headers, result order, exact URLs and web references. Searches used Portuguese and English terms for protests, demonstrations, marches, strikes, stoppages, vigils and gatherings; week/date windows; place and island names; and labor, teacher, student, health, transport, price, utility, justice and resident concerns. Outlets searched or targeted included RTC, A Nação, Inforpress, Tiver, Mindel Insite, Expresso das Ilhas and A Semana.
- I also tried three Kriolu-form search variants. They did not surface a clearly relevant January 2022 gathering. This does not establish that no such action was reported in Kriolu.
- Search results are discovery evidence only. The current source ledger contains 14 cards, all marked search_only; no article bodies have been read in this pass. Open-web results are not a substitute for the source universe or LexisNexis-style archival coverage. Search-engine date filters and source ordering are not comprehensive.

Potential January events

The event is retained as pending_evidence. Attendance and the >=50 threshold, the precise domestic state addressee, organizer, duration, and any response remain unknown. The exact body could not be retrieved: one web-tool timeout happened before content and a later ordinary HTTPS fallback failed DNS before HTTP. The corrected 233-input post-attempt map also changed across six sibling ledgers. No publisher denial or body read is recorded; under the coordinator's explicit no-retry instruction, no further request will be made for S008.
- E002: BUALA search-result text reports a 20 January Marxa Cabral at Achada Grande Frente in Praia, framed as cultural resistance and Cabralist/pan-African organizing. It is retained as pending_evidence, not as an eligible protest. Physical assembly details, attendance and any specific demand directed at Cape Verde's government or policy are unverified; this may be a commemorative/cultural event outside the dataset target rule.

Actual onset, form, turnout, private-employer versus domestic-state demands, and the episode boundary remain unknown; it is not coded as a January event or continuation.

Search-only results also report a February 1 occurrence. February 1 lies outside this unit, and no earlier January physical assembly is identified. A distinct March 22 Expresso das Ilhas report is recorded as S014, search_only, and used only as a bounded indication that the action occurred after this unit.

Union-front stories forecast labor instability without identifying a specific January action. These leads remain traceable in excluded.json.
