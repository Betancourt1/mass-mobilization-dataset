Cambodia — January 2022 first-pass research (completed; pending review)

Scope and method: 45 captured web-search invocations in English and Khmer covered weekly/monthly windows, Phnom Penh and regional leads, and labor, land/forest, political/rights, student/teacher, price, fuel and transport terms. Every search response was captured promptly; the headers are discovery evidence, not event proof. The work surfaced 38 relevant source cards. Seven articles were opened after exact-URL fresh access checks and stable canonical/registered-sibling ledger review. No private article-body copies were saved. The Sithi timeline page remained search-only after a tool Cache miss and a subsequent DNS failure before HTTP; this is a technical retrieval failure, not a publisher denial. S040’s earlier manual-gate attempt failed before a stable review completed, so its body was not requested.

January observations and unresolved episode ownership:

E001 — Jan. 3 NagaWorld workers gathered around the Australian Embassy/NagaWorld II area. CamboJA reports about 500 at the embassy; VOD reports around 300–400 near NagaWorld and several hundred at an adjacent strike site. Do not add or average these potentially overlapping/moving groups. The physical Jan. 3 gathering ended around 6 p.m. according to VOD; this is not the end of the broader strike. Arrest accounts differ: RFA’s Jan. 3 article says 14; CamboJA says at least 15; VOD updates 14 to 15; RFA’s Jan. 4 article gives 17 for Monday. Preserve the estimates as different reports/updates, not a sum. RFA’s Jan. 3 article describes hundreds of riot police deployed to suppress the strike but not a specific crowd-clearing act; E001 dispersal is based on VOD’s described barricades and park clearing. VOD carries an attributed beating allegation; embedded media was not independently inspected. Eligibility remains pending_unit.

E002 — RFA reports a Jan. 4 gathering near the National Assembly demanding release of three arrested union leaders; LICADHO says Chhim Sithar was arrested while trying to join striking workers in front of the Australian Embassy. No source gives an event-specific Jan. 4 crowd count. RFA’s cumulative “hundreds since Dec. 18” and LICADHO’s later estimate of about 600 each afternoon as of Jan. 18 are not assigned to Jan. 4. Keep pending_threshold and pending_unit review; preserve both location descriptions without forcing a precise site match.

E003 — CamboJA reports around 400 workers peacefully continuing the strike on Jan. 6, with demands to resolve the labor dispute and release union leaders/members. The exact gathering site and physical end are unspecified. Prior arrests are not coded as Jan. 6 event responses. The Labor Ministry issued a same-day letter about final payments, but the article does not establish an event-specific agreed meeting or accommodation. Eligibility remains pending_unit.

All three January dates are documented observations in recurring NagaWorld protest activity; they are not automatically new January episode onsets or proof of one uninterrupted month-long street assembly. CamboJA S018 says the broader strike had been underway since late November. RFA and LICADHO point to daily demonstrations/drawn participation from around Dec. 18, while CamboJA S019 reports Dec. 19. Strike onset and public-demonstration onset may differ; the related December episode remains the monthly owner pending review. No crowd estimate is carried across dates.

Other leads and exclusions/gaps: CamboJA S027 describes a local land redistribution dispute and reports five families had protested local authorities, but supplies no date or turnout for any physical gathering. A livestream and Jan. 29 arrests of three people do not establish a January mass assembly, so no land event candidate is coded. Weak/unclear leads retained as gaps include the search-only Jan. 17 embassy-petition report (S040; petition signatures are not attendance), Oral forest camp/patrol and proposed blockade reports (S029, S031, S032), and a search-only human-rights timeline (S022). No candidate is inferred from a proposed plan, patrol, signature count, nearby arrest or crowd figure from another date. The LICADHO 64-worker detention result concerns Feb. 21 and is outside January; no February arrests/counts are transferred.

Coverage limits: Searches did not surface another clearly documented January gathering at or above 50 in student/teacher, transport, prices/fuel or other factory sectors. Local-language archive coverage and LexisNexis-equivalent hemeroteca access were unavailable, so this first pass does not establish absence or exhaustive coverage.
