Scope and discovery
The assignment covers events beginning 1–31 January 2022. The saved search log contains 34 captured English- and Danish-language search invocations. Queries used month-wide and weekly windows, focused on Copenhagen, Aarhus, Odense and Aalborg, with student/education, labor, Covid restrictions, climate, farming, prices, racism, police, rights, and December/February boundary terms. Follow-up discovery refined the January 8 Aarhus and Copenhagen leads and the January 12 Student Rebellion ’22 actions. This is documented open-web coverage; it is not an exhaustive review of all regions or sectors.
