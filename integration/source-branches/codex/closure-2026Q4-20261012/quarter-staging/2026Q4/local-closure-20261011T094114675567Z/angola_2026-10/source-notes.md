## Confirmed event candidate A — Caminhada Outubro Rosa / Caminhada Rosa

- **Event date:** 2026-10-03 (local date, Luanda/Angola; planned start 08:00 in the PRESSdigi report).
- **Place/route:** Ingombota, Luanda; Avenida 4 de Fevereiro from the Porto de Luanda to Praça de Eventos da Baía de Luanda.
- **Type:** public awareness / health solidarity walk; mass mobilization, not described as protest.
- **Organizers:** Administração Municipal de Ingombota / Município da Ingombota.
- **Reported size:** RNA says “Milhares de pessoas participaram” (thousands participated). PRESSdigi says “Mais de 15 mil pessoas participaram” (>15,000).
- **Publication dates:** RNA, 2026-10-04 (article metadata says 14:40:09 in alternate `turn27search25`; accessible site page `turn31view0` dates it Oct 4). PRESSdigi report, 2026-10-06 19:41 (page `turn25view0`, lines 15–17).

PRESSdigi also reports the event; its page credits Government of Luanda as principal source (`turn25view0`, lines 52–56; https://press.digi.ao/artigo/ingombota-veste-se-de-rosa-e-reune-mais-de-15-mil-participantes-em-caminhada-pela-prevencao-do-cancro-da-mama/). Its separate related report says >15k and a second story says “milhares”; treat the participant count as an attributed report, not independently audited.
- **Short excerpts:** RNA: “Milhares de pessoas participaram neste sábado, 03 de Outubro” (`turn31view0`, lines 53–54). PRESSdigi: “Mais de 15 mil pessoas participaram, no sábado, 3 de Outubro” (`turn25view0`, lines 27–30).
