# Venezuela — public protest discovery notes (staging only)

Scope: events in Venezuela occurring 2026-10-01–2026-10-07 inclusive; local date America/Caracas. Managed searches and opens on 2026-10-11, approx. 03:20–03:21 UTC. Exploratory only.

## Candidates

**2026-10-01 onward, multiple states:** protests over daily electricity blackouts, including street marches, road blockades, cacerolazos, and protests at public offices. El Pitazo, on Oct 2, reports actions on the night of Oct 1 in six states (Zulia, Sucre, Aragua, Falcón, Carabobo, Portuguesa). El Impulso reports the Oct 1 night actions in at least five states. La Jornada reports an ongoing multi-day wave with around 20 local protests in seven states, with Oct 1 described as the day with the most protests (at least 10 in Carabobo); the sequence began before Oct 1, so treat as continuing mobilization with in-window activity, not a newly starting event. Strong multi-location mobilization candidate; exact attendance totals unavailable.

**2026-10-01, Caracas:** El Diario reports a gathering of retirees, pensioners, and civil-society members at Parque Carabobo seeking improved pensions and benefits. Attendance size is not supplied; retain as a separate small/unknown-size protest candidate.

A PSUV mobilization scheduled for Oct 7 surfaced, but the query results did not yield enough accessible after-action evidence to include it as an event (`turn319search0`, `turn319search9` announcement; Facebook result `turn319search1` is not a suitable verification source). Treat as planned/unverified.
