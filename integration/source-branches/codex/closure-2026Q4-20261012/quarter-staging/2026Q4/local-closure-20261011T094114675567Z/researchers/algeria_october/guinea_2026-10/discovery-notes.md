## Excluded in-window civic event

The Guinean Presidency’s homepage reports that an Oct. 2, 2026 national-independence celebration included a large civil-military/paramilitary parade on Conakry’s Boulevard de la Nation, with civilians and official bodies participating. It is a state civic celebration, not evidence of a protest or oppositional mass mobilization, so it is excluded. Publication/event date: Oct. 2, 2026. Short excerpt: “le grand défilé civilo-militaire et paramilitaire marquant le 68ᵉ anniversaire de l’indépendance nationale”. Source: https://presidence.gov.gn/accueil/ ; opened `turn99view6` / `turn101view3` (retrieved 2026-10-11 03:08:35 UTC; lines 124–137).

Guinee360 published Oct. 1, 2026 a report on truck traffic restrictions in Conakry from Oct. 1 at 16:00 to Oct. 3 at 08:00, expressly “à l’occasion de la célébration de la fête du 2 octobre.” This is transport/safety administration for the holiday, not a protest lead. Source: https://www.guinee360.com/01/10/2026/fete-du-2-octobre-la-circulation-des-gros-porteurs-camions-et-camionnettes-interdite-a-conakry/ ; `turn85search16`, `turn99view5`, `turn101view2` (publication Oct. 1; opened 2026-10-11 03:09:07 UTC; lines 33–49).
