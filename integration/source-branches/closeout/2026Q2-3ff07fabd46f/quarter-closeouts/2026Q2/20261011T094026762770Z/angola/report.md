# Angola — 2026 Q2 public-source discovery

**Status: provisional discovery only.** The coordinator confirmed the audited handoff identifies Angola's three monthly units as new, unclaimed, and without partial or completed work. The portable schema and formal validation kit remain pending; this is not a canonical dataset or a claim of completion.

## First-pass findings

- **April:** CPJ reports a protest by former Sonangol employees in Luanda on April 6 and says the same protest was being covered on April 1. CPJ published its account on April 17. Treat the dates as one unresolved event lead until event-level review determines whether there were separate gatherings. The report attributes the details to the journalist and his lawyer.
- **May:** Public-media workers' union SJA announced a strike for May 18–20 in a May 11 report. A May 15 report says workers accepted a government proposal and suspended the planned first phase before it began. Do not record it as an observed May strike based on this discovery pass. The scheduled end date varies by outlet (May 20 or May 21).
- **June:** ELISAL workers in Luanda began an intermittent strike reported as underway by June 12. A Lusa-attributed account says June 12 was day two and reports union allegations of police use of tear gas and detentions; the company disputed the strike's legality. Novo Jornal and government press center CIAM both reported the first phase and return-to-work around June 16. Sources differ on the strike duration, so exact boundaries need validation.

A Makamavulo page claims former Sonangol workers announced protest dates for June 1–6, but its search listing says May 28 while the page displays September 8, 2026. Since those dates conflict and targeted follow-up did not find corroboration, it remains excluded as an event claim pending verification.

## Search and source handling

This pass ran targeted Portuguese and English searches for April, May, and June, then opened accessible relevant public pages. The full query ledger, UTC windows, source URLs, publication dates, separately stated event dates, bounded excerpts, provenance, uncertainty, and failed-access notes are in `discovery.json`. Initial results included many older Angola events. In particular, HRW's 2026 report labels its coverage “Events of 2025”; it is not evidence of Q2 2026 events. Search results also included a large papal mass in Angola (not a protest), Angola, Indiana, and Portugal/Brazil strike notices.

Search access was uneven: VerAngola and Angola24Horas pages returned 403 responses, Correio da Kianda returned a verification interstitial, and a DW search was blocked by robots.txt. Available snippets are identified as snippets and not described as opened source content.

This bounded pass found candidate leads, not a complete event census. A no-findings result for any month would not establish that no protest occurred.
