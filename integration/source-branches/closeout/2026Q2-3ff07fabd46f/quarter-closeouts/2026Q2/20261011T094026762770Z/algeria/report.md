# Algeria — Q2 2026 public-source discovery

**Status:** provisional first discovery pass only. This is not a validated event inventory, a completeness claim, or a formal scientific-kit result. No canonical schema or migration was created.

## Search outcome

I ran 30 targeted public web searches on 2026-10-11: three multilingual batches covering April, May, and June (English, French, and Arabic for each month), followed by three searches focused on the June health-sector strike. Exact query strings are preserved in [discovery.json](discovery.json). The web interface did not expose a per-query UTC timestamp. The search/open sequence is bracketed by clock readings of 03:03:23–03:04:17 UTC on 2026-10-11; that is the available access-time precision.

The pass found one potentially relevant June labor action, but only as a **planned strike whose suspension was reported**. It did not verify that the action occurred. For April, a widely circulated call for an April 20 national strike was explicitly denied by UGTA and should be excluded as an event. For May, the pass found a general May Day mobilization appeal without a specified event, plus a commemoration report snippet that does not establish a protest. Search results did not support a verified demonstration for April or May. These are search outcomes only; no finding does not establish that no event occurred.

## Provisional leads

- **June — health-sector strike announcement, occurrence unverified.** Radio Algérienne reported on June 2 at 17:03 that a court in Blida ordered suspension of a national SNAMARSP strike scheduled for June 2–3. The article attributes this to a Ministry of Health statement and notes a conciliation meeting on May 24. This supports recording a planned labor action and a suspension report, but not claiming that a strike or street protest took place. [Radio Algérienne source](https://news.radioalgerie.dz/fr/node/87423). An Algérie Éco page republishes the same APS/Ministry account and is not independent corroboration. Follow-up should seek union communications or independent reporting from June 2–3.

- **April — purported April 20 national strike call denied.** Algérie 360 reported on April 19 that UGTA rejected an anonymous online document calling for an indefinite national strike starting April 20 as fabricated. Treat this as an excluded rumor/denial, not a protest event. [Algérie 360 source](https://www.algerie360.com/greve-nationale-le-20-avril-lugta-met-fin-a-la-rumeur/).

- **May — May Day mobilization appeal, no event details.** PADS issued a statement dated April 30 calling for May 1 to be a day of worker mobilization. It gives no venue, time, attendance, or report that a mobilization occurred, so retain it only as a low-specificity advocacy lead. [Solidnet/PADS statement](https://www.solidnet.org/article/PADS-Algeria-May-1st-2026-A-Day-of-Mobilization-and-Struggle-of-Workers-Against-Capitalist-Exploitation-and-Imperialist-Wars/).

## Exclusions and source limitations

The official May 19 student-day page describes a wreath-laying/remembrance ceremony for the historical May 19, 1956 student strike, not a 2026 protest. The May 2 APS/UGTA search snippet describes International Workers' Day commemorations in Algiers; opening the APS page returned a maintenance shell, so I did not classify the item as a protest. A June 8 *Le Monde* profile surfaced through the parent handoff, but it is subscriber-only; no bypass was attempted, and the public preview concerns the Hirak's 2019 movement and events in 2021 rather than a June 2026 protest. Search results also surfaced 2019 and 2025 protest/strike coverage, which was excluded as wrong year.
