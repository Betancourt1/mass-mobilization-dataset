2. **COSATU nationwide protest action — Oct. 7, 2026.** eNCA (`turn233search5`, opened `turn237view0`, https://www.enca.com/news-top-stories/cosatu-stages-nationwide-protests-over-jobs-wages-and-cost-living) is dated Wednesday, Oct. 7, 09:00; lines 26-37 report COSATU was embarking on nationwide protests over retrenchments, minimum wage compliance, collective bargaining, unemployment, poverty and inequality. The Oct. 6 notice establishes announced intent; eNCA's Oct. 7 report supports that action took place but does not quantify turnout or enumerate locations.
3. **Cape Town silent demonstration and solidarity walk — Oct. 7, 2026.** Voice of the Cape (`turn233search13`, opened `turn237view2`, https://vocfm.co.za/cape-town-groups-hold-silent-demonstration-and-solidarity-walk-for-palestine/) is dated Oct. 7; lines 76-82 report organizations held a demonstration outside the Holocaust Museum, followed by a CBD solidarity walk ending with vigil at St George’s Cathedral.
