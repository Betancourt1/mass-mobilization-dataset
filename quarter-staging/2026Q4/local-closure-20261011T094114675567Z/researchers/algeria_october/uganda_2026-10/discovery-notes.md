## Candidate: political mobilization, not protest

**PLU Wakiso District Baraza / endorsement gathering — Oct. 3, Kira Playground, Wakiso.** The Patriotic League of Uganda's own report, published Oct. 3 (`turn337search9`, opened `turn341view0`, https://plu.org.ug/news/wakiso-endorsements-reach-5-000-as-plu-baraza-deli/), says at lines 35 and 45 the event was held Saturday at Kira Playground, bringing together national leaders, grassroots mobilizers, and members of the public. Lines 48-50 report over 300 new member registrations and an endorsement exercise. Kampala Post's independent same-day report (`turn337search14`, opened `turn341view1`, https://kampalapost.com/articles/pastor-bugingo-fires-up-kira-plu-baraza-as-toyota-pushes-for-ubuntu) says at lines 80-95 that thousands attended and frames the baraza as mobilization for Muhoozi Kainerugaba's future presidential bid. This is party-aligned electoral/community mobilization, not an oppositional protest. Include only if the project's mass-mobilization scope includes political rallies/barazas; exact total turnout is only a press estimate.
