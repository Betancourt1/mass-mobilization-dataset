## Candidate

**2026-10-04, Warsaw:** “Nie dla klatek” (No to cages) demonstration outside the Ministry of Agriculture on World Animal Day against proposed minimum kennel-cage rules for dogs. Accessible Polish reporting says over 100 attended, some with their dogs. Candidate public demonstration; crowd over 100 is reported secondhand from RMF FM and remains a relatively small event, so the mass threshold is uncertain.
