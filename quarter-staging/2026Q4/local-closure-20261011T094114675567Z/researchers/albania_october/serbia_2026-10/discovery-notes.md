## Candidate

**2026-10-07, Belgrade:** student-organized protest in front of the Republic Election Commission (RIK), named “Neće moći ove noći,” opposing an election-commission decision on diaspora voting arrangements. Nova.rs live reporting says students and citizens gathered, filed an objection, and streets were closed; Reuters Connect has a dated event photo of participants.
