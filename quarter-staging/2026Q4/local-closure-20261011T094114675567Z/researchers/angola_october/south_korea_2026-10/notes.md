Search provider reported robots.txt blocks for `news.naver.com` and `yna.co.kr` in the initial batch; this was an access restriction, not circumvented. Other indexed results exposed accessible Yonhap English and AFP reporting.

**Confirmed mass demonstration candidate:** Yonhap reports that the main opposition People Power Party held a street rally in central Seoul on Saturday, Oct 3, criticizing President Lee Jae Myung’s prosecution reform and government response to a DMZ mine blast. Nearly 70 lawmakers attended, and the party claimed about 200,000 protesters joined (`turn268view0`, lines 189–218; published Oct 3 at 18:43 Korea time). The turnout figure is the party’s estimate as reported by Yonhap, not an independent count. Accessible article: `https://en.yna.co.kr/view/AEN20261003003700315`.

AFP fact-checking published Oct 8 also says “tens of thousands” gathered in Seoul on Oct 3 for rallies demanding Lee’s ouster, noting separate rallies in the area and debunking an unrelated 2019 video falsely recirculated as 2026 footage (`turn268view2`, lines 19–35, 41–49). This supports occurrence of additional rallies but does not disambiguate attendance or exact organizing groups for each. Accessible secondary report by NV Ukraine repeats Yonhap’s PPP account and the party-estimated 200,000 (`turn268view1`, lines 10–36). A screenshot/video misrepresentation should not be counted as event imagery.

Other results concerned May Day, April union rallies, July strikes, and a teachers’ mass rally announced for Oct 9, all outside Oct 1–7 (`turn267search0`, `turn267search13`, `turn267search16`, `turn267search21`).
