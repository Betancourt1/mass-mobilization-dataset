## Candidate

**Candidate surfaced, supported by two accessible local news accounts:** On Wednesday 2026-10-07, residents in multiple neighbourhoods of constituency 15 (La Caverne–Phoenix), Mauritius, protested prolonged water cuts. The reports describe road blockades, burning tires/mattresses, residents assembling in the streets and demanding restoration of water; L'Express says actions lasted until about 23:00. Publication dates: L'Express via AllAfrica, Oct 8, 2026; Le Mauricien, Oct 8, 2026. Sources:
- L'Express via AllAfrica: https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202610080655.html (`turn200view0`, lines 3–14, 17–24)
- Le Mauricien: https://www.lemauricien.com/actualites/societe/coupures-deau-la-colere-setend-de-saint-paul-a-phoenix/719730/ (`turn200view1`, lines 60–76)

The record supports street assembly and collective road obstruction in response to a public-service grievance. Formal event-classification criteria remain pending the canonical scientific kit; this is a discovery candidate, not a final validated record.
