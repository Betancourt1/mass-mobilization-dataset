## Initial findings

Two completed public walks in Luanda on October 3 are supported by dated reporting:

1. **Caminhada Outubro Rosa / Caminhada Rosa**, Ingombota, Luanda — awareness walk for breast-cancer prevention/early diagnosis. RNA reported on Oct 4 that thousands participated on Oct 3 (`turn31view0`, lines 41–54). PRESSdigi reported Oct 6 that **more than 15,000** participated and credited the Government of Luanda as its principal source (`turn25view0`, lines 10–17, 27–34, 56). Count as mass public mobilization; it is not described as an oppositional/grievance protest. The two outlets report the same event, not two events.
2. **“Stop Rixa — Kilamba é um Lugar de Paz”** walk, Vila Flor commune, Kilamba — anti-violence/civic awareness walk. PRESSdigi reported Oct 5 that the Oct 3 event drew hundreds and was organized by the Kilamba municipal administration with the Municipal Council of Christian Churches; it attributes the report to the Government of Luanda (`turn31view1`, lines 11–18, 28–43, 60–66). This is civic mobilization, not an anti-government protest.

**Unconfirmed call:** A Sep 26 Portal TV Nzinga article says Movimento Icolo e Bengo em Acção announced a protest for Oct 3 in Calumbo over alleged land administration/sale irregularities (`turn22view1`, lines 58–65, 91–97). Searches for whether it actually occurred returned no confirming report in this pass. Preserve it as an announced protest with occurrence unverified, not as a confirmed event.

**Adjacent civic/political activity:** Search results surfaced the Movimento Cívico TODOS “Fórum do Cidadão,” held Oct 6, with multiple sectors discussing civic/political participation ahead of the 2027 election (`turn18search0`, `turn18search12`). This is a forum, not a reported street protest or mass gathering; no crowd size was found. A possible Malanje protest was discussed in an Oct 7 post, but sources described it only as possible and in connection with a presidential visit scheduled for Oct 8–9, outside the requested event dates (`turn22view2`, lines 24–42). Excluded from confirmed candidates.

These findings are **not evidence of absence**. They reflect a bounded search pass, with major discovery limitations noted in `query-log.md`.
