# Argentina — 2026 Q2 public discovery (provisional)

**Coverage:** April 1–June 30, 2026. This is a bounded initial pass for strikes and public demonstrations. Search results were judged by the event's date and physical location, separately from the source publication date. Accesses occurred October 11, 2026 UTC; see `discovery.json` for query strings, source URLs, provenance, dates, bounded excerpts and uncertainties.

## Provisional event leads

- **April 21 — ATE strike and protest.** LA NACION's same-day report describes workers gathering at Aeroparque Jorge Newbery in Buenos Aires, while the Buenos Aires Herald describes the nationwide stoppage and protests at other agencies. ATE had announced the action beforehand. The physical on-scene detail is strongest for Aeroparque; nationwide participation details should be treated as reported, not independently measured.
- **April 30 — labor march toward Plaza de Mayo.** A Reuters Connect public caption supplied by NurPhoto dates a march by members of Argentina's main labor confederation in Buenos Aires. This is a caption rather than full news coverage, and the page disclaims Reuters Connect verification. No image was copied.
- **May 12 — Federal University March.** EFE's May 13 article and image caption place the event in Buenos Aires on May 12 and report similar demonstrations elsewhere in Argentina. Organizers' attendance figure is not adopted here.
- **June 3 — Ni Una Menos.** Infobae's same-day live report documents a march at Congress in Buenos Aires and reports related marches in other Argentine cities.
- **June 26 — Kosteki/Santillán anniversary march.** TN's same-day report documents a midday march from the former Avellaneda station area to Puente Pueyrredón and a full bridge closure. A July 6 UBA-affiliated research roundup provides retrospective corroboration.

## Exclusions and limits

The March 24, 2026 Day of Remembrance demonstration in Buenos Aires and elsewhere surfaced in dedicated Spanish and English searches. It is explicitly excluded because its **event date** falls before Q2, even though later sources may mention it. Search outputs also contained unrelated dates, places and topics; those were not included. No source denial or access failure was encountered. Reuters Connect's licensable image is not included, and no source restriction was bypassed.

The source set is provisional and does not establish completeness. The portable scientific workflow/code kit is pending, so no formal validation, canonical record migration, or completion claim is made.
