# Senegal — Q2 2026 provisional public-source discovery

Six targeted searches were run on 2026-10-11 UTC, two per month in English and French. Queries and approximate UTC session brackets are preserved in `discovery.json`; the search tool did not expose exact timestamps for individual query execution.

AP’s opened April 8 report documents a Dakar march by workers, unions, and opposition supporters over government promises and rising costs. An opened APA article is the earlier schedule announcement, not independent proof of occurrence. A May 1 Dakar May Day march and June 29 protests outside the National Assembly were surfaced only in search results; the former is a social-media snippet and the latter’s AP page returned an internal error. They remain leads, not confirmed records. February student protests, July demonstrations, older protests, and diaspora events were excluded by date or geography.

Search non-findings are not evidence of absence. This is provisional discovery only; no canonical schema integration or formal validation was performed.
