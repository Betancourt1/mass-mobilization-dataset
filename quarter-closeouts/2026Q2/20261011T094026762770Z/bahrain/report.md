# Bahrain Q2 2026: provisional public-source discovery

Prepared 2026-10-11 03:06:23 UTC. This is a bounded public-source discovery pass for April 1 through June 30, 2026. It is provisional staging, not canonical data, formal validation, or a completion claim. Search coverage is uneven; an empty or inaccessible result does not establish that no event occurred.

## Leads and monthly coverage

- **April:** Targeted English and Arabic searches did not surface an individually dateable in-country demonstration. The Bahrain Center for Human Rights (BCHR) reports 43 peaceful protests in April in its first-half summary, but the opened page gives no event-level dates or locations. I retained that as an aggregate signal, not as 43 event records. April stories about March 1 demonstrations and March funeral-related activity are excluded because their event dates fall outside Q2. Bahrain's April 13 diplomatic protest to Iraq is also excluded because it was a state-to-state action.
- **May:** Al-Manar's Arabic report, published May 10, says mobilizations took place in several Bahraini cities and towns after arrests of Shiite scholars. The date is provisional from the report date; the source does not give individual locations, crowd sizes, or whether it is describing one or multiple gatherings. ACLED's September analysis says a chant supporting scholars had been heard in Bahrain's streets since the May 9 arrests but does not date specific gatherings in the opened passage. Government and advocacy sources give different arrest counts/categories, recorded separately in `discovery.json`.
- **June:** No individually dateable demonstration was found in this pass. BCHR reports four peaceful protests during June, but its opened summary has no event list. ACLED's later report covers a broader March-August trend. These are aggregate/trend signals, not event rows.

## Source claims and limits

BCHR reports 43 April, 23 May, and 4 June peaceful protests, totaling 260 for January-June. This is BCHR's monitoring tally; the underlying event list and methodology were not validated here. A May 10 Al-Manar story is a useful Arabic-language lead but needs independent event-level corroboration. An Al-Wefaq social post appeared in search results describing protests in Sitra, but the source could not be opened, so its relative date and details remain unverified.

The sources also contain official claims and denials. Reuters reports Bahrain's Interior Ministry said 41 people arrested on May 9 were linked to Iran's IRGC. AP records the government saying the death of Mohamed al-Mousawi was a heart attack and denying abuse and sectarian bias; rights groups and forensic accounts dispute this. Keep those statements attached to their sources and scope.
