# Sri Lanka — Q2 2026 provisional public-source discovery

Two targeted searches per month (English and Sinhala) were run on 2026-10-11 UTC. The exact query strings and search brackets are in `discovery.json`.

Two accessible June reports were opened: a residents’ protest at Sahaspura housing complex in Borella, Colombo, on June 3 (article published June 18/19), and a June 25 demonstration outside Colombo Fort Railway Station. A later CIVICUS Monitor search result points to a June 5 Tamil landowners’ protest in Valikamam North; the page was not opened, so it remains a lead rather than a confirmed item. April and May yielded no qualifying opened report in this bounded search. A Colombo social-media-only result was excluded for lack of corroboration; earlier-period and overseas diaspora results were excluded by scope.

Publication dates are kept distinct from event dates. Search non-findings do not establish absence. These files are provisional and have not been mapped to a canonical schema or formally validated.
