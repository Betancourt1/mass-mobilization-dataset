# Albania — Q2 2026 public-source discovery

This is a provisional, bounded first discovery pass for April–June 2026. The owner-confirmed handoff states that all three Albania country-month units are new, unclaimed, and have no partial or completed work. The portable scientific kit is still pending, so this is not a canonical migration, formal validation, or completion claim.

## Search and access record

The exact English and Albanian search strings, UTC windows, returned leads, and noisy/irrelevant results are in [discovery.json](discovery.json). At least two targeted searches were made for each month, in English and Albanian. The web tool returns no exact per-request time, so UTC windows bounded by adjacent clock reads are recorded. Public pages were opened where accessible; each page's publication date and the event date are separately recorded when available. Quoted excerpts are kept below 25 words per page. No access restrictions were bypassed.

## Provisional event leads

- **April 17, Tirana:** Anadolu Agency's photo page says an opposition demonstration took place in Tirana that day. The U.S. Embassy and OSAC pages were only schedule alerts; their planned event is not used as occurrence evidence.
- **April 14, Tirana:** A search result surfaced a Wikipedia timeline claim about a Lëvizja Bashkë action at the diaspora summit. This was not verified in an opened news page and remains a low-confidence follow-up lead.
- **May 8, Tirana:** Albanian-language pre-event reporting gives the rally schedule; a May 9 Dosja story describes the prior Friday evening's opposition protest and the same page contains May 8 live updates. Treat occurrence as a medium-high confidence lead pending primary-source review.
- **May 30, Zvërnec:** Reuters' June 3 report describes several hundred people gathering and clashing with private guards “on Saturday.” May 30 is inferred from the report date and weekday, not printed directly in that passage.
- **May 31 and June 1, Tirana:** Albanian Daily News' June 1 report identifies a Monday rally and separately refers to a march “a day earlier.” It is one source for two provisional dates; the May 31 turnout estimate is uncorroborated here.
- **June 2/3, Tirana:** Reuters and Al Jazeera describe demonstrations over the resort development, but their weekday text and image captions conflict between June 2 and June 3. Keep the date unresolved; do not merge the possible dates without further source review.
- **June 9, Tirana:** An AP-credited photo caption carried by Euronews explicitly dates a rally to Tuesday, June 9.
- **June 11, Tirana:** Euronews says “Thursday” in a June 12 article. The date is inferred and remains unverified separately.
- **June 13, Rrjoll near Shkodra:** Reuters reports a local protest against a separate resort project. It explicitly distinguishes this project from the Kushner-linked coastal development.

This list is not exhaustive. No cumulative movement totals were accepted, and search-result counts were not treated as event-level counts.

## Exclusions and limitations

Foreign-location rallies in the Albanian diaspora were excluded from the Albania event leads. A Balkanweb result that looked like a May 13 item was opened and found to be about May 13, 2017, so it was excluded as wrong-period. Embassy and police advisories are treated as schedules, not occurrence evidence. Search noise, failed opens, and missing results do not establish absence of events.

The Embassy page returned 403 Forbidden and the OSAC page returned an open error; their visible search snippets are retained with that limitation. Other selected public pages opened successfully. Counts, event boundaries, and date conflicts require further adjudication under the forthcoming scientific kit. No official database, original migration, Git, or publication changes were made.
