# Tunisia — Q2 2026 provisional discovery

Four events surfaced. On April 11, civil, political and youth groups marched in Tunis against racism and criminalization of civil society, according to a Reuters Connect/Sipa USA photo record. On May 16, Reuters reports hundreds protested President Kais Saied over arrests and economic conditions; the government rejected political-repression accusations and said it was enforcing the law. In June, Africanews/AP reports a march outside UNHCR in Tunis against undocumented sub-Saharan African migrants, and Reuters Connect documents a separate June 20 anti-racism/migration-policy march. The June 6 source says “Saturday” in its body, while an image caption dates a protest image June 5; both dates are preserved as an unresolved discrepancy.

The April and June 20 Reuters Connect records are contributor-supplied assets, which the platform says it has not independently verified.
