## Leads surfaced

- **Herat education protests, early April (date and count unresolved).** EL PAIS published a field report on April 20 with a caption dating a symbolic textbook protest outside a closed Herat school to early April. Its text says several similar acts occurred in the days before publication. Treat this as one or more candidate events pending date-level separation, not as a single confirmed row.
- **Kabul graffiti action, May 7.** CIVICUS Monitor's June 15 country page says members of the Women's History Transformation Movement painted protest messages about domestic violence on Kabul walls on May 7. It cites an Afghanistan International English post dated May 7; original post details were not independently opened here.
- **Herat/Jebrail protest, June 9.** Reports from AP, Reuters, The Guardian/Rukhshana, Etilaat Roz, Kabul Tribune, and CIVICUS converge on a protest over reported arrests of women and girls under dress-code enforcement and a forceful dispersal. They do not agree on crowd size or deaths. AP gives about 100–150 participants and at least three injured but does not mention deaths in its initial story. Reuters says one death and several wounded. The Guardian reports about 70 participants, two deaths including a child, three wounded, and at least 13 detained. Rukhshana's headline reports two killed/three injured, while its cited hospital source details one 30-year-old man who died after transfer and three injured men. Etilaat Roz reports dozens and at least three injured. Keep these as source-specific variants; do not reconcile them at this stage. Taliban/Herat officials denied dress-code arrests in Reuters' account.
