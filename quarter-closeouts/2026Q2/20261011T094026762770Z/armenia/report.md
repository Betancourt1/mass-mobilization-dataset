# Armenia, 2026 Q2 — provisional public discovery

This is a bounded discovery note for the three owner-confirmed new units, Armenia April, May, and June 2026. It is not a canonical dataset record, validation, or claim of complete coverage. All source excerpts are short (25 words or fewer per source), and each source record keeps report date separate from event date.

## Candidate events

- **April 9, Yerevan — driver-license protest.** Alpha News reported license-revoked citizens protesting outside the government building. The report is contemporaneous but appears under its Opinion section and is the only source found; the same-day event date is inferred from the page timestamp. [Alpha News](https://alphanews.am/en/protest-takes-place-outside-the-armenian-government-building/)
- **April 23–24, Yerevan — remembrance march with protest elements.** Euronews reports thousands in the torchlight march and says participants protested genocide denial and voiced frustration over Armenia’s relations with Azerbaijan and Turkey. It is an annual commemoration as well as a public march; whether to count it depends on the project’s event rules. Diaspora gatherings in London, Paris, and Los Angeles were excluded as outside Armenia. [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/video/2026/04/24/armenia-marks-111th-anniversary-of-armenian-genocide-in-yerevan)
- **May 4, Yerevan — protest during the European Political Community summit.** Reports identify Artsakh Armenians and opposition representatives demonstrating near the sports and concert complex. Demands included EU attention to Armenian prisoners in Azerbaijan, the rights of displaced Karabakh residents, and cultural heritage. An opposition announcement also framed the action around democracy, political prisoners, and alleged attacks on the Armenian Apostolic Church; those descriptions are attributed to organizers. Participant count remains unknown. [Caucasian Knot](https://www.eng.kavkaz-uzel.eu/articles/74963), [PAN.am](https://www.pan.am/en/332931)
- **May, Ijevan — Grand Textile worker strike/protest.** Azatutyun’s May 14 Armenian report says a protest at the factory over wages and working conditions ended with one worker arrested. EVN Report’s May 20 article places the second work stoppage in the prior week and links it to increased production quotas. The sources support a May event, but not an exact start date; the videos and arrests may refer to separate points during the episode. [Azatutyun](https://www.azatutyun.am/a/33756643.html), [EVN Report](https://evnreport.com/law-society/strike-against-the-sweatshop-migrant-workers-in-ijevan/)
- **June 14, Yerevan — opposition protest at the CEC.** The Kyiv Independent reports hundreds outside the commission as final results were announced, demanding annulment and a new election; Interfax also reports a mass opposition protest. Event date is June 14; both reports were published June 15. [The Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/armenia-releases-final-election-results-as-pro-russian-opposition-demands-new-vote/), [Interfax](https://interfax.com/newsroom/top-stories/118056/)

The June 3 Strong Armenia event was kept as a screening note rather than counted: Hetq describes it as a final campaign rally, without a separate protest demand. Election day itself was also not treated as a protest. Search results about the 2024 Sacred Struggle protests, 2022 protests, and older Armenian demonstrations were excluded as wrong-year hits. The bounded search did not establish a June church or labor protest; that is not evidence that none occurred.
